Day 3 — Social Media Captions & Ad Copy: Engagement Se Conversion Tak

Concept

Social captions aur ad copy do alag cheez hain lekin inka maqsad ek hi hai: audience ko attract karke unse action lena. Caption wo text hai jo post ke saath jata hai (engagement ke liye), jabke ad copy wo text hai jo direct conversion ke liye design kiya gaya hai (clicks, sales, sign-ups). In dono mein hook, benefit, aur CTA ka combination hota hai—lekin ad copy mein urgency aur social proof zyada hota hai.

Example

Ek Instagram post ke liye caption:
*"Ghar baithe paise kamane ka sabse aasan tareeka? 💰
Humara FREE ‘Content Writing Masterclass’ aaj hi join karein aur 10,000+ students ke saath seekhein! 👇
🔗 Link in bio"*

Ad copy (Facebook/Google Ads ke liye):
*"🚀 30 Dino Mein Freelance Content Writer Ban Jaeyein!
✅ 10+ Real Clients Se Kaam
✅ Har Mahine ₹50,000+ Kamayein
✅ 24/7 Support
👉 Abhi Apply Karen—Seats Limited Hain!"*

Practice

Apne favourite brand ka ek Instagram post ya ad copy likhein. Use 3-4 lines mein likhein, jismein hook (attention grabber), benefit (kyun follow/click karein), aur CTA (action) ho.

Mini Project

Ek 3-post series banayein (ek caption + ad copy) ek hypothetical product ke liye (jaise ek online course, fitness supplement, ya local service). Har post ka alag angle ho—ek educational, ek emotional, aur ek discount-focused.
