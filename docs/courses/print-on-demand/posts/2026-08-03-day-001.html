👕 Print on Demand · Day 01

Day 1 — Print on Demand Kya Hai? Basics Se Shuru

Concept

Print on Demand (POD) ek aisa business model hai jahan aap kisi product (T-shirt, mug, poster, etc.) ka design upload karte hain, aur jab koi customer order deta hai, tab hi wo product print hota hai aur deliver kiya jata hai. Aapko koi inventory rakhne ki zaroorat nahi hoti—sab kuch supplier handle karta hai. Yeh ek low-risk business hai kyunki aapko pehle se paise lagane ki zarurat nahi hoti.

Example

Imagine aap ek T-shirt design banate hain "Dil Se Pakistan" aur usko Printful par upload karte hain. Koi customer wo T-shirt order karta hai, to Printful usko print karta hai, pack karta hai, aur customer tak pohancha deta hai. Aapko sirf design aur marketing ka kaam hai—baaki sab supplier ka.

Practice

Apne mobile ya laptop se yeh check karo: [Printful](https://www.printful.com/) ya [Redbubble](https://www.redbubble.com/) ki website par jao aur dekhho ki kaunse products par designs upload kiye ja sakte hain.

Mini Project

Ek simple design banao (Canva ya phone ke drawing app se) jo aapko pasand ho—jaise "Pakistan Zindabad" ya "Love Pakistan"—aur usko ek POD platform par upload karne ka screenshot le lo (agar possible ho).
