Day 3 — **Effective Pricing Strategies for Daraz Sellers**

Concept

Aaj hum seekhenge ke kaise apne products ki pricing karni hai jo customers ko attract kare aur profit bhi ho. Price set karne se pehle market research, competition analysis, aur cost calculation zaroori hai. Zyada kam ya zyada zyada price lagane se behtareen results nahi aate—balance banaana hai.

Example

Ek seller 1000 rupees ka T-shirt bechta hai, lekin market mein same quality ka T-shirt 800 rupees mein mil raha hai. Agar uska cost 600 hai, toh woh 750-850 ke beech price set kar sakta hai. Agar quality better hai, toh 900-950 bhi ho sakta hai. Discounts (e.g., 10% off) lagane se bhi sales increase hoti hain.

Practice

Apne 3 best-selling products ke liye ek pricing plan banao. Har product ke liye:
1. Cost price (CP) note karo.
2. Competitors ki prices check karo (Daraz par same product search kar ke).
3. Apni ideal price range decide karo (CP + 30-50% profit).

Mini Project

Ek "Price Comparison Sheet" banao (Excel/Google Sheets mein). 3 columns hon:
- Product Name
- Competitor Price (lowest/highest)
- Apni Proposed Price
Is sheet ko future reference ke liye save kar ke rakhna.
