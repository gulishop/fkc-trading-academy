Privacy Policy

Aakhri update: 2 August 2026

FKC Trading Academy ("hum", "humara") is website ke sab visitors ki privacy ka khayal rakhta hai. Yeh policy batati hai ke hum kaunsi information collect karte hain, kaise use karte hain, aur aapke pass kya rights hain.

1. Hum Kya Collect Karte Hain

Yeh website ek open lesson-archive hai — koi login ya account system nahi hai. Hum khud se koi personal form data collect nahi karte. Agar aap kisi lesson ko WhatsApp, Telegram, ya Facebook par share karte hain, to woh sirf us platform (Meta, Telegram) ke apne share tools ke through hota hai — hamare paas is data ka koi record nahi jata.

2. Cookies aur Advertising

Yeh site third-party advertising companies (jaise Google AdSense) use kar sakti hai. Yeh companies cookies aur similar technologies use karti hain taake aapko relevant ads dikha saken, aapki pichli visits ke ilm ke sath — is site par ya doosri websites par.

  • Google aur uske partners cookies use karte hain (jaise DoubleClick DART cookie) ads serve karne ke liye, based on aapki visits is site aur doosri sites par.
  • Aap Google ke ad-personalization ko is link se manage kar sakte hain: Google Ads Settings.
  • Third-party vendors bhi apni cookies is site par ads dikhane ke liye use kar sakte hain — inke apni privacy policies alag hoti hain, jo hamare control mein nahi.

3. Third-Party Links

Yeh site WhatsApp, Telegram, aur Facebook jaisi external services ke share links use karti hai. In platforms par jane ke baad unki apni privacy policies apply hoti hain — hum unke data-handling ke zimmedar nahi.

4. Bachon Ki Privacy

Yeh site 13 saal se kam umar ke bachon ke liye knowingly koi personal information collect nahi karti.

5. Is Policy Mein Tabdeeli

Hum yeh Privacy Policy waqt ke sath update kar sakte hain. Koi bhi tabdeeli isi page par post ho jayegi, "Aakhri update" date ke sath.
