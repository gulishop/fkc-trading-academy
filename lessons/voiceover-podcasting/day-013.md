# Day 13 — **Podcast Editing: Advanced Techniques for Pro-Level Sound**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum advanced podcast editing techniques seekhenge jo aapki audio ko professional level par le jaayein. Basics ke baad ab hum advanced noise reduction, EQ adjustments, compression, aur dynamic range control par focus karenge. Yeh steps aapki voice ko clear, warm aur engaging banayenge.

**Example:**
1. **Noise Reduction:** Audacity mein "Noise Reduction" effect use karke background hiss ya fan noise remove karen. Pehle ek hiss-free portion select karen, "Get Noise Profile" click karen, phir pura audio select karke effect apply karen.
2. **EQ (Equalization):** Low-end rumble (20-200Hz) cut karen aur mid-range (1kHz-4kHz) ko boost karen taake voice clear ho. High-end (8kHz+) ko thoda soft karen taake harshness na ho.
3. **Compression:** "Compressor" effect use karen taake loud aur soft parts ka difference kam ho. Ratio 2:1 ya 3:1 set karen, threshold -18dB se -24dB tak adjust karen.

**Practice:**
Aaj apni kisi purani recording ko lekar Audacity mein open karen aur upar bataye gaye 3 steps (Noise Reduction, EQ, Compression) apply karen. Har step ke baad audio play karke check karen ki kya improvement hua.

**Mini Project:**
Ek 2-minute ka "clean" podcast clip taiyaar karen jismein:
- Background noise free ho
- Voice natural aur warm ho
- Loudness consistent ho (pehle aur baad mein same level)

**Answer Key:**
noise profile removed, EQ mid boosted, compression applied, consistent loudness
