# Day 5 — **Podcast Cover Art: Design Karna Seekhein**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke ek engaging podcast cover art kaise banaya jata hai. Cover art hi pehla impression hai jo listeners ko attract karta hai. Isme logo, text, aur color scheme ka balance hona chahiye taake yeh professional aur memorable lage.

**Example:**
1. **Canva** ya **Adobe Spark** khol kar ek new project select karen.
2. **1400x1400 pixels** (square) size choose karen (Spotify/YouTube standard).
3. **Background color** choose karen jo topic reflect kare (e.g., blue for tech, green for health).
4. **Title text** bold font mein add karen (e.g., "Tech Talks with [Your Name]").
5. **Relevant icon/image** add karen (e.g., mic for voice over, headphones for podcast).

**Practice:**
Apne podcast topic ya voice over niche ke hisab se ek simple cover art design karen (Canva ya Paint se). 3-4 elements (text + 1-2 images) se zyada na jayein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **300x300 pixels** ka thumbnail bhi design karen jo YouTube par use ho sakta hai. Isme title aur ek eye-catching element (e.g., arrow, highlight) add karen.

**Answer Key:**
title-bold, relevant-icon, color-scheme, square-dimensions
