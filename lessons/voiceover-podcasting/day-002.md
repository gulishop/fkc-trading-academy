# Day 2 — Script Padhne Ka Art: Emotion aur Tone Control

**Concept:**
Script padhna sirf words bolna nahi, balki unke peeche ki *emotion* aur *intention* ko samajh kar unhe aise deliver karna hai jo listener ke dil tak pahunch sake. Tone control ka matlab hai apne voice ki pitch, speed, aur energy ko script ke mood ke hisaab se adjust karna. Yeh skill beginner se intermediate level tak sabhi ke liye zaroori hai, chahe aap podcast host ho ya voice-over artist.

**Example:**
Ek simple line hai: *"Main aaj bahut khush hoon!"*
- **Happy tone:** Fast speed, high pitch, cheerful energy → *"Main aaj bahut khush hoon!"* (😊)
- **Sad tone:** Slow speed, low pitch, heavy energy → *"Main aaj bahut khush hoon..."* (😢)
- **Angry tone:** Sharp pitch, fast speed, stressed words → *"Main aaj bahut khush hoon!"* (😡)

**Practice:**
Apne mobile ya laptop ke recorder se 3 baar yeh line bolen:
1. Ek baar *excited* tone mein (jaise koi sports commentator ho).
2. Ek baar *whisper* karke (jaise secret share kar rahe ho).
3. Ek baar *dramatic pause* ke saath (jaise koi horror story sunayein).

**Mini Project:**
Ek 10-second ka *emotional* script likhein (jaise: *"Meri ma ki aawaaz sun kar mere aansu nikal aaye..."*). Uske liye 3 alag tones (happy, sad, angry) record karen aur compare karen ki kaun sa zyada effective hai.

**Answer Key:**
excited, whisper, dramatic pause
