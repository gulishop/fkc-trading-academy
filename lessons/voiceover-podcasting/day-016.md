Day 16 — **Podcast Hosting Platforms: Kaise Choose Karein Aur Setup Karein?**

**Concept:**
Podcast hosting platforms aapki audio files ko store karte hain aur unhe streaming services (Spotify, Apple Podcasts, etc.) tak reach karne mein madad karte hain. In platforms par hosting karne se pehle inke features (storage, analytics, monetization options) samajhna zaroori hai. Aaj hum dekhenge ki kaise ek achha hosting platform choose karna hai aur usko setup karna hai.

**Example:**
Jaise agar aapka podcast "Tech Ki Baat" hai, to aapko ek platform chahiye jo unlimited storage, detailed analytics, aur easy integration (Spotify, YouTube) offer kare. **Anchor.fm** (free) ya **Buzzsprout** (paid) aise platforms hain jo beginners ke liye suitable hain. Setup ke liye:
1. Platform par account banayein.
2. "New Podcast" par click karein aur details (title, description) fill karein.
3. Audio file upload karein aur "Publish" par click karein.
4. Platform se provided RSS feed ko Spotify/Apple Podcasts mein submit karein.

**Practice:**
Apne podcast ka hosting platform choose karke uska account banayein aur ek test episode upload karein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek 2-minute ka test episode record karein, usko hosting platform par upload karein, aur uska RSS feed copy karke Spotify ya Apple Podcasts par submit karein.

**Answer Key:**
Anchor.fm, Buzzsprout, RSS feed, Spotify submission
