# Day 10 — **Podcast Monetization: Sponsors aur Ads Se Paise Kamane ka Sahi Tarika**

**Concept:**
Podcast monetization ka matlab hai apne content se earning karna. Sponsors aur ads sabse common tareeqe hain, lekin inke liye aapke podcast ka niche, audience size aur engagement zaroori hai. Sponsors ke liye brands ko pitch karna, ads ke liye networks join karna aur performance-based deals (CPM/CPA) samajhna seekhein.

**Example:**
1. **Sponsors:** Agar aapka podcast fitness par hai, to protein powder ya gym equipment brands ko email karke sponsorship offer dein. Example pitch: *"Mera podcast 5,000 weekly listeners hai, aur hum fitness enthusiasts ko target karte hain. Kya aapka brand is audience ke liye suitable hai?"*
2. **Ads:** Networks jaise Anchor ya AdSense se auto-ads laga sakte hain. Example: 30-second ad break har episode mein, CPM (cost per 1000 listeners) ke hisaab se earning.

**Practice:**
Apne podcast ke niche ka 1 brand ya company dhundhein jo aapke listeners ko target kar sakti ho. Unko ek short email ya DM draft karein (3-4 lines).

**Mini Project:**
Ek "Sponsorship Pitch Template" taiyaar karein (Word/Google Doc mein) jo aap future mein use kar sakte hain. Ismein aapka podcast ka introduction, audience stats, aur sponsorship options shamil hon.

**Answer Key:**
pitch email, niche relevance, audience stats, call-to-action
