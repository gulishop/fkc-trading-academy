# Day 14 — **Podcast Promotion: Social Media Se Apne Content Ko Kaise Grow Karein?**

**Concept:**
Podcast promotion sirf upload karne se nahi hoti—social media par strategic approach se share karna zaroori hai. Har platform (Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts) ke alag engagement rules hote hain. Aaj hum seekhenge ki kaise apne podcast clips ko viral banane wale formats mein convert karke, hashtags aur timing ka istemal karke reach badhaye.

**Example:**
Ek 10-minute podcast episode ka 60-second highlight clip banaye. Usmein:
1. Ek shocking fact ya question add karein (e.g., *"90% listeners ne yeh galti ki hai!"*).
2. Caption mein relevant hashtags (#PodcastHacks #UrduPodcast) aur CTA (e.g., *"Full episode link bio mein!"*) likhein.
3. Post karne ka best time test karein (e.g., 9 AM ya 7 PM).

**Practice:**
Apne last podcast episode se ek 30-60 second ka engaging clip banaye. Usmein:
- Ek interesting moment (laughter, debate, surprising fact) select karein.
- Caption mein 3 relevant hashtags aur ek CTA add karein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek "Podcast Teaser Series" banaye:
1. Har episode ke liye 3 teaser clips banaye (15-30 sec har clip).
2. Har clip ko alag platform par post karein (e.g., Instagram Reels, Twitter, LinkedIn).
3. 1 week tak daily post karein aur engagement track karein.

**Answer Key:**
highlights, hashtags, timing, CTA
