# Day 15 — **Podcast Guests: Kaise Invite Karein Aur Unse Best Content Kaise Nikalein**

**Concept:**
Podcast guests se conversation ki quality badhti hai—kyunki unke experiences, opinions, aur stories listeners ko engage karti hain. Lekin unhe invite karne se pehle planning zaroori hai: guest ka topic se relevance, unki audience match, aur unke schedule ke hisaab se timing decide karni hoti hai. Guest ko prepare karna bhi zaroori hai—unhe episode ke goal, expected discussion points, aur technical details (mic setup, recording time) batani chahiye.

**Example:**
Ek fitness podcast host ko ek nutritionist guest invite karna hai. Pehle host unse discuss karega ki episode ka focus "weight loss myths" par hoga. Nutritionist ko 3-4 main points diye jayenge jo discuss karein ge (e.g., "protein timing ka role", "sugar ke alternatives"). Recording se 1 hour pehle host unhe remind karega aur mic setup check karega.

**Practice:**
Apne podcast ya voice-over project ke liye ek guest list banayein (3-5 names) jo aapke niche se relevant hon. Har guest ke liye 1-2 sentence ka reason likhein ki unhe kyun invite kar rahe hain.

**Mini Project:**
Ek "Guest Outreach Email" draft karein (5-6 lines ka). Ismein aapka podcast introduce karna, episode ka topic mention karna, aur guest ko invite karna hoga. Example format:
*"Hi [Name], I run [Podcast Name], a show about [topic]. I loved your work on [specific thing], and I’d love to have you on to discuss [topic]. The episode will be [format, e.g., 45-min interview]. Would you be available on [date]?"*

**Answer Key:**
guest relevance, outreach email, episode goal, scheduling
