# Day 8 — **Freelance Voice Over Projects: Kahan Dhundhein? (Fiverr/Upwork Se Aage)**

**Concept:**
Fiverr aur Upwork voice over gigs ke liye popular platforms hain, lekin competition bhi zyada hai. Iske alawa bhi bohat se platforms hain jo beginner-friendly hain aur niche-specific projects offer karte hain. Aaj hum in platforms ke alawa aur bhi sources explore karenge jo voice over artists ke liye projects dhundne mein madadgar hain.

**Example:**
1. **Voices.com** – Professional voice over projects ke liye trusted platform. Registration free hai, lekin gigs ke liye fee lagti hai.
2. **ACX (Audible)** – Audiobook voice over projects ke liye best. Audible par audiobooks publish karne ka option bhi hai.
3. **Casting Call Club** – Free platform jahan casting directors directly projects post karte hain. Voice over artists apply kar sakte hain.

**Practice:**
Apne liye 1-2 platforms select karen (jaise Voices.com aur ACX) aur unpar account banayein. Apna profile complete karen aur basic gigs/bids par nazar rakhein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek 1-paragraph ka voice over script select karen (jaise "Welcome to our podcast!" ya "Thank you for listening!") aur usko 3 different platforms par post karne ka plan banayein.

**Answer Key:**
Voices.com, ACX, Casting Call Club
