# Day 11 — **Voice Over ki Quality Check: Professional Sound Kaise Banaye?**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke apne voice over ya podcast ki audio quality ko kaise professionally check karein. Yeh sirf recording ke baad hi nahi, balki recording se pehle aur recording ke dauran bhi zaroori hai. Mic setup, room acoustics, aur editing ke baad final check—yeh sab kuch professional sound ke liye zaroori hai.

**Example:**
1. **Recording se pehle:** Mic ko 6-12 inches door rakhein, pop filter use karein, aur room ke background noise check karein (AC, fridge, etc.).
2. **Recording ke dauran:** 1-2 inches ke distance par mouth se mic ko point karein, aur -12dB to -6dB ke beech mein audio level maintain karein.
3. **Recording ke baad:** Audacity mein "Analyze > Contrast" se audio levels check karein. Peak level -3dB se zyada na ho.

**Practice:**
Apne aaj ke recording (ya kisi purane clip) ko Audacity mein kholein. "Effect > Normalize" apply karein taake audio -3dB tak adjust ho. Phir "Analyze > Silence Finder" se long pauses ya unwanted noise detect karein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek 1-minute ka voice over record karein (topic: "Apna Pyaara Ghar"). Uske baad:
- Room noise remove karein (Noise Reduction effect use karein).
- Audio levels ko normalize karein.
- Final file ko MP3 (192kbps) mein export karein.

**Answer Key:**
room noise, -3dB, normalize, 192kbps
