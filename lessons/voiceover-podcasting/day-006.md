# Day 6 — **Podcast Intro aur Outro: Professional Banane ka Tarika**

**Concept:**
Podcast ka intro aur outro uske "pehchaan" ki tarah hote hain. Intro mein listener ko topic, host ki personality, aur podcast ki vibe samajh aati hai. Outro mein listeners ko next episode ka teaser milta hai aur unhein subscribe karne ka yaad dilaaya jata hai. Professional sound ke liye intro/outro mein consistent music, clear voice, aur brand message hona chahiye.

**Example:**
1. **Intro:** "Namaskar! Main [Host Name], aur aapke saath hai [Podcast Name] — ek show jahan hum discuss karte hain [topic] ke rochak aspects. Har episode mein hum [format, e.g. 'experts se baat karte hain' ya 'real stories share karte hain']. Aaj ke episode mein hum baat karenge [topic]." (10-15 seconds)
2. **Outro:** "Yeh tha aaj ka episode! Agar aapko yeh podcast pasand aaya ho, toh please subscribe karein, rate karein, aur apne doston ke saath share karein. Next week hum lautenge [next topic ka teaser]. Dhanyavaad!"

**Practice:**
Apne podcast ka 10-15 second ka intro record karein. Use Audacity mein trim karke save karein. Intro mein apna naam, podcast ka naam, aur 1-2 sentences ka topic introduction shamil karein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek 20-30 second ka podcast intro/outro banayein jismein:
- Background music (free se le sakte hain, e.g. YouTube Audio Library)
- Apni voice (clear, confident tone)
- Brand message (e.g. "Yeh hai [Podcast Name], jahan hum [topic] ke raaz kholte hain")

**Answer Key:**
host name, podcast name, topic introduction
