# Day 4 — **Podcast Structure: Kya, Kyun aur Kaise Banaye?**

**Concept:**
Ek achha podcast sirf achhe audio ya topic se nahi banta—uski **structure** (banawat) se hota hai. Aaj seekhenge ki podcast ka opening, body aur conclusion kaise design karein taake listeners engaged rahein. Har segment ka purpose clear ho (intro, main content, CTA, outro) aur flow natural ho.

**Example:**
Ek fitness podcast ka structure:
1. **Intro (0:00-1:30):** Host ka introduction + episode ka topic ("Aaj hum baat karenge 'Home Workouts' ke bare mein").
2. **Main Content (1:30-25:00):** 3-4 points (e.g., "1. Gym ke bina exercise, 2. 10-minute routine...").
3. **CTA (25:00-26:00):** "Aaj ke episode ka summary, aur agle week ka teaser."
4. **Outro (26:00-27:30):** "Doston, yeh the aaj ke tips. Like-share zaroor karein!"

**Practice:**
Apne podcast ka **outline** banayein—3-5 bullet points mein likhein ki aapka next episode ka structure kya hoga (intro, 2-3 main points, CTA, outro).

**Mini Project:**
Ek **1-minute ka script** likhein jo aapka podcast ka intro ho. Use 3 lines mein divide karein:
- Line 1: Host ka introduction + podcast ka naam.
- Line 2: Episode ka topic/teaser.
- Line 3: "Chalo shuru karte hain!"

**Answer Key:**
host introduction, episode topic, teaser, CTA
