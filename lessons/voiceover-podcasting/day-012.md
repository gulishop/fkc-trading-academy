# Day 12 — **Podcast Script Writing: Storytelling Techniques**

**Concept:**
Podcast script likhna sirf information dena nahi hai—yeh ek *story* sunana hai. Har episode ko ek **intro, body, aur conclusion** ke saath structure do, jismein **conflict, emotion, aur resolution** ho. Tone control karne ke liye **pitch, pace, aur pauses** ka istemal karo—jaise ek kahani sunate waqt karte hain.

**Example:**
Ek *true crime podcast* ki script likhte waqt:
- **Intro:** "Aaj hum baat karenge ek aise case ki jo 20 saal tak unsolved raha..." *(slow pace, mysterious tone)*
- **Body:** "Police ko pehli clue milti hai jab victim ki diary milti hai..." *(faster pace, suspenseful)*
- **Conclusion:** "Is case ka sabse chilling detail yeh hai ki..." *(pause, then slow delivery)*

**Practice:**
Apne man pasand topic par ek **30-second script** likho jismein:
1. Ek *hook* (attention grabber)
2. Ek *main point* (1 line)
3. Ek *call-to-action* (e.g., "Aage sunne ke liye subscribe karen")

**Mini Project:**
Ek **5-minute podcast script** likho (kisi bhi niche ka), jismein:
- **Intro** (15 sec)
- **3 main points** (1 min each)
- **Conclusion + CTA** (30 sec)
- **Tone** ko deliberate change karo (e.g., serious → excited → calm)

**Answer Key:**
hook, main point, CTA, tone shift
