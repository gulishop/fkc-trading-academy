# Day 7 — **Podcast Hosting Platforms: Kaha Upload Karen?**

**Concept:**
Podcast ko Spotify ya YouTube par publish karne se pehle ek **hosting platform** chahiye jo aapki audio files store kare aur RSS feed generate kare. Bina hosting ke, platforms par upload nahi ho sakta. Popular options hain Anchor (free), Buzzsprout, Podbean, ya Libsyn. Har platform ke apne features hain—Anchor simple hai, Buzzsprout advanced analytics deta hai.

**Example:**
1. **Anchor.fm** par account banayein → "New Episode" par click karein → Apni audio file upload karein → Title/description add karein → "Publish" par click karein.
2. **Spotify par publish hone ke liye**, Anchor automatically RSS feed generate karta hai. Spotify par "Submit a Show" par jayein aur RSS feed paste karein.

**Practice:**
Apna ek **test episode** (5-10 seconds ka) upload karein kisi bhi hosting platform (Anchor/Buzzsprout) par. Title mein "Test Episode - [Apna Naam]" likhein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **3-minute ka test podcast episode** record karein (intro + 2 lines ka content + outro), usse hosting platform par upload karein, aur RSS feed ko Spotify par submit karein.

**Answer Key:**
Anchor, Buzzsprout, RSS feed, Spotify submit
