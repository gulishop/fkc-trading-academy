# Day 1 — Voice Over Ki Basics: Apna Mic Set Karein

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke voice over ke liye sabse pehle kya zaroori hai—ek accha mic setup. Ek behtar mic aur proper positioning se aapki awaaz professional aur clear aati hai. Basics mein microphone types (USB vs XLR), room acoustics, aur positioning jaise cheezon par focus karenge.

**Example:**
1. **Mic Type:** Agar aap beginner hain, toh USB mic (jaise Blue Yeti ya FIFINE K669B) istemal karen. XLR mic zyada advanced hain aur extra interface ki zaroorat hoti hai.
2. **Room Setup:** Ek quiet room mein recording karen. Agar aapke paas soundproof room nahi hai, toh ek blanket ya foam panels laga kar echo kam karen.
3. **Positioning:** Mic ko apne munh se 6-12 inches door rakhein. "Pop filter" use karen taake "P" aur "B" jaise words se aane wale harsh sounds kam hon.

**Practice:**
Apne mic ko connect karen aur ek 30-second ka recording karen—apni awaaz ko describe karen (jaise "Mera naam hai [X], aur main voice over artist banna chahta hoon"). Check karen ke awaaz clear hai ya nahi.

**Mini Project:**
Ek 1-minute ka intro script likhein (jaise podcast ya voice over ke liye) aur usko record karen. Try karen ke tone natural ho aur mic se 6-12 inches door rahein.

**Answer Key:**
USB mic, room quiet, 6-12 inches, pop filter
