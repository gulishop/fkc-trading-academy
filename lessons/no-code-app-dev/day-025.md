# Day 25 — **AI + No-Code: Smart Tools Se Kaam 10x Fast Karein**

**Concept:**
AI no-code tools ke saath milkar kaam ko 10x fast banata hai. AI se content generate karo, designs optimize karo, aur automation ko smart banaya ja sakta hai. AI-powered no-code tools jaise **Durable.co**, **Bubble + AI plugins**, ya **Zapier AI** ka istemal karke zyada efficient workflows banaye ja sakte hain.

**Example:**
1. **Website Builder + AI:** Durable.co mein AI se apni business ki details daal kar automatically ek professional website generate karwa lo.
2. **App Builder + AI:** FlutterFlow ke AI mode se app screens design karwa lo, phir unhe customize karo.
3. **AI Automation:** Zapier mein "AI by Zapier" action use karke emails ya messages ko automatically reply karwao.

**Practice:**
AI-powered no-code tool (Durable.co ya Bubble AI) ka istemal karke ek **1-pager landing page** generate karo. Apne business ya idea ke hisaab se prompt likho (e.g., "AI-powered fitness app landing page with dark theme").

**Mini Project:**
**"AI Assistant for Small Business"** – Ek no-code AI chatbot banao jo customer ke questions ka jawab de (tools: Glide + AI21 Labs ya Zapier AI). Chatbot ko apne business ke FAQs ya product details se train karo.

**Answer Key:**
AI prompt, landing page template, chatbot flow, no-code AI tools
