# Day 11 — **No-Code aur AI: Smart Combinations for Faster Development**

**Concept:**
No-code tools AI ke integration se aur bhi powerful ho gaye hain. AI se aapko code generation, content creation, aur design suggestions milte hain jo aapke no-code projects ko 2-3x faster bana dete hain. AI ke bina no-code tools bhi kaam karte hain, lekin AI ke saath unki efficiency badh jati hai.

**Example:**
1. **AI-powered design:** Framer ya Webflow mein AI se "Generate a landing page" button press karke ek professional design mil jata hai.
2. **AI content:** Notion AI ya Airtable AI se apne database ke records ke liye auto-generated descriptions likhwa sakte hain.

**Practice:**
Apne kisi existing no-code project (jaise Wix website ya Glide app) mein AI tool (jaise Gamma.ai ya Durable.co) use karke ek naya section ya page add karein.

**Mini Project:**
**AI + No-Code MVP:** Ek simple "AI-powered business idea generator" banayein:
- Glide ya Softr se ek basic app banaen.
- AI prompt (jaise "Generate 5 profitable no-code business ideas") ko integrate karein.
- Users ko idea input dene par AI response mile.

**Answer Key:**
AI prompts, no-code integration, auto-generated content, faster prototyping
