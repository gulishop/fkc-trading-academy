# Day 8 — **No-Code se Freelancing: Paise Kamane ke 5 Asaan Raste**

**Concept:**
No-code skills se freelancing karke extra income banai ja sakti hai. Clients ko websites, apps ya automation solutions chahiye hote hain lekin unhe developers ki zaroorat nahi hoti. Freelancing platforms (Upwork, Fiverr), social media (LinkedIn, Twitter), aur local businesses se projects mil sakte hain. No-code tools ki speed se tum 1-2 din mein hi projects deliver kar sakte ho.

**Example:**
Ek client ko ek simple business website chahiye thi. Tumne **Framer** se 3 pages ki website 4 ghante mein banadi. Client ko pasand aayi, tumne Rs. 15,000 charge kiye. Client ne payment diya aur tumne project deliver kiya—sab kuch no-code se hua.

**Practice:**
Apne LinkedIn ya Twitter par ek post likh: *"Main no-code se websites/apps banata hoon. 24-48 ghante mein deliver. Contact karein."* 1-2 logon ko DM karke poochh.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **Fiverr/Gumroad** account bana kar ek "No-Code Website in 24 Hours" service list karein. Pricing Rs. 5,000-15,000 rakhein.

**Answer Key:**
Framer/Webflow, Upwork/Fiverr, 5000-15000, 24-48 ghante
