# Day 21 — **No-Code ka Future: 2024 ke Trending Tools & Opportunities**

**Concept:**
No-code industry 2024 mein kaise evolve ho raha hai? AI integration, niche-specific builders (jaise AI-powered chatbots, e-commerce automation), aur emerging trends jaise "AI agents" aur "hyper-personalization" par focus hai. Students ko pata chalega ke kaunse tools 2024 mein sabse zyada demand mein hain aur kaise unse apne skills ko upgrade kar sakte hain.

**Example:**
*AI Agents Builder:* Tools jaise **Zapier + AI** ya **Make.com** ka istemal karke ek AI-powered customer support chatbot bana sakte hain jo automatically questions ke answers de. Bas AI model (jaise Mistral ya Llama) ko integrate karke, predefined responses aur real-time data se connect karo.

**Practice:**
Apne phone ya laptop par **Make.com** ya **Zapier** open karein. Ek simple workflow banayein jo jab koi email aaye (Gmail trigger), to uska summary AI (jaise Perplexity ya Poe) se generate ho aur aapko notification mile.

**Mini Project:**
**"AI-Powered Business Card Generator"**
Ek Framer/Webflow website banayein jo:
1. User se name, title, aur contact details input le.
2. AI (jaise **Canva’s AI** ya **Microsoft Designer**) se ek professional business card design generate kare.
3. User ko download link provide kare.
*Tools:* Framer + AI API (jaise **DALL·E 3** ya **Stable Diffusion**).

**Answer Key:**
Make.com, Zapier, AI agents, Framer + AI
