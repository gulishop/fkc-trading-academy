# Day 15 — **Fixing Exposure Mistakes: Shadows, Highlights & Midtones**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum Lightroom mein exposure ko correct karna seekhenge. Shadows (andhere), highlights (rooshni) aur midtones (beech ka balance) ko adjust karke photo ko natural ya dramatic look dena hai. Over/under exposure ko fix karne ke liye histogram aur basic sliders ka use karna seekhenge.

**Example:**
Ek photo hai jo zyada dark hai (underexposed). Lightroom mein:
1. **Basic Panel** → **Exposure** slider ko +0.3 se +0.7 tak adjust karen.
2. **Shadows** slider ko +20 se +50 tak le jaen (andhere saf karne ke liye).
3. **Highlights** slider ko -20 se -40 tak adjust karen (rooshni ko control karne ke liye).
4. **Whites** aur **Blacks** sliders se contrast badhaen.

**Practice:**
Apne phone ya camera se li hui ek underexposed photo ko Lightroom mein import karen aur upar diye steps follow karke exposure correct karen.

**Mini Project:**
Ek photo lo jo zyada bright (overexposed) hai. Lightroom mein **Highlights** ko -30, **Shadows** ko +20, aur **Exposure** ko -0.5 karke natural look den.

**Answer Key:**
Exposure +0.5, Shadows +30, Highlights -25, Whites -10
