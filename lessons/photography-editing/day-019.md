# Day 19 — **Creative Composition: Breaking Rules for Impactful Shots**

**Concept:**
Traditional composition rules (like rule of thirds) are great, but sometimes breaking them creates *more* powerful images. Learn when to bend or ignore these "rules" for dramatic, unconventional, or emotionally charged photos—like extreme angles, negative space, or off-center subjects.

**Example:**
For a portrait, instead of placing the subject in the center, shoot from *below* (low angle) to make them look dominant, or use *negative space* (empty area) to highlight isolation or loneliness. Try tilting the horizon slightly (Dutch angle) for tension in street photography.

**Practice:**
Pick a subject (e.g., a tree, person, or building) and take **5 shots**—2 following the rule of thirds, 2 breaking it intentionally, and 1 with extreme framing (e.g., macro or wide-angle distortion). Compare the mood of each.

**Mini Project:**
**"Rule Breaker Challenge"** – Choose one composition rule (e.g., leading lines, symmetry) and take **10 photos** where you deliberately *break* it. Edit one favorite in Snapseed/VSCO to enhance the effect. Post on Instagram with hashtags #RuleBreakerChallenge and tag your course.

**Answer Key:**
low angle, negative space, Dutch angle, macro distortion
