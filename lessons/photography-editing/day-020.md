# Day 20 — **Freelance Photography: Clients Se Deal Karna aur Proposals Likhna**

**Concept:**
Freelance photography mein clients se deal karne ka art hai—unhe aapki skills yaqeen dilana, pricing samjhana, aur professional proposals bhejna. Aaj seekhein ke kaise ek strong first impression banaen, client ki expectations clear karen, aur negotiation ke basic rules follow karen.

**Example:**
Ek wedding photographer ko client se call aaya. Usne:
1. **Listening** ki (client ki theme, location, budget poochha).
2. **Proposal** bheja (3 packages + unke features, pricing, aur delivery time).
3. **Follow-up** ki (2 din baad call kiya, doubts clear kiye).

**Practice:**
Apne ek imaginary client (jaise koi friend ya family member) ko message karke poochhein:
*"Mujhe [event type] photography karni hai. Aapki expectations kya hain?"*
Unki response ko note karen aur next step (proposal draft) taiyaar karen.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **1-page proposal** taiyaar karen (Google Docs ya Canva se) jismein:
- Project scope (kitne photos, editing level)
- Pricing (fixed ya hourly)
- Delivery time
- Payment terms (50% advance)
Include ho.

**Answer Key:**
client expectations, pricing structure, delivery timeline, advance payment
