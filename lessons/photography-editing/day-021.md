# Day 21 — **White Balance: True Colors Ko Capture Karna**

**Concept:**
White balance (WB) yeh control karta hai ke photo mein colors "real" dikhein ya na. Agar WB galat hai, to photo either too warm (yellow/orange) ya too cool (blue) ho sakti hai. Har light source (sunlight, bulb, shade) ka apna temperature hota hai—WB usko neutral white mein adjust karta hai.

**Example:**
1. **Auto WB (AWB):** Camera ka default setting—lekin kabhi kabhi yeh bhi galat ho sakta hai (e.g., tungsten light mein).
2. **Manual WB:** Camera mein preset options use karo (Daylight, Cloudy, Shade, Tungsten, Fluorescent) ya custom WB set karo (white/grey card se).
3. **Mobile trick:** Snapseed mein "WB" tool use karke photo ke neutral area (e.g., white wall) ko tap karke colors adjust karo.

**Practice:**
Apne phone/camera se ek white/grey object (e.g., A4 paper, grey card) ki photo lo—pehle AWB se, phir manual WB (preset ya custom) se. Compare karo ke konsi photo zyada natural colors dikha rahi hai.

**Mini Project:**
Ek hi scene ki 3 photos lo:
1. Daylight preset se
2. Cloudy preset se
3. Custom WB (white card se)
Phir decide karo ke konsi setting sabse accurate colors dikha rahi hai.

**Answer Key:**
neutral white, warm, cool, tungsten
