# Day 16 — **Mood Boarding: Visual Storytelling ke Liye Inspiration Collect Karna**

**Concept:**
Mood boarding ek powerful technique hai jo photographers ko consistent aur professional look achieve karne mein help karti hai. Isme different images, colors, textures, aur visual elements ko collect karke ek cohesive vision banaya jata hai. Yeh client projects, personal portfolio, ya editing style develop karne ke liye bohat useful hai.

**Example:**
1. Ek wedding photographer ke liye mood board banane ke liye:
   - Pinterest ya Google Images se soft pastel colors, candid shots, aur romantic lighting ke photos collect karo.
   - Ek Pinterest board ya physical board banakar sabko arrange karo.
   - Dekho ki sab elements ek "dreamy" aesthetic ko reflect kar rahe hain ya nahi.

2. Apne editing style ke liye:
   - 5-10 photos select karo jo tumhe pasand hain.
   - Unke colors, contrast, aur mood ko analyze karo.
   - Ek consistent color palette ya preset create karne ke liye in observations ko use karo.

**Practice:**
Apne phone ya laptop par ek digital mood board banane ke liye:
- Pinterest ya Canva use karke ek board create karo.
- 10-15 images collect karo jo ek specific mood ya theme ko represent kar rahe hain (e.g., "moody urban," "fresh and clean," "vintage film").
- Unko ek order mein arrange karo taake ek cohesive story ban sake.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **"Photography Style Guide"** create karo:
1. Ek mood board banane ke baad, 3-5 keywords select karo jo tumhara style describe karte hain (e.g., "warm tones," "minimalist," "dramatic shadows").
2. Ek simple 1-page document banakar in keywords ko explain karo ke yeh tumhare photography/editing decisions ko kaise guide karenge.
3. Is guide ko apne future projects ke liye reference ke taur par use karo.

**Answer Key:**
Pinterest board link, keywords: warm tones, minimalist, dramatic shadows
