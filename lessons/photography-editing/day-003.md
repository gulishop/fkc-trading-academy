# Day 3 — **Understanding Natural Light: The Foundation of Great Photos**

**Concept:**
Natural light photography is the simplest yet most powerful way to improve your images. Learn to observe how light changes throughout the day (golden hour, blue hour, harsh midday sun) and how to use it to create mood—soft, dramatic, or even moody. Mastering natural light means less reliance on expensive gear and more control over your shots.

**Example:**
*Golden Hour Portrait:* At sunset, position your subject with the sun behind them (backlighting). Use a reflector or white wall to bounce light onto their face. Shoot at f/2.8, ISO 100, and adjust shutter speed to avoid overexposure. Result: warm, glowing skin with soft shadows.

**Practice:**
Step outside right now. Identify 3 types of natural light around you (e.g., direct sun, shade, diffused light through clouds). Take 1 photo of the same subject in each light condition. Compare the mood—notice how shadows and colors change.

**Mini Project:**
*"Light Hunt"* – Spend 1 hour photographing the same object (a plant, cup, or building) in 5 different natural lighting scenarios. Label each photo with the light type (e.g., "morning side light," "overcast diffused"). Share 2 favorites with a caption describing the light’s effect.

**Answer Key:**
golden hour, backlighting, reflector bounce, soft shadows
