# Day 10 — **Stock Photography: Sell Your Photos Online**

**Concept:**
Stock photography mein aap apne photos ko online platforms par bech sakte hain, jahan businesses, bloggers aur creators unhe use kar sakte hain. Iske liye aapko high-quality, versatile aur trending topics par focus karna hoga. Aaj hum seekhenge ki kaise apne photos ko stock sites par upload karein aur unse earning karein.

**Example:**
1. **Topic Choose karein:** "Work from home setup" ya "Healthy breakfast" jaise trending topics par photos click karein.
2. **Platform select karein:** Shutterstock, Adobe Stock ya Alamy par account banayein.
3. **Upload karein:** Photos ko keywords, description aur category ke saath upload karein taake wo easily search ho sakein.

**Practice:**
Apne mobile ya camera se 5 photos click karein jo stock photography ke liye suitable hon (e.g., nature, lifestyle, food). Unhe apne computer par save karein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek simple stock photo shoot plan karein—ek topic choose karein (jaise "morning coffee routine") aur uske 10-15 photos click karein, har photo ko alag angle aur lighting mein capture karein.

**Answer Key:**
trending topics, high-quality, keywords, versatile
