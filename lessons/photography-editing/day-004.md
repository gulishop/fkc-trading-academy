# Day 4 — **Portrait Photography: Posing & Expressions**

**Concept:**
Portrait photography ka heart hai posing aur expressions—bas yahi hai jo photo ko zindagi deta hai. Agar subject natural feel kare aur photographer unki energy capture kar paye, to photo zaroor impactful banti hai. Aaj hum seekhenge ki kaise subject ko comfortable banaya jaye, natural poses find ki jaen, aur expressions ko guide kiya jaye bina forced lage.

**Example:**
Ek subject ko "thinking pose" ke liye guide karne ka tareeka:
1. Unhe koi interesting object (jaise phone, cup, ya book) dikhao.
2. Kaha: "Is object ko dekh kar socho ki aaj tumhara sabse productive din raha hai."
3. Camera se unki aankhein capture karo—yeh natural curiosity aur emotion laata hai.

**Practice:**
Apne friend ya family member se ek natural portrait lo. Unhe koi simple action perform karne ko kaha (jaise hair adjust karna, ya phone check karna). Focus rakhna ki unki body language relaxed ho.

**Mini Project:**
**"Emotion Hunt"**
Ek series banayein: 5 different expressions (happy, thoughtful, surprised, relaxed, confident). Har expression ke liye ek pose aur lighting setup choose karen. Har photo ke saath 1-2 word note likhein jo emotion ko describe kare (jaise "soft smile," "intense gaze").

**Answer Key:**
natural pose, relaxed shoulders, eye contact, soft lighting
