# Day 13 — **Golden Hour vs. Blue Hour: Mastering Time-Based Lighting**

**Concept:**
Har din ke do golden periods hote hain—*Golden Hour* (sunrise/sunset) aur *Blue Hour* (just before sunrise/sunset)—jahan light soft, warm, aur dramatic hoti hai. In waqt ki lighting se photos mein depth, mood, aur professional look aata hai. Har ek ka apna color temperature aur mood hota hai, jise samajhkar aap apne shots ko behtareen bana sakte hain.

**Example:**
1. **Golden Hour (1 hour after sunrise / before sunset):**
   - Light warm (orange/red tones), shadows soft.
   - *Example:* Portrait shoot—subject ko sun ke side se light mile, background mein bhi soft glow ho.
2. **Blue Hour (just before sunrise / after sunset):**
   - Sky blue, city lights glow kar rahi hoti hain.
   - *Example:* Cityscape photography—tripod use karke long exposure se traffic light trails capture karo.

**Practice:**
Apne phone/DSLR se *Golden Hour* aur *Blue Hour* mein ek hi subject ke 2 photos lo:
1. Golden Hour: Warm tones, soft shadows.
2. Blue Hour: Cool tones, city lights in background.

**Mini Project:**
Ek mini photo series banayein (3-5 photos) jismein:
- 2 photos *Golden Hour* mein leni hain (ek portrait, ek landscape).
- 2 photos *Blue Hour* mein leni hain (ek cityscape, ek architecture).
Har photo ke saath 1 line ka caption likhein jo mood describe kare (e.g., "Golden glow se zindagi ka fresh start").

**Answer Key:**
Golden Hour: warm, soft shadows, portrait, landscape; Blue Hour: blue tones, city lights, long exposure, moody
