# Day 5 — **Editing Workflow: From Raw to Polished**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum editing ke *process* par focus karenge—kaise ek photo ko "raw" se "ready-to-share" banaya jata hai. Lightroom mein basic edits (exposure, contrast, white balance) ke *order* aur *priority* samjhenge taake editing natural aur professional lage. Har step ka logic samajhna zyada important hai than just buttons dabana.

**Example:**
Ek *underexposed* portrait ko edit karne ka step-by-step:
1. **White Balance** fix karo (agar photo blue/cold hai, "Temp" slider ko right move karo).
2. **Exposure** badhao taake subject clear ho (agar zyada ho gaya, "Highlights" ko kam karo).
3. **Contrast** add karo (basics mein +10–20 hi kaafi hai).
4. **Shadows** ko lift karo taake dark areas detail retain karein.
5. **Sharpening** (Detail panel) se edges crisp hon.

**Practice:**
Apne phone ya camera se liye gayi *kisi bhi* photo ko Lightroom mein import karo. Pehle 3 edits (WB, Exposure, Contrast) manually adjust karo—*over-edit mat karna*, bas natural look chahiye.

**Mini Project:**
Ek *before/after* pair banayein: 1 raw photo + uska edited version (Lightroom preset ya manual edits se). Side-by-side compare karo aur doosre students ke saath share karo.

**Answer Key:**
White balance adjust, exposure +1, contrast +15, shadows +10
