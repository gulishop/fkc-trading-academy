# Day 14 — **Mastering Depth of Field: Blur vs. Sharpness Control**

**Concept:**
Depth of field (DoF) control karein ke photo mein background blur ho ya sab kuch sharp rahe. Aperture (f-number), focal length aur subject distance kaise DoF par asar dalte hain—yeh samjhaein. Portrait photography mein blur background (bokeh) kaise create karte hain, aur landscape ya street photography mein sab kuch sharp rakhne ke tips bhi batayein.

**Example:**
1. **Portrait:** 50mm lens, f/1.8 par shoot karo—background blur ho jayega.
2. **Landscape:** 24mm lens, f/11 par shoot karo—sab kuch sharp rahega.
3. **Street:** 35mm lens, f/5.6 par shoot karo—middle ground sharp, background thoda blur.

**Practice:**
Apne phone ya camera se ek hi scene ke 3 photos lo:
- **f/1.8** (blur background),
- **f/8** (medium blur),
- **f/16** (sab sharp).
Compare karo ke DoF kaise badla.

**Mini Project:**
Ek portrait shoot karo jismein:
- Background blur ho (f/1.4–f/2.8),
- Ek landscape shoot karo jismein sab sharp ho (f/11–f/16).
Dono photos ko Lightroom mein edit karo ke DoF effect aur strong ho.

**Answer Key:**
f/1.8: blur background, shallow DoF, bokeh; f/8: balanced blur; f/16: deep DoF, sharp all
