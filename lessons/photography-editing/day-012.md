# Day 12 — **Leading Lines & Framing: Guiding the Viewer’s Eye**

**Concept:**
Leading lines aur framing photography mein aise composition techniques hain jo viewer ki nazar ko photo ke important parts tak guide karte hain. Leading lines natural ya man-made lines hoti hain (sadiyan, rail tracks, buildings ki edges) jo photo ke center of interest tak le jati hain. Framing mein hum subject ko natural ya artificial frames (darwaze, windows, trees) se surround karte hain taki background distract na ho aur subject highlight ho.

**Example:**
1. **Leading Lines:** Ek long exposure shot lo jismein road ya railway track center se shuru ho aur photo ke end tak chale jaye. Camera ko ground ke level par rakh ke shoot karo taaki lines zyada prominent nazar aayein.
2. **Framing:** Ek portrait shoot karte waqt, subject ke peeche ek darwaza ya window ka frame use karo. Subject ko frame ke center par place karo taaki wo naturally highlight ho.

**Practice:**
Apne ghar ya street par 3 alag-alag leading lines wali photos lo (jaise stairs, fence, ya building ki side). Har photo mein lines ko subject tak guide karne ka notice karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek single photo shoot karo jismein **dono techniques** use ki gayin hon:
- Leading lines: Kisi road ya path ko use karo jo photo ke center tak chale.
- Framing: Photo ke edges par natural frame (jaise trees ya pillars) add karo taaki subject center mein achhe se dikhe.

**Answer Key:** lines, subject, frame, center
