# Day 11 — **Understanding White Balance: Fixing Color Casts**

**Concept:**
White balance (WB) controls the color temperature of your photo—cool (blue) or warm (orange). Incorrect WB makes whites look off (e.g., yellow under tungsten lights). Learn to set WB manually or use presets (Daylight, Cloudy, Shade) to match the light source.

**Example:**
1. Shoot a white wall under warm indoor light (yellowish).
2. Set WB to "Daylight" (blueish) → wall looks too blue.
3. Switch to "Incandescent" (tungsten) → wall turns white. Correct!

**Practice:**
Take 3 photos of the same subject under different light (sunlight, shade, indoor bulb). Compare WB settings: Auto vs. Manual. Note which looks most natural.

**Mini Project:**
*"White Balance Challenge"* – Shoot a neutral-colored object (e.g., white mug) in 3 lighting conditions. Edit WB in Lightroom/Snapseed to make it look white. Share side-by-side comparisons.
