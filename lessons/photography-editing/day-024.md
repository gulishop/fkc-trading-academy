# Day 24 — **Shadow Play: Portrait Lighting Secrets**

**Concept:**
Portrait photography ka raaz hai "shadows ka istemal". Aaj seekhenge ki kaise light ki positioning aur intensity se mood, texture aur depth badhate hain. Hard vs soft light ka farak samajh kar apne subjects ko 3D look do.

**Example:**
Ek model ko window ke paas khada karo (soft light). Ab uske 45° side se ek reflector (white card ya white wall) use karke shadows ko soft karo. Hard light ke liye direct sunlight ya flash use karo aur shadows ko harsh rakho. Compare karo!

**Practice:**
Apne phone ya camera se ek portrait lo—pehle direct sunlight mein, phir reflector ke saath. Dono ke lighting patterns note karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek series banayein: 3 portraits—(1) Soft window light, (2) Hard side light, (3) Reflector ke saath soft side light. Har ek ka mood describe karo (e.g., "romantic," "dramatic").

**Answer Key:**
soft light, reflector, shadows, 3D effect
