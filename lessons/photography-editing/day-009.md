# Day 9 — **Creative Composition: Breaking Rules for Unique Shots**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum composition ke advanced techniques seekhenge—jab aap rules ko break karke visually striking photos banayein. Rule of thirds ya symmetry jaise basics se aage badhte hue, hum seekhenge ki kaise unhe intentionally ignore karke bhi powerful images banayi ja sakti hain. Har rule ka purpose samajhne ke baad hi usse break karna hai—warna yeh "creative" nahi, "confusing" ho jayega.

**Example:**
Ek sunset photo lete hain. Rule of thirds ke hisab se horizon neeche (1/3) rakhni hai, lekin aap usse bilkul upar (2/3) rakh kar sky ka zyada hissa show karenge—jisse clouds aur colors ka drama badh jayega. Ya fir subject ko frame ke bilkul center mein rakh kar symmetry ka use karenge (jaise kisi building ke darwaze ka photo), jo minimalist aur strong look deta hai.

**Practice:**
Apne phone ya camera se ek photo lo jisme aap intentional rule-breaking kar rahe hain. Ya to:
- Horizon ko rule of thirds se bilkul opposite rakh kar, ya
- Subject ko bilkul center mein rakh kar symmetry ka use karke.

**Mini Project:**
**"Rule-Breaking Challenge"**
Ek hi subject ke 3 different compositions lein:
1. Rule of thirds (classic)
2. Horizon bilkul upar ya neeche (drama)
3. Subject bilkul center mein (symmetry)
Compare karen—kaun sa version zyada engaging lagta hai aur kyun?

**Answer Key:**
horizon upar, drama badha, symmetry strong
