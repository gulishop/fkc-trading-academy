# Day 18 — **Social Media Photography: Platform-Specific Optimization**

**Concept:**
Har social media platform (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn) ke apne alag rules hote hain. Photo ki aspect ratio, resolution, crop, aur even colors ko platform ke hisaab se adjust karna zaroori hai. For example, Instagram Reels ke liye vertical 9:16 format chahiye, jabki LinkedIn par square ya horizontal photos best hote hain. Alag platforms par engagement bhi alag hota hai—Instagram par bright aur contrasty photos zyada pasand kiye jaate hain, jabki Pinterest par soft aur pastel tones achhe lagte hain.

**Example:**
Instagram ke liye ek photo ko optimize karne ka step-by-step tarika:
1. **Crop:** Original photo ko 4:5 (1080x1350) ya 1:1 (square) mein crop karo.
2. **Resolution:** 1080x1080 (square) ya 1080x1350 (portrait) ensure karo—low-res photos blur ho jaate hain.
3. **Colors:** Vibrant aur warm tones (reds, oranges) zyada engagement dete hain, lekin over-saturation avoid karo.
4. **Text/Overlays:** Agar caption mein text hai, to photo ke bottom 25% mein place karo (Instagram text overlay restrictions ke hisaab se).

**Practice:**
Apne phone ya camera se ek photo lo (portrait ya landscape). Uske 3 alag versions banayein:
- Instagram Feed (1:1 ya 4:5)
- Instagram Story/Reel (9:16 vertical)
- LinkedIn (1.91:1 ya 1:1). Har version ko Lightroom/Snapseed mein edit karke save karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **"Social Media Content Calendar"** banao:
1. 3 alag platforms (e.g., Instagram, LinkedIn, Pinterest) ke liye 1-1 photo select karo.
2. Har photo ko platform ke hisaab se crop, edit, aur resize karo.
3. Ek simple caption banao (platform ke hisaab se).
4. Result ko ek PDF ya Canva poster mein arrange karo aur share karo (optional: class group mein).

**Answer Key:**
1:1 crop, 9:16 vertical, 1.91:1, vibrant colors
