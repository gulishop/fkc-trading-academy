# Day 7 — **Mobile Editing Apps: Snapseed vs. VSCO – Advanced Techniques**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum mobile editing apps ke advanced features par focus karenge. Snapseed (Google ka powerful app) aur VSCO (aesthetic presets aur film-like effects ke liye famous) mein deep edits karne ke tarike seekhenge. Focus rahega selective adjustments, healing tool, aur presets ko customize karne par.

**Example:**
*Snapseed mein "Selective" tool ka istemal:*
1. Photo open karen.
2. "Tools" > "Selective" select karen.
3. Adjustment point place karen (e.g., sky ya face par).
4. Brush size adjust karke exposure, contrast, ya saturation badhaye/ghataen.

**Practice:**
Apne phone se kisi photo ko Snapseed mein open karen aur "Selective" tool ka istemal karke **sirf clouds** ki brightness 20% badhayein (sky ko zyada clear banane ke liye).

**Mini Project:**
Ek photo ko **Snapseed** (advanced edits) aur **VSCO** (presets + film grain) dono mein edit karen, phir compare karen ki konsa style zyada pasand aaya. (Hint: VSCO mein "A6" ya "HB2" preset try karen.)

**Answer Key:**
Selective tool, sky exposure +20, VSCO preset A6/HB2
