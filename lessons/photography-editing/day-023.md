# Day 23 — **Natural Light vs Artificial Light: Har Scene Ka Perfect Balance**

**Concept:**
Natural light (sun, moon) aur artificial light (flash, LED panels) ki properties samajhna photography ka foundation hai. Har light source ki "quality" (soft/hard), "direction" (front, side, back) aur "color temperature" (warm/cold) ka scene par kya asar hota hai—yeh seekhna hai. Ghar ke andar, baahar, aur low-light conditions mein kaise light ko control karna hai, yeh aaj seekhein.

**Example:**
*Indoor Portrait:* Ek window se aane wali soft light (morning 9-11 AM) ko subject ke peeche rakh kar "rim light" banaya ja sakta hai. Agar light too harsh ho, ek sadee kapra (white sheet) window par laga kar soften karo. Artificial light ke liye, ek cheap LED panel ko 45° angle se face par rakh kar balanced lighting milegi.

**Practice:**
Apne phone ya camera se ek indoor shot lo. Pehle natural light (window) use kar ke photo lo, phir ek LED torch (mobile flash bhi chalega) se side light add kar ke doosri photo lo. Compare karo ki kaun si light ne scene ki mood badli.

**Mini Project:**
Ek 3-photo series banayein:
1. **Natural Light Only** (window se)
2. **Artificial Light Only** (LED torch)
3. **Mixed Light** (window + torch). Har photo ka caption likhein ki kaun si light ne kya effect dala.

**Answer Key:** soft, harsh, rim, balanced
