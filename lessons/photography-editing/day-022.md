# Day 22 — **Leading Lines: Composition Ko Dynamic Banana**

**Concept:**
Leading lines wo visual elements hote hain jo viewer ki nazar ko photo ke main subject ki taraf guide karte hain. Ye lines natural (sadak, nadi, rail tracks) ya man-made (buildings, fences) ho sakti hain. Inka istemal photo ko depth aur movement ka ehsaas deta hai, aur composition ko zyada engaging banata hai.

**Example:**
Ek road photo lo jo horizon tak chali gayi hai. Road ki lines viewer ki nazar ko photo ke center (mountain view) tak le jati hain. Alag alag angles try karen: low angle se lines zyada dramatic lagti hain, high angle se lines flat ho jati hain.

**Practice:**
Apni photo library se 3 photos nikalein jinme leading lines clear nazar aati hain. Har photo ke liye yeh bataye:
1. Line ka type (straight, curved, zigzag)
2. Line viewer ko kahan le jati hai
3. Line photo ko kaise dynamic banati hai

**Mini Project:**
Ek photo shoot plan karen jisme leading lines ka istemal ho. Example:
- Urban: Building ki stairs ya escalator
- Nature: Nadi ya path
- Abstract: Shadows ya reflections
Photo ko edit karen (cropping, contrast adjust) taake lines aur clear ho jayein.

**Answer Key:**
straight lines, horizon, depth, dynamic
