# Day 6 — **Color Grading: Mood & Style Banana**

**Concept:**
Color grading photography ka heera hai—yeh photo ko sirf "achha" nahi, *unka personality* deta hai. Isse aap photo ka mood (warm, cool, moody, vibrant) aur style (cinematic, retro, minimal) decide karte hain. Lightroom ya mobile apps mein yeh presets ke zariye hota hai, lekin samajhne ke liye pehle color theory ka basics hona zaroori hai.

**Example:**
Ek portrait liya hai—skin tone natural hai, lekin photo "flat" lag rahi hai. Lightroom mein:
1. **HSL Panel** → Saturation ko adjust karo (reds/oranges ko thoda kam karo, blues ko badhao).
2. **Color Grading Panel** → Shadows ko cool blue (teal) mein, highlights ko warm orange mein shift karo.
3. **Curves** → S-curve lagao (shadows dark, highlights bright).
Ab photo "dramatic" ho gayi hai—jaise koi film ka scene ho!

**Practice:**
Apne phone ya Lightroom mein koi 1 photo kholo. **HSL Panel** mein jake:
- Reds ki saturation -20 karo
- Blues ki hue ko 200 (teal) par adjust karo
- Shadows ko -10 brightness se dark karo
Change ko observe karo—photo ka mood kaise badla?

**Mini Project:**
Ek hi scene ki 3 photos lo (e.g., sunset, indoor, shade). Har photo par **alag color grading** apply karo:
1. Warm & vibrant (sunset jaise)
2. Cool & moody (rainy day jaise)
3. High contrast (cinematic jaise)
Compare karo—har style ka effect kaise alag hai?

**Answer Key:**
warm orange shadows, teal highlights, increased contrast, HSL adjustments
