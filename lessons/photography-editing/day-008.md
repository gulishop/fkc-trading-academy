# Day 8 — Social Media Photography: Platform-Specific Optimization

**Concept:**
Har social media platform (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn) ki apni requirements hoti hain. Photos ko resize, crop, aur format karna zaroori hai taake wo platform par best dikhein. Har platform par engagement badhane ke liye different strategies hoti hain—jaise Instagram par square/carousel, TikTok par vertical videos, aur LinkedIn par professional cropping.

**Example:**
Instagram Reels ke liye photo ko 9:16 ratio (1080x1920) mein crop karo. Pehle photo ko Snapseed mein crop tool use karke vertically adjust karo, phir export karte waqt correct ratio select karo. Caption mein relevant hashtags (#travelphotography #igers) add karo taake reach badhe.

**Practice:**
Apne phone ke gallery se kisi ek photo ko lekar usko Instagram Reels cover photo ke liye 9:16 ratio mein crop karo. Phir usko VSCO ya Lightroom mein edit karke save karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek hi photo ko 3 different platforms (Instagram post, Twitter header, LinkedIn cover) ke liye 3 alag ratios mein crop aur edit karo. Har version ka ek screenshot save karo.

**Answer Key:**
1080x1920, 1080x1080, 1584x396
