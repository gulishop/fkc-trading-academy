# Day 10 — "Chhote Alfaz aur Numbers Likhein!"

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke chhote shabd aur numbers likhna kitna asaan hai! Lekin yaad rakhna, tumhari khaatir koi bhi cheez likhne ya chhune ka haq sirf tumhara hai. Agar koi tumse pooche ke "Apna naam likh do" ya "Yeh number likh do", toh tum pooch sakte ho, "Mummy/Daddy se pooch lo?" Agar koi tumhe dabav de, toh zaroor batana!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Areeba ne school mein likhna seekha. Uske teacher ne kaha, "Areeba, likh '1, 2, 3'." Areeba ne muskurate hue likh diya. Phir uske dost ne poocha, "Areeba, likh 'mummy'." Areeba ne socha, "Mummy ne kaha hai ke sirf main apna naam likh sakti hoon." Usne poocha, "Mummy se pooch lo?" Uske dost ne haan kar diya. Areeba ne likha, "Mummy."

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi tumse pooche ke 'Apna naam likh do' ya 'Yeh number likh do', toh tum kya karogi?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhari khaatir koi bhi cheez likhne ka haq sirf tumhara hai—kabhi dabav nahin! Agar koi ajeeb lage, toh zaroor batana! 💖
