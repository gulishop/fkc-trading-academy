# Day 5 — "Mera Dil Khush Hai!"

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhein ge ke chhote jumle kaise banayein! Jaise "Main khush hoon" ya "Yeh ek ball hai". Ye jumle hamari feelings aur cheezon ke baare mein batate hain. Agar koi cheez aapko achha nahi lagti, toh aap "Nahi" keh sakte—yeh aapka haq hai!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rohan ne apne dost ko bola, "Main khush hoon!" kyunke usko ice cream pasand hai. Par jab uske dost ne usko zor se pakadna chaha, Rohan ne "Nahi!" kaha. Uske dost ne samajh liya aur chhor diya. Rohan ne apne ammi ko bataya—unko bahut khushi hui!

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi cheez tumhe pasand na ho, toh tum kya karenge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhare dil ki feelings sabse important hain—"Nahi" kehne ka haq tumhara hai!
