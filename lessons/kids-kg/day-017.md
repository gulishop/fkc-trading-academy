# Day 17 — "Aaj Seekhein: Rectangle aur Oval ki Pehchan!"

**Concept:**
Bachon, aaj hum seekhenge do aise shapes ke baare mein jinhe aap rozana zindagi mein bhi dekhte hain! Rectangle ek aisa shape hai jismein 4 sides hoti hain aur sabhi sides do do karke barabar hoti hain. Oval ek gol shape hai, jaise egg ya moon. In shapes ko pehchanna seekhna bahut zaroori hai taake aapko koi cheez samajhne mein asaani ho.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Ameer ke paas ek khubsurat notebook hai. Uske pages rectangle shape ke hain. Uske dadaji ne usko kaha ke "Ameer, yeh rectangle shape hai, jaise tumhare darwaze ya windows." Ameer ne gaur kiya ke uske school bag ka base bhi rectangle hai. Phir usne apne plate mein dekha ke uska sandwich oval shape ka hai. Ameer ne socha, "Aaj maine do naye shapes seekh liye!"

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, tumne aaj kya kya rectangle ya oval shape dekhe hain? Chalo, mil kar unke naam likhein!"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Jab bhi koi cheez tumhe pehchane mein mushkil ho, ya phir koi aisa shape ho jo tumhe ajeeb lage, toh foran mujhe ya kisi trusted bade insaan ko batana.
