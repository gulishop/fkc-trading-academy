# Day 3 — "1, 2, 3... Jor Kar Dekhein!"

**Concept:**
Bachon, jab hum do cheezon ko jodte hain, toh woh ek naya number ban jata hai. Jaise 2 aur 3 jodne se 5 banta hai. Yeh bilkul safe hai, jaise doodh aur roti ka combination. Koi cheez chori nahi hoti, na hi chhupana hota hai.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rani ne apne dost se 2 chhoti chidiya leni thi. Uske dost ne 3 aur chidiya de di. Ab Rani ke paas kitni chidiya hain? (Thoda ruko, socho!) 2 + 3 = 5. Dekh, kitna mazedaar combination hai!

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi tumse pooche ke tumne kitne rang ke kapde pehen rakhe hain, toh tum kya karogi?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhara jism sirf tumhara hai. Agar koi cheez ajeeb lage, toh foran ammi/abbu ko bata do—chahe woh koi bhi ho!
