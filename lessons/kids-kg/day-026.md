# Day 26 — **Din, hafte, aur mahine ke naam**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke din, hafte, aur mahine ke naam kaise hote hain. Har din ka apna naam hota hai, jaise Somvaar, Mangalvaar... aur har hafte mein 7 din hote hain. Yeh sab seekhne se tumhe time ki samajh aati hai—jaise tum kab school jaate ho, kab holidays hain, aur kab tumhare dost ka birthday hai!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Tumhari dost Ayesha ne kaha, "Mera birthday toh Friday ko hai!" Tumne poochha, "Friday kaun sa din hai?" Usne kaha, "Uske baad wala din!" Tumne socha, "Aah! To kal hi hai!" Phir tumne apne calendar mein dekha—haan, Friday hi tha. Aise hi tum bhi din, hafte, aur mahine ke naam yaad kar sakte ho!

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, tumhare favourite din kaun sa hai? Aur us din tum kya karna pasand karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Har din ek naya chance hai—kuch naya seekhne, khush rehne, aur apne aap ko strong banane ka!
