# Day 28 — "Apne baaray mein bolna!"

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke apne baaray mein kaise shaan se apna naam, umar aur school ka naam batana hai! Yeh isliye zaroori hai kyunke agar koi bhi aapko pooche, toh aap confidently jawab de saken. Lekin yaad rakhna, aapka jism sirf aapka hai - koi bhi aapko "na" kehne ka haq nahi rakhta.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rani ne apne school ke pehle din apne naye teacher ko apna naam bataya: "Main Rani hoon, main 6 saal ki hoon aur main ABC School mein padhti hoon!" Teacher ne muskurate hue kaha, "Bahut achha! Tumhara school kitna pyara hai!" Rani ko bahut khushi hui kyunke usne apne baaray mein poori shaan se baat ki.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi tumse tumhara naam, umar ya school pooche, toh tum kya jawab doge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Aapka jism sirf aapka hai - koi bhi aapko "na" kehne ka haq nahi rakhta. Agar koi cheez ajeeb lage, toh turant Ammi Abbu ko batana!
