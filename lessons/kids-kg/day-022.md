# Day 22 — "Ginti 1 se 50 tak"

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhein ge ke ginti 1 se 50 tak kaise hoti hai. Yeh seekhna bhi zaroori hai, lekin iske saath hi yeh yaad rakhna bhi zaroori hai ke tumhara jism tumhara apna hai. Koi bhi insaan tumse yeh pooch sakta hai ke tumhari umar kya hai ya tumhara naam kya hai, lekin agar koi tumse yeh pooche ke tumhara jism tumhara apna hai ya nahi, ya tumse koi cheez karne ko kahe jo tumhe achha na lage, toh tumhe foran ammi/abbu ya kisi trusted bade ko batana hai.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Ameer ki school mein ek naya bhaiya aaya tha. Usne Ameer se poocha, "Beta, tumhari umar kya hai?" Ameer ne jawab diya, "Main 6 saal ka hoon." Bhaiya ne phir poocha, "Tumhara jism tumhara apna hai?" Ameer ne haan mein sar hila diya. Bhaiya ne kaha, "Bilkul sahi! Koi tumhe jabardasti cheez karne ko na kahe, toh tumhe ammi-ko batana hai."

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi tumse pooche ke tumhara jism tumhara apna hai ya nahi, tum kya jawab doge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhara jism tumhara apna hai. Agar koi cheez tumhe achha na lage, toh foran ammi/abbu ko batana.
