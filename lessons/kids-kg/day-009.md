# Day 9 — "Kahani sunna aur us par sawal ka jawab dena"

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhein ge ke kaise koi kahani sunne ke baad uske baare mein soch kar sawal poochna aur jawab dena hai. Jab koi kahani sunte hain, toh hum uske characters, events, aur details par dhyaan dete hain. Isse humari samajh bhi badhti hai aur hum confident bhi mehsoos karte hain.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Aunty ne ek chhoti kahani sunai: "Bilal ne park mein ek chhota sa kutta dekha. Kutta thoda dhadak raha tha. Bilal ne usko pani diya aur uska dost ban gaya." Ab tum socho: Bilal ne kutta ko kya diya? Kutta kaisa lag raha tha?

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
Tumhari favourite kahani kaun si hai? Usmein kya hota hai?

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Har kahani mein kuch na kuch naya seekhne ko hota hai. Agar tumhe koi baat samajh na aaye, toh pooch lena—kuch bhi sharm nahi!
