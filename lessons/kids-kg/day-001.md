# Day 1 — "Chhote Alfaz Jorr Kar Padhna"

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke chhote shabd kaise jorr kar padhte hain, jaise 'cat', 'sun', 'bag'. Yeh seekhna zaroori hai taake tum aur achhe se padh sako. Lekin yaad rakhna, tumhara jism tumhara hai—koi tumse 'na' kehne ka haq nahi rakhta. Agar koi cheez ajeeb lage, toh foran ammi/abbu ko batana.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rohan ne 'sun' padha. Uske dost ne kaha, "Chup reh, nahi toh main tera 'bag' chura lunga!" Rohan ne ammi ko bataya. Ammi ne kaha, "Tumhara 'no' kehne ka haq hai, chahye koi tumse 'secret' rakhne ko kahe."

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi tumse koi ajeeb baat kahe, toh tum kya karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhara jism tumhara hai—koi tumse 'na' kehne ka haq nahi rakhta!
