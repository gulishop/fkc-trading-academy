# Day 4 — "1, 2, 3... Nikal Kar Dekhein!"

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhein ge ke kaise cheezon ko kam kar ke dekhna hai! Jaise agar tumhare paas 5 toffees hain aur tumne 2 kha diin, to tumhare paas kitni bachti hain? Yeh "tafreeq" hai. Yad rakhna, tumhare jism par kisi ko bhi haath uthane ka haq nahi hai—chahe woh koi bhi ho!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Billi Rani ke paas 7 makkhi thi. Usne 3 makkhi pakad liin. Ab uske paas kitni makkhi bachti hain? (Thoda ruko... 7 se 3 nikalo... 4 reh gayi!) Dekha, kaise kam ho gaya? Tum bhi apne toys ya mehengaar se aise hi khel sakte ho!

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi tumhe kisi cheez ke liye dabav daal raha ho, jaise 'yeh secret rakh' ya 'kisi ko mat batana', to tum kya karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhare dil aur jism ki hifazat sabse pehle hai—kisi ko bhi tumhare saath "na" kehne ka haq nahi! Agar koi cheez ajeeb lage, to foran humein batana. 💖
