# Day 2 — "1 se 50 tak Gintein"

**Concept:**
Bachay, gintein humari zindagi mein har jagah hain! Jaise jab hum school jate hain, jab hum khelte hain, ya jab hum khelon mein hisaab karte hain. Gintein humein samajhne mein madad karti hain ke kitna hai, kitna zyada hai, ya kitna kam hai. Lekin yaad rakhna, tumhari ginti sirf tumhari hai—kisi ko tumse yeh poochne ka haq nahi ke tum kitni gintein jaante ho.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rohan ne ek naya school bag liya. Usne apni ammi se poochha, "Ammi, kitne bacche school mein hain?" Ammi ne kaha, "Chalo, hum mil kar gintein!" Rohan ne 1 se 50 tak gintein, aur pata chala ke school mein 30 bacche hain. Usne socha, "Mera bag bhi toh 30 bacchon ke liye hai!" Gintein ne uski madad ki.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi tumse pooche ke tum kitni gintein jaante ho, toh tum kya kahoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Gintein tumhari dost hain—har jagah tumhari madad karti hain!
