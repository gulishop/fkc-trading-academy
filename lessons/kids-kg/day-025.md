# Day 25 — Chhoti jumlay banana!

**Concept:**
Bachay, aaj hum seekhenge ke kaise chhoti si jumle bana sakte hain! Yeh jumle hamari feelings ya cheezon ke baare mein batate hain. Yaad rakhna, tumhara jism sirf tumhara hai, koi tumhe 'na' kehne ka haq nahi rakhta. Agar koi cheez ajeeb lage, toh foran ammi/abbu ko batana.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Aman ne park mein ek lal ball dekha. Usne kha "Yeh mera lal ball hai!" Aur uske dost ne kaha "Main bhi khush hoon!" Dekho, kitne aasan jumle hain!

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
Aaj tumne kya koi aisa jumla suna jo tumhe pasand aaya?

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhare jumle tumhari feelings aur cheezon ke baare mein batate hain - unhe poori izzat do!
