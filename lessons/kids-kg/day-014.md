# Day 14 — "Ginti ka Khel: 1 se 10 tak ka Tafreeq!"

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhein ge ke kaise ek cheez se do cheezein nikalne se kya hota hai! Tafreeq ka matlab hai "nikalna" ya "kam karna". Yeh bhi ek khel hai jaise ginti ka, bas ismein hum numbers ko chhota karte hain. Yaad rakhna, tumhara jism tumhara hai, koi tumhe "na" kehne ka haq nahi rakhta.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Billi Rani ne ek din 5 mackerel machhliyan khariid li. Usne 2 kha liin. Ab uske paas kitni machhliyan bachin? (Thoda sochne do) Haan, 3! Tafreeq ka yeh khel hai.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi tumhe kisi cheez ke liye 'na' kehne ko bole, toh tum kya karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhara jism tumhara hai, use koi "na" nahi keh sakta! Agar koi ajeeb baat ho, toh ammi-abbu ko zaroor batana.
