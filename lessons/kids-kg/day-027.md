# Day 27 — **Aankhein, chehre, aur shapes ki pehchan!**

**Concept:**
Bachon, aaj hum seekhenge ke kaise hum apni aankhon se chehre aur shapes ko pehchaan sakte hain! Har shape ka apna khas roop hota hai, jaise rectangle (jaise notebook) ya oval (jaise egg). Inhe pehchaan karna seekhna aapke liye ek fun game ki tarah hai!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Aun Ali ke paas ek kalaai hai jismein ek rectangle aur ek oval shape ka design hai. Unhe pata hai ke rectangle ka har side seedha hota hai, aur oval ka shape gol hota hai. Jab woh apni kalaai dekhta hai, toh usko lagta hai jaise woh ek chhota sa puzzle solve kar raha hai!

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar tumhe kisi shape ke baare mein yaad rakhna ho, toh tum kya karoge? Kya tum apni aankhon se us shape ko dekhoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Apni aankhon aur hathon se shapes ko dekhna aur mahsoos karna seekhna ek accha tareeka hai!
