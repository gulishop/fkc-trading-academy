# Day 11 — "Chhote Alfaz Jorr Kar Padhna!"

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke chhote shabdon ko jod kar kaise padhte hain! Jaise 'cat', 'sun', 'bag' — yeh sab chhote shabdo se bante hain. Yeh seekhna bhi zaroori hai taake tum aur achi tarah padh sako.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Billi Mimi ne ek din dekha ke uske bag mein ek chhota sa 'sun' (sooraj) chhupa hai! Usne socha, "Main isko padh kar dekhti hoon!" Usne 's-u-n' ko jod kar 'sun' kaha, aur uske bag mein ek chhota sa sooraj jaise toy mila. Mimi bohat khush hui!

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
Tumhari favourite chhoti cheez ka naam batao — jaise 'pen', 'cup', ya 'mat' — aur usko jod kar kaise padhte hain?

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Chhote shabdon ko jod kar padhna seekhna, tumhari reading aur bhi strong banayega!
