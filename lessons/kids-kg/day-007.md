# Day 7 — "Aankhein Kholo, Shaklein Pehchano!"

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke kaise alag-alag shaklein pehchanein. Jaise rectangle (jaise notebook ya darwaza) aur oval (jaise egg ya moon). Har shakl ka apna ek khas tareeqa hota hai—kuch seedhe, kuch gol!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Aunty ke ghar mein ek pyara sa box hai. Usko rectangle kehlata hai. Aur uske paas ek chhota sa ball hai, jise oval kehte hain. Aunty ne kaha, "In shaklein ko pehchano, beta! Rectangle mein 4 side hain, aur oval mein koi side nahi—bas gol shape hai."

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar tumhe koi ajeeb shakl ya cheez dikhe jo tumhe pasand na aaye, toh kya karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Har shakl ka apna ek khas tareeqa hota hai—jaise rectangle ke 4 side, aur oval bilkul gol!
