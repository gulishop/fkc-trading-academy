# Day 23 — "Chhoti Ginti ka Khushal Add karna!"

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhein ge ke kaise 1 se 10 tak ki chhoti ginti ko jama (add) karna hai. Yeh ek aisa khushal tareeqa hai jisse hum apne roz ke kaam mein bhi istemal kar sakte hain, jaise ke do seeb aur teen seeb mila kar paanch seeb banana.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Babli ne apne school se do seeb aur apne dost ne teen seeb laye. Ab Babli ke paas kitne seeb hain? Agar tum do aur teen ko jama karo, toh paanch ho jayein ge. Yeh bhi yaad rakhna ke tumhari ginti mein koi galati ho sakti hai, lekin practice karne se yeh sab theek ho jayega!

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar tumhe koi cheez ya situation ajeeb lage, toh tum foran mujhse ya Abbu se batana, chahe woh koi bhi cheez ho. Kya tumhe yaad hai ke humne pehle bhi is baat par baat ki thi?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhari jismani aur manvi aazadi tumhari hai. Koi bhi tumhe 'na' kehne ka haq nahi rakhta. Agar koi tumse aise kahe, toh foran apne trusted bade ko batana.
