# Day 18 — "Apne Baaray Mein Bolna: Mera Naam, Meri Umar, Mera School"

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke apne baaray mein kaise achha se baat karein. Jab koi tumse pooche, "Tumhara naam kya hai?" ya "Tumhara school kya hai?" toh tum confidently jawab do. Yeh seekhna zaroori hai taake log tumhe pehchane aur tumhara khayal rakh sakein.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rohan apne school ke playground mein khush tha. Uske teacher ne poocha, "Tumhara naam kya hai?" Rohan ne muskurate hue kaha, "Mera naam Rohan hai, main 5 saal ka hoon, aur mein ABC School mein padhta hoon." Teacher ne uski taareef ki. Yeh sun kar Rohan aur bhi zyada khush ho gaya.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi tumse pooche ke tumhara naam kya hai ya tumhara school kya hai, toh tum kya jawab doge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhara jism tumhara hai, use kisi ko bhi chuna chunai ka haq nahi. Agar koi cheez ajeeb lage, toh foran ammi/abbu ko batana.
