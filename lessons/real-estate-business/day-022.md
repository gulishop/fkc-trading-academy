# Day 22 — **Property Deal Karne Ka Legal Framework: Registration aur Compliance**

**Concept:**
Har property deal ke liye legal compliance zaroori hai. Pakistan mein property deal karne ke liye **Sales Deed, Transfer of Property Act, Stamp Duty, aur Registration** jaise steps follow karne hote hain. Agar legal process nahi samjoge to deal future mein problems create kar sakti hai.

**Example:**
1. **Sales Deed Banwana:** Jab koi property beche, to **Sales Deed** (registry paper) banwana zaroori hai. Isme property details, buyer/seller ki details, aur price mention hoti hai.
2. **Stamp Duty Pay Karna:** Government ko **1-2%** property value ka stamp duty dena hota hai (har province mein alag).
3. **Registry Karna:** Deed ko **Sub-Registrar Office** mein register karwana hota hai. Bina registry ke deal legally valid nahi hoti.

**Practice:**
Apne local **Sub-Registrar Office** se poochhein:
- **Stamp Duty ki rate** kya hai aapke area mein?
- **Registry ke liye** kon kon se documents chahiye?

**Mini Project:**
Ek **hypothetical property deal** ka case study taiyar karo:
- Property ki details (location, size, price)
- Buyer/seller ke documents
- Stamp duty aur registry process ka flowchart

**Answer Key:**
Sales Deed, Stamp Duty, Registry, Sub-Registrar Office
