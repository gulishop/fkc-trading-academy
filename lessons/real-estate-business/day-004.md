# Day 4 — **Property Valuation: 3 Asaan Tarike Gharelu Jaankari Se Professional Tak**

**Concept:**
Property ki sahi valuation samajhna real estate ka backbone hai. Gharelu level par aap apne ghar ya doston ki property ke rates check kar sakte hain, lekin professional valuation ke liye 3 key methods hain: **Market Comparison (CMA), Cost Approach, aur Income Approach**. Har method ki apni importance hai—jaise CMA market ke current rates se milti hai, cost approach se construction cost nikalte hain, aur income approach se rental income ka analysis hota hai.

**Example:**
1. **CMA (Comparative Market Analysis):** Apne area ki 3-4 similar properties ke rates check karo (size, location, condition same honi chahiye). Jaise Lahore ke Johar Town mein 10 marla house ka 2023 ka rate Rs. 1.2 crore tha, 2024 mein Rs. 1.35 crore ho sakta hai.
2. **Cost Approach:** Land ki value + construction cost (Rs. 3000/sq ft) = Total value. Agar 10 marla (250 sq yd = 2250 sq ft) ka plot Rs. 50 lac ka hai aur construction Rs. 67.5 lac ka, to total Rs. 1.175 crore.
3. **Income Approach:** Agar property rent par hai, to "Capitalization Rate" (6-8% typically) se value nikalte hain. Jaise Rs. 50,000/month rent par 7% rate se value = (50,000 x 12) / 0.07 = Rs. 85.7 lac.

**Practice:**
Apne ghar ya kisi dost ke plot ki valuation 3 methods se nikalo. Har method ka result note karo aur compare karo—kya difference hai?

**Mini Project:**
Ek **Property Valuation Report** taiyar karo (1 page) jisme:
- Property details (size, location, age)
- 3 methods se calculated value
- Final valuation (average ya weighted)
- Recommendation (buy/sell/hold)

**Answer Key:**
CMA, Cost Approach, Income Approach, Market Rates, Construction Cost
