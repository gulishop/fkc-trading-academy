# Day 9 — **Property Investment Strategies: Safe vs. High-Return Approaches**

**Concept:**
Property investment mein do main approaches hain: *safe (low-risk)* aur *high-return (high-risk)*. Safe investments mein steady income (rental) aur long-term appreciation (land, residential) shamil hain, jabke high-return options mein commercial properties, under-construction projects, ya flipping (quick buy-sell) aate hain. Risk tolerance aur financial goals ke hisaab se strategy choose karni chahiye.

**Example:**
*Safe Approach:* 2020 mein Lahore ke Defence Housing Authority (DHA) mein 5 marla plot ₹5M mein khareeda. 2024 tak uski value ₹12M ho gayi, aur monthly rent ₹50,000 mil raha hai.
*High-Return Approach:* 2023 mein Rawalpindi ke under-construction commercial plaza ₹20M mein khareeda. 2025 tak completion hone par uski value ₹40M tak pohanch sakti hai, lekin risk hai construction delay ya market crash ka.

**Practice:**
Apne local market mein do properties dhundhain:
1) Ek "safe" investment (e.g., residential plot ya fully constructed house).
2) Ek "high-return" opportunity (e.g., under-construction project ya commercial property).
Dono ke pros-cons aur estimated ROI (Return on Investment) ka hisaab likhein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **Property Investment Plan** banayein:
- Apna budget batayein (e.g., ₹5M).
- 3 potential properties select karin (1 safe, 1 high-return, 1 hybrid).
- Har ek ke liye 1-page summary likhein: location, expected ROI, risks, aur exit strategy.

**Answer Key:**
Safe: residential, rental income, long-term, low volatility; High-return: commercial, under-construction, high risk, quick profit
