# Day 19 — **Property Investment Strategies: Safe aur Profitable Rastoon Ka Tajurba**

**Concept:**
Property investment mein success ka raaz hai *strategic planning* aur *risk management*. Har investor ka goal hota hai maximum return with minimum risk. Is lesson mein hum 3 key strategies par focus karenge: **Buy-to-Let (Rental Income)**, **Fix & Flip (Renovation Profit)**, aur **Long-Term Appreciation (Land Banking)**. Har strategy ke apne faide aur challenges hain—jaise capital requirement, market timing, aur legal complications.

**Example:**
*Scenario:* Aapke paas Rs. 5M hain aur Lahore mein ek 2-marla plot mil raha hai Rs. 4M ka. Aap isko 3 tareeqon se invest kar sakte hain:
1. **Buy-to-Let:** Plot par 2-storey house banwa kar Rs. 50K/month rent par de dein (ROI: 15% annually).
2. **Fix & Flip:** House banwa kar Rs. 6M mein bech dein (Profit: Rs. 2M, minus construction cost).
3. **Land Banking:** Plot ko 5 saal tak hold kar ke Rs. 8M mein bech dein (ROI: 100% over 5 years).

**Practice:**
Apne local market se ek *undervalued property* (jaise old house, commercial plot, ya farm land) identify karein jo in 3 strategies mein se kisi ek ke liye suitable ho. 2 reasons batayein kyun yeh property aapki strategy ke liye best hai.

**Mini Project:**
*Property Comparison Chart* banayein:
- 3 properties (different types/locations) select karein.
- Har property ke liye calculate karein:
  - Initial investment
  - Expected ROI (1/3/5 years)
  - Risk level (Low/Medium/High)
- Final decision: Konsi property sabse behtar hai aur kyun?

**Answer Key:**
undervalued property, ROI calculation, risk assessment, final decision
