# Day 14 — **Property Dealings Mein "Fraud Detection" Kaise Karein**

**Concept:**
Property frauds mein fake documents, forged signatures, ya maloom na ho kar galat land records shamil hain. Is lesson mein seekhainge ki kaise asli aur fake documents pehchanen, red flags identify karen, aur apne clients ko fraud se bachane ke liye kya check karna chahiye.

**Example:**
Ek buyer ne Islamabad ki ek property khareedi. Jab documents verify kiye gaye, to pata chala ki seller ka naam "Ali Ahmed" hai, lekin property record mein "Ali Ahmad" likha hai. Ye small spelling difference fraud ka sign ho sakta hai—kyunki documents mein consistency hona chahiye.

**Practice:**
Apne local property dealer se poochhein: *"Aapke paas koi aisa case aaya hai jisme documents mein inconsistency thi?"* Aur yeh note karen ki unke experience se kya common fraud patterns hain.

**Mini Project:**
Ek fake property document (jaise fake sale deed ya forged power of attorney) ko online search karke dekhain aur uske red flags (jaise poor print quality, inconsistent fonts, ya missing stamps) note karen.

**Answer Key:**
spelling mismatch, poor print quality, missing stamps, inconsistent fonts
