# Day 17 — **Negotiation Skills: Property Deal Karne Ka Art**

**Concept:**
Negotiation real estate business ka backbone hai. Yeh ek art hai jismein aapki communication, patience aur strategy ka combination hota hai. Aapko buyer aur seller ke interests ko balance karna hai taake dono parties ko win-win situation mile. Aapki body language, tone aur timing bhi negotiation mein crucial role play karte hain.

**Example:**
Imagine a seller ne property ka price Rs. 50,000 zyada rakha hai. Aapko pata hai market mein uski value Rs. 40,000 zyada hai. Aap poochhein: *"Sir, aapko property ka price Rs. 50,000 zyada lag raha hai, lekin market mein uski value Rs. 40,000 zyada hai. Kya hum Rs. 45,000 par agree ho sakte hain?"* Isse seller ko lagta hai aap realistic ho aur negotiation fair hai.

**Practice:**
Apne kisi friend ya family member ke saath ek mock negotiation session karo. Ek role seller ka aur ek role buyer ka nibhao. Try karo ki dono parties ko satisfied karne ki koshish karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek property listing ko analyze karo aur uske liye negotiation strategy likho. Consider karo:
- Property ki actual market value
- Seller ki expectations
- Buyer ki budget
- Possible counter-offers

**Answer Key:**
market value, seller expectations, buyer budget, counter-offer
