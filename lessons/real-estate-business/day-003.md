# Day 3 — Legal Documents aur Property Verification: Sabse Pehle "Haan" Kyon?

**Concept:**
Property deal karte waqt sabse risky cheez hoti hai *legal documents* aur *property verification*. Agar koi document fake hai ya property par koi *encumbrance* (jaise loan, dispute, ya government ka notice) hai, to aapka investment aur reputation donon khatre mein pad sakta hai. Is lesson mein hum seekhenge ki kaise *Fard Malkiat* (ownership deed), *Mutation*, *Encumbrance Certificate*, aur *Power of Attorney* verify karein. Pakistan mein *Land Revenue Department* aur *District Courts* ki records check karna seekhna zaroori hai.

**Example:**
Maulvi Sahab ne 5 kanal ki zameen bechi, lekin jab buyer ne *Encumbrance Certificate* check kiya, pata chala ki zameen par *bank ka loan* hai jo abhi tak clear nahi hua. Isliye *Mutation* aur *Fard* ke saath *No Objection Certificate (NOC)* bhi lena zaroori hai. Agar seller NOC nahi de raha, to deal cancel kar deni chahiye.

**Practice:**
Apne ghar ke *Fard Malkiat* (ownership deed) ka *serial number* aur *khewat number* note karen. Agar aapke paas zameen hai, to *Land Revenue Office* jaakar *Mutation* ki copy lekar aayein. Agar nahi hai, to kisi relative ya friend ki zameen ke documents ka *format* dekhein.
