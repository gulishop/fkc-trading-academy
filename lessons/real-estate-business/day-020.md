# Day 20 — **Property Business Ko Scale Karne Ke 3 Powerful Ways**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke apni property business ko kaise grow karain—team banane se lekar office setup tak. Pehle freelance agent se lekar team-based business banane tak ka safar samjhein. Har step par focus hai efficiency, trust, aur long-term growth par.

**Example:**
Ek agent shuru mein akela kaam karta hai (1-2 deals/month). Phir 1 assistant rakhta hai (leads handle karne ke liye), phir 1 sales executive (client meetings ke liye). End mein 5-10 agents ka team banakar har mahine 20+ deals karne lagta hai.

**Practice:**
Apne current business model ka analysis karain:
1. Kitne deals aap akela handle kar sakte hain?
2. Kitne leads ko follow-up ki zarurat hai lekin aap time nahi de sakte?
3. Kya aapko ek assistant ya sales person ki zarurat hai?

**Mini Project:**
Ek simple **Team Hiring Plan** taiyar karain:
- Role: Assistant (leads filter karne ke liye)
- Salary: Rs. 20,000–30,000/month (local market ka hisab)
- Kya skills chahiye? (Excel, WhatsApp/Call handling, basic property knowledge)

**Answer Key:**
freelance se team, assistant role, leads filter, Rs. 20K-30K salary
