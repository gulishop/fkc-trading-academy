# Day 6 — **Property Dealings Mein Digital Presence: Online Se Le Kar Offline Tak**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke property marketing mein digital tools kaise use kiye jaen—online listings se le kar social media tak. Zyada buyers/tenants online search karte hain, isliye ek strong digital presence zaruri hai. Ismein property ke photos, videos, aur accurate details share karna shamil hai.

**Example:**
Ek 5 marla plot Lahore ke Johar Town ka hai. Aap:
1. **Zameen.com** ya **OLX** par listing banayein (₨1.2 crore, 10 marla, possession ready).
2. **Instagram/Facebook** par reel banayein—plot ke 360° video, location map, aur amenities show kartein hain.
3. **WhatsApp Business** par auto-reply set karen (e.g., "AOA! Plot details share ki ja rahi hain…").

**Practice:**
Apne mobile se ek 10-second video banayein—ek property (ya koi chhota sa model) ka 360° tour. Video ke caption mein property ke 3 key points likhein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **Google Form** banayein jismein property ke 5 key details (price, size, location, amenities, contact) poochhein. Link ko WhatsApp groups ya Facebook par share karen aur 3 responses collect karen.

**Answer Key:**
property details, 360° video, auto-reply, Google Form
