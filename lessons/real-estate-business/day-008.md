# Day 8 — **Rental Property Management: 3 Key Rules for Smooth Operations**

**Concept:**
Rental property management ka matlab hai kisi property ko tenant ke zariye use karne ke liye maintain karna, rent collect karna, aur property ki dekhbhaal karna. Isme tenant selection, rent pricing, maintenance, aur legal compliance shamil hain. Agar aap ek property dealer hain ya investor, toh rental management aapki income ka ek major source hai.

**Example:**
Imagine aapke paas Lahore mein 2 marla ka flat hai. Aapko ek reliable tenant chahiye jo time par rent de aur property ko damage na kare. Pehle, aapko market research karna hoga: similar flats ka average rent kitna hai? (e.g., Rs. 25,000–30,000). Phir, tenant ke liye criteria set karo (e.g., salary slip, reference check). Agar tenant mil jata hai, toh rent agreement banana hoga (11 months ka, stamp duty ke saath).

**Practice:**
Apne area mein 3 rental properties ka rent rate find karo (Zameen.com ya OLX se). 1 property ka rent agreement ka draft likho (terms: rent, security deposit, maintenance charges).

**Mini Project:**
Ek simple **Rental Property Checklist** banayein jismein shamil ho:
1. Tenant screening criteria (salary, job, references)
2. Rent agreement ke essential points
3. Monthly maintenance checklist (e.g., plumbing, electricity)

**Answer Key:**
market research, tenant criteria, rent agreement draft, maintenance checklist
