# Day 2 — Property Dealer/Agent Banne ke 5 Asaan Steps

**Concept:**
Property dealer ya agent banne ke liye sabse pehle apko real estate market ki basics aani chahiye (Day 1 mein cover hui). Phir apko apna license/professional registration karana hota hai, local laws samajhni hoti hain, aur ek strong network banana hota hai. Yeh role flexible hai—part-time bhi kar sakte hain aur full-time business bhi.

**Example:**
1. **Registration:** Punjab mein, Punjab Real Estate Regulatory Authority (PREA) se registration karni hoti hai (₨50,000-100,000 fee).
2. **Training:** PREA ya BREA (Balochistan) se 3-5 din ki training mandatory hai.
3. **Networking:** Local builders, lawyers, aur existing agents se milna shuru karen.

**Practice:**
Apne shehar ki real estate regulatory authority ki website se registration process ke 3 main steps likhein (keywords: fee, documents, training).

**Mini Project:**
Ek fake (practice) property listing banayein—property details (location, size, price), seller/buyer ke liye benefits, aur apka commission ka percentage (e.g., 2% of sale price).

**Answer Key:**
PREA website, documents list, training certificate
