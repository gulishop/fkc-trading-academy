# Day 16 — **Social Media Se Property Bikhe: 5 Must-Follow Techniques**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke social media (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok) aur online listings (Zameen.com, OLX) ko istemal karke property kaise bechein. Basics se lekar advanced techniques tak—kyun ke 90% buyers pehle online hi property search karte hain. Content strategy, engagement aur trust-building ke tareekay bhi cover honge.

**Example:**
Ek 5 marla plot Lahore ke Johar Town mein hai. Aap:
1. **Zameen.com** par high-quality photos + 3D virtual tour upload karen.
2. **Facebook Marketplace** par "Lahore Johar Town 5 Marla Plot - Rs. 1.2 Cr" likh kar 2-3 minute ka video bana kar share karen.
3. **Instagram Reels** par "5 Reasons Why This Plot is a Goldmine" naam se reel banayein (trending audio use karke).
4. WhatsApp groups (local buyers/sellers) mein link share karen + daily 2-3 follow-ups karen.
5. **Google Ads** (Rs. 5,000 budget) se targeted audience tak reach karen.

**Practice:**
Apne mobile se ek 1-minute ka reel banayein—property ke 3 unique selling points highlight karen (location, facilities, ROI). Caption mein "DM karke details lein" likhein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **property listing template** banayein (Word/Canva) jismein:
- Property details (size, location, price)
- 3 high-quality photos
- 1 video link (YouTube/Google Drive)
- Contact info + WhatsApp link
- 3 bullet points (kyun yeh property behtar hai)

**Answer Key:**
high-quality photos, virtual tour, trending audio, targeted ads, follow-up
