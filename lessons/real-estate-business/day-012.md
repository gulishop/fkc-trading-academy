# Day 12 — **Property Dealings Mein "Due Diligence" Kaise Karein**

**Concept:**
Property dealings mein "due diligence" ka matlab hai property aur uske saare legal, financial aur physical aspects ki poori research karna taake future mein koi surprise na ho. Isse aap fraud se bach sakte hain, property ki real value samajh sakte hain aur deal ko confidently final kar sakte hain.

**Example:**
Maan lo aap ek plot khareed rahe hain. Due diligence ke liye:
1. **Land Record Check:** Patwari se malum karein ki plot ka malik kaun hai, kya koi mortgage ya dispute hai.
2. **Approvals Verify:** Check karein ki property ke liye building approvals (NA, CDA, LDA etc.) hain ya nahi.
3. **Physical Inspection:** Plot visit karein, boundaries check karein, koi illegal construction toh nahi hai.

**Practice:**
Apne area ki kisi property ka due diligence checklist banaein (3-5 points). Jaise:
- [ ] Land record (Fard) check karna
- [ ] Approvals (NA, layout plan) verify karna
- [ ] Seller ki identity aur title verify karna

**Mini Project:**
Kisi local property dealer se poochhein ki wo due diligence ke liye konsi 3 sabse common mistakes dekhte hain. Unke answers note karein aur apne checklist mein add karein.

**Answer Key:**
land record, approvals, seller identity, physical inspection
