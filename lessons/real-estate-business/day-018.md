# Day 18 — **Rental Property Management: Landlord Se Tenant Tak**

**Concept:**
Rental property management mein aapko property ki dekhbhaal, tenant selection, rent collection, aur legal compliance ka dhyaan rakhna hota hai. Yeh sirf "property rent par dena" nahi, balki ek systematic process hai jismein tenant satisfaction, maintenance, aur financial planning shamil hain. Aapko tenant screening, lease agreements, aur property ki regular maintenance ka plan banana hota hai.

**Example:**
Imagine aapka 2-marla flat Lahore mein hai. Aapko tenant dhundhne ke liye:
1. **Tenant Criteria** set karo (e.g., monthly income 50k+, job stability, no pets).
2. **Listing** karo (OLX, Facebook Groups, local brokers).
3. **Screening** karo (ID verification, salary slips, previous landlord reference).
4. **Lease Agreement** banayo (11 months, security deposit, maintenance rules).
5. **Rent Collection** ka system rakho (bank transfer, due date reminders).

**Practice:**
Apne ghar ya kisi relative ke rental property ke liye 3 tenant criteria likho (e.g., "Tenant must have a job in same city").

**Mini Project:**
Ek **sample lease agreement** draft karo (Google par "rental agreement Pakistan format" search karke base le sakte ho). Include: rent amount, security deposit, maintenance responsibility, notice period.

**Answer Key:**
Tenant criteria, lease agreement, rent collection, maintenance
