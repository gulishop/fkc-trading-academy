# Day 15 — **Property Dealings Mein "Title Verification" Kaise Karein (Complete Guide)**

**Concept:**
Property dealings mein "Title Verification" sabse zaroori step hai. Yeh confirm karta hai ke property kisi ke naam hai ya nahi, kya koi mortgage/lien hai, aur kya seller real owner hai. Bina title verify kiye deal karne se fraud ya legal disputes ka risk rehta hai. Pakistan mein title verification land records (FARD), mutation, aur property cards se ki jaati hai.

**Example:**
1. **FARD Check:** Punjab mein [Punjab Land Records Authority](https://plra.punjab.gov.pk/) se property ka FARD (land record) nikalo.
2. **Mutation Verify:** Check karo ke seller ke naam mutation (record of ownership transfer) hui hai ya nahi.
3. **Encumbrance Certificate:** 12-20 saal ke record se pata karo ke property par koi loan/lien toh nahi hai.

**Practice:**
Apne local tehsil office ya [Land Record Authority website](https://plra.punjab.gov.pk/) se kisi property ka FARD aur mutation report nikal kar dekhhein.

**Mini Project:**
Kisi 2-3 properties ke FARD aur mutation reports collect karin, unke owner, area, aur koi pending dues check karin. Compare karin ke konsi property legally safest hai.

**Answer Key:**
FARD, mutation, encumbrance, lien
