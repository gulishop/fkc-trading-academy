# Day 23 — **Freelancing Mein "Upselling" Kaise Karein: Extra Services Se Extra Income**

**Concept:**
Upselling ka matlab hai client ko extra services ya add-ons offer karna jo unke project ko enhance karein. Yeh trust build karne ke baad hota hai—client ko samajh aata hai ke tum unke liye valuable ho. Upselling se 20-50% extra income generate hoti hai, bina naye clients dhundne ke.

**Example:**
Ek logo design ka order mila. Client ko bas ek logo chahiye tha, lekin tum usko samjhao ke ek **brand identity package** (logo + business card + social media banners) bhi le sakte hain. Agar client agree karta hai, to tumhara commission double ho jata hai.

**Practice:**
Apne 1-2 existing clients ko ek email bhej kar ek relevant upsell offer karo. Jaise:
*"Maine aapka [service] complete kar diya hai. Kya aapko [related service] bhi chahiye? Yeh aapke project ko [benefit] dega!"*

**Mini Project:**
Ek **upsell offer template** taiyar karo jo tum future mein use kar sako. Ismein in points ko zaroor shamil karo:
1. Main service ka reference
2. Upsell service ka description
3. Extra value (time/cost saving)
4. Clear CTA (Call to Action)

**Answer Key:**
main service reference, upsell description, extra value, CTA
