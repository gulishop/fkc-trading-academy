# Day 27 — **Freelancing Mein "Follow-Up" Kaise Karein: Lost Opportunities Ko Dobara Hasil Karne Ka Formula**

**Concept:**
Follow-up ka matlab hai client ke response ka intizar karne ke baad dobara contact karna. 80% freelancers proposals bhejne ke baad follow-up nahi karte, jisse unke chances 97% tak kam ho jate hain. Follow-up se aap professional rehte hain, client ko yaad dilaate hain, aur "busy" ya "forgotten" responses ko dobara active kar sakte hain.

**Example:**
Ek client ne aapka proposal reject kar diya lekin koi reason nahi bataya. Aap 3 din baad email bhej sakte hain:
*"Hi [Client Name], I understand you might be busy. Just wanted to follow up on my proposal for [project name]. If you need any clarifications or have questions, I’m happy to discuss further. Looking forward to your thoughts!"*

**Practice:**
Apne inbox se 1 proposal ya pitch select karo jo 3-5 din se pending ho, aur us client ko polite follow-up email bhej do.

**Mini Project:**
10 clients ko follow-up karne ka plan banaen (konse platform ke, kaise, kab). Har follow-up ka draft likhein.

**Answer Key:**
polite tone, clear subject, 3-4 lines, professional closing
