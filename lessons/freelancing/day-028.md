# Day 28 — **Freelancing Mein "Exit Strategy" Banana: Project Khatam Karne Ka Professional Tor**

**Concept:**
Har freelancer ko project khatam karne ka professional tareeqa aana chahiye. Yeh sirf payment secure karne ke liye nahi, balki agle projects aur referrals ke liye bhi zaroori hai. Exit strategy mein delivery, feedback, payment, aur agle opportunities ke liye groundwork include hota hai.

**Example:**
Ek logo designer ne ek client ko final design deliver karne ke baad ek email bheja:
*"Dear [Client], main aapka project complete kar chuka hoon. Aap isko review kar sakte hain aur agar koi changes chahiye to 48 hours ke andar batayein. Payment ke liye aapko invoice bhej diya gaya hai. Is project ke baad main aapko ek feedback form bhej raha hoon—yeh sirf improvement ke liye hai. Agar aapko future mein koi aur help chahiye to main yahan available hoon."*

**Practice:**
Apne kisi purane project ya imaginary scenario ke liye ek "Exit Email" draft karo. Ismein delivery confirmation, payment reminder, feedback request, aur agle opportunities ka zikr zaroor ho.

**Mini Project:**
Ek "Project Completion Checklist" banayein jismein yeh steps hon:
1. Final delivery (files/links)
2. Invoice send karna
3. Feedback request
4. Follow-up for testimonial/reference

**Answer Key:**
delivery confirmation, payment reminder, feedback request, future collaboration
