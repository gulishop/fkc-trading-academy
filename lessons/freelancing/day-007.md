# Day 7 — **Apna Pehla Order Milne Ke Baad: Kaise Shuru Karein (Step-by-Step Guide)**

**Concept:**
Pehla order milne ke baad sabse pehle **clear communication** aur **professional setup** zaroori hai. Is stage par confusion ya delay se project kharab ho sakta hai. Pehle order ko successfully complete karne ke liye **scope, deadlines, aur deliverables** ko define karna, payment terms agree karna, aur client ke expectations manage karna seekhna hai.

**Example:**
1. **Scope Define Karein:** Client ko email karke poochhein, *"Aapko yeh project kitne din mein chahiye? Kitne revisions allowed hain?"*
2. **Payment Terms:** Half payment advance lein (50%) aur baaki project complete hone ke baad. Agar client reject kare, toh aapka nuqsan na ho.
3. **Deliverables List:** Har cheez likh dein (e.g., "3 logo designs, 2 revision rounds, source files").

**Practice:**
Apne pehle client ko ek **professional email** bhej dein jismein:
- Project scope (kya deliver hoga)
- Timeline (kitne din mein)
- Payment terms (50% advance, 50% delivery par)
- Revision policy (kitne changes allowed hain)

**Mini Project:**
Ek **Google Doc** taiyar karke rakhein jismein aap apne pehle client ke liye **project agreement** likhein (scope, timeline, payment). Isse future disputes avoid honge.

**Answer Key:**
scope define, payment terms, deliverables list, professional email
