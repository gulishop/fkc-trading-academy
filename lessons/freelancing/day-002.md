# Day 2 — **Winning Gig/Proposal Likhne Ki 5 Step-by-Step Guide**

**Concept:**
Ek achha gig ya proposal client ko impress karne ka pehla kadam hai. Ismein **clear value proposition**, **specific benefits**, aur **call-to-action** hona zaroori hai. Copywriting ki basic techniques (like AIDA: Attention, Interest, Desire, Action) use karo taake client ka focus tum par rahe.

**Example:**
Imagine a client searching for "logo design". Ek **bad** proposal:
*"I can design your logo. Contact me."*

Ek **achha** proposal:
*"I’ll design a **modern, scalable logo** in 48 hours that matches your brand’s personality. My past clients saw **30% more engagement** after rebranding. Let’s discuss your vision—reply with your color preferences!"*

**Practice:**
Apne **Day 1** ke skill ke liye ek **3-line proposal** likho jo:
1. Client ki **specific problem** mention kare
2. Tumhari **unique solution** bataye
3. **Next step** (e.g., "DM me your deadline")

**Mini Project:**
Ek **fake gig** create karo (Upwork/Fiverr par) for a service you can offer. Include:
- **Title** (e.g., "I’ll Design a Professional LinkedIn Banner in 24 Hours")
- **3 bullet points** of benefits
- **Pricing** (fixed or starting at $X)

**Answer Key:**
problem solve, unique solution, clear CTA, pricing transparency
