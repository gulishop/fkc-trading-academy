# Day 10 — **Freelancing Ko Agency Banane Ka Roadmap: Team Se Kaise Shuru Karein**

**Concept:**
Freelancing se agency banane ka matlab hai apne ek-person business ko scale karke ek team banana jo multiple projects handle kar sake. Pehle freelancer ke roop mein experience gain karne ke baad, aap apne work ko delegate kar sakte hain, specialized roles assign kar sakte hain, aur clients ko larger scope ke projects offer kar sakte hain.

**Example:**
1. **Step 1:** Apne freelancing ke 1-2 saal ke experience se "most profitable services" identify karein (e.g., logo design + social media content).
2. **Step 2:** Ek "virtual assistant" ya junior freelancer hire karein jo repetitive tasks (e.g., client communication, basic edits) sambhal le.
3. **Step 3:** Apne services ko "package" mein convert karein (e.g., "Social Media Management Package" = 10 posts + 2 graphics + weekly reports).

**Practice:**
Apne current freelancing services list karein aur decide karein ki kaun sa task aap delegate kar sakte hain (e.g., email responses, basic design edits). Ek job post create karein (Upwork/Fiverr par) ek junior assistant ke liye.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **1-page "Agency Proposal"** taiyar karein jismein aap batayein:
- Aapki agency ke services
- Team structure (aap + 1 assistant)
- Pricing model (e.g., "Logo Design: $150 (you) + $50 (assistant)")
- Client ko kya benefit milega (e.g., "Faster delivery, 24/7 support")

**Answer Key:**
freelancer se agency, delegate tasks, junior hire, package services
