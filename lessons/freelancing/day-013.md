# Day 13 — **Freelancing Mein "Cold Pitching": Client Milne Ka Sabse Powerful (Aur Kam Use) Tarika**

**Concept:**
Cold pitching ka matlab hai kisi client ko unki permission ke bina directly approach karna (email, DM, ya platform message). Yeh freelancers ke liye bohat effective hai jab profile weak ho ya competition zyada ho. Key: **Personalization + Value Offer + Clear CTA**. 90% freelancers yeh nahi karte, isliye yeh 10x chances badha deta hai.

**Example:**
Ek graphic designer ne 50+ Dribbble designers ke portfolios check kiye, unke designs mein improvements suggest kiye (free), aur unse bola: *"Maine aapke project X mein Y improvement suggest kiya hai—agar aapko yeh pasand aaya to main aapke next project par bhi same approach apna sakta hoon. 30-minute call par discuss kar sakte hain?"*

**Practice:**
Apne niche ke 3 potential clients ke LinkedIn/Instagram profiles check karo. Unke **recent work** ya **challenges** ko identify karo. Ab unko ek **3-line DM** bhej do jisme:
1. Unke work ki **specific praise** ho
2. Unke **kisi problem** ka solution suggest karo (free)
3. **Next step** ka proposal do (call, email, ya proposal link)

**Mini Project:**
Ek **cold pitch template** banao jo aap har bar use kar sako (3-4 lines max). Isme:
- Client ka naam
- Unke project ka reference
- Aapki service ka **1 unique benefit**
- Call-to-action (e.g., "Kya aapko 15-minute call pasand hai?")

**Answer Key:**
client ka naam, specific praise, free solution, CTA
