# Day 14 — **Freelancing Mein "Portfolio Banane Ke 5 Sabse Effective (Aur Kam Use) Tarike"**

**Concept:**
Portfolio freelancer ka digital business card hai. Bina experience ke bhi aap apne skills ko real-world projects, personal projects, aur client kaam se showcase kar sakte hain. Focus hona chahiye: **diversity (alag-alag types ke kaam), quality (best samples select karna), aur storytelling (har project ka context batana)**. Clients ko impress karne ke liye portfolio **visual, easy-to-navigate, aur niche-specific** hona chahiye.

**Example:**
Ek graphic designer ka portfolio:
1. **Personal Project:** Ek fake logo design kiya (e.g., "Local Café ke liye branding").
2. **Volunteer Work:** Kisi NGO ke liye social media graphics banaye (screenshot + "Pro bono" mention).
3. **Mock Project:** Ek real client ka brief lekar uska solution diya (e.g., "McDonald’s ke liye digital menu redesign").
4. **Testimonial:** "Maine apne portfolio mein yeh kaam add kiya kyunki isse mujhe confidence mila" — client ka 1 line ka review.

**Practice:**
Apne niche ke 3 potential clients ke briefs ko Google par search karo (e.g., "logo design for startup"). Unke requirements ko padhkar ek **mock project** taiyar karo (figma/Canva/Word mein). Apne portfolio mein add karne ke liye 1-2 best samples select karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **1-page PDF portfolio** banayein jisme:
- 2-3 best samples (screenshot + 1 line description).
- 1 client testimonial (agar hai to, warna apne mock project ka "client" ka role khelo).
- Contact info + niche (e.g., "UI/UX Designer | Mobile App Specialist").

**Answer Key:**
mock projects, personal work, testimonial, niche-specific
