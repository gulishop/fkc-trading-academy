# Day 25 — **Freelancing Mein "Automation" Ka Powerful Use: Time & Money Dono Bachao**

**Concept:**
Freelancing mein automation ka matlab hai manual tasks ko software/tools se automate karke apna time aur effort save karna. Isse aap repetitive kaam (jaise proposals likhna, invoices bhejna, follow-ups) ko 10x faster kar sakte hain. AI tools, bots, aur no-code platforms se aap client communication, project management, aur billing ko automate kar sakte hain.

**Example:**
Ek graphic designer har roz 5-10 clients ko proposals bhejta hai. Instead of manually typing, wo **Canva** + **Zapier** use karta hai:
1. Canva mein proposal template banata hai.
2. Zapier se har naye client ka email automatically proposal send hota hai.
3. Follow-up bhi 3 din baad auto-trigger hota hai.

**Practice:**
Apne freelancing workflow mein **1 repetitive task** identify karo jo aap roz karte ho (jaise invoices bhejna, social media posts schedule karna). Uske liye **1 automation tool** dhundo (jaise **Trello + Slack** for task reminders, ya **TextExpander** for email templates).

**Mini Project:**
**"Auto-Proposal System" banana**:
1. Google Docs mein proposal template taiyar karo.
2. **Zapier** ya **Make.com** se connect karo taake naye client ka email automatically proposal receive kare.
3. 3 din baad auto-follow-up bhi set karo.

**Answer Key:**
Canva template, Zapier integration, auto-follow-up, time saved
