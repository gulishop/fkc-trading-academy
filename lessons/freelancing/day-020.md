# Day 20 — **Freelancing Se Long-Term Wealth Kaise Banayein: Passive Income Sources**

**Concept:**
Freelancing se aap sirf active income hi nahi, balki passive income bhi generate kar sakte hain. Iske liye aapko apne skills ko re-usable products (jaise templates, courses, ebooks) mein convert karna hoga. Aise sources se aap baar baar paise kamate hain bina har roz naye clients dhundne ke.

**Example:**
Ek graphic designer apne logo templates ko Creative Market ya Etsy par bechta hai. Har beche gaye template se usko $10-50 milte hain, aur usko sirf ek baar design karna hota hai. Is tarah, ek hi template se saal bhar mein $1000+ passive income ho sakti hai.

**Practice:**
Apne freelancing skill se ek simple product (jaise Canva template, Notion planner, ya audio clip) banayein aur kisi platform (Fiverr, Gumroad, ya apni website) par upload karein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek "Social Media Content Calendar" template banayein jo freelancers ko bechein. Uske liye:
1. Canva par ek professional-looking template design karein.
2. Uske 3-5 variants banayein (jaise Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter ke liye).
3. Gumroad ya Etsy par upload karein aur $5-10 ka price set karein.

**Answer Key:**
Template design, Gumroad/Etsy upload, $5-10 price, passive income
