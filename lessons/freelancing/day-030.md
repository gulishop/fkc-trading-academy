# Day 30 — **Freelancing Se "Passive Income" Generate Karna: Apne Time Ko Free Karein, Earnings Ko Badhayein**

**Concept:**
Freelancing se passive income ka matlab hai apne time ke bina bhi paise kamana. Yeh freelancers ke liye ek game-changer hai jo ek baar ka effort karke baar-baar income chahte hain. Examples: digital products (templates, ebooks), online courses, membership sites, ya affiliate marketing. Sabse pehle apne expertise ko identify karo, phir use scalable format mein convert karo.

**Example:**
1. **Templates:** Agar aap graphic design karte hain, toh apne designs ke templates (Canva/Photoshop) bechein. Har sale par 100% profit.
2. **Ebooks:** Writing skills hain? Ek "Freelancing Guide" ebook likhein aur Gumroad/Kindle par bechein.
3. **Affiliate:** Apne niche se related products (hosting, tools) promote karein aur commission kamayein.

**Practice:**
Apne freelancing skills se ek simple digital product banayein (e.g., Canva template, Notion planner, ya 10-page PDF guide). Use Gumroad ya Etsy par upload karein aur promotion ke liye social media par share karein.

**Mini Project:**
**"Freelancer’s Toolkit" Bundle Banayein:**
- 3 Canva templates (resume, invoice, social media post)
- 1 Notion planner (client management)
- 1 PDF guide ("How to Land First 5 Clients")
- Price: $20
- Promote: LinkedIn/Facebook groups par share karein.

**Answer Key:**
Canva templates, Gumroad listing, social media promotion, $20 price
