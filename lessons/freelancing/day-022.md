# Day 22 — **Freelancing Mein "Cold Pitching" Kaise Karein: Direct Clients Tak Kaise Pahunchein**

**Concept:**
Cold pitching ek powerful technique hai jismein aap potential clients ko unke inbox mein message bhejte hain—bina kisi introduction ke. Yeh Fiverr/Upwork se alag hai kyunki yahan aap direct communication karte hain, jisse trust banta hai aur high-paying projects milte hain. Key hai: **short, relevant, aur value-driven** messages bhejna.

**Example:**
Ek graphic designer ko ek local restaurant ka owner milta hai. Uske Instagram par unki posts dekhte hain aur unko yeh message bhejte hain:
*"Hi [Name], main [Your Name] hun, ek freelance graphic designer hun. Aapke Instagram par dekha ke aapke posts mein design consistency achi hai—lekin ek professional touch se yeh aur bhi engaging ho sakte hain. Main aapko 3 logo design concepts bhej sakta hun free mein, taake aap dekh saken kaise aapki brand aur strong ho sakti hai. Agar interest ho to reply karen!"*

**Practice:**
Apne niche ke 5 potential clients ke LinkedIn/Instagram profiles dekhein. Har ek ke liye **3-line ka personalised message** likhein jo unki pain points address karein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek cold email draft taiyaar karen jo aap kisi local business (jaise dentist, gym, boutique) ko bhej sakte hain. Email mein:
1. Unki current service ka mention
2. Aapka solution (1-2 lines)
3. Call-to-action (free consultation ya sample)

**Answer Key:**
personalized, pain-point, free-value, clear-CTA
