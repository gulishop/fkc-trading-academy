# Day 5 — **Apna Portfolio Banana: Bina Experience Ke Bhi Professional Laga Sakta Hai**

**Concept:**
Freelancing mein portfolio aapki credibility ka sabse pehla saboot hai. Bina real clients ke bhi aap apne skills ka proof de sakte hain—chahye wo fake projects ho, case studies ho, ya phir apni learning ke samples. Ek strong portfolio se client ko pata chalta hai ke aapka style kya hai, aapki quality kya hai, aur aap unki problem kaise solve kar sakte hain.

**Example:**
1. **Fake Project Banana:** Agar aap graphic designer hain to ek dummy logo design karo jo real business ki tarah dikhe (e.g., ek fake coffee shop ka logo).
2. **Case Study Likhna:** Ek chhota sa document banao jisme bataya ho ke "Is project ko maine is tarah solve kiya" (even if wo real na ho).
3. **Learning Samples:** Agar aap writer hain to apne blogs ya social media posts ko showcase karo jo aapne likhe hain.

**Practice:**
Apne skills ke hisaab se **ek mini portfolio page** banao (Google Docs, Notion, ya Canva par). Isme:
- 2-3 "projects" add karo (real ya fake).
- Har project ke neeche 2-3 lines ka description likho ke aapne kya kiya aur kya result mila.

**Mini Project:**
**"Portfolio in a Day" Challenge:**
- Ek simple portfolio website banane ke liye 3 options mein se ek choose karo:
  1. **Canva** (template use karke)
  2. **Google Sites** (free aur easy)
  3. **Notion** (portfolio template use karke)
- Usme apne 3 best samples upload karo aur link ko apne social media par share karo.

**Answer Key:**
dummy projects, case studies, learning samples, Canva/Google Sites/Notion
