# Day 15 — **Freelancing Mein "Repeat Clients" Banane Ka Sabse Asaan Tarika**

**Concept:**
Repeat clients freelancing ki success ka backbone hote hain. Naye client lena zaroori hai, lekin existing client se bar bar kaam lena time, effort aur commission fees bachata hai. Aaj seekhoge ki kaise loyal clients build karne hain, unke satisfaction ko kaise maintain karna hai, aur unhe bar bar kaise hire karna hai.

**Example:**
Ek graphic designer ka client hai jo logo design karwana chahta hai. Pehle project ke baad usko 3 additional services offer karo:
1. Branding guidelines (₹5,000)
2. Social media posts (₹3,000/month)
3. Website banner updates (₹2,000)
Har service ke liye ek discount offer karo (e.g., "3 services lene par 15% off"). Agar client satisfied hai, toh yeh additional services easily approve ho jayengi.

**Practice:**
Apne ek existing client (ya kisi friend/relative se bhi) ko ek email bhejiye jismein aap 1-2 extra services suggest karein jo unke project ko enhance kar sakein. Email mein clear benefits batayein aur ek small discount offer karein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek "Client Retention Plan" banao:
1. Apne top 3 clients ki list banao.
2. Har client ke liye 1-2 extra services ya upsell ka idea likho.
3. Ek automated follow-up system (e.g., WhatsApp/email template) taiyar karo jo har 2 weeks baad unse milne ka request kare.

**Answer Key:**
loyal clients, upsell, automated follow-up, satisfaction
