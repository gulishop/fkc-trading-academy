# Day 17 — **Freelancing Mein "Client Retention": Repeat Business Ka Sabse Asaan Formula**

**Concept:**
Client retention ka matlab hai kisi client ko ek baar service dene ke baad dobara hire karne ke liye convince karna. Yeh freelancing ka "sweet spot" hai—kyunki repeat clients se 80% income aati hai, lekin iske liye consistency aur trust build karna zaroori hai. Aaj seekhenge kaise client ko "wow" experience do, taaki wo dobara aaye aur refer bhi kare.

**Example:**
Ek graphic designer ne ek client ko logo design diya. Usne project ke baad ek follow-up email bheja: "Aapka logo ready hai! Agar aapko koi aur design chahiye (business card, banner, social media graphics), toh main 10% discount offer kar raha hun." Client ne social media graphics ka order diya, aur iske baad har 3 mahine mein repeat order aate rahe.

**Practice:**
Apne 1-2 past clients ko ek short, friendly email bhejo. Message yeh ho: *"Mera [service] aapko pasand aaya? Agar aapko future mein koi aur [service] chahiye, toh main aapko priority support aur [discount/free add-on] offer kar sakta hun. Kya aapko abhi koi help chahiye?"*

**Mini Project:**
Ek "Client Retention Plan" banao—3 steps:
1. Follow-up email template likho (3-4 lines ka).
2. Ek free add-on ya discount decide karo jo repeat clients ko do.
3. Excel sheet mein 5 past clients ki list banao, aur unko follow karne ka schedule set karo (e.g., 1 week, 1 month, 3 months baad).

**Answer Key:**
follow-up email, discount offer, priority support, repeat order
