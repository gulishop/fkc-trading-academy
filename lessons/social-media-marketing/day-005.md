# Day 5 — **Email Marketing: Apne Audience ko Loyal Banane ka Power Tool**

**Concept:**
Email marketing ek powerful tool hai jo direct audience tak pahunchta hai—bina algorithm ke interference ke. Isse aap apne followers ko valuable content, offers, aur updates bhej sakte hain, jisse unki trust aur engagement badhe. Har $1 spent par $42 ka ROI milta hai (DMA 2023), isliye yeh beginner se advanced tak sabke liye zaroori hai.

**Example:**
Ek fashion brand ka example lete hain:
1. **Signup Incentive:** Website par "10% Discount" ke liye email ID enter karne par offer milta hai.
2. **Welcome Series:** Pehle email mein discount code, doosre mein styling tips, teesre mein exclusive sale ka link.
3. **Engagement:** Weekly newsletters mein latest trends, user-generated content (UGC), aur limited-time offers share karein.

**Practice:**
Apne ya kisi fake brand ke liye ek **simple email draft** likhein:
- Subject line: "Aapka 20% Discount 🎁 (Sirf 24 ghante)"
- Body: 3-4 lines ka promotional message + CTA ("Shop Now" button)
- Signature: Brand name + social media links

**Mini Project:**
Ek **email sequence** banayein (3 emails):
1. **Welcome Email** (brand introduction + first offer)
2. **Value Email** (blog post/guide link)
3. **Promotional Email** (sale announcement)

**Answer Key:**
discount code, welcome series, engagement, CTA
