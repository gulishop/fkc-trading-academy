# Day 6 — **YouTube Shorts aur TikTok Ads: Paid Campaigns ka A-Z Guide**

**Concept:**
YouTube Shorts aur TikTok par paid ads chalaana seekhna ek beginner ke liye bhi bohat fayde ka hai. In platforms par ads run karne ke liye "Campaign Manager" ya "Ads Manager" ka interface use hota hai. Targeting options (audience, location, interests) aur budget control karke apne content ko zyada logon tak pahunchaya ja sakta hai. Reels/Shorts ki viral nature ki wajah se ads bhi organic reach se zyada effective hote hain.

**Example:**
1. **Campaign Setup:** TikTok Ads Manager mein jao → "Create" par click karo → Objective select karo (e.g., "Traffic" ya "Conversions").
2. **Audience Targeting:** Age 18-35, interests: "Fashion" ya "Technology", location: Pakistan/India.
3. **Ad Format:** 15-60 second ka vertical video upload karo (jo pehle se viral ho chuka hai ya high engagement wala).
4. **Budget:** Rs. 500/day set karo aur 7 days ka duration choose karo.
5. **Tracking:** Pixel ya UTM links add karo taake conversions track ho sakein.

**Practice:**
Apne phone se TikTok ya YouTube par ek 15-second ka vertical video banayein (jo aapke niche ke content se related ho). Us video ko TikTok Ads Manager ya YouTube Ads mein upload karke ek test campaign run karein (Rs. 200-300 ka budget set karke).

**Mini Project:**
Ek **3-day TikTok/YouTube Shorts ad campaign** plan karo:
- Objective: Website traffic ya engagement
- Target audience: Apne niche ke hisab se (e.g., students, professionals, ghar baithe business walay)
- Ad creative: Pehle se viral content ya naya bana hua
- Budget: Rs. 500/day
- Expected CTR: 2-5% (agar kam hai to video improve karo)

**Answer Key:**
TikTok Ads Manager, vertical video, audience targeting, CTR
