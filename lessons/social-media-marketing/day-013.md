# Day 13 — **AI-Powered Viral Reels/Shorts: Algorithms Ko Samajh Kar Engagement Badhao**

**Concept:**
AI tools se viral Reels/Shorts banane ka raaz algorithms ko samajhna hai. TikTok aur Instagram Reels ke algorithms "watch time," "completion rate," aur "shares" ko priority dete hain. AI se aap yeh metrics predict kar sakte hain—jaise ki sabse engaging hooks, trending sounds, aur best posting times—taaki aapke content ka reach badhe.

**Example:**
Ek AI tool (jaise CapCut ya InVideo) use karke aap 3 viral Reels ke patterns analyze karen:
1. **Hook (0-3 sec):** "Aap yeh nahi jaante…" ya "Yeh trick aapki zindagi badal degi!"
2. **Trending Sound:** AI se top 5 sounds download karke unke lyrics ko apne script mein integrate karen.
3. **CTA (Call-to-Action):** "Agar aapko yeh video pasand aaya toh like karein aur comment mein apna experience share karein!"

**Practice:**
Apne niche (e.g., fitness, tech, food) ka 1 Reel script likhein jo AI se suggest kiye gaye 3 trends ko follow kare. Hook, trending sound, aur CTA zaroor add karen.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **AI-generated Reel** banayein (CapCut ya Canva AI use karke) jismein:
- AI se suggest kiya gaya trending sound ho.
- Hook 3 seconds se pehle aaye.
- End mein "Agar video pasand aaya toh follow karein!" type ka CTA ho.
Post karein aur 24 ghante mein engagement (likes, shares, comments) note karen.

**Answer Key:**
hook+trending+CTA, watch time+completion, niche+trend, 24ghante+metrics
