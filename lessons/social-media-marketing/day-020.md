# Day 20 — **AI Se Social Media Ads Ko Optimize Karke ROI Badhao**

**Concept:**
AI tools se social media ads ko kaise optimize karna hai, yeh seekhiye. AI algorithms data analyze karke best audience, bidding strategy aur ad creative suggest karte hain, jisse CPL (Cost Per Lead) aur CPA (Cost Per Acquisition) kam hote hain. AI-powered tools jaise Facebook Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns ya Google Performance Max ads ko automatically optimize karte hain.

**Example:**
1. **Meta Ads Manager mein AI ka istemal:**
   - Campaign setup karte waqt "Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns" select karein.
   - AI automatically best audience, placements aur bidding strategy choose karega.
   - Ad creatives (images/videos) upload karein, AI unhe test karega aur best-performing ko zyada budget dega.

2. **Google Ads mein Performance Max ka istemal:**
   - Performance Max campaign banayein.
   - AI multiple channels (YouTube, Search, Display) par ads ko optimize karega.
   - Audience signals (demographics, interests) provide karein, AI unhe refine karega.

**Practice:**
Apne ek existing ad account mein AI-powered feature (jaise Meta ka Advantage+ ya Google ka Performance Max) enable karein aur 3 din tak results track karein. CPL/CPA mein kya farq aaya?

**Mini Project:**
Ek naya Facebook/Instagram ad campaign banayein jo AI (Advantage+) ka istemal kare. 7 din tak results analyze karein aur ek 1-paragraph ka report likhein ki AI ne kaise performance improve ki.

**Answer Key:**
Audience targeting, Bidding optimization, Creative testing, CPL reduction
