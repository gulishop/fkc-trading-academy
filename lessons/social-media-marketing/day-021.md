# Day 21 — **Crisis Management: Social Media Par Brand Ko Safeguard Karna**

**Concept:**
Social media par koi bhi brand kabhi bhi crisis ka shikaar ho sakta hai—negative comments, viral complaints, ya fake news. Aaj seekhein ke kaise aise waqt mein brand ki reputation protect karni hai, response plan banayein, aur negative sentiment ko positive mein convert karna hai.

**Example:**
Ek fast-food chain ke Twitter par customer ne video post ki ke uske burger mein plastic mila hai. Team ne:
1. **24-hour response window** mein official statement diya ("Hum is par gaur kar rahe hain...").
2. **Direct message** karke customer se details maangi.
3. **Video response** upload ki ke investigation show ho.
4. **Follow-up post** kiya ke issue resolve ho gaya hai aur compensation di gayi.

**Practice:**
Apne ya kisi brand ka **crisis response plan** likhein (3 steps):
1. Crisis type (e.g., product failure, PR scandal)
2. Response time (kitne ghante mein react karna hai)
3. Key message (kya kehna hai)

**Mini Project:**
Ek **social media crisis simulation** taiyaar karo:
- Ek fake negative comment/post create karo (e.g., "Aapki service itni kharab hai ke main dobara nahi aunga!").
- Uske liye **3-line ka professional response** likho jo brand ki reputation bacha sake.

**Answer Key:**
acknowledge, investigate, respond, follow-up
