# Day 4 — **Engagement aur Community Building: Loyal Followers ka Secret**

**Concept:**
Engagement matlab followers ke saath interaction—comments, DMs, shares, aur polls. Community building ka matlab hai ek loyal audience create karna jo aapki content ko share kare aur brand ko trust kare. Bina engagement ke followers bas numbers hote hain, loyal community nahi.

**Example:**
Ek fitness page "FitWithAli" har post ke comments mein followers se poochta hai: *"Aaj ka workout kya hai?"* aur har reply ka like/dislike karke respond karta hai. Result: 30% zyada engagement aur followers dusre posts bhi share karte hain.

**Practice:**
Aaj ke post par **3 followers ke comments ka reply** karo—personalized (e.g., "Shukriya! Aapka feedback hamari next video mein include karenge 👍").

**Mini Project:**
Ek **Engagement Challenge** shuru karo: 1 week tak har din ek post par **poll/story** add karo (e.g., "Aaj ka topic: Diet ya Workout?") aur results share karo.

**Answer Key:**
personalized reply, poll/story, weekly results, community trust
