# Day 18 — **AI-Powered Chatbots: Customer Service Ko Automate Karke Engagement Badhao**

**Concept:**
AI chatbots aajkal social media par customers ke queries ko instantly handle karte hain, engagement badhate hain aur time bachate hain. Unhe setup karne se pehle apne brand voice ko define karna zaroori hai. Chatbots se FAQs, order tracking aur basic support provide karke team ko complex issues par focus karne ka mauka milta hai.

**Example:**
Ek local restaurant ka Instagram page chatbot setup karna:
1. **Tool choose karo** (jaise ManyChat ya Chatfuel).
2. **Flow design karo** (e.g., "Menu dekhna hai" → "Chicken Biryani / Paneer Tikka" → Order placement).
3. **AI responses add karo** (e.g., "Sorry, hamara kitchen 30 min mein band hoga").
4. **Testing karo** (fake Instagram account se message bhej kar check karo).

**Practice:**
Apne business ya niche ke liye ek simple chatbot flow design karo (3-4 steps). Jaise:
- Welcome message
- Service choose karne ka option
- Contact details provide karna

**Mini Project:**
Ek **ManyChat/Chatfuel account create karo** aur ek **3-step chatbot flow** setup karo (jaise "Hi! Main XYZ ka assistant hoon. Aapko kya chahiye?" → "1. Menu 2. Delivery 3. Contact"). Screenshot ya GIF le kar share karo.

**Answer Key:**
ManyChat setup, 3-step flow, customer service automation, engagement increase
