# Day 22 — **AI-Powered Social Media Content Repurposing: 1 Post Se 10+ Platforms Par Publish Karna**

**Concept:**
Har platform ke liye alag content banana time-consuming hai. AI tools se ek hi post ko reformat karke multiple platforms (Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, Pinterest, YouTube Shorts, etc.) par publish kar sakte hain. Isse consistency bhi maintain hoti hai aur effort bhi half ho jata hai.

**Example:**
1. Ek blog post likhein (e.g., "5 AI Tools for Social Media Growth").
2. AI tool (jaise **Repurpose.io** ya **Descript**) use karke:
   - Blog se **LinkedIn post** banayein (key points + CTA).
   - **Twitter thread** mein convert karein (10 tweets sequence mein).
   - **Instagram carousel** banayein (har slide par 1 point).
   - **YouTube Shorts script** generate karein (hook + 3 tips).
3. Scheduler (jaise **Buffer** ya **Hootsuite**) se sab platforms par schedule kar dein.

**Practice:**
Apne phone ya laptop ke screenshot lekar batayein ki aapne ek existing post ko kaise repurpose kar sakte hain (e.g., ek Instagram Reel script se Twitter thread kaise bana sakte hain).

**Mini Project:**
Ek **1000-word blog post** ya **YouTube video script** lein aur usse **3 alag platforms ke liye content** banayein (e.g., LinkedIn post, Twitter thread, Instagram carousel). Screenshot saath mein submit karein.

**Answer Key:**
blog se LinkedIn post, Twitter thread, Instagram carousel
