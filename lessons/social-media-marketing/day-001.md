# Day 1 — Social Media Strategy: Kya hai aur Kaise Banaye?

**Concept:**
Social media strategy ek roadmap hai jo batati hai ke aap apne business ya personal brand ke liye social media par kya achieve karna chahte hain, kaise karna hai, aur kaise measure karna hai. Ismein aapke target audience, goals, content type, posting schedule, aur engagement plan shamil hote hain. Bina strategy ke, aap blindly post karte rahenge aur results bhi blind rahenge.

**Example:**
Maana ke aap ek fitness coach hain. Aapki strategy yeh ho sakti hai:
1. **Goal:** 3 months mein 10,000 followers aur 50 leads (free consultation sign-ups).
2. **Audience:** 20-35 saal ke gym jaane wale young professionals.
3. **Content:** Monday - Workout Tips, Wednesday - Diet Plans, Friday - Client Success Stories, Sunday - Q&A Reels.
4. **Platforms:** Instagram (main), YouTube Shorts (secondary), LinkedIn (for B2B clients).
5. **Engagement:** Har post par 10-15 comments ka reply, weekly polls, aur DMs ka 24-hour response.

**Practice:**
Apne business ya ek fictional brand ke liye 3 main goals likhein (e.g., brand awareness, leads, sales). Phir un goals ko achieve karne ke liye 3 content ideas sujhao jo aapke target audience ko engage karein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek 1-week ka rough content calendar banayein (platform, post type, aur topic ke saath). Example:
- Instagram Reel: "5 Minute Warm-up Routine" (Monday)
- LinkedIn Post: "How Fitness Boosts Productivity" (Wednesday)
- YouTube Short: "Gym Mistakes Beginners Make" (Friday)

**Answer Key:**
goals define karna, audience research, content types
