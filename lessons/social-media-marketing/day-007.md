# Day 7 — **AI-Powered Content Creation: Apne Workflow ko 10x Fast Karein**

**Concept:**
AI tools (jaise Canva AI, MidJourney, CapCut AI, Jasper) ka istemal karke content banane ka time 50% tak kam kar sakte hain. AI se drafts generate karne, thumbnails design karne, aur captions optimize karne mein madad milti hai. Bas AI prompts ko sahi tareeke se likhna aana chahiye.

**Example:**
1. **Caption likhne ke liye:** "Write a 3-line Instagram caption for a fitness brand promoting protein powder, tone: motivational, emojis: 💪🔥"
2. **Thumbnail banane ke liye:** Canva AI se "futuristic gym background, neon text 'GAINS 2024', bold font" prompt use karke 10 variants generate karo.

**Practice:**
Apne niche ke hisaab se 1 AI prompt likhkar ek caption ya thumbnail generate karo. (e.g., "Write a 2-line LinkedIn post for a digital marketing course targeting beginners in Hindi.")

**Mini Project:**
Ek 3-day content calendar banayein jisme har post ke liye AI se 3 caption options generate karein aur sabse engaging choose karein. (Use: Notion ya Google Sheets)

**Answer Key:**
caption draft, thumbnail variant, engagement score, AI prompt
