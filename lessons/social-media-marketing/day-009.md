# Day 9 — **AI-Powered Chatbots: 24/7 Customer Support ka Secret**

**Concept:**
AI chatbots aajkal businesses ke liye customer support ka game-changer ban gaye hain. Ye 24/7 available rehte hain, bar-bar repeat hone wale sawalon ka jawab dete hain, aur manual effort bhi kam karte hain. Aap inhe Instagram DMs, WhatsApp Business, ya website par laga sakte hain. AI tools jaise ManyChat, Chatfuel, ya Tidio se bina coding ke bhi chatbots banaye ja sakte hain.

**Example:**
Ek local restaurant ka chatbot:
1. Customer: "Menu kya hai?"
2. Chatbot: "Yahan se apna menu download karein: [link] 📲"
3. Customer: "Timings?"
4. Chatbot: "Hum subah 9 se raat 11 tak khulte hain. 🕒"

**Practice:**
Aaj hi ManyChat ya Chatfuel par ek free account banayein aur ek simple chatbot create karein jo "Hello" ka jawab "Assalamu Alaikum! Aap kaise help kar sakta ho?" de.

**Mini Project:**
Ek 3-message ka AI chatbot design karein jo ek hypothetical e-commerce store ke liye "Order Status" poochne par automatic response de. (Example: "Aapka order #1234 abhi dispatch ho chuka hai! 🚚")

**Answer Key:**
ManyChat, Chatfuel, Tidio, 24/7 support, automated responses
