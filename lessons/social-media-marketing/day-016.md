# Day 16 — **Meta Ads Manager Se Paid Campaigns Setup Karna: Zero Se Hero Banein**

**Concept:**
Meta Ads Manager (Facebook & Instagram Ads ka dashboard) se paid campaigns setup karna seekhiye. Target audience, budget, aur ad creatives kaise choose karein—yeh sab practical steps ke saath samjha jaega. AI tools ki madad se best targeting kaise karein, yeh bhi cover hoga.

**Example:**
1. **Campaign Objective Choose Karein:** "Traffic" ya "Conversions" select karein.
2. **Audience Targeting:** "Men, 25-35, interested in fitness" jaise filters lagayein.
3. **Budget & Schedule:** Rs. 500/day, 7 days tak run karne ka plan.
4. **Ad Creative:** 1080x1080 image ya 9:16 video upload karein.
5. **Placement:** "Automatic Placements" ya "Instagram Feed" select karein.

**Practice:**
Apne business ya kisi fake brand ke liye Meta Ads Manager mein ek basic campaign create karein. Target audience, budget, aur ad type choose karke screenshot lein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek 3-step Facebook/Instagram ad campaign design karein:
1. **Audience:** "Students in Lahore interested in online courses"
2. **Ad Creative:** 15-sec Reel jo "Free Trial" offer kare
3. **Budget:** Rs. 1000, 5 days tak run karne ka plan

**Answer Key:**
Traffic, Conversions, Audience Insights, Creative Testing
