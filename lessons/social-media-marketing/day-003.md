# Day 3 — Instagram Growth Tactics: 30K+ Followers ka Secret

**Concept:**
Instagram par organic growth ka formula hai "Engagement + Consistency + Niche Authority". Aaj hum 3 tactics seekhenge jo 30K+ followers tak le ja sakte hain: 1) **First 60 Minutes Rule** (algorithm ko signal do ke video viral ho rahi hai), 2) **Comment Pods** (engagement boost karne ka chhupa hua tareeqa), 3) **Collab Posts** (niche ke influencers ke saath cross-promotion).

**Example:**
Step-by-step **First 60 Minutes Rule**:
1. Reel upload karne ke **pehle 10 minute** mein 5 close friends ko DM karke video dekhne ko kahen.
2. Upload ke **pehle 30 minute** mein 3-4 comments likhein (apne niche ke keywords ke saath).
3. Pehle **60 minute** mein 100+ views hone ke baad, algorithm "trending" maanta hai aur reach badha deta hai.

**Practice:**
Apne niche ke **3 influencers** ko DM karke poochhein ke woh aapke Reel par comment karenge ya nahin. (Template: *"Hi [Name], main [Your Name] hun. Aapke content se inspire ho kar maine ek Reel banaya hai. Agar aapko pasand aaye to pehle 60 minute mein ek comment kar dena — isse hum sabki reach badhegi! 🙏"*)
