# Day 12 — **AI-Powered Social Media Scheduling: Timing Se Engagement Badhao**

**Concept:**
AI se social media posts ko automatically schedule karna seekhiye taake aapka content right audience tak right time par pahunch sake. AI tools like Buffer, Hootsuite, aur Later AI-powered scheduling features offer karte hain jo post timing, engagement patterns, aur audience behavior analyze karke best time suggest karte hain.

**Example:**
1. **Buffer ya Hootsuite** mein apna account connect karein.
2. Post type (Reel, Story, Carousel) select karein.
3. AI "Best Time to Post" feature enable karein—yeh aapke followers ke active hours detect karke suggest karega.
4. Schedule karne ke baad AI monitor karega aur agar engagement low ho to automatically timing adjust kar sakta hai.

**Practice:**
Apne Instagram/Facebook page ke liye 3 posts (Reel, Story, Carousel) AI scheduling tool (jaise Later ya Buffer) ka istemal karke schedule karein. Post timing AI se select karne de.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **1-week content calendar** banayein jisme har din ke liye 2 posts ho (Reel + Story). AI se timing suggest karke schedule karein aur 3 din baad check karein ki engagement kaise badha.

**Answer Key:**
optimal timing, audience active hours, auto-adjust, higher reach
