# Day 17 — **AI Se YouTube Growth: Smart Strategy Se 10x Views Badhao**

**Concept:**
YouTube algorithm AI-based hai jo content quality, engagement aur viewer retention par focus karta hai. AI tools se aap:
1) **Keyword research** kar sakte hain (Tubebuddy, VidIQ).
2) **Thumbnail & title optimization** ke liye AI-generated suggestions le sakte hain (Canva AI, MidJourney).
3) **Video ideas** generate kar sakte hain jo trending hain (Google Trends + AI prompts).
4) **Audience retention** improve karne ke liye AI-powered editing tips (CapCut AI, Descript).

**Example:**
Step-by-step YouTube video banane ka process:
1) **Topic selection:** Google Trends se "AI tools for students" search volume check karo.
2) **Keyword:** Tubebuddy se "best AI tools for students 2024" ka search difficulty score dekh.
3) **Title:** AI se 5 title options generate karke sabse engaging choose karo (e.g., *"10 AI Tools 2024 Jo Har Student Ko Free Mein Milein!"*).
4) **Thumbnail:** Canva AI se 3 thumbnail options le aur sabse clickable choose karo.
5) **Script:** AI se outline generate karke human touch add karo (e.g., ChatGPT se "YouTube script for AI tools for students" prompt karo).

**Practice:**
Apne niche ka ek YouTube video topic choose karo. Google Trends ya Tubebuddy se keyword research karo. AI se 2 title options aur 2 thumbnail options generate karo. Sabse engaging combination select karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek YouTube video script outline banayein (5-10 bullet points) jo AI tools se generate ho. Usme apne personal experience ya examples add karke human touch den.

**Answer Key:**
keyword research, Tubebuddy/VidIQ, AI title/thumbnail, audience retention
