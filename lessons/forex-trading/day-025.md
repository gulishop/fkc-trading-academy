# Day 25 — **Forex Market Participants: Kaun Hai Aur Kaise Influence Karte Hain?**

**Concept:**
Forex market mein sirf traders hi nahi, balki banks, corporations, governments, aur hedge funds bhi active hain. Har participant ka apna role hai—kuch liquidity provide karte hain, kuch long-term trends banate hain, aur kuch short-term volatility increase karte hain. In participants ko samajhna isliye zaroori hai kyunki inke actions se market movements predict karne mein help milti hai.

**Example:**
Ek **central bank** (jaise RBI) jab interest rates badhata hai, toh uski currency (INR) strong hoti hai—kyunki investors zyada return ke liye usko khareedte hain. Vise versa, ek **hedge fund** jab koi currency pair par bulk order lagata hai, toh uski price briefly move kar sakti hai, chahe fundamentals kuch bhi ho.

**Practice:**
Apne trading platform par **Economic Calendar** check karein aur dekhiye ki aaj konse major participants (jaise ECB, Fed, ya large banks) ke announcements hain. Unke statements ka currency pairs par immediate asar note karein.
