# Day 23 — **Live Trading Se Pehle: Real Account Setup Ka Complete Guide**

**Concept:**
Ab tak aap demo account par practice kar chuken hain. Ab real account open karne ka time hai, lekin ismein bhi planning zaroori hai. Real account setup karne se pehle apne broker ki policies (spread, leverage, withdrawal), KYC requirements, aur account verification process ko samajh lo. Exness ya kisi bhi broker par real account open karne ke baad, pehle chhote lot size se trading shuru karen (jaise 0.01 lot) taake risk manage rahe.

**Example:**
Exness par real account open karne ke steps:
1. Website par "Open Real Account" par click karen.
2. Apna personal info (name, email, phone) fill karen.
3. KYC ke liye ID proof (CNIC/Passport) aur address proof upload karen.
4. Account type choose karen (Standard, Pro, Raw Spread).
5. MetaTrader 4/5 download karen aur login credentials se connect karen.
6. Pehle trade mein 0.01 lot EUR/USD pair par 10 pips stop-loss lagayein.

**Practice:**
Apne selected broker ki website par jayein aur real account open karne ke steps ko follow karen. KYC documents ready rakhein.

**Mini Project:**
Apne real account ki settings (leverage, currency, notification alerts) ko optimize karen taake trading convenient ho.

**Answer Key:**
KYC documents, account type selection, 0.01 lot, stop-loss
