# Day 5 — **Trading Psychology: Emotions Ko Control Karke Consistent Profit Banana**

**Concept:**
Forex trading mein 90% traders emotional decisions ki wajah se fail hote hain. Fear, greed, aur overconfidence aapke trades ko bigad deti hai. Aaj hum seekhenge ke kaise apne emotions ko control karke disciplined trading ki jaaye. Trading plan follow karna, risk-reward ratio maintain karna, aur losses ko accept karna seekhein.

**Example:**
Maana aapne ek trade liya aur market aapke khilaaf chala gaya. Aapka stop-loss trigger ho gaya, lekin aap "ek baar aur try karte hain" ka soch kar trade band nahi karte. Aap apna stop-loss hata dete hain aur market thoda recover hota hai to aap profit bhi le lete hain. Lekin agle din market phir se aapke khilaaf chala jata hai aur aapko zyada loss ho jata hai. Yeh greed ki wajah se hua.

**Practice:**
Aaj se har trade lene se pehle 1 minute ka break lijiye. Poochiye: "Kya yeh trade mera trading plan ke hisaab se sahi hai?" Agar nahi, to trade na lijiye. Apne emotions ko control karne ka yeh pehla kadam hai.

**Mini Project:**
Apne trading journal mein 5 trades ka record likhiye. Har trade ke baad likhiye:
1. Aapka emotion (fear/greed/overconfidence)
2. Aapne kya action li (stop-loss hata diya, trade badha diya, etc.)
3. Aapko kya seekh mili

**Answer Key:**
discipline, trading plan, risk-reward, emotions control
