**Day 2 — Currency Pairs, Pips aur Lot Size: Trading Ki Duniya Ki "Mili-Metri"**

**Concept:**
Forex trading mein har trade ek *currency pair* (do currencies ka combination) par hoti hai, jaise EUR/USD ya USD/JPY. *Pip* (Percentage in Point) ek currency pair ki smallest price movement hoti hai—jaise EUR/USD mein 0.0001 ka change. *Lot size* batata hai kitni units trade ho rahi hain (Standard Lot = 100,000 units, Mini Lot = 10,000, Micro Lot = 1,000). In teen cheezon ko samajhna zaroori hai taake aapka profit/loss calculate ho sake.

**Example:**
Agar aap **EUR/USD** par trade kar rahe hain aur price 1.1000 se 1.1005 tak jaati hai, to aapka *pip gain* hai (1.1005 – 1.1000 = 0.0005 = **5 pips**). Agar aap **1 Mini Lot (10,000 units)** trade kar rahe hain, to har pip ka value **$1** hota hai (10,000 × 0.0001). Isliye 5 pips × $1 = **$5 profit**.

**Practice:**
Apne MetaTrader par **GBP/JPY** pair select karein. Chart par dekhein ki current price kya hai, aur agar aap **1 Micro Lot (1,000 units)** buy karte hain, to har pip ka value kitna hoga? (Hint: GBP/JPY mein pip = 0.01, lekin value calculate karne ke liye currency ke hisaab se sochna hoga.)

**Mini Project:**
Ek Excel sheet banayein jismein aap **3 different currency pairs** (jaise EUR/USD, USD/JPY, GBP/USD) ke liye **1 Standard Lot, 1 Mini Lot, aur 1 Micro Lot** ka har pip ka value calculate karein. (Example: EUR/USD ka 1 pip = $10 (Standard Lot), $1 (Mini Lot), $0.10 (Micro Lot)).

**Answer Key:**
GBP/JPY, 1 Micro Lot = $10 (Standard), $1 (Mini), $0.10 (Micro), pip value = 0.01
