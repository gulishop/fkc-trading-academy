# Day 15 — **Trading Journal Banana: Apne Trades Ka Hisaab Rakho**

**Concept:**
Trading journal ek notebook ya spreadsheet hai jisme aap har trade ka detail record rakhte hain. Isse aap apni mistakes, patterns, aur emotional decisions ko identify kar sakte hain. Yeh discipline badhata hai aur future trades ko improve karta hai.

**Example:**
1. Trade ka date/time likhein.
2. Pair (e.g., EUR/USD), entry/exit price, lot size, aur profit/loss (P&L) likhein.
3. Reason for trade (technical/fundamental) aur emotions (e.g., "FOMO se entry ki") note karen.
4. Agar stop-loss hit hua to reason likhein.

**Practice:**
Apne MetaTrader se last 5 trades ke details nikalein aur ek simple journal banayein (Excel ya Google Sheets mein). Include karen:
- Trade ka pair
- Entry/exit price
- P&L (points mein)
- Trade ka reason

**Mini Project:**
Ek trading journal template banayein jo aap future mein use kar sakte hain. Include karen:
- Trade ka summary (pair, direction, timeframe)
- Entry/exit conditions
- Emotional state (e.g., "Confident", "Stressed")
- Lessons learned

**Answer Key:**
Date, Pair, Entry/Exit, P&L, Reason, Emotions
