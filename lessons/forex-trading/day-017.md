# Day 17 — **Trading Psychology: Emotions Ko Control Karne Ke 3 Golden Rules**

**Concept:**
Trading mein 80% success psychology par hai – emotions (fear, greed, revenge trading) aapki sabse badi enemy hain. Jab loss hota hai, traders "ek aur try karte hain" ya "abhi recover karunga" ke chakkar mein zyada risk le lete hain. Jab profit hota hai, greed aati hai aur woh trade zyada chhoda dete hain. Aaj hum seekhenge ke kaise apne emotions ko recognize karke un par control rakha jaaye.

**Example:**
Imagine aapne EUR/USD mein long trade liya, aur market ne 50 pips profit ke baad reverse kar diya. Ab aapka loss ho raha hai – aapka brain "chalo recover karte hain" ke signal bhejta hai. Aap 2x lot size se trade karte hain, lekin market aur girti hai. Yeh hai "revenge trading" ka classic trap. Solution: Pehle se hi decide kar lo ke loss kitna hone do (stop-loss), aur profit ke peeche na bhago.

**Practice:**
Apne aaj ke trading journal mein 1 trade select karo jismein aapko emotions feel hue the. Likho:
1. Trade enter karne se pehle aapki emotion kya thi?
2. Trade ke dauran kya emotions badle?
3. Aapne kaise react kiya?

**Mini Project:**
"Emotion Tracker" chart banao:
- X-axis: Time (trade enter se exit tak)
- Y-axis: Emotion scale (-10 to +10, -10 = extreme fear, +10 = extreme greed)
- Apne trade ke dauran emotions ka graph draw karo. Aapko pata chalega ke kab aapki emotions ne decision li.

**Answer Key:**
fear, greed, revenge trading, stop-loss discipline
