# Day 21 — **Trading Strategy Ko Backtest Karna: Apne Plan Ki Kamzoriyaan Nikalo**

**Concept:**
Backtesting ek trading strategy ko historical data par test karne ka process hai. Ye batata hai ke strategy past market conditions mein kitni effective thi. Is se pata chalta hai ke strategy mein kya kamzoriyaan hain aur unko improve karna hai. Yeh confidence bhi deta hai ke strategy live trading mein kaise perform kar sakti hai.

**Example:**
1. TradingView par ek strategy select karo (e.g., "Moving Average Crossover").
2. Chart par right-click karo > "Add Alert" > "Strategy Tester" select karo.
3. Apne strategy ke parameters set karo (e.g., MA 50 aur MA 200).
4. "Test" button press karo aur results dekho (win rate, profit factor, max drawdown).

**Practice:**
Apne ek existing strategy ko (ya ek naya simple strategy) ko TradingView ya MetaTrader par backtest karo. 6 mahine ya 1 saal ka data use karo aur results note karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek simple backtest report taiyar karo jismein:
- Strategy ka naam
- Tested period (e.g., Jan 2023 - Jun 2023)
- Win rate (%)
- Profit factor (total profit / total loss)
- Max drawdown (%)

**Answer Key:**
win rate, profit factor, drawdown, historical data
