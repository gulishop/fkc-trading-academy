# Day 20 — **Position Sizing: Apne Risk Ko Smartly Manage Karein**

**Concept:**
Position sizing ka matlab hai har trade mein kitna capital lagana hai taake risk ko control kiya ja sake. Har trade ka risk 1-2% se zyada nahi hona chahiye. Iske liye apko apne account balance, stop-loss level, aur pip value ka calculation karna aata hona chahiye.

**Example:**
Maana aapka account balance $1000 hai aur aap har trade ka risk 1% rakhna chahte hain. Matlab har trade ka maximum loss $10 hona chahiye. Agar EUR/USD ka trade kar rahe hain aur stop-loss 50 pips hai, toh:
- Pip value = $1 (standard lot ke liye)
- Risk per pip = $1
- Total risk = $10
- Required position size = $10 / 50 pips = 0.2 lots

**Practice:**
Apne MetaTrader mein ek demo account khol kar ek currency pair select karein. Apne account balance ke hisaab se position size calculate karein taake har trade ka risk 1-2% se zyada na ho. Stop-loss lagakar dekhain ki kitne lots mein trade karna hai.
