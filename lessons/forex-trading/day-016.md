# Day 16 — **Trading Orders: Market, Limit & Stop Orders Ko Samajhna**

**Concept:**
Trading orders hei woh instructions jo aap broker ko dete hain ke kab, kaise aur kis price par trade enter ya exit karna hai. **Market Order** abhi ka price par execute hota hai, **Limit Order** ek specific price par enter/exit karta hai jab market uss level tak pahunch jaye, jabki **Stop Order** ek trigger price par market order ban jata hai (jaise stop-loss ya take-profit). Orders timing aur strategy ke liye zaroori hain.

**Example:**
Maana aap **EUR/USD** par **1.0800** par **Buy** karna chahte hain lekin abhi market **1.0795** par hai. Aap:
1. **Market Order** se abhi **1.0795** par buy kar sakte hain.
2. **Limit Order** set kar sakte hain ke jab market **1.0800** tak aaye to buy ho.
3. **Stop Order** set kar sakte hain ke agar market **1.0790** tak gir jaye to **sell** ho (stop-loss).

**Practice:**
MetaTrader 4/5 mein:
1. **Market Order** banane ke liye: Chart par right-click > "New Order" > Type = "Market Execution".
2. **Limit Order** banane ke liye: Same process > Type = "Pending Order" > Order Type = "Buy Limit" ya "Sell Limit".
3. **Stop Order** banane ke liye: Order Type = "Buy Stop" ya "Sell Stop".

**Mini Project:**

- 1 **Buy Market Order**
