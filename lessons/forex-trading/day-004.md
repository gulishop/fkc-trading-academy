# Day 4 — **Trading Strategy Banana: Simple Price Action Setup**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum ek beginner-friendly trading strategy banayenge jo sirf **price action** (chart ke movements) par based hai. Isme koi indicators nahi honge—bas support/resistance, trend lines aur candlestick patterns ka use hoga. Yeh strategy **trend-following** hai, matlab hum trend ke saath trade karenge, na ke uske against.

**Example:**
1. **Trend Line Draw Karna:** Agar market **upward trend** mein hai, toh **higher lows** ko connect karke ek rising trend line banayein.
2. **Entry Point:** Jab price trend line ko **touche karke wapas bounce** kare, toh **buy** enter karen.
3. **Stop Loss:** Trend line ke **nichay** (agar buy kar rahe hain) ya **upar** (agar sell kar rahe hain) lagayein.
4. **Take Profit:** Next **support/resistance level** par lagayein.

**Practice:**
Apne **MetaTrader** par **EUR/USD** ya **GBP/USD** chart khol kar:
- 15-minute ya 1-hour timeframe select karen.
- Ek **upward/downward trend** identify karen.
- Trend line draw karke **entry, stop-loss aur take profit** points mark karen.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **trading journal** banayein jisme:
- 5 trades (real ya demo) record karen.
- Har trade ke liye **entry/exit points, stop-loss, take profit aur reason** likhein.
- **Profit/Loss** calculate karen aur **win rate** nikalen.

**Answer Key:**
trend line draw, bounce confirmation, stop-loss below/above line, take profit at next level
