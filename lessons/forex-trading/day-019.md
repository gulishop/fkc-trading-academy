# Day 19 — **Economic Calendar: News Se Profit Kaise Banaye?**

**Concept:**
Economic calendar forex trading ka "weather forecast" hai. Isme important economic events (like interest rate decisions, GDP reports, employment data) ki timing aur impact ka pata chalta hai. In news se market volatility badhti hai—traders ko pata hota hai kab price move hoga. Fundamental analysis ka yeh backbone hai.

**Example:**
1. **US Non-Farm Payrolls (NFP)** — Har mahine ka pehla Friday, US mein jobs report aata hai.
2. **Impact:** Agar jobs zyada badhe (e.g., +300K vs +200K expected), USD strengthen hoga, EUR/USD pair gir sakta hai.
3. **Trading Plan:** NFP se pehle EUR/USD short karo (agar positive news expected ho), ya stop-loss zyada wide rakho kyunki volatility badhegi.

**Practice:**
1. **Economic Calendar Check:** [Forex Factory](https://www.forexfactory.com/) ya [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/economic-calendar/) kholo.
2. **Aaj ke 3 important news events** dhundo (country, impact level, expected vs actual result).
3. **1-2 currency pairs** select karo jin par yeh news asar karegi (e.g., USD/JPY, GBP/USD).

**Mini Project:**
