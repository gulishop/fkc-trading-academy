# Day 13 — **Forex Market Participants: Who Moves the Market?**

**Concept:**
Forex market mein trading sirf aap aur aapka broker nahi karte — iske peeche bohat bade players hain jo rates ko control karte hain. Inhe **market participants** kehte hain, jinmein **banks, hedge funds, central banks, corporations, aur retail traders** shamil hain. Har participant ka apna role hai: banks liquidity provide karte hain, hedge funds trends banate hain, central banks stability maintain karte hain, corporations apne business ke liye currency exchange karte hain, aur retail traders (jaise aap) in sab ke beech mein trading karte hain.

**Example:**
Imagine aap EUR/USD pair par buy kar rahe ho. Aapka order directly market tak nahi jaata — sabse pehle aapka broker us order ko **liquidity providers** (jaise large banks) tak bhejta hai. Agar koi bank us rate par sell karne ko ready hai, toh aapka trade execute ho jata hai. Agar nahi, toh broker dusre providers se match karta hai. Isi tarah, jab **European Central Bank (ECB)** interest rates badhata hai, toh EUR ki demand badh jati hai, aur rates upar chalte hain.

**Practice:**
Apne MetaTrader par **Market Watch** window mein jayein aur koi bhi currency pair select karke **right-click → "Specification"** par click karein. Dekhein ki **"Spread"** aur **"Swap"** ke alawa kya kya details dikhai deti hain. In details se pata chale ga ki aapka broker kis liquidity provider se connected hai.
