# Day 6 — **Trading View Ka Use: Charts Ko Samajhna Aur Analyze Karna**

**Concept:**
TradingView ek powerful free tool hai jo real-time forex charts, indicators aur trading ideas provide karta hai. Yahan par aap different timeframes (1m se lekar 1M tak) dekh sakte hain, indicators add kar sakte hain aur dusre traders ke analysis bhi dekh sakte hain. Charts ko samajhna trading ka foundation hai—har candle ka color, size aur position market ke mood ko batata hai.

**Example:**
1. **Chart Open Karein:** TradingView.com par jaein, EUR/USD pair search karein.
2. **Candlestick Colors:** Green candle = price up, Red candle = price down.
3. **Timeframe Badlein:** 1H (1 hour) se 4H par switch karein aur dekhein ki price kaise move kar rahi hai.
4. **Indicators Add Karein:** Simple Moving Average (SMA 50) add karein aur dekhein ki price uske respect mein kaise behave kar rahi hai.

**Practice:**
Apne TradingView account mein jaein, GBP/USD pair ka 1D (1 day) chart kholein. 3-4 indicators (jaise SMA 20, RSI, MACD) add karein aur dekhein ki price unke respect mein kaise move kar rahi hai. 10 minutes tak observe karein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek 10-minute ka screencast record karein jisme aap TradingView par ek currency pair ka chart kholein, indicators add karein aur 2-3 observations share karein (jaise "Price SMA 50 se upar hai, isliye bullish mood hai").

**Answer Key:**
EUR/USD, 1H chart, green candle, SMA 50, bullish trend
