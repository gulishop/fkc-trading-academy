# Day 26 — **Trading Orders: Market, Limit, Stop aur Stop-Limit Orders Samajhna**

**Concept:**
Trading orders Forex mein aapki trade enter aur exit ko control karte hain. **Market order** abhi ke price par instantly execute hota hai, jabki **limit order** ek specific price tak wait karta hai. **Stop order** ek trigger price par market order banata hai (jaise stop-loss), aur **stop-limit order** ek limit price bhi set karta hai taake execution guarantee ho.

**Example:**
1. **Market Order:** Agar EUR/USD 1.1000 par hai aur aap turant buy karna chahte hain, to market order place karne par 1.1000 ke price par trade ho jayega.
2. **Limit Order:** Agar aap sochte hain ki price 1.0950 tak gir sakti hai, to 1.0950 par buy limit order place kar sakte hain.
3. **Stop Order:** Agar aapka stop-loss 1.0980 par hai, to jab price 1.0980 par pahunchti hai, to automatically sell ho jayega.
4. **Stop-Limit Order:** Agar aap 1.0980 par stop order place karte hain lekin 1.0975 tak limit bhi set karte hain, to sirf 1.0975 ya usse behtar price par hi trade execute hoga.

**Practice:**
Apne MetaTrader par jaakar ek **buy limit order** place karein (kisi bhi pair par) 0.5% neeche jisme aapko lagta hai ki price gir sakti hai. Order ko cancel karne se pehle screenshot lekar save karein.
