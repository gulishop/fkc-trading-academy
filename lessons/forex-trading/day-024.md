# Day 24 — **Trading Journal: Apne Trades Ka Hisaab Rakhein aur Improve Karen**

**Concept:**
Har successful trader ek **trading journal** rakhta hai—ek digital ya physical record jisme har trade ki details (entry/exit points, reason, emotion, result) likhi hoti hain. Isse aap repeat mistakes identify kar sakte hain, strategies improve kar sakte hain, aur discipline maintain kar sakte hain. Yeh sirf "profit/loss" se zyada hai—psychology aur decision-making ka bhi record hai.

**Example:**
1. **Trade Setup:** EUR/USD buy kiya @ 1.0800 (reason: RSI oversold + support breakout).
2. **Entry/Exit:** 1.0800 par enter, 1.0850 par exit (50 pips profit).
3. **Risk:** 1% risk per trade (₹500 loss if stop-loss @ 1.0750).
4. **Emotion:** "FOMO tha, lekin plan follow kiya."
5. **Mistake:** "Chart pe zyada zoom kiya, entry thoda late hui."

**Practice:**
Apne **MetaTrader ke "Account History"** se last 5 trades ko copy karke ek simple table banao (columns: Pair, Entry/Exit, Pips, Reason, Profit/Loss, Emotion). Excel ya Google Sheets use kar sakte ho.

**Mini Project:**
1. **10 trades ka journal** banayein (demo ya live—kuch bhi).
2. Har trade ke baad **3 lines ka reflection** likhein:
   - "Kya sahi hua?"
   - "Kya galti hui?"
   - "Agla trade kaise improve karunga?"

**Answer Key:**
trade details, emotion notes, profit/loss, repeat mistakes, improvement plan
