# Day 29 — **Margin Call Se Bachne Ke 3 Golden Rules**

**Concept:**
Margin call tab aata hai jab aapka trading account ka balance itna kam ho jata hai ki broker aapke open positions ko close kar deta hai. Ye tab hota hai jab aapki losses aapki available margin se zyada ho jati hain. Isse bachne ke liye **margin level** (equity/margin × 100) ko **100% se zyada rakhna** zaroori hai. Agar margin level 100% se kam ho jata hai, to broker automatically aapke trades close kar deta hai.

**Example:**
Maana aapke account mein $1000 hain aur aap 1 lot EUR/USD buy karte hain (1:100 leverage). Agar EUR/USD 1% gir jata hai, to aapki loss $100 hogi (1000 × 1% = $100). Agar aapka margin level 100% se kam ho jata hai, to broker aapke position ko close kar dega.

**Practice:**
Apne trading account ka **margin level** check karein. Agar yeh 200% se kam hai, to ek chhota trade (0.1 lot) karke dekhein ki margin level kaise change hota hai.

**Mini Project:**
Ek Excel sheet banaein jismein aap har trade ke baad apna **margin level** aur **equity** track karein. Har din ke end par yeh check karein ki aapka margin level kitna hai.

**Answer Key:**
margin level, 100%, close, equity
