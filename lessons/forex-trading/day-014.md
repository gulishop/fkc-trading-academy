# Day 14 — **Support aur Resistance: Market Ki "Zindabad" Lines**

**Concept:**
Support aur resistance forex trading mein wo invisible lines hain jo market ke "udne" ya "girne" ki had ko dikhati hain. Support wo level hai jahan buyers zyada hote hain aur price wapas upar chalta hai, jabki resistance wo level hai jahan sellers zyada hote hain aur price wapas neeche girta hai. Ye levels traders ko entry/exit points suggest karte hain.

**Example:**
Imagine EUR/USD chart par price 1.1000 se gir raha hai lekin har baar 1.0950 par ruk jaata hai. Yahan 1.0950 "support" hai. Agar price 1.1100 tak chala jaye lekin har baar 1.1050 par ruk jaata hai, to 1.1050 "resistance" hai. Aapko yeh lines identify karni hain taake pata chale kab buy karna hai (support par) ya sell karna hai (resistance par).

**Practice:**
Apne MetaTrader par kisi currency pair (e.g., GBP/USD) ka 1-hour chart kholo. Pichle 2-3 din ke price action ko dekh kar 2 support aur 2 resistance levels mark karo. Hint: Look for "bounces" ya "rejections" in price.

**Mini Project:**
Ek screenshot lo jismein aapne apne marked support/resistance levels ke saath chart ho. Us screenshot ko apne trading journal mein add karo aur next 24 hours mein kya hota hai, note karo.

**Answer Key:**
support level, resistance level, bounce, rejection
