# Day 9 — **Trading Strategy Ko Backtest Karna: Apni Strategy Ki Asliyat Check Karo**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke apni trading strategy ko real market data par test karna kaise hai. Backtesting se pata chalta hai ke strategy past mein kaise perform karti thi, jisse hum future predictions aur risk management improve kar sakte hain. Isse hum emotional decisions se bachte hain aur strategy ki strength ko measure karte hain.

**Example:**
Maana ke aapki strategy hai: "Agar EUR/USD daily chart par 50-day moving average ke upar close ho, to buy karo." Ab aapko 2023 ke poore saal ka data lekar check karna hai ke:
1. Kitne signals generate huye?
2. Win rate kya raha (kitne trades profitable the)?
3. Average profit/loss kitna raha?

**Practice:**
Apni kisi bhi strategy ko (ya agar nahi hai to Day 4 ki price action strategy ko) MetaTrader ya TradingView par backtest karo. Last 6 months ka data use karo aur niche ke points note karo:
- Total trades
- Winning trades %
- Max drawdown (sabse bura loss)

**Mini Project:**
Ek simple Excel sheet banayein jisme:
1. Har trade ka date, entry/exit price, profit/loss.
2. Win rate aur average profit/loss calculate karo.
3. 1-2 graphs banayein (e.g., equity curve).

**Answer Key:**
Total trades, win rate %, max drawdown, profit factor
