# Day 10 — **Trading Discipline: Rules Ko Follow Karke Losses Ko Kaise Rokein**

**Concept:**
Trading discipline ka matlab hai apne trading plan aur rules ko 100% follow karna, chahe emotions kaisay bhi chalein. Bina discipline ke, even best strategy bhi fail ho sakti hai. Discipline mein shamil hai: stop-loss hamesha lagana, over-trading se bachna, aur emotional decisions (jaise revenge trading) ko roka jaana.

**Example:**
Maana aapne EUR/USD pair par long trade liya, stop-loss 1.0800 par lagaya, aur target 1.0900 hai. Price 1.0810 tak girta hai, aapka stop-loss trigger ho jata hai. Ab agar aap "ek baar aur try karte hain" ke liye trade band kar dete hain, yeh discipline ki kami hai. Discipline ka rule hai: "Ek baar stop-loss hit hone ke baad, trade band karo aur agle setup ka wait karo."

**Practice:**
Apne trading journal mein 3 trades ko check karo:
1. Kya aapne stop-loss lagaya tha?
2. Kya aapne plan se zyada trade liya?
3. Kya aapne emotional decision liya (jaise FOMO ya revenge trading)?

**Mini Project:**
Apne trading plan mein ek "Discipline Checklist" add karo:
✅ Har trade ke liye stop-loss lagana
✅ 1 din mein max 3 trades (over-trading se bachne ke liye)
✅ Loss hone ke baad agle trade ke liye 15-30 min wait karna

**Answer Key:**
stop-loss lagaya, plan se zyada trade, emotional decision
