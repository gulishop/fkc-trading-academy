# Day 12 — **Trading Performance Ko Metrics Se Analyze Karna**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke apne trading performance ko numbers ke zariye kaise analyze karen. Har trade ke baad apne results ko track karne se pata chalta hai ke aapki strategy kaise chal rahi hai, kahan galtiyan ho rahi hain, aur kaise improve kiya ja sakta hai. Performance metrics jaise win rate, profit factor, aur risk-reward ratio se trading ki quality ka pata chalta hai.

**Example:**
Maana aapne 10 trades kiye hain:
- 6 trades jeet gaye (60% win rate)
- 4 trades haren (40% loss rate)
- Total profit: $300
- Total loss: $200
Profit factor = Total Profit / Total Loss = 300/200 = 1.5 (1 se zyada hona achha hai)
Risk-reward ratio = Average profit per trade / Average loss per trade = (300/6) / (200/4) = 50/50 = 1:1

**Practice:**
Apne last 5 trades ke records ko nikalein aur in metrics ko calculate karen:
1. Win rate (%)
2. Profit factor
3. Average profit per trade
4. Average loss per trade

**Mini Project:**
Ek simple Excel sheet ya Google Sheets banayein jisme:
- Har trade ka date, pair, entry/exit price, profit/loss (pips mein bhi)
- Weekly/monthly performance summary (win rate, profit factor, max drawdown)
- Notes: "Kya galti thi? Kya improve karna hai?"

**Answer Key:**
win rate, profit factor, risk-reward ratio, max drawdown
