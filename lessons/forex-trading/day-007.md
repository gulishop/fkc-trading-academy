# Day 7 — **Economic Calendar Aur News Trading Ka Asar**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke kaise economic calendar aur major news events forex market par asar dalte hain. Economic calendar mein important economic indicators (jaise GDP, interest rates, employment data) aur central banks ke announcements ki timing aur expected results hote hain. In news ke results actual se match hote hain to market mein sudden volatility aati hai, jo traders ke liye opportunities ya risks paida kar sakti hai.

**Example:**
1. **US Non-Farm Payrolls (NFP):** Har mahine ki pehli Friday ko 8:30 AM EST par release hota hai. Agar expected 200K jobs ke bajaye 300K jobs bane to USD strengthen hoga, EUR/USD pair gir sakta hai.
2. **Step-by-Step:**
   - Economic calendar (TradingView ya Forex Factory) open karo.
   - Aaj ki important news check karo (e.g., "US CPI" 10:00 AM).
   - News release se 15 minutes pehle position close karo ya stop-loss set karo.
   - News ke baad 5-10 minutes tak wait karo, market stabilize hone do, phir trade karo.

**Practice:**
Apne trading platform par economic calendar open karo (Forex Factory ya Investing.com se). Aaj ki 3 major news events note karo, unke expected aur actual results ka difference dekho, aur socho ke inke baad market ka reaction kya hoga.

**Mini Project:**
Ek simple news trading strategy banayein:
1. News release se pehle EUR/USD pair par 1-hour chart par ek pending order (buy/sell) set karo.
2. News ke baad 15 minutes tak wait karo, agar price 50 pips move kare to order trigger hoga.
3. Agar 50 pips nahi hote to order cancel kar do.
