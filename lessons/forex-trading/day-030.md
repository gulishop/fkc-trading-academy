# Day 30 — **Trading Strategies: Trend Following vs. Mean Reversion**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum do popular trading strategies par baat karenge: *Trend Following* (jab market ek taraf move kar raha ho) aur *Mean Reversion* (jab market apni "average" price par lautne ki koshish kare). Trend following mein aap market ke saath chalte hain, jabke mean reversion mein aap "overbought" ya "oversold" levels par reversal ki umeed rakhte hain.

**Example:**
1. **Trend Following:**
   - Agar EUR/USD chart 1.1000 se 1.1200 tak upar ja raha hai, to aap *buy* kar sakte hain jab price pullback (thoda girne) kare, aur stop-loss 1.0950 par lagayein.
   - Indicator: Moving Average (200-period) ya Parabolic SAR.

2. **Mean Reversion:**
   - Agar GBP/USD 1.3000 par hai aur RSI 75 (overbought) dikha raha hai, to aap *sell* kar sakte hain, expecting price 1.2900 tak girne ki.
   - Indicator: Bollinger Bands ya RSI.

**Practice:**
Apne TradingView par kisi bhi currency pair ka 1-hour chart khol kar:
- **Trend Following:** Dekhein ki kya market upar ya neeche trend mein hai. Agar trend hai, to pullback par entry ka point dhundhein.
- **Mean Reversion:** Check karen ki kya price kisi support/resistance level se gir raha hai ya overbought/oversold hai.

- Agar market trend mein hai (200 MA se upar/below), to trend following strategy use karen.
