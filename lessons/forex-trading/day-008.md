# Day 8 — **Trading Journal: Apne Trades Ka Hisaab Rakho Aur Improvement Ka Rasta Banaye**

**Concept:**
Har successful trader apne trades ka detailed record rakhta hai. Trading journal ek diary hoti hai jismein aap har trade ke entry/exit points, profit/loss, emotions, aur market conditions note karte hain. Isse aap repeat mistakes identify kar sakte hain aur apni strategy ko improve kar sakte hain. Yeh discipline banane aur long-term success ke liye zaroori hai.

**Example:**
Maana aapne EUR/USD pair par long position li hai. Aap apne journal mein likh sakte hain:
- Entry: 1.0800 (1 lot)
- Exit: 1.0850 (50 pips profit)
- Time: 2 hours
- Reason: Support level breakout
- Emotions: Confident (lekin overconfident nahi)
- Mistake: Koi nahi (agar hota to note karna hai)

**Practice:**
Aaj ke 3 trades (demo ya live) ka record apne trading journal mein likhein. Har trade ke liye:
1. Pair, time, entry/exit price
2. Trade ka reason (technical/fundamental)
3. Apne emotions (fear/greed/confidence)
4. Kya aapne stop-loss correctly lagaya?

**Mini Project:**
Ek week tak har trade ka journal maintain karne ka challenge. Har din ke end par 5 minutes nikal kar:
- Best trade ka analysis
- Worst trade ka analysis
- 1 improvement point (e.g., "Zyada greed se avoid karna hai")

**Answer Key:**
trade details, emotions, SL/TP, weekly summary
