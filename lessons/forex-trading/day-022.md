# Day 22 — **Trading Discipline: Apne Plan Ko 100% Follow Karne Ka Rahasya**

**Concept:**
Trading psychology ke baad bhi bohat traders apne plan se hatte hain—ek trade mein zyada profit ka redha ya loss ka dar. Discipline ka matlab hai apne rules ko 100% follow karna, chahe market kaisa bhi ho. Iske liye **pre-trade checklist** banana zaroori hai: har trade se pehle apne entry/exit points, risk level, aur market conditions ko verify karo. Discipline se hi long-term success milti hai.

**Example:**
Ek trader ne apne plan mein likha hai: "Sirf London session ke 2nd hour mein EUR/USD par trade karunga, risk 1% se zyada nahi hoga." Lekin jab market ne 1.5% ka sudden move dikhaya, toh usne greed mein extra lot size le liya. Result: 3% loss. Agar woh apne plan par chalta, toh yeh loss avoid hota.

**Practice:**
Apne trading plan ka ek **pre-trade checklist** banao (3-5 bullet points). Har trade se pehle usko follow karo aur ek note bana lo ki aaj kitne % rules follow hue.

**Mini Project:**
Ek hypothetical trade lo (jaise EUR/USD par 1.1000 par buy, stop-loss 1.0950, take-profit 1.1050). Apne checklist se check karo ki:
1. Market condition (trend/range) sahi hai?
2. Risk reward ratio 1:2 se zyada hai?
3. News calendar mein koi major event nahi hai?
Har point ka answer "Haan/Nahi" mein likho.

**Answer Key:**
pre-trade checklist, rules follow, risk reward check, hypothetical trade verify
