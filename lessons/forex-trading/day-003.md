# Day 3 — Leverage aur Margin: Apne Trading Ko Powerful Banane ka Secret

**Concept:**
Leverage ek aisa tool hai jo aapko chhote amount se bade positions kholne deta hai. Jaise agar aapka account $100 ka hai aur leverage 1:100 hai, to aap $10,000 ki position bana sakte hain. Margin wo amount hai jo aapka broker aapke account se "lock" kar leta hai taake yeh position maintain ho sake. Zyada leverage se profit bhi zyada ho sakta hai, lekin loss bhi utna hi zyada hota hai!

**Example:**
Maana aap EUR/USD pair par 0.1 lot (10,000 units) ka buy order lagana chahte hain. Broker ka leverage 1:100 hai.
- Lot size = 10,000 units
- Leverage = 1:100 → Required margin = (10,000 / 100) = $100
- Agar EUR/USD 1.1000 se 1.1010 ho jata hai (10 pips gain), to aapka profit = 10 pips × $1 = $10 (har pip ka value 0.1 lot ke liye $1 hota hai).

**Practice:**
Apne MetaTrader 4/5 mein:
1. Kisi bhi currency pair par 0.01 lot ka order lagao.
2. Leverage 1:100 set karke margin calculate karo.
3. Order ko cancel kar do.

**Mini Project:**
Ek spreadsheet banao jismein:
- 3 alag-alag leverage levels (1:10, 1:50, 1:100) ke liye margin calculation karo.
- Har leverage ke liye 0.1 lot ka order lagane par kitna margin chahiye, yeh likho.
- Agar price 50 pips badhta hai, to har leverage ke hisaab se profit/loss kitna hoga.
