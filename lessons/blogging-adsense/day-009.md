# Day 9 — **Google Analytics Se Apne Blog Ki Performance Track Karna Seekhein**

**Concept:**
Google Analytics ek free tool hai jo aapke blog par visitors ki activity track karta hai—jaise kitne visitors aaye, woh kahan se aaye, konse pages par zyada time spent kiya, aur conversion rate kya hai. Isse aap samajh sakte hain ke aapka content kaise perform kar raha hai aur traffic badhane ke liye kya improve karna hai.

**Example:**
1. **Real-time data:** Agar aapne ek naya post publish kiya hai, toh Google Analytics ke "Real-time" section mein dekh sakte hain ke kitne visitors abhi uss post par hain.
2. **Audience insights:** "Audience" tab mein ja kar dekh sakte hain ke aapke visitors ki age group, location, aur device (mobile/desktop) kya hai.

**Practice:**
Apne Google Analytics account mein login karein, "Audience Overview" section mein ja kar pichle 7 dinon ka data check karein. Yeh dekhne ke liye ke aapke blog par sabse zyada visitors kaunsi country se aaye hain.

**Mini Project:**
Ek simple report taiyar karein jismein aapke blog ke top 3 most visited pages aur unke bounce rate (agar 70% se zyada hai toh improvement ki zarurat hai) likhein.

**Answer Key:**
traffic sources, bounce rate, top pages, audience demographics
