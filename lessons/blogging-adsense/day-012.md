# Day 12 — **Blog Ke Liye Best Platform Kaise Choose Karein (Blogger vs WordPress)**

**Concept:**
Blog setup karne ke liye sabse pehle platform choose karna zaroori hai. **Blogger** (free, Google ka) aur **WordPress** (self-hosted) do sabse popular options hain. Har ek ke apne faide-nuksan hain—Blogger beginner-friendly hai, jabki WordPress zyada flexible hai. Aaj hum in dono ke beech ka farq samjhenge taake aap apne goals ke hisaab se best choice kar saken.

**Example:**
Agar aap **shauq se blogging** kar rahe hain aur paise kamane ka immediate plan nahi hai, to **Blogger** aapke liye best hai:
1. Free hosting (blogspot.com domain)
2. Google Adsense approval easy
3. Setup in 5 minutes

Lekin agar aap **serious business** karna chahte hain aur long-term growth chahte hain, to **WordPress** (self-hosted) choose karein:
1. Custom domain (example.com)
2. Hazar plugins for SEO, speed, security
3. Full control over design & monetization

**Practice:**
Apne blog ke goal likhein (hobby, part-time income, full-time business) aur decide karein ki aap Blogger ya WordPress use karenge. Reason bhi likhein (2-3 lines).

**Mini Project:**
Ek **Blogger account** create karein aur ek test post publish karein. (Ya agar WordPress choose kiya hai, to ek hosting provider se domain khareed kar basic setup karein.)

**Answer Key:**
Blogger: free, easy, Google Adsense, limited, blogspot.com
WordPress: flexible, self-hosted, plugins, custom domain, scalable
