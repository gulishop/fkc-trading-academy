# Day 13 — **Blog Ke Liye Evergreen Content Kaise Banayein (Jo Hamesha Traffic Laye)**

**Concept:**
Evergreen content wo articles hote hain jo kabhi outdated nahi hote—jaise "How to lose weight" ya "Best budget smartphones 2024". Inki demand hamesha rehti hai, isliye yeh long-term traffic ka sabse powerful source hote hain. Aaj seekhein ke kaise aise topics choose karein, research karein, aur unhe SEO-friendly banayein taake wo months/years tak rank karein.

**Example:**
Ek blogger ne "Best budget smartphones under 20,000" likha. Usne:
1. Google Trends se check kiya ke yeh topic kabhi trend nahi hota.
2. Amazon aur Flipkart se latest models ki list banayi.
3. Har phone ke pros/cons likhe + affiliate links add kiye.
4. 6 months baad bhi yeh article #1 position par rank kar raha hai.

**Practice:**
Apne niche se ek evergreen topic choose karein (jaise "best running shoes for beginners") aur uske liye 5 relevant keywords find karein (Google Keyword Planner ya Ubersuggest use karein).

**Mini Project:**
Ek 500-800 words ka evergreen article draft karein jisme:
- Introduction (problem + solution)
- Top 5-7 items ki list (with pros/cons)
- Conclusion (call-to-action)

**Answer Key:**
evergreen topics, high-demand keywords, long-term traffic, affiliate potential
