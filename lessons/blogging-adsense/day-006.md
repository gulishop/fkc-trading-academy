# Day 6 — **Google Adsense Approval Ka 100% Working Formula (Step-by-Step Guide)**

**Concept:**
Google Adsense se approval lena ek blog ke liye sabse important step hai. Iske liye aapka blog **unique content, professional design, aur organic traffic** hona chahiye. Yahan hum 5 key factors par focus karenge jo approval ke chances 90% se zyada badha dete hain.

**Example:**
1. **Content Quality:** 10+ detailed articles (min. 800 words) with proper formatting.
2. **Website Design:** Clean layout, mobile-friendly, fast loading (use PageSpeed Insights).
3. **Traffic Source:** 20+ daily visitors (organic + social media se).
4. **Privacy Policy & About Page:** Must add (Google inhe check karta hai).
5. **No Copyright Content:** Original images + no copied text (use Copyscape).

**Practice:**
Apne blog par **Privacy Policy page** add karein (free template: [TermsFeed](https://www.termsfeed.com/) ya [PrivacyPolicies.com](https://www.privacypolicies.com/) se).

**Mini Project:**
Ek **sample ad placement** banayein (336x280 ya 728x90 size) aur blog ke homepage par add karein (test ke liye).

**Answer Key:**
Privacy Policy, About Page, 1000+ words content, mobile-friendly, organic traffic
