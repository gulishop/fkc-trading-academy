# Day 8 — **Affiliate Marketing Se Paise Kamane Ke 3 Sabse Asaan Tarike (Step-by-Step Guide)**

**Concept:**
Affiliate marketing ek aisa tareeka hai jismein aap kisi company ke product ya service ka link apne blog par share karte hain. Jab koi visitor us link se khareedta hai, to aapko commission milti hai. Yeh ek passive income ka source hai jo bloggers ke liye bohat faydemaand hai. Aaj hum seekhenge ke kaise apne blog par affiliate links add karein aur unko effectively use karein.

**Example:**
Maana aap ek "Tech Reviews" blog chalate hain. Aap Amazon Associates program join karte hain aur ek smartphone review likhte hain. Review ke beech mein aap likhte hain:
*"Yeh smartphone [Amazon Affiliate Link](https://amzn.to/3xyz123) par available hai. Agar aap isko khareedna chahte hain, to yahan se best deal payein!"*
Jab koi reader us link se smartphone khareedta hai, to aapko commission milti hai.

**Practice:**
1. Apne blog ke niche se related affiliate program join karein (jaise Amazon Associates, Daraz Affiliate, etc.).
2. Ek blog post ya article likhein jismein aap kisi product ka review ya comparison kar rahein hain.
3. Product ka affiliate link add karein (text ya button ke zariye).

**Mini Project:**
Ek 500-700 words ka blog post likhein jismein aap 3-4 affiliate products ka review karein. Post publish karne ke baad, 2-3 social media platforms par share karein.

**Answer Key:**
Amazon Associates, Daraz Affiliate, commission, passive income
