# Day 19 — **Google Analytics 4 (GA4) Ko Samajhna: Apne Blog Ki Performance Track Karein**

**Concept:**
Google Analytics 4 (GA4) aapke blog ki har activity track karta hai—traffic sources, page views, user behavior, aur conversions. Is se aap samajh sakte hain ke visitors kya dekh rahe hain, kitna time waste kar rahe hain, aur kahan se aapke blog par aa rahe hain. Yeh data SEO, content strategy, aur monetization ke decisions lene mein madad karta hai.

**Example:**
1. **Real-time traffic check:** Apne blog par abhi kitne visitors hain? GA4 ke "Realtime" section mein ja kar dekh sakte hain.
2. **Top pages:** "Engagement > Pages and screens" mein ja kar dekh sakte hain ke sabse zyada kon se articles par traffic aa raha hai.

**Practice:**
Apne GA4 account mein login karein, phir "Reports > Realtime" section mein ja kar check karein ke abhi aapke blog par kitne active users hain.

**Mini Project:**
Ek simple report taiyar karein:
1. Apne blog ki **top 3 most visited pages** ke URLs likhein.
2. **Top traffic source** (Google Search, Social Media, Direct, etc.) identify karein.

**Answer Key:**
top_pages_urls, top_traffic_source
