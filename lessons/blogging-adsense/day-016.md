# Day 16 — **Google Adsense Approval Ke Baad Pehle 5 Steps (Approval Milne Ke Bad Kya Karein?)**

**Concept:**
Google Adsense approval milne ke baad sabse pehle aapke blog par ads show hone lagenge, lekin adsense account ko properly setup karna aur blog ko optimize karna zaroori hai. Pehle 5 steps mein aapko ads placement, ad types, performance check, and fraud prevention jaise important points samajhne hain.

**Example:**
1. **Ads Placement:** Sidebar ya footer mein ek "Responsive Ad Unit" banayein (300x250 ya 728x90 size).
2. **Ad Types:** Auto ads enable karne ke baad bhi manual ad units (like display ads) add karein.
3. **Performance Check:** Google Adsense dashboard mein "Performance Reports" check karein aur low CTR wale ads ko hata dein.
4. **Fraud Prevention:** Kisi bhi tarah ke "click fraud" se bachein (e.g., apne hi ads par bar-bar click na karein).

**Practice:**
Apne Adsense dashboard mein jaayein, "Ads" > "By Ad Unit" par click karein, aur ek naya "Display Ad" banayein (300x250 size). Phir is ad unit ka code copy karein aur apne blog ke sidebar mein add karein.

**Mini Project:**
Apne blog par 3 different ad placements test karein (e.g., sidebar, below post, footer) aur 1 week tak unki performance track karein. Phir sabse behtar placement choose karein.

**Answer Key:**
responsive ad unit, sidebar placement, performance report, click fraud prevention
