# Day 7 — **E-commerce Product Mockups: Realistic Product Visuals Banana**

**Concept:**
E-commerce product mockups mein hum real-world products ke realistic visuals banate hain taaki customers ko samajh aaye ke product kaise dikhega. Iske liye Canva ke "Mockup" templates aur "Frame" tools ka istemal hota hai. Yeh design skills se product listings ko professional aur attractive banaya ja sakta hai.

**Example:**
1. Canva mein "Mockup" search karke ek product template select karo (e.g., T-shirt, mug, book).
2. Apna design upload karo ya Canva ke elements use karke product ko customize karo.
3. Background aur lighting adjust karke realistic look dene ke liye "Effects" (shadow, glow) apply karo.

**Practice:**
Apne laptop ya phone ke liye ek simple "T-shirt mockup" banane ka try karo. Canva mein "T-shirt mockup" template search karke apne design ko upload karo aur background ko plain white ya gradient se replace karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek chhota e-commerce store ke liye 3 different product mockups banaye (e.g., T-shirt, mug, notebook). Har mockup mein apna logo aur brand colors use karo taaki consistency ho.

**Answer Key:**
white background, shadow effect, brand colors, realistic lighting
