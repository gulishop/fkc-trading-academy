# Day 4 — **Brand Identity: Professional Brand Kit Banana**

**Concept:**
Ek strong brand identity ke liye logo ke sath-sath **brand kit** bhi zaroori hai. Ismein colors, fonts, brand voice aur visual elements ka set hota hai jo sabhi designs ko consistent rakhta hai. Aaj hum seekhenge ke Canva mein apna brand kit kaise banayein taake har project professional dikhe.

**Example:**
1. Open Canva → "Brand Kit" section mein jao.
2. Apna logo upload karo (ya Canva se banao).
3. 3-5 colors choose karo jo aapki brand ko represent kartein hain (use [coolors.co](https://coolors.co) for help).
4. 2-3 fonts select karo (heading + body text).
5. Save karo — ab sabhi templates mein yeh automatically apply hojayega.

**Practice:**
Apne brand kit mein:
- 3 colors (primary + secondary + accent)
- 2 fonts (heading + body)
- Apna logo upload karo
- "Save to Brand Kit" par click karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **social media post** design karo jismein aapka brand kit apply ho (colors + fonts + logo). Use for Instagram story ya Facebook post.

**Answer Key:**
logo-upload, 3-colors, 2-fonts, brand-kit-save
