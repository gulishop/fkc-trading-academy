# Day 10 — **Canva Se Pro-Level Designs Banane Ke Advanced Tips**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum advanced Canva techniques seekhenge jo aapke designs ko professional aur unique banayenge. Hum focus karenge: **Grids aur frames ka istemal**, **custom shapes aur effects**, aur **advanced typography tricks** ke zariye apne designs ko next level par le jaane ke.

**Example:**
1. **Grids ka use:** Apne design ko structured banane ke liye `Elements > Grids` se ek grid select karen, phir uske andar apne elements (text, images) ko arrange karen.
2. **Custom shapes:** `Elements > Shapes` se ek shape select karen, phir `Effects > Shadow` ya `Glow` add karen taake depth aaye.
3. **Advanced typography:** Text box select karen, phir `Effects > Curve` ya `3D` apply karen taake text 3D effect dekhne ko mile.

**Practice:**
Apne existing design mein ek **grid layout** apply karen aur 3 alag elements (text, image, shape) ko uske andar arrange karen taake design balanced ho.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **Instagram post** design karen jisme:
- 2-3 images ka grid layout ho
- Custom background shape (jaise hexagon ya rounded rectangle)
- Bold typography (effects jaise shadow ya outline ke saath)

**Answer Key:**
grid layout, custom shapes, 3D text, balanced composition
