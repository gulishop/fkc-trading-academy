# Day 5 — **YouTube Thumbnails: Click-Worthy Design Secrets**

**Concept:**
YouTube thumbnails are the first impression of your video. Learn how to design thumbnails that grab attention using contrast, bold text, and strategic placement. We’ll cover size requirements (1280x720px), safe zones, and psychology tricks like the "rule of thirds" to make your thumbnails stand out in crowded feeds.

**Example:**
1. Open Canva → Search "YouTube Thumbnail" template.
2. Pick a high-contrast background (e.g., dark red with yellow text).
3. Add **3-4 words max** in bold font (e.g., "GONE WRONG!" in Impact font).
4. Use an arrow or circle to highlight a key element (e.g., a shocked face).
5. Export as PNG (72dpi).

**Practice:**
Create a thumbnail for a hypothetical video titled *"5 Canva Hacks You NEVER Knew!"* Use a bright background, 1-2 words in bold, and a simple icon (e.g., a magic wand).

**Mini Project:**
Design **3 thumbnails** for different video topics (e.g., tutorial, vlog, reaction). Save them in a folder named "YouTube Thumbnails."

**Answer Key:**
high-contrast, bold text, safe zones, PNG
