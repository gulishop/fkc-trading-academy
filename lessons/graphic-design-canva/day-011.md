# Day 11 — **Brand Style Guides: Apna Design System Banana**

**Concept:**
Har successful brand ki pehchaan uska consistent design system hota hai. Aaj seekhein ke kaise ek **Brand Style Guide** banaya jaye jo aapke fonts, colors, logo usage, aur visual elements ko define kare. Isse aapke saare designs cohesive aur professional dikhenge—chahe wo social media post ho, logo ho, ya presentation.

**Example:**
1. **Colors:** Apne brand ke liye 3-5 primary colors choose karo (e.g., #FF6B6B for primary, #4ECDC4 for secondary).
2. **Fonts:** 2 fonts select karo—ek heading ke liye (e.g., Poppins Bold), ek body text ke liye (e.g., Open Sans Regular).
3. **Logo Rules:** Logo ka size, spacing, aur background (white/transparent) specify karo.
4. **Templates:** Pre-made Canva templates mein yeh guidelines apply karo taake consistency bani rahe.

**Practice:**
Apne brand (ya kisi fictional brand) ke liye ek **Brand Style Guide** banane ke liye:
1. Canva mein ek new document kholo.
2. 3-5 colors, 2 fonts, aur logo usage rules likho.
3. Ek simple template design karo jismein yeh guidelines apply hon.

**Mini Project:**
**"Mera Brand Kit"**
- Ek Canva document banayein jismein:
  - Apne brand colors (hex codes ke saath)
  - Apne selected fonts
  - Logo usage ke 2-3 rules (e.g., "Logo ka size 100px se kam nahi hona chahiye")
  - Ek sample design (e.g., Instagram post ya business card) jismein yeh guidelines follow ki gayi hain.

**Answer Key:**
color palette, font pair, logo rules, template example
