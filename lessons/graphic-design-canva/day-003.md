**Day 3 — Social Media Post Templates: Design Karna Seekhein (Instagram, Facebook, Twitter)**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum social media post templates banayenge jo engaging, professional aur brand ke hisab se suitable hon. Har platform ke liye alag dimensions aur style hote hain (e.g., Instagram square/post, Facebook cover, Twitter header). Templates se time bachta hai aur consistency bani rehti hai.

**Example:**
1. **Instagram Post (1080x1080px)**:
   - Background: Gradient ya solid color (#FF6B6B se #4ECDC4).
   - Text: Bold font (Montserrat Bold) + emoji (🔥).
   - Image: Product photo ya stock image (Pexels se free download).
   - Overlay: 20% opacity black ya white box for text readability.

2. **Facebook Cover (820x312px)**:
   - Left side: Brand logo + tagline.
   - Right side: Call-to-action button ("Shop Now" ya "Learn More").
   - Background: Blurred product image ya abstract pattern.

**Practice:**
Apna ek Instagram post template banayein (1080x1080px) niche diye hue style se:
- Background: Light blue (#E6F7FF).
- Text: "Summer Sale! 30% Off 🌞" (font: Poppins SemiBold, white color).
- Image: Free stock photo of sunglasses (Unsplash se).

**Mini Project:**
Ek **3-post series** banayein (Instagram/Facebook ke liye):
1. Post 1: Product image + discount offer.
2. Post 2: Customer testimonial (text + quote).
3. Post 3: Behind-the-scenes (team photo ya workspace).

**Answer Key:**
Gradient background, bold font, emoji, CTA text
