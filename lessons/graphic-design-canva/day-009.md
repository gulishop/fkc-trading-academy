# Day 9 — **Freelance Design Ka Business: Fiverr aur Etsy par Apna Design Kaise Beche**

**Concept:**
Freelance design bech kar paisa kamana ek popular side hustle hai. Fiverr par logo, social media posts, ya presentations bech sakte hain jabke Etsy par digital products (templates, mockups, etc.) list kiye ja sakte hain. Aaj seekhenge kaise apne design skills ko monetize karna hai, pricing strategies, aur kaise attract kare clients.

**Example:**
1. **Fiverr par Gig Create karna:**
   - "I will design a professional logo for your brand" likh kar gig title do.
   - 3-5 design samples upload karo (Canva se bana hua).
   - Pricing: $5-$20 (beginner ke liye) ya $30+ (advanced).
   - Gig description mein batado ki aap Canva expert hain aur unlimited revisions offer karte hain.

2. **Etsy par Digital Product List karna:**
   - "Canva Instagram Story Template Pack" naam ka product banayo.
   - 3-5 high-quality preview images upload karo.
   - $5-$15 price tag lagao (competitors ko check kar ke).

**Practice:**
Apne Fiverr ya Etsy account create karo aur ek simple gig/product list karo (example: "I will design a YouTube thumbnail in Canva").

**Mini Project:**
Ek Canva template (jaise Instagram post, logo, ya resume) banayo aur usko Fiverr (services) ya Etsy (digital product) par list karo. Screenshot ya link share karo (agar possible ho).

**Answer Key:**
Fiverr gig title, 3-5 samples, pricing strategy, Etsy product name, preview images
