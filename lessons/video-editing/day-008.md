# Day 8 — **YouTube SEO: Thumbnails aur Titles Se Views Badhaein**

**Concept:**
YouTube SEO sirf video content ka hai—thumbnails aur titles bhi search aur click-through rate (CTR) par bade asar dalte hain. Ek achha thumbnail aur title video ko "clickable" banata hai, chahe content kitna bhi accha ho. Aaj seekhein ke kaise **high-CTR thumbnails** banayein aur **SEO-friendly titles** likhein jo algorithm ko bhi impress karein.

**Example:**
1. **Thumbnail Design:**
   - **Contrast:** Background se text/face alag ho (e.g., white text on dark bg ya vice versa).
   - **Emotion:** Ek cheez jo dekhne wale ko "yeh dekh!" kahe—jaise surprise, curiosity, ya urgency.
   - **Text:** Max 3-5 words, bold font (e.g., "I Tried $100 vs $1000 Lighting!").
   - **Tools:** Canva (pre-made templates), Photoshop, ya CapCut ka "Text" tool.

2. **Title Writing:**
   - **Formula:** [Keyword] + [Promise/Result] + [Curiosity Hook]
     - Example: *"I Spent 24 Hours in a $1M Mansion… Here’s What Happened"* (vs *"Mansion Tour"*).
   - **Keywords:** YouTube search bar mein type karke "suggested searches" dekhein (e.g., "how to edit videos in CapCut" → "how to edit videos in CapCut for beginners 2024").
   - **Length:** 60 characters tak (mobile pe full show hota hai).

**Practice:**
Apne **Day 7 ke Short-Form video** (ya kisi existing video) ka **3 different thumbnail + title** banayein. Har ek mein:
- Ek "high-contrast" design ho.
- Ek "curiosity hook" ho (e.g., "You Won’t Believe What Happens Next!").
- Ek "keyword-rich" title ho (e.g., "CapCut Se Viral Reels Kaise Banayein? 5 Tips 2024").

**Mini Project:**
Ek **YouTube video idea** choose karke uska **thumbnail + title** banayein jo **CTR 5%+** paida kar sake. (Hint: "Top 10" ya "Mistakes" wale titles zyada click hote hain.)

**Answer Key:**
high contrast, curiosity hook, keyword placement, mobile-friendly
