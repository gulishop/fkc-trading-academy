# Day 1 — "CapCut/Premiere Ka Interface Samajhiye"

**Concept:**
Aaj hum video editing software (CapCut ya Premiere Pro) ka interface samajhenge. Har tool ki location, menus, timeline, preview panel — sabko pehchaan karna seekhenge. Yeh foundation hai editing ka, iske bina aage kuch nahi ho sakta.

**Example:**
1. **CapCut:** Open karo → Top par "Media" (import files), "Timeline" (cutting ka area), "Preview" (right side).
2. **Premiere Pro:** "Project Panel" (left, files), "Timeline" (bottom), "Program Monitor" (right, preview).

**Practice:**
Apne software mein ek naya project banayein. 3-4 photos/videos import karo. Timeline par drag karo. Preview mein dekhein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek 10-second clip banayein — 2 photos ko timeline par lagayein, unke beech ek transition add karo. Export karo.

**Answer Key:**
Media import, Timeline, Preview, Transition
