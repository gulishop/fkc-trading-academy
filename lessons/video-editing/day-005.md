# Day 5 — **Color Grading: Mood Banane Ka Rahasya**

**Concept:**
Color grading ek video ka mood, tone aur professional look set karne ka process hai. Yeh bas "brightness/contrast" se zyada hai – yeh colors ko adjust karke ek consistent aur engaging visual style banata hai. Har platform (Reels, YouTube, Shorts) ke liye alag mood hota hai: Reels mein vibrant colors, YouTube mein cinematic tones, aur shorts mein high-contrast pop.

**Example:**
1. **CapCut mein:** "Adjust" > "Color" par click karein.
2. **Temperature slider** se warm (orange) ya cool (blue) mood set karein.
3. **Vibrance** badhaein taake colors zyada lively dikhein (lekin overdo na karein).
4. **Shadows/Highlights** ko adjust karein taake details saaf dikhein.

**Practice:**
Apne ek pehle se edit kiye hue video mein "Color" tab kholein aur:
- Temperature ko -10 se +10 tak adjust karein (warm vs cool effect dekhne ke liye).
- Vibrance ko 5-15% badhaein aur effect note karein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek 10-second clip (apni choice ka) lein aur usko 3 alag moods mein color grade karein:
1. **Happy/Vibrant** (high vibrance, warm tones)
2. **Moodi/Cinematic** (cool tones, low contrast)
3. **Dark/Drama** (high contrast, shadows boost)

**Answer Key:**
warm tones, high vibrance, cool tones, low contrast
