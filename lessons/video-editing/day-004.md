# Day 4 — **Advanced Transitions: Motion aur Energy Badhaein**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum advanced transitions par focus karenge jo aapke edits ko professional aur engaging banayein. Simple cuts ke alag, motion-based transitions (jaise zoom, spin, slide) aur effects (glitch, blur, color shifts) use karke apne videos ko dynamic banayein. Yeh transitions pacing ko maintain karte hue visual interest bhi add karte hain.

**Example:**
CapCut mein:
1. Do clips select karo.
2. "Transition" tab mein "Zoom" ya "Slide" choose karo.
3. Transition duration adjust karo (0.5-1 second ideal hai).
4. "Apply" par click karo — ab clip change hone par zoom-in/out ya slide effect aayega.

Premiere Pro mein:
1. Razor tool se clips cut karo.
2. Transition folder se "Dip to Black" ya "Cross Dissolve" drag karo clips ke beech.
3. Duration ko 15-30 frames par set karo (Ctrl+K se trim adjust karo).

**Practice:**
Apne phone ya laptop se 2-3 clips record karo (e.g., walking, talking, object move karte hue). Unhe CapCut/Premiere mein import karo aur **2 alag advanced transitions** apply karo (ek motion-based, ek effect-based).

**Mini Project:**
Ek 15-second ka "transition showcase" video banayein jismein:
- 3 alag transitions hon (e.g., zoom, glitch, slide).
- Har transition ke beech 1-2 second ka gap ho.
- Background music ho jo transitions ke timing se match kare.

**Answer Key:**
zoom, slide, glitch effect, dip to black
