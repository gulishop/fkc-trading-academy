# Day 19 — "Doctor-Doctor aur Dukan-Dukan: Khel ke seekhein!"

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhein ge ke khel ke zariye kaise hum apne aap ko aur doosron ko safe rakh sakte hain. Jab tum doctor-doctor ya dukaan-dukaan khelte ho, yaad rakhna ke tumhari body sirf tumhari hai. Agar koi tumse pooche ke "Mera hath pakad lo" ya "Mujhe gale lagao", toh tum "Na, meri body meri hai" keh sakte ho. Koi bhi cheez secret rakhne ki zaroorat nahi!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Aman aur Ayesha ne doctor-doctor khela. Aman ne kaha, "Ayesha, tumhari temperature check karte hain." Ayesha ne haath badhaya. Aman ne socha, "Agar main uska haath pakad loon toh kya hoga?" Usne Ayesha se poochha, "Kya main tumhara haath pakad sakta hoon?" Ayesha ne muskurate hue kaha, "Nahi, meri body meri hai!" Aman ne samajh liya ke yeh theek baat hai.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi tumse pooche ke 'Chalo, tumhara haath pakad lo' toh tum kya karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhari body tumhari hai—kisi ko bhi "Na" kehne ka haq hai! Agar koi cheez ajeeb lage, toh ammi/abbu ko zaroor batana.
