# Day 18 — "Puzzle aur jigsaw se sabar aur soch badhaen!"

**Concept:**
Bachon, jab hum puzzle ya jigsaw khelte hain, toh humein ek ek karke sab parts jodni hoti hain. Isi tarah, zindagi mein bhi kabhi-kabhi humein chhoti chhoti cheezein karni hoti hain, lekin agar hum sabar rakhein aur achhe se sochen, toh hum har mushkil ko hal kar sakte hain!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Aman ko ek naya jigsaw puzzle mila. Usne socha, "Mujhe pehle sabse edge ke pieces dhoondne hain." Usne thoda sabar kiya, aur ek ek karke sab pieces jodte gaye. Aakhiri mein, usko ek puri tasveer mil gayi! Usne samjha, "Agar main ek saath sab jodna chahta, toh mushkil ho jata, lekin ek ek karke karne se, main jeet gaya!"

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar tum koi puzzle khel rahe ho aur usko hal karne mein mushkil ho rahi ho, toh tum kya karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Har mushkil ko hal karne ke liye sabar aur soch zaroori hai—chahe woh puzzle ho ya zindagi ka koi sawal!
