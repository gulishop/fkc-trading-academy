# Day 13 — "Ginti wala hopscotch: Khel ke seekhein!"

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke kaisa khel khel kar hum apni ginti aur balance ko mazboot bana sakte hain! Yeh hopscotch ka khel hai jo hum chhote chhote number likh kar khubsurat banate hain. Isse humare pair bhi mazboot hote hain aur dimag bhi tezi se kaam karta hai!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Areeba ne apne school ke playground par ek rangin hopscotch dekha. Usne socha, "Main bhi yeh khel kar dekhti hoon!" Usne apne number 1 se 10 tak likhe aur dekhne laga ke kaise woh ek pair se doosre pair par jump kare. Har number par woh ek number ke hisaab se ginti karte hue aage badhti rahi. Areeba ko itna maza aaya ke woh roz is khel ko khelne lagi!

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar tum kisi khel mein koi number ya step chhod do, toh tum kya karogi?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Hamesha yaad rakhna ke tumhara jism tumhara hai. Agar koi tumhe koi ajeeb baat ke liye kaha raha ho, toh usko "Na" keh do aur apne ammi-abbu ko zaroor batana!
