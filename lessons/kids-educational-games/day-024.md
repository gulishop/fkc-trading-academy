# Day 24 — "Rang milaan: Har cheez ka apna rang!"

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhein ge ke har cheez ka apna rang hota hai, aur rang milaana bhi ek mazaak khel hai! Lekin yaad rakhna, agar koi cheez tumhare rang se mehsoos na ho, ya tumhe ajeeb lage, toh tumhe usko karne ki zaroorat nahi. Tumhara jism tumhara hai—tumhe koi cheez karne ya na karne ka poora haq hai!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Arsh ki maan ne usko ek khel diya: "In rang-baato ko alag karo!" Arsh ne hansa, "Maa, yeh toh aasan hai!" Lekin jab usko ek cheez ka rang pasand nahi aaya, toh usne kaha, "Nahi, main yeh nahi karna." Maan ne usko gale lagaya, "Bilkul theek kaha! Tumhe jo pasand ho, woh karo."

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi cheez tumhe ajeeb lage ya tumhe usse karne mein maza na aaye, toh tum kya karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhara jism tumhara hai—tumhe koi cheez karne ya na karne ka poora haq hai! Agar koi cheez tumhe comfortable na lage, toh foran humein batana.
