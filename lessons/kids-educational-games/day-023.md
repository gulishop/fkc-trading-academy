# Day 23 — "Ginti wala hopscotch: Khel ke seekhein!"

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhein ge ke kaisa hai jab koi humse zyada chahne ka naam pe apna hath lagaye? Ya phir humari izzat ka khayal na rakhe? Aise waqt mein humein apne dil ki aawaaz sunni chahiye. Sabse pehle, yaad rakhna ke tumhara jism tumhara hai—kisi ko tumhari izzat karni hi hogi!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Bilal ne dekha ke uske chacha ne uske dost ko gusse se hath uthaaya. Bilal ko bura laga. Usne apne abbu ko bataya. Abbu ne kaha, "Tumne bilkul theek kiya, Bilal. Kisi ko tumhari izzat karni hi chahiye!"

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi tumse aise cheez pooche jo tumhe achha na lage, to tum kya karogi?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhara jism tumhara hai—kisi ko tumhari izzat karni hi hogi! Agar koi cheez ajeeb lage, to foran ammi/abbu ko batana.
