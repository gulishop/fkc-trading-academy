# Day 8 — "Tukre jod ke puzzle banaana: Sabar seekho!"

**Concept:**
Bachon, jab hum puzzle ya jigsaw ke tukde jodte hain, toh humein thoda sabar rakhna hota hai. Agar ek tukda match nahi ho raha, toh gussa nahi aana chahiye—bas thoda dhyaan se dekhna hai. Sabar se kaam karne se hi puzzle poora hota hai, aur hum bhi mehnat karna seekh jaate hain!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Areeba ne ek naya puzzle liya. Pehle usko lag raha tha ke yeh toh bahut mushkil hai! Ek tukda match karne ke liye usko 5 baar try karna pada, lekin usne hara nahi maana. Uske baad usko ek tukda mil gaya, phir doosra… aur aise hi poora puzzle ho gaya! Areeba ko bahut khushi hui ke usne sabar rakha aur mehnat ki.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar tumhe koi kaam karte waqt gussa aaye toh kya karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Puzzle ya koi bhi kaam karte waqt, agar thoda mushkil lage toh gussa mat karna—bas thoda aur try karna! Sabar se hi har cheez poori hoti hai. 💙
