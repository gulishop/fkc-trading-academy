# Day 21 — **Yaadasht ka khel: Yaad rakhne ka maza lo!**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhein ge ke yaad rakhna kitna asaan hai! Yaad rakhna zaroori hai, lekin agar koi cheez yaad rakhne ke liye dabbawala ho, ya koi cheez aapko pareshan kare, toh aapko "na" kehne ka haq hai. Koi bhi cheez aapke jism ya dil ko pareshan kare, toh turant ammi-abbu ya kisi bade ko batana hai—chahe woh koi secret ho ya na ho!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Areeba ne ek khel khela jisme usko cards ke pair milane the. Usne ek card dekha jisme ek sundar phool tha. Usne usko yaad rakha. Phir usko doosra card mila jisme ek phool ka hi picture tha. "Yeh toh meri yaad mein hai!" usne socha aur cheers kiya. Khel khatam hone par usne ammi ko kaha, "Mummy, maine yeh phool yaad rakha!" Ammi ne usko gale lagaya aur kaha, "Bahut achha, beta! Yaad rakhna toh acha hai, lekin agar koi cheez tumhein pareshan kare, toh turant batana."

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar tum kisi khel mein ya kisi cheez mein itna maza le rahe ho ke tumhe pata hi na chale ke tum thak gaye ho, toh kya tum mujhe batayenge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Yaad rakhna achha hai, lekin agar koi cheez tumhein pareshan kare, toh turant ammi-abbu ko batana!
