# Day 17 — "Aankhein band karke aawaz pehchanno!"

**Concept:**
Bachon, aaj hum seekhein ge ke kaise aankhein band karke bhi aawaz pehchanni hai! Yeh game humein aur bhi tez aur dhyaan se sunein ka tareeqa sikhata hai. Kabhi-kabhi aise bhi hota hai ke humein kisi cheez ko pehchanna hota hai bina usko dekhay, jaise ke koi dost door se bulata hai ya phir koi instrument bajta hai. Aise waqt humari aawaz pehchanna bohat zaroori hota hai!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Ek din, Ayaan ne aankhein band ki aur kaha, "Abbu, main sun raha hoon!" Abbu ne ek ghanti bajayi aur Ayaan ne turant kaha, "Ye toh ghanti hai!" Abbu ne muskura kar kaha, "Bilkul sahi! Aankhein band karke bhi hum aawaz pehchanna seekh sakte hain."

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar tum kisi aise aawaz ko sun rahe ho jo tumhein ajeeb lage, toh kya karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Hamesha yaad rakhna, tumhari aawaz pehchanna aur tumhara imaan tumhare liye sabse pehle hai!
