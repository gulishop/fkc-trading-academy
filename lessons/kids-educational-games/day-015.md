# Day 15 — Shaklein chhupaao aur dhoondo

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhein ge ke kaise apne aap ko chhupana aur doosron ki shaklein dhoondna ek mazedaar game ho sakta hai. Lekin yaad rakhna, agar koi tumhe chhupne ke liye majboor kare ya tumhe takleef de, toh yeh *theek nahi* hai. Tumhara jism tumhara hai, aur tumhe *na* kehne ka poora haq hai!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Areeba ne apne dost ke saath "Shaklein chhupaao" khela. Usne ek darwaze ke peeche chhup kar apni aankhein band ki. Par ek bura admi usko chhupne ke liye majboor karne laga. Areeba ne jaldi se apne dost ko bulaya, aur woh dono mil kar apne teacher ko bataya. Teacher ne us admi ko roka, aur Areeba ko samjhaya ke agar koi tumhe takleef de, toh tumhe foran baat karni chahiye.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi tumhe chhupne ke liye koi cheez kahe, toh tum kya karogi?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhara jism tumhara hai—kisi ko tumse chhupne ya takleef dene ka haq nahi hai. Agar koi aise kahe, toh foran apne maata-pita ya kisi trusted bade insaan ko batana.
