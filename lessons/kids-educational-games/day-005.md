# Day 5 — Shaklein chhupaao aur dhoondo

**Concept:**
Bachon, aaj hum seekhenge ke agar koi cheez ya shakal chhupayi jaaye, toh hum use dhoondh sakte hain! Jaise pehle humne rangon aur gintein seekhi, ab hum seekhenge ke apne aap ko ya apni cheezon ko kaise chhupana hai aur kaise dhoondhna hai. Yeh humein samajhne mein madad karega ke koi cheez ya insaan kahin chhup gaya ho, toh hum use pehchaan sakte hain.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rohan apne ghar ke baahar khel raha tha. Usne ek patthar lekar ek darakht ke peeche chhupa diya. Uske baad usne apni ammi se poochha, "Ammi, kya aap yeh patthar dhoondh sakte hain?" Ammi ne muskurate hue kaha, "Haan beta, agar main thoda dhyaan se dekhoon, toh main use dhoondh sakti hoon!" Rohan ne hansa aur kaha, "Ab main bhi seekh jaaoonga!"

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
Beta, agar tumhari koi cheez chhup jaaye, toh tum kya karoge?

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Apne aap ko ya apni cheezon ko chhupana aur dhoondhna ek achha tareeqa hai! Lekin yaad rakhna, tumhara jism tumhara hai, aur agar koi tumhein chhupane ke liye kahe, toh foran ammi/abbu ko batana.
