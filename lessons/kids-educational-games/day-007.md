# Day 7 — "Aankhein band karke aawaz pehchanna!"

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke aawazon ko pehchanna kaise hai! Kabhi kabhi aise log hote hain jo humse baat karte hain aur humein unki aawaz pehchanne mein madad milti hai. Lekin yaad rakhna: agar koi aapki aawaz sun raha hai aur aap usse comfortable nahi hain, toh aap "na" keh sakte hain. Koi bhi aapko "secret" rakhne ke liye majboor nahi kar sakta!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Areeba ne aankhein band ki aur kaha, "Aunty, aapki aawaz sunai de rahi hai!" Aunty ne hassi ke saath kaha, "Bilkul sahi! Areeba, tumhari aawaz bhi pehchan sakti ho?" Areeba ne muskurate hue kaha, "Haan, main aapki aawaz pehchanti hoon!"

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi tumse phone par baat kare aur tum uski aawaz pehchan na sako, toh kya karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
"Mera jism mera hai, main kisi ko bhi 'haan' ya 'na' kehne ke liye aazad hoon!"
