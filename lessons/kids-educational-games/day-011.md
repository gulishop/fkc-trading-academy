# Day 11 — Yaadasht ka Jadoo: Card Matching Game

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke yaad rakhna bhi ek khel hai! Par yaad rakhna zaroori hai ke koi bhi cheez humara jism ya dil pe jabar nahi kar sakti. Agar koi cheez aapko yaad rakhne ke liye dabav de raha ho, ya aapko ajeeb lage, toh foran ammi/abbu ko batana hai—chahe woh koi bhi ho.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Aman ko ek naya card game mila. Usne do cards ko match kiya, aur usko yaad aaya ke uske dost ne usko pehle bhi yehi card dikhaya tha. Usne khush hokar kaha, "Mujhe yaad aa gaya!" Ammi ne usko gale lagaya aur kaha, "Yaad rakhna bhi ek talent hai, lekin koi bhi cheez tum pe jabar nahi kar sakta."

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi game ya khel khelte waqt tumhe yaad rakhne ke liye koi ajeeb tareeqa bataye, toh kya karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhara jism aur dil sirf tumhara hai. Koi bhi cheez tumse jabar nahi kar sakta—chahe woh yaad rakhna ho ya koi aur kaam. Agar koi cheez ajeeb lage, toh foran ammi/abbu ko batana hai!
