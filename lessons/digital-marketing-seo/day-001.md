# Day 1 — Digital Marketing Ka Poora Landscape: Kya Hai Aur Kaise Shuru Karen?

**Concept:**
Digital marketing sabhi online tareeqon se apne product ya service ko promote karne ka naam hai. Ismein SEO, social media, email, ads, aur content marketing sab aate hain. Pehle samajhna zaroori hai ke yeh ek puzzle hai—har piece alag hai lekin mil kar ek picture banati hai.

**Example:**
Imagine a local bakery. Unke liye digital marketing ka matlab hai:
1. Google par "best cake in [city]" search karne walon ko unki website show karna (SEO)
2. Instagram par daily cake photos share karna (Social Media)
3. Customers ko birthday discounts ke liye email bhejna (Email Marketing)
4. Facebook par Rs. 500 ka ad chalana (Paid Ads)

**Practice:**
Apne business ya interest ke hisaab se 3 digital marketing channels choose karen jinse aap apne audience tak pohanch sakte hain. (E.g., agar aap freelance writer hain to LinkedIn + Blog + Cold Email.)

**Mini Project:**
Ek simple mind map banaen jismein aap apne ideal customer (e.g., "25-35 age ki working women jo healthy snacks khareedti hain") ke liye 3 best digital marketing channels select karen.

**Answer Key:**
SEO, Social Media, Email Marketing
