# Day 4 — **Content Marketing Strategy: Apne Audience Ko Attract Aur Retain Karen**

**Concept:**
Content marketing ek long-term strategy hai jisme valuable, relevant aur consistent content banaya jata hai taake apne target audience ko attract, engage aur retain kiya ja sake. Ye SEO ka bhi backbone hai kyunke search engines chahte hain ke users ko useful content mile. Isme blogs, videos, social media posts, infographics, aur email content shamil hote hain.

**Example:**
Ek fitness trainer ka example lete hain. Unka target audience "home workout beginners" hai. Unka content strategy yeh ho sakta hai:
1. **Blog Posts:** "5 Minute Home Workouts for Beginners"
2. **YouTube Videos:** "How to Do Squats Correctly at Home"
3. **Social Media:** Instagram reels par "Quick 10-Minute Workout Routine"
4. **Email Newsletter:** Weekly workout tips aur discounts on their premium plans

**Practice:**
Apne business ya interest ke hisaab se 3 types ke content ideas likhein (jaise blog, video, social media post). Har idea ka 1 line ka description bhi add karen.

**Mini Project:**
Ek simple **content calendar** banayein (Excel ya Google Sheets mein) jisme aapke 1 week ke content plan hon. Columns hon: Date, Content Type (Blog/Video/Social), Topic, Platform (Website/YouTube/Instagram).

**Answer Key:**
content ideas, content calendar, audience engagement, value-driven
