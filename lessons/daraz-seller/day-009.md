# Day 9 — **Daraz Par Competitor Analysis Se Profit Margin Badhane Ka Tarika**

**Concept:**
Competitor analysis se pata chalta hai ke market mein kaunse products beche ja rahe hain, unki pricing kya hai, aur unke weaknesses kya hain. Isse apne product ko behtar banaya ja sakta hai aur profit margin increase kiya ja sakta hai.

**Example:**
1. **Product Research:** Daraz par ek product search karein (e.g., "Wireless Earbuds").
2. **Top Sellers Check:** Top 5 listings ke pricing, reviews, aur product details ko note karein.
3. **Gap Analysis:** Dekhein ke kya features missing hain ya pricing zyada hai. (e.g., agar competitors Rs. 5,000 mein bech rahe hain, lekin koi Rs. 4,500 mein beche to uski pricing strategy ko analyze karein.)

**Practice:**
Apne niche ke top 3 competitors ke listings ko open karein aur unki pricing, reviews, aur product specifications ko note karein. Phir ek short comparison table banayein.

**Mini Project:**
Apne product ke liye ek **competitor pricing chart** banayein jismein:
- Competitor ka product name
- Unka price
- Unke reviews (rating)
- Unke weaknesses (agar koi ho)

**Answer Key:**
competitor names, pricing range, review ratings, product gaps
