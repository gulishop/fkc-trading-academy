# Day 12 — **Daraz Par Seasonal Products Ka Fikar Karna: Eid, Ramadan, Aur Mehndi Season Mein Profit Kaise Badhaye**

**Concept:**
Har season ke saath Pakistani market mein demand badalti hai. Eid, Ramadan, aur Mehndi season mein specific products (jaise dresses, jewelry, gifts) ka demand 3-5x badh jata hai. Aaj seekhoge ki kaise seasonal trends ko pehle se identify karke, inventory aur promotions plan karne se sales aur profit increase hota hai.

**Example:**
Ramadan 2024 ke liye, Daraz par "Eid Dresses" category mein search volume 80% badh gaya tha. Ek seller ne 15 days pehle se 500+ listings add ki, aur "Ramadan Discount" vouchers chalaye. Result: 3x sales aur 40% zyada profit margin.

**Practice:**
Apne Daraz Seller Center mein jaye → "Reports" → "Trending Searches" check karein. Apne category ke 3 seasonal keywords (jaise "Eid Dresses 2024", "Ramadan Gift Sets") note karein jo aane wale 30 days mein trend mein aa sakte hain.

**Mini Project:**
1. Apne niche ke 3 seasonal products select karein (jaise Mehndi dresses, Eid jewelry).
2. Google Trends ya Daraz ke "Trending Searches" se unke demand ka pattern check karein.
3. Ek simple Excel sheet banayein jismein:
   - Product name
   - Expected demand month
   - Current stock
   - Planned discount/voucher

**Answer Key:**
seasonal trends, demand forecasting, inventory planning, promotional strategy
