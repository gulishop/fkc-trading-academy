# Day 8 — **Daraz Store Ko Monthly Scale Karne Ki Strategy**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke apne Daraz store ko har mahine kaise grow karain. Iske liye hum "Monthly Sales Target" set karna, best-selling products identify karna, aur inventory management ke basics cover karenge. Agar aapka store consistently grow kar raha hai, toh aapko bhi isi tarah plan banani hogi.

**Example:**
1. **Target Set Karein:** Agar aapko 50,000 rupees ka monthly target hai, toh har product ke liye realistic sales goal decide karein (e.g., Product A: 10,000, Product B: 15,000, etc.).
2. **Best-Sellers Analyze Karein:** Daraz Seller Center mein "Sales Report" check karein aur top 3 products ko identify karein. In products ko promote karne ke liye extra vouchers ya ads ka istemal karein.
3. **Inventory Plan Karein:** Agar koi product fast-selling hai, toh zyada stock order karein taake out-of-stock na ho.

**Practice:**
Apne Daraz Seller Center mein ja kar "Sales Report" download karein aur apne top 3 best-selling products ko note karein. In products ke liye next month ka sales target decide karein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek simple Excel sheet banayein jismein aap:
- Apne top 3 products ke liye current month ka sales data
- Next month ka target
- Stock availability (agar applicable ho)
Likhein. Isse aapko future planning mein madad milegi.

**Answer Key:**
Sales report download, top 3 products identify, monthly target set, inventory check
