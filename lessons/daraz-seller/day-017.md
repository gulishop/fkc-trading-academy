# Day 17 — **Daraz Par Negative Reviews Ko Professional Tarike Se Handle Karna**

**Concept:**
Negative reviews Daraz par seller ki credibility par asar dalte hain. Inko ignore karne ki jagah, unhe professionally handle karna zaroori hai. Sabse pehle review ke root cause samjho (product defect, delivery issue, ya customer ki expectation mismatch), phir prompt response do taake buyer ka trust bana rahe.

**Example:**
Ek customer ne "Product damaged" ka review diya hai. Aap usko private message karke poochhein: *"Assalamu Alaikum, hum aapki concern ko samajhte hain. Hum aapke order ko check kar rahe hain. Kya aap humein product ki photo share kar sakte hain?"* Agar product defect hai, toh replacement ya refund offer karein.

**Practice:**
Apne Daraz store ke kisi 1 negative review ko dhyaan se padhein. Uske liye 1 professional response draft karein (2-3 lines mein).

**Mini Project:**
Ek "Review Response Template" taiyar karein jismein common negative reviews (late delivery, wrong product, damaged item) ke liye ready-made responses hon.

**Answer Key:**
acknowledge issue, apologize, solution offer, follow-up
