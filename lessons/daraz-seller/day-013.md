# Day 13 — **Daraz Par Dynamic Pricing Strategy: AI Aur Manual Tarike Se Profit Kaise Badhaye**

**Concept:**
Dynamic pricing ka matlab hai apne product ki price ko real-time market conditions, demand, aur competitor actions ke hisab se adjust karna. AI tools (jaise Daraz ka built-in pricing optimizer) aur manual strategies (jaise discount bundles ya flash sales) se aap apne margin ko 10-30% tak badha sakte hain. Sabse zyada behtar pricing wo hoti hai jo customer ko value deti ho *aur* aapki profit bhi maximize kare.

**Example:**
Ek electronics seller ka "Bluetooth Speaker" 3,500 PKR par list hai. Usne dekha ke competitor ne 3,200 PKR par offer ki hai. Usne **Daraz Pricing Tool** use kiya, jisme AI ne sujhaya ke 3,399 PKR par bhi wo #1 rank par rahega *aur* 15% zyada profit milega. Usne price 3,399 PKR ki aur **Daraz Ads** ke zariye "Limited Time Deal" ka banner lagaya. Result: 2x orders aur 22% zyada margin.

**Practice:**
Apne Daraz Seller Center mein jaiye > "Pricing" section mein apne top 3 products ke liye **Daraz Pricing Tool** check karein. AI ke sujhay ko follow karke price ko adjust karein (agar tool available ho).

**Mini Project:**
Ek **Flash Sale Campaign** plan karein:
1. Apne 1 best-selling product ko select karein.
2. 20% discount offer karein lekin **Daraz Ads** mein "Flash Sale" keyword use karein.
3. 3 din ke liye run karein aur result note karein (orders, conversion rate, profit).

**Answer Key:**
AI pricing optimizer, competitor tracking, flash sale banner, profit margin increase
