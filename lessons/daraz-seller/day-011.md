# Day 11 — **Daraz Par Best Selling Products Ki Top 10 Categories Ko Samajhna**

**Concept:**
Daraz par sabse zyada bechne wale products ki categories ko identify karna ek seller ke liye bohat zaroori hai. In categories mein competition zyada hota hai, lekin demand bhi high hoti hai. Aaj hum seekhenge ke kaise in top categories ko analyze karke apne business ke liye best products choose karein.

**Example:**
1. **Daraz Seller Center** mein jao → **Analytics** → **Top Selling Categories** par click karo.
2. **Filters** laga kar "Pakistan" select karo aur **last 30 days** ka data dekho.
3. Dekho ke konsi categories mein **highest sales volume** hai (e.g., Electronics, Fashion, Home Appliances).
4. **Competition check karo** — agar top 5 sellers mein 100+ reviews hain, toh competition tough hai.

**Practice:**
Apne Daraz Seller Center mein jao, **Top Selling Categories** section mein ja kar **Pakistan** ke liye **last 30 days** ke data ko dekho. Top 3 categories note karo jin mein aapka interest hai.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **simple Excel sheet** banayein jismein aap neechay di gayi details fill karein:
- **Top 3 Categories** (e.g., Mobile Accessories, Women’s Clothing, Home Decor)
- **Average Price Range** (e.g., Rs. 1,000–5,000)
- **Competition Level** (Low/Medium/High)
- **Your Potential Product Idea** (e.g., Wireless Earbuds under Rs. 3,000)

**Answer Key:**
Top 3 categories, price range, competition level, product idea
