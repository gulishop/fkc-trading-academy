# Day 4 — **High-Converting Product Images Banana (Jo Daraz Par Bikhein)**

**Concept:**
Daraz par behtareen product images wo hain jo customer ko instantly attract karain, trust banain aur "add to cart" click karne par majboor kar dein. Achhe photos mein clear background, multiple angles, zoom-in details, aur lifestyle shots hote hain jo product ki quality aur use-case ko highlight karain.

**Example:**
Ek mobile cover bechne wale seller ne pehle sirf 1 photo lagayi thi (front view). Usne bad mein 5 photos add ki:
1. Front view (main photo)
2. Back view (pattern/design)
3. Side view (thickness)
4. Zoom-in (material quality)
5. Lifestyle shot (cover phone ke saath bag mein)
Result: CTR (Click-Through Rate) 30% badh gaya.

**Practice:**
Apne Daraz account mein jayein, kisi bhi product ka listing edit mode mein kholain. Ab "Photos" section mein jayein aur check karain:
- Kya sabhi important angles cover hain?
- Kya main photo sabse attractive hai?
- Kya koi zoom-in ya lifestyle shot add kar sakte hain?

**Mini Project:**
Ek naya product (ya existing product) select karain aur uske liye 5-6 high-quality photos banayein ya lein. In photos ko Daraz listing mein upload karain aur compare karain ki pehle aur baad mein CTR ya views mein kya difference aaya.

**Answer Key:**
clear background, multiple angles, zoom-in, lifestyle shot
