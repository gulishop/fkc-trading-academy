# Day 6 — **Daraz Par Orders Aur Delivery Ko Perfectly Manage Karna**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke Daraz par orders kaise handle karein, delivery options (Daraz Express vs Dropship) ka istemal kaise karein, aur customer ko timely delivery kaise ensure karein. Har order ka status track karna aur delivery issues ko solve karna bhi seekhenge.

**Example:**
1. **Order Confirmation:** Jab koi customer order kare, toh "Orders" section mein jake order ko "Ship" par click karke confirm karein.
2. **Delivery Option Choose:** Agar product Daraz Express se deliver karna hai, toh "Daraz Express" select karein. Agar Dropship karna hai, toh supplier se confirm karke address provide karein.
3. **Tracking:** Order ke status (e.g., "Processing," "Shipped," "Delivered") ko regularly check karte rahein.

**Practice:**
Apne Daraz Seller Center mein jake ek test order create karein aur usko "Ship" karne ka process follow karein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek simple Excel sheet banaen jismein aap apne 5 orders ka status track karein (Order ID, Customer Name, Delivery Method, Status, Tracking Number).

**Answer Key:**
Order confirm, Daraz Express/Dropship select, tracking check, status update
