# Day 18 — **Daraz Par "Flash Sales" Aur Limited-Time Offers Se Traffic Kaise Badhayein**

**Concept:**
Flash sales aur limited-time offers Daraz par traffic aur sales boost karne ka powerful tareeka hain. Ismein short-duration discounts, bundles, ya exclusive deals offer kiye jate hain jo customers ko urgency feel karate hain. Sahi timing aur promotion se yeh strategy instant results deti hai.

**Example:**
Ek seller ne apne "Wireless Earbuds" ka 24-hour flash sale kiya:
1. Daraz Vouchers section mein "20% OFF" ka limited-time coupon banaya.
2. Daraz Ads mein "Flash Sale" ka banner chalaaya.
3. WhatsApp groups aur social media par 12-hour countdown shuru kiya.
Result: 3x sales increase aur 50% extra traffic.

**Practice:**
Apne 3 best-selling products ke liye ek 12-hour flash sale plan banayein:
- Discount % ya free shipping offer decide karein.
- Daraz Vouchers section mein coupon create karein (minimum 30% discount).
- Daraz Ads mein "Limited Time Offer" ka campaign chalaayein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek WhatsApp group ya Facebook page post draft karein jo flash sale promote kare. Include:
- Product image + discount details.
- Countdown timer (e.g., "Only 6 hours left!").
- Clear CTA (e.g., "Order now before stock ends!").

**Answer Key:**
discount coupon, urgency, 12-hour window, Daraz Ads
