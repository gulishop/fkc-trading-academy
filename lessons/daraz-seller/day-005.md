# Day 5 — Daraz Ads (Sponsored Discovery) Ko Samajhna Aur Use Karna

**Concept:**
Daraz Ads (Sponsored Discovery) ek paid tool hai jo sellers ko apne products ko Daraz search aur category pages par top par dikhane mein help karta hai. Isse organic reach badhati hai, clicks milte hain, aur sales increase hoti hain. Ye CPC (Cost Per Click) model par chalta hai—har click par aapka budget kharch hota hai.

**Example:**
Maana aap "Wireless Earbuds" bech rahe hain. Daraz Ads mein campaign banate hain, budget set karte hain (e.g. Rs. 5000), aur keywords jaise "cheap wireless earbuds Pakistan" ya "best earbuds under 5000" select karte hain. Jab koi user in keywords par search karega, aapka product sponsored listing mein upar show hoga.

**Practice:**
Abhi apne Daraz Seller Center mein jao, "Ads" section mein click karo, aur ek naya campaign create karne ki koshish karo. Budget Rs. 1000 set karo aur ek relevant keyword choose karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek 3-day Daraz Ads campaign plan banao:
1. Apne top 3 products select karo jin par ads lagani hai.
2. Har product ke liye 2-3 keywords likho.
3. Har product ke liye Rs. 500 ka daily budget decide karo.

**Answer Key:**
Campaign setup, keyword selection, budget Rs. 500/day, CPC model
