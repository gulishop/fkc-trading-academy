# Day 16 — **Daraz Par Orders Aur Delivery Ko 100% Smooth Banane Ka Complete Guide**

**Concept:**
Daraz par orders aur delivery ko efficiently manage karna ek successful seller ki pehchaan hai. Is lesson mein hum Daraz Express (Daraz ki own delivery service) aur Dropshipping model ko step-by-step samjhenge. Orders ko track karna, delivery time ko optimize karna, aur customer ko timely updates dena sales badhane ka raaz hai.

**Example:**
1. **Order Confirmation:** Jab koi customer order kare, toh "Order Confirmed" status par click karke customer ko email/SMS notification bhej do.
2. **Daraz Express:** Agar product Daraz Express se deliver karna hai, toh "Ready to Ship" par click karke Daraz ko product bhej do. Daraz team delivery handle karegi.
3. **Dropshipping:** Agar Dropshipping model use kar rahe ho, toh supplier ko order forward karke customer ko tracking number share kar do.

**Practice:**
Apne Daraz Seller Center mein jake "Orders" section mein jayein. Koi 1 pending order select karke "Ready to Ship" par click karen. Agar Daraz Express use kar rahe hain, toh "Daraz Express" option choose karen.

**Mini Project:**
Ek simple Excel sheet banayein jismein aap apne 5 recent orders ka status (Confirmed, Ready to Ship, Shipped, Delivered) track karen. Har order ke liye estimated delivery date bhi likhein.

**Answer Key:**
order confirmed, ready to ship, daraz express, dropshipping tracking
