# Day 10 — **Daraz Par AI Tools Se Product Research Kaise Tez Karein**

**Concept:**
AI tools se product research karna ab seller ke liye zaroori ho gaya hai. AI se aap competitors ke best-selling products, pricing trends, aur customer reviews analyze kar sakte hain—bas 2-3 clicks mein! Tools jaise **Jungle Scout, Helium 10, oRDS, ya Daraz ke hi AI-powered search filters** use karke aap time bacha sakte hain aur accurate data pa sakte hain.

**Example:**
1. **Jungle Scout** par "Pakistan" select karein, category choose karein (e.g., "Home & Kitchen").
2. "Top Products" section mein AI-generated best sellers dekhhein—jismein sales volume, rating, aur pricing trends hon.
3. **Daraz ke search bar** mein AI filter use karein: "Best Selling" + "Low Competition" select karein, phir "Price: Low to High" sort karein.

**Practice:**
Aaj ke liye, **ek category choose karke** (e.g., "Men's Fashion" ya "Baby Products") **Daraz par AI filters use karke top 3 trending products** nikalain. Har product ka **current price, rating, aur estimated sales** note karein.

**Mini Project:**
**"AI-Powered Product Research Report"** banayein:
- 1 page ka simple report likhein jismein:
  1. 3 trending products ke naam + links.
  2. Har product ka **AI se nikalta hua "opportunity score"** (e.g., "High demand, low competition").
  3. Apna **next listing ka plan** (kon sa product add karna hai aur kyun).

**Answer Key:**
trending products list, AI filters used, opportunity score, next listing plan
