# Day 7 — **Daraz Par Negative Reviews Ko Professional Tarike Se Handle Karna**

**Concept:**
Negative reviews Daraz par seller ke reputation par bura asar dalte hain. Inko ignore karne ki bajaye, unko **professional response** dena chahiye taake customer trust bana rahe. Sabse pehle review ke **root cause** samajhein (late delivery, wrong product, quality issue) aur **empathy** ke saath jawab dein. Daraz ke **Review Management System** se bhi inko track kar sakte hain.

**Example:**
Ek customer ne "Product damaged" ka review diya hai. Aapka response:
*"Assalamu Alaikum! Hum aapki pareshani ke liye maazrat chahte hain. Hum aapko **immediately replacement** bhej rahe hain. Kripya apna order ID ya address share karen. Shukriya!"*
Iske baad Daraz ko **return request** accept karne ka message bhi bhej dein.

**Practice:**
Apne Daraz Seller Center mein jayein → **Reviews** section mein ja kar **1 negative review** ko dhondhein. Uske liye **3-line ka professional response** likhein (Hindi/Urdu mein).

**Mini Project:**
Ek **template** taiyar karen jo sabhi negative reviews ke liye use ho sakta ho. Ismein:
1. **Empathy** (maazrat)
2. **Solution** (replace/repair/refund)
3. **Follow-up** (contact details)

**Answer Key:**
maazrat, replacement, Daraz support, follow-up
