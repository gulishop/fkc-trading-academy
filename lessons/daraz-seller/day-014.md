# Day 14 — **Daraz Par High-Converting Product Videos Banane Ka Complete Guide**

**Concept:**
Product videos Daraz par conversion rate 2-3x badha dete hain. Aaj seekhein ke kaise 15-30 second ke engaging videos banayein jo customers ko "Add to Cart" par click karne par majboor kar dein. Focus rahein: clear lighting, close-up shots, aur product ke unique features ko highlight karna.

**Example:**
Ek "Bluetooth Earbuds" ka video banane ka tarika:
1. 5 sec: Earbuds ko box se nikalte hue (unboxing style).
2. 10 sec: Close-up shot jisme buttons aur charging case ka zoom-in ho.
3. 5 sec: Earbuds ko kisi ke ears par lagate hue (real usage show karo).
4. 5 sec: "Super bass, 20hr battery" jaise key features text ke saath.

**Practice:**
Apne ek existing product ka video banane ke liye:
- Mobile se 10-15 second ka clip record karo.
- Editing app (CapCut/InShot) se text add karo aur background music lagao.
- Video ko Daraz listing ke "Media" section mein upload karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek "Daraz Par Top 5 Products Ke Video Examples" naam ka folder banaen aur har product ke video ka link save karo. Next class mein discuss karenge ke unmein kya common hai.

**Answer Key:**
unboxing, close-up, usage demo, text overlay
