# Day 12 — List ke sath Loop

- **Concept:** List ki har value pe loop chalana
- **Example:**
  ```python
  expenses = [500, 200, 1000, 150]
  for e in expenses:
      print("Kharcha:", e)
  ```
- **Practice:** List ke sab numbers ka total nikalwayen (`sum()`)
