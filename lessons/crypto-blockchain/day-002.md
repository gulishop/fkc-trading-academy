# Day 2 — **Bitcoin vs Ethereum: Digital Gold vs Programmable Money**

**Concept:**
Bitcoin (BTC) aur Ethereum (ETH) crypto ki do sabse famous currencies hain. Bitcoin ko "digital gold" kaha jata hai—yeh ek decentralized currency hai jo transactions record karta hai. Ethereum, on the other hand, ek "programmable blockchain" hai jo smart contracts (self-executing agreements) aur decentralized apps (DApps) ko support karta hai. Bitcoin limited supply (21M coins) ke saath inflation-resistant hai, jabki Ethereum flexible hai aur developers ko innovative projects build karne deta hai.

**Example:**
Imagine Bitcoin ek **gold coin** hai—limited supply, stable value, aur sirf transactions record karta hai. Ethereum ek **digital computer** hai—jisme developers "if-this-then-that" rules (smart contracts) likh sakte hain, jaise ek vending machine jo automatically payment lekar product deta hai.
