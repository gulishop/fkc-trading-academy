# Day 9 — **Crypto Portfolio Diversification: Apne Investments Ko Safe Aur Balanced Banana**

**Concept:**
Crypto market mein volatility bohat zyada hai—ek coin 90% gir sakta hai, doosra 1000% chadh sakta hai. Isliye apne saare eggs ek basket mein na rakhein. Diversification ka matlab hai apne investments ko alag-alag assets (Bitcoin, Ethereum, altcoins, stablecoins, NFTs, etc.) aur alag-alag sectors (DeFi, Gaming, AI, etc.) mein baantna. Isse risk kam hota hai aur long-term growth ke chances badh jaate hain.

**Example:**
Maana aapke paas 10,000 USD hai. Aap isko yeh tarah diversify kar sakte hain:
- 50% (5,000 USD) → Bitcoin + Ethereum (70-30 split)
- 20% (2,000 USD) → Top 5-10 altcoins (e.g., Solana, Cardano, Polkadot)
- 15% (1,500 USD) → Stablecoins (USDT, USDC) — emergency fund ke liye
- 10% (1,000 USD) → DeFi yield farming ya staking
- 5% (500 USD) → NFTs ya early-stage projects (high risk, high reward)

**Practice:**
Apne current crypto holdings (ya agar nahi hain to hypothetical) ko 3 categories mein classify karo:
1. **Low Risk:** Bitcoin, Ethereum, stablecoins
2. **Medium Risk:** Major altcoins (e.g., Solana, Avalanche)
3. **High Risk:** Small-cap coins, meme coins, NFTs
Ab socho ke tumhara portfolio kitna balanced hai. Agar koi category 60% se zyada hai, toh diversification ki zaroorat hai.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **simple spreadsheet** banayein jisme:
- Har asset ka naam, allocated amount, aur percentage likhein.
- Market crash ke baad (e.g., -30% overall) har asset ka hypothetical loss calculate karo.
- Diversification ke baad total loss kitna hoga? (Hint: Har asset ka weightage multiply karo loss ke saath)

**Answer Key:**
Bitcoin/Ethereum 50%, Altcoins 20%, Stablecoins 15%, DeFi 10%, NFTs 5%
