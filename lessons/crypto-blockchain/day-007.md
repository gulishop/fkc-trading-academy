# Day 7 — **DeFi: Blockchain Ka Naya Duniya — Bina Bank Ke Banking!**

**Concept:**
DeFi (Decentralized Finance) blockchain ka use karke traditional banking services (jaise loans, interest, trading) ko peer-to-peer (P2P) tareeqe se offer karta hai—bina kisi bank, broker ya middleman ke. Smart contracts (Day 4) in services ko automate karte hain, aur users apne funds par full control rakhte hain. Isse global access, low fees, aur censorship resistance milti hai.

**Example:**
Imagine aapko Rs. 10,000 ka loan chahiye bina bank ke. DeFi platform jaise **Aave** ya **Compound** par:
1. Apna crypto (e.g., ETH) as collateral deposit karo.
2. Platform automatically interest rate batayega (e.g., 5% APR).
3. Loan approve hoga—paisa directly aapke wallet mein aa jayega.
4. Loan chukta karne ke baad collateral return ho jayega.

**Practice:**
Aaj hi **DeFiLlama** ya **DefiPulse** par jayein aur top 3 DeFi protocols (jaise Aave, Uniswap, MakerDAO) ke TVL (Total Value Locked) check karein. Koi 1 protocol ka 1-line summary likhein (e.g., "Aave: Lending/borrowing ke liye smart contracts use karta hai").
