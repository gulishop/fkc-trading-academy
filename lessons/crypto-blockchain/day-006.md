# Day 6 — **Phishing aur Social Engineering: Crypto Ka Sabse Bada Khauf**

**Concept:**
Phishing aur social engineering crypto mein sabse common scams hain. Hackers log fake emails, messages, ya websites banate hain jo real crypto platforms jaise wallets, exchanges, ya support teams ke jaise dikhte hain. Unka maqsad tumhare private keys, seed phrases, ya login details churaana hota hai.

**Example:**
Ek scammer tumhare Telegram/Discord par "Binance Support Team" se message karta hai: *"Aapka account hack ho gaya hai! Is link par click karein aur apna password reset karein."* Link par click karne par ek fake Binance login page khulta hai jo real page jaisa hi dikhta hai. Agar tumne apna password daal diya, to scammer ne tumhara account hack kar liya.

**Practice:**
1. Apne email ya social media par koi suspicious message ya link aaya hai? Agar haan, usko report karein (phishing@binance.com ya platform ki official support).
2. Kisi bhi crypto platform par login karne se pehle URL check karein: `https://` hona chahiye aur spelling sahi honi chahiye (e.g., `binance.com` not `binancee.com`).

**Mini Project:**
Ek fake phishing email ya message create karein (sirf learning ke liye) jo real scam ki tarah dikhe lekin warning message ho. Apne classmates ko bhejein aur unse feedback lo ke kya unko scam samajh aaya ya nahi.

**Answer Key:**
fake link, URL check, report scam, seed phrase share na kare
