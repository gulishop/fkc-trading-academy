# Day 8 — **Staking vs Yield Farming: Passive Income Ka Secret**

**Concept:**
Staking aur yield farming crypto mein passive income kamane ke do popular tareeqe hain. Staking mein aap apne coins ko lock karke rewards hasil karte hain (jaise Ethereum 2.0 mein), jabke yield farming mein aap DeFi protocols par liquidity provide karke interest/commission earn karte hain. Dono mein risk bhi hai—impermanent loss, smart contract vulnerabilities, ya market volatility.

**Example:**
1. **Staking:** Agar aapke paas 10 ETH hain, to unhe Binance ya Kraken par stake karke ~4-6% APY earn kar sakte hain.
2. **Yield Farming:** Agar aap Uniswap par ETH-USDC liquidity provide karte hain, to trading fees + extra tokens (LP rewards) ke roop mein ~10-20% APY mil sakta hai.

**Practice:**
Apne favorite exchange (Binance/Kraken) ya DeFi platform (PancakeSwap/Aave) par check karein ke staking ya yield farming ke liye kaunse coins available hain. Apne portfolio ka 5-10% ismein allocate karne ka sochiye.

**Mini Project:**
Ek fake wallet (jaise MetaMask) banayein aur testnet (Goerli ya Sepolia) par 0.1 ETH "stake" karne ka try karein. Agar successful ho, to real staking ke liye research karein.

**Answer Key:**
Staking: lock coins, earn rewards, low risk; Yield farming: provide liquidity, earn fees, high risk
