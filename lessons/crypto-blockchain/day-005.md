# Day 5 — **DYOR: Apne Investments Ka Research Kaise Kare**

**Concept:**
DYOR (Do Your Own Research) crypto investing ka sabse zaroori step hai. Kisi bhi coin ya project mein invest karne se pehle uske whitepaper, team, use case, aur market trends ko analyze karna zaroori hai. Baseless hype ya FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out) se bachne ke liye DYOR karna must hai.

**Example:**
Agar koi "XYZ Token" ka hype sun raha hai, to pehle check kare:
1. **Whitepaper** (project ka blueprint) — kya yeh real problem solve kar raha hai?
2. **Team** (LinkedIn/GitHub) — kya inke background credible hain?
3. **Tokenomics** (supply, distribution) — kya supply zyada toh nahi hai?
4. **Community** (Twitter, Discord) — kya log actively discuss kar rahe hain?

**Practice:**
Aaj ek naya crypto project (jaise "SOL" ya "ADA") lein aur 10 minutes mein uske whitepaper ya official website se 3 key points nikalein jo aapko investing ke liye important lagin.

**Mini Project:**
Ek simple spreadsheet banaein jismein aap 3 crypto projects (ek Bitcoin, ek Ethereum, aur ek altcoin) ko compare karein — unke market cap, circulating supply, aur recent price movement ke hisab se.

**Answer Key:**
whitepaper analysis, team credibility, tokenomics, community sentiment
