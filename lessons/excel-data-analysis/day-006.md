# Day 6 — **PivotTables: Data ko Powerful Insights mein Badalna**

**Concept:**
PivotTables Excel ka sabse powerful tool hai jo bade data ko chand clicks mein summarize aur analyze kar deta hai. Yeh automatically categories, sub-totals, aur comparisons generate karta hai bina formulas likhe. Har business ya freelance project mein iski zaroorat hoti hai—chahe sales report ho ya expense tracking.

**Example:**
1. Ek Excel sheet mein `Product`, `Region`, `Sales` aur `Month` columns hain.
2. Insert > PivotTable select karo. Data range choose karo.
3. PivotTable Fields mein:
   - Rows mein `Region` aur `Product` drag karo.
   - Values mein `Sales` add karo (yeh sum by default show karega).
4. Ab dekho: Har region aur product ka total sales automatically calculate ho gaya!

**Practice:**
Apni Excel sheet mein ek `Employee` (Name, Department, Salary, Joining Date) dataset banao. Uspar PivotTable banake:
- Har department ka average salary nikalna hai.
- Joining Date ke hisab se employees count karna hai.

**Mini Project:**
Ek fake e-commerce ka dataset banao (Order ID, Customer, Product, Quantity, Price, Order Date). Uspar PivotTable banake:
- Har customer ka total spending nikalna.
- Har month ki total sales ka bar chart banaana.
- Top 3 best-selling products identify karna.

**Answer Key:**
Department-wise average salary, Joining Date by year, Customer total spending, Month-wise sales, Top 3 products
