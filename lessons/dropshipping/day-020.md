# Day 20 — **Store Ko Scale Karne Ke Liye: TikTok Ads Se Traffic Kaise Badhayein (2024 Strategy)**

**Concept:**
TikTok ads dropshipping ke liye 2024 mein sabse powerful traffic source hai—kyun ke iska algorithm aapki videos ko unlimited audience tak phailata hai. Lekin bas ads chala dene se kaam nahi hoga; aapko "creative testing" aur "lookalike audiences" ka istemal karna hoga taake CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) kam ho aur ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) badhe.

**Example:**
Ek student ne TikTok ads chalayi thi 1000$ budget par, lekin ads fail ho gayi. Phir usne yeh strategy try ki:
1. TikTok Creative Center se trending sounds aur hashtags copy kiye.
2. 3-5 second ke videos banaye (product ka close-up + text overlay "Free Shipping").
3. Lookalike audience banayi (previous buyers ya website visitors se).
4. Budget ko $5/day per ad set par set kiya taake testing fast ho.
Result: ROAS 3.5x ho gaya aur CAC $4 se $1.5 tak gir gaya.

**Practice:**
Aaj hi TikTok Business account mein jao, apne best-selling product ka 3-5 second ka video banayein (mobile se bhi ho sakta hai), aur $5/day budget par 3-4 ads test karein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek TikTok ad campaign setup karo jismein:
- 1 trending sound use ho
- 1 text overlay ho ("Discount Ends Soon!")
- 1 lookalike audience target ho (agar available ho)
- Budget $10 ho (2-3 days ke liye)

**Answer Key:**
creative testing, lookalike audience, trending sound, $1.5 CAC
