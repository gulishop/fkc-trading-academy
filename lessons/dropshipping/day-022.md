# Day 22 — **Winning Product Ki Pehchaan: AI Tools Se Fast & Accurate Dhundna**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum AI-powered tools (free/paid) use karke winning products dhundenge jo high demand + low competition + high profit margin wale hon. Manual research ke bajaye AI se time bachate hue accurate results nikalna seekhenge. Tools jaise **Dropship Spy, EcomHunt, Thieve.co, TikTok Creative Center** aur **Google Trends** ka istemal karke data-driven decisions lenge.

**Example:**
1. **Dropship Spy** par jayein → "Trending Products" section mein filter lagayein: "Last 7 days", "Price $20-$50", "Engagement >10K likes".
2. **TikTok Creative Center** mein "Top Products" section check karen → viral products ki list dekhein.
3. **Google Trends** par product name search karen → interest over time graph dekh kar seasonal trends identify karen.

**Practice:**
Apne niche (e.g., fitness, kitchen, gadgets) ke liye **Dropship Spy ya EcomHunt** par jayein, top 3 trending products ko shortlist karen, aur har product ki **Facebook/TikTok ad engagement** check karen (likes, comments, shares).

**Mini Project:**
1. **3 winning product ideas** nikal kar ek Google Sheet mein save karen.
2. Har product ke liye **supplier (AliExpress/1688)** se price, shipping time, aur reviews check karen.
3. **Profit margin** calculate karen (Product Price - Supplier Price - Ads Cost - Other Fees).

**Answer Key:**
Dropship Spy, TikTok Creative Center, Google Trends, profit margin calculation
