# Day 2 — Winning Product Ki Pehchaan: 5 Key Metrics Se Filter Karna

**Concept:**
Winning product dhoondna dropshipping ka sabse important step hai. Aise product chunne hain jo:
1) **High demand** ho (trending ya problem solve karne wala),
2) **Low competition** ho (niche mein unique value ho),
3) **High perceived value** ho (logo ko lagta ho yeh zaroori hai),
4) **Easy to ship** ho (lightweight, no fragile items),
5) **Emotional trigger** ho (happy, angry, curious ya "must-have" feeling).

**Example:**
Ek product search karte waqt:
- **Trending niche:** "Pet lovers" → "Automatic pet feeder" (demand high, competition medium).
- **Problem solve:** "Back pain" → "Lumbar support pillow" (emotional trigger + value).
- **Avoid:** "Custom jewelry" (high competition, long shipping).

**Practice:**
Apne niche ke 3-5 trending products ko AliExpress par search karo. Har product ke liye yeh check karo:
✅ Price range (₨500–₨3000 ideal hai)
✅ Orders count (1000+ orders = demand proof)
✅ Reviews (4+ stars, 50+ reviews = trust)
✅ Shipping time (AliExpress Standard ya ePacket)
