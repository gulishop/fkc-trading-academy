# Day 12 — **Product Research Ka Advanced Formula: AI Tools Se Winning Products Dhoondna**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum AI-powered tools (jaise Dropship Spy, EcomHunt, AliExpress Dropshipping Center) ka istemal karke *data-driven* product research karna seekhenge. Basics se aage badh kar, hum *trending niches*, *competitor gaps*, aur *high-retention products* pehchannen ge. AI se real-time demand, competition level, aur profit margins ka analysis karna seekhenge.

**Example:**
1. **AliExpress Dropshipping Center** mein jao → "Trending Products" section check karo.
2. **Dropship Spy** par "Winning Products" filter lagao aur *engagement rate* (likes/comments) dekho.
3. **EcomHunt** par "New Arrivals" mein se *high-converting* products ko shortlist karo jo *10-30% margin* de sakte hain.

**Practice:**
Apne niche (e.g., fitness, kitchen gadgets, pet supplies) ke liye **3 products** AI tools se dhundho jo:
- TikTok/Instagram par *viral* ho rahe hain (high engagement).
- AliExpress par *low competition* aur *high rating* (4.5+ stars) rakhte hain.
- **$15-$30** ke price range mein hain (COD-friendly).
