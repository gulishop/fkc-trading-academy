# Day 16 — **Email Marketing Se Repeat Customers Kaise Banayein (Dropshipping Ka Silent Killer)**

**Concept:**
Email marketing dropshipping ka sabse underrated lekin sabse powerful tool hai. 70% customers doosri baar khareedte nahi, lekin agar aap unko 3-5 emails se follow karte hain, toh 20-30% repeat customers ban sakte hain. Aaj hum seekhenge ki kaise ek simple email sequence banayein jo trust banaye aur sales badhaye.

**Example:**
Ek customer ne aapka product khareeda (order #12345). Aapko yeh emails bhehni hain:
1. **Order Confirmation** (1 hour baad) – "Order #12345 confirmed! Tracking link: [link]"
2. **Shipping Update** (2 days baad) – "Your order is shipped! Expected delivery: [date]"
3. **Review Request** (5 days baad) – "How was your product? Leave a review & get 10% off next order!"
4. **Upsell Email** (10 days baad) – "Same product ka 2nd pack 15% off! [link]"

**Practice:**
Apne Shopify store ke "Customers" section mein jayein, koi 5 recent customers select karen, aur unke liye ek simple email draft karen (subject line + 1-2 lines ka body).

**Mini Project:**
Apne store ke liye ek **3-email sequence** banayein (Klaviyo ya Mailchimp free plan use karen) aur test karne ke liye 1-2 fake emails bhejen.

**Answer Key:**
order confirmation, shipping update, review request, upsell
