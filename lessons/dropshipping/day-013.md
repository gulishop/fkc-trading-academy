# Day 13 — **AliExpress vs Local Suppliers: Kaun Sa Choose Karein?**

**Concept:**
AliExpress aur local suppliers ke beech ka farq samajhna zaroori hai. AliExpress global suppliers hai, cheap rates lekin long shipping (15-45 days), jabke local suppliers fast delivery (3-7 days) offer karte hain lekin zyada cost. Pakistan mein local suppliers ka selection depend karta hai product type, budget aur delivery speed par.

**Example:**
1. **AliExpress:** Agar aap "Wireless Earbuds" bech rahe hain, to AliExpress se Rs. 1500 mein mil jayega, lekin shipping 25 days lagegi.
2. **Local Supplier:** Lahore mein ek supplier Rs. 2200 mein same earbuds deta hai, lekin 5 days mein deliver kar dega.

**Practice:**
Apne **Day 12** ke winning product ke liye **2 suppliers** dhundhein:
1. **AliExpress** par search karke 3-5 options note karein.
2. **Local suppliers** ke liye Google par "Lahore electronics suppliers" ya "Karachi dropshipping suppliers" search karein.
