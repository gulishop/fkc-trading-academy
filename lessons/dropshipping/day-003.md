# Day 3 — **Local vs AliExpress Suppliers: Kaunsa Choose Karein?**

**Concept:**
Dropshipping mein supplier hi aapka business ka backbone hai. AliExpress global suppliers deta hai lekin shipping time zyada hai (15-45 days), jabki local suppliers (Pakistan se) 3-7 days mein delivery kar sakte hain lekin prices thode zyada hoti hain. Har ek ka apna faida-nuksan hai—samajh kar choose karna zaroori hai.

**Example:**
Agar aap *wireless earbuds* bech rahe hain:
- **AliExpress:** Supplier China se ₹800 mein lekar ₹1500 mein bech sakte hain, lekin customer ko 20-30 days wait karna hoga.
- **Local Supplier:** Karachi se ₹1200 mein lekar ₹2000 mein bech sakte hain, delivery 3-5 days mein ho jayegi.

**Practice:**
Apne 1-2 winning products ke liye **AliExpress** aur **local suppliers** (Facebook Groups, WhatsApp, OLX) par search karke prices, shipping time, aur reviews compare karo. Ek Excel sheet bana kar note kar lo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **supplier comparison table** banao jismein:
- Product name
- AliExpress vs Local price
- Shipping time
- Return policy
- Customer reviews (rating)

**Answer Key:**
AliExpress: cheap, slow, global; Local: fast, expensive, local trust
