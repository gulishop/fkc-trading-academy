# Day 4 — **Store Setup: Shopify vs WhatsApp Store (Pros & Cons)**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum store setup ke do popular tareeqon par focus karenge: **Shopify** (full-fledged eCommerce) aur **WhatsApp Store** (low-cost, local-focused). Shopify offers scalability, branding, aur ads ke liye flexibility, jabki WhatsApp store chhote budget walon ke liye behtareen hai—khud ka social proof bana sakta hai aur COD orders manage karne mein aasan hai.

**Example:**
1. **Shopify Store Setup (3 Steps):**
   - Go to [Shopify.com](https://www.shopify.com), 3-day free trial start karein.
   - "Online Store" > "Themes" > Free theme (e.g., Dawn) install karein.
   - Product add karein > "Add product" > Title, description, images upload karein > Price set karein > Save.

2. **WhatsApp Store Setup (2 Steps):**
   - Ek WhatsApp Business account banayein (name: "BrandName Store").
   - Catalog feature enable karein: *Settings > Business Tools > Catalog > Add Products*.

**Practice:**
Apne selected product ka **Shopify aur WhatsApp store** donon ke liye setup karein. Product title, description, aur 3 images (1 main + 2 extra) add karein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **WhatsApp Business Catalog** banayein jismein 5 products hon—har product ka naam, price, aur 1 image ho. Catalog link ko apne social media par share karein.

**Answer Key:**
Shopify: theme install, product add, save; WhatsApp: catalog enable, 5 products add
