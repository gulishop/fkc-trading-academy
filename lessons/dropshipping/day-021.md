# Day 21 — **Dropshipping Ka Hidden Profit Formula: Order Value Ko Double Kaise Karen**

**Concept:**
Dropshipping mein profit sirf product margin se nahi, balki **Average Order Value (AOV)** badha kar bhi hota hai. AOV wo average amount hai jo har customer har order par kharch karta hai. Isko double karne ke liye **upselling, cross-selling, aur bundle offers** ka istemal karte hain. Yeh strategy repeat customers banane aur CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) kam karne mein bhi madadgar hai.

**Example:**
Ek customer ne $20 ka product order kiya. Ab usko **upsell** karke $10 ka related product offer karo (e.g., phone case agar wo phone cover khareed raha hai). Agar wo accept karta hai, to AOV $30 ho gaya. Aur agar **bundle offer** bhi add kar do (e.g., "Buy 2, Get 10% Off"), to AOV aur bhi badh sakta hai.

**Practice:**
Apne store ke **top 3 best-selling products** select karo. Har product ke liye **1 upsell offer** aur **1 bundle offer** banayo (e.g., "Add $5 for Fast Shipping" ya "Buy 2, Get Free Gift"). Offer ko product page par clearly display karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **AOV Increase Challenge** shuru karo: Apne store ke 10 customers ko upsell/cross-sell karke dekhna hai. Har successful upsell ke liye $10 bonus reward rakh sakte ho (e.g., free shipping ya discount coupon).

**Answer Key:**
upsell offer, bundle discount, AOV increase, repeat purchase
