# Day 7 — **Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders: Pakistan Mein Risks Se Kaise Bachain?**

**Concept:**
COD Pakistan mein dropshipping ka common option hai, lekin fraud orders aur high return rates ka khauf hota hai. Aaj seekhein ke COD orders ko kaise verify karein, trusted areas se orders ko prioritize karein, aur fraud se kaise bachein. Local suppliers ya cash pickup options bhi explore karein.

**Example:**
1. **Order Verification:** Agar koi customer Lahore se order karta hai lekin supplier Karachi se hai, toh COD reject kar dein.
2. **Cash Pickup Option:** Local suppliers ke saath baat karein ke wo cash pickup offer kar sakte hain (fraud risk kam ho jata hai).

**Practice:**
Apne store ke 3 recent COD orders ko check karo:
- Customer ka city supplier ke city se match karta hai?
- Order amount Rs. 5,000+ hai? (High-value orders fraud ke liye attractive hote hain)

**Mini Project:**
Ek simple COD policy draft karo:
1. "COD sirf Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad ke liye available hai."
2. "Rs. 3,000+ orders par hi COD."

**Answer Key:**
City match, high-value fraud, local pickup, policy draft
