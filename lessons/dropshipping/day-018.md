# Day 18 — **Trust Badhaane Ke 5 Psychological Tricks (Jo Har Customer Ko Convert Kar Denge)**

**Concept:**
Customer support sirf "response" nahi hai—yeh ek **trust-building machine** hai. 90% stores isko ignore karte hain aur sirf "we'll get back to you" bolte hain. Aaj seekhoge ki kaise **psychological triggers** (social proof, urgency, authority) use karke customer ka trust instantly badhaana hai—chahe wo complaint ho ya simple query.

**Example:**
Ek customer ne product ke baare mein poocha: *"Isme yeh feature hai?"* Instead of *"Yes"* bolne ke, aise respond karo:
*"Bilkul! Ye product [competitor ka naam] se bhi zyada advanced hai kyunki isme [specific feature] hai jo [customer ka pain point] solve karta hai. 80% customers isko sabse zyada pasand karte hain—dekhein aapko kaise pasand aata hai!"*
**Result:** Customer ko lagta hai aap **expert** hain aur product **proven** hai.

**Practice:**
Apne store ke **FAQ section** mein 3 aise sentences add karo jo **trust badhaayein** (jaise: *"9/10 customers isko 3 din mein receive karte hain"*, *"Our team has 5+ years of dropshipping experience"*).

**Mini Project:**
Apne store ke **About Us page** ko rewrite karo—bas 3 lines mein:
1. **Social proof** (e.g., *"10,000+ happy customers in Pakistan"*),
2. **Authority** (e.g., *"Trusted by 50+ influencers"*),
3. **Urgency** (e.g., *"Only 50 units left at this price!"*).

**Answer Key:** social proof, authority, urgency, FOMO
