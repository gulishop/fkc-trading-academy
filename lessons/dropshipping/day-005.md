# Day 5 — **High-Converting Product Page Ka Secret: Psychology + Design Hacks**

**Concept:**
Ek product page sirf "achha" design nahi hota—yeh customer ki *emotions* aur *decision-making* ko trigger karta hai. Aaj seekhoge ki kaise **scarcity, social proof, aur frictionless checkout** use karke conversion rates 2-3x badha sakte hain. Har element (headline, images, CTA, trust badges) customer ke *hesitation* ko dur karne ke liye design kiya jana chahiye.

**Example:**
Ek *weight loss supplement* ka product page:
1. **Headline:** "30 Days Mein 10 KG Kam Karne Ka *Secret* (Limited Stock: 47 Left!)"
2. **Images:** Before/After + *real customer* ki video review (social proof).
3. **CTA:** "Abhi Order Karein – 50% Off + Free Shipping (24 Hours Mein)"
4. **Trust Badges:** "100% Natural Ingredients", "Money-Back Guarantee", "4.9/5 Star Reviews (12,345 Customers)"

**Practice:**
Apne current store (ya demo product) ka product page kholo. **3 elements** hatado jo customer ko *doubt* mein dalte hain (e.g., vague headline, missing trust badges, unclear shipping time).

**Mini Project:**
Ek **A/B test plan** banao:
- **Version A:** Simple product page (basics).
- **Version B:** "Scarcity + Social Proof" version (jaise example mein).
- **Goal:** 7 days mein traffic split karke dekhna ki kaunsa version zyada conversion deta hai.

**Answer Key:**
scarcity, social proof, trust badges, frictionless checkout
