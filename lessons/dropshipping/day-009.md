# Day 9 — **Automated Email Sequences: Apne Customers Ko Repeat Khareedne Par Majboor Karne Ka Formula**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke kaise automated email sequences (welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase) se apne customers ko dobara khareedne par majboor kar sakte hain. Har email ka specific purpose hota hai—trust build karna, urgency create karna, ya discount offer karna. Yeh system bina manual follow-up ke 20-30% extra sales generate kar sakta hai.

**Example:**
Ek customer ne aapka product cart mein add kiya lekin payment complete nahi kiya. Ab aapko 3 automated emails bhejne hain:
1. **1st Email (1 hour baad):** "Aapka cart khali ho gaya!" + 10% discount code.
2. **2nd Email (24 hours baad):** "Aapka favorite product wait kar raha hai!" + scarcity ("Only 3 left!").
3. **3rd Email (48 hours baad):** "Last chance! 24 hours mein discount khatam ho jayega."

**Practice:**
Apne Shopify store mein **Klaviyo** ya **Mailchimp** setup karein aur ek abandoned cart email sequence create karein (3 emails, 10% discount offer).

**Mini Project:**
Ek **post-purchase email sequence** design karein jismein:
- Thank you email (order confirmation)
- Review request (5 days baad)
- Upsell offer (10 days baad, related product)

**Answer Key:**
welcome email, abandoned cart reminder, scarcity, post-purchase upsell
