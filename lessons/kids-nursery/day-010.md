# Day 10 — "Pencil Pakadna: Seedhi Lakeerein Banayein!"

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhein ge ke pencil ko theek se kaise pakadna hai taake seedhi aur sundar lakeerein banein! Yeh bhi yaad rakhna zaroori hai ke tumhari body tumhari hi hai—agar koi tumhe zor se pakadne ki koshish kare ya ajeeb feel karaye, toh foran ammi/abbu ko batana hai.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Areeba ne pencil ko theek se pakadna seekha. Usne ek sundar sundar sunhera ghar banaya. Ek din, ek bura admi usko zor se pakadne laga. Areeba ne jaldi se apni pencil chhod di aur chilla kar ammi ko bulaya. Ammi ne usko gale lagaya aur kaha, "Tumne theek kiya!"

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi tumhe aise pakade ke ajeeb lage, toh kya karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhari body tumhari hai—kisi ko bhi tumhe zor se pakadne ya ajeeb feel karane ka haq nahi!
