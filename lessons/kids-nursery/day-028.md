# Day 28 — Phal, Sabzi aur Janwaron ki Angrezi Mein Pehchan

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhein ge ke phal, sabzi aur janwaron ke naam angrezi mein kaise hote hain. Har cheez ka apna naam hota hai, aur yeh seekhna bachon ke liye bohat maza hai! Yeh bhi yaad rakhna zaroori hai ke har cheez ka apna haq hai—chahe woh janwar ho, phal ho ya sabzi—kisi ko bhi pareshan karne ka haq nahi.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Aman ko school se aate hi pita ne kaha, "Beta, aaj tum ne kya khaaya?" Aman ne muskura kar kaha, "Pita ji, main ne ek apple, ek banana aur ek orange khaaya!" Pita ne kaha, "Wah! Tum ne to teen alag-alag phal khaye. Kya tum inke angrezi naam bhi jaante ho?" Aman ne soch kar kaha, "Apple—seb, Banana—kela, Orange—santara!"

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar tum kisi janwar, phal ya sabzi ka naam angrezi mein jaante ho, to mujhe bata sakte ho? Jaise 'dog' ya 'cat'?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Har cheez ka apna naam hota hai—uske saath pyaar se pesh aao, aur kabhi bhi kisi ko pareshan mat karo. Agar koi cheez ajeeb lage, to turant ammi/abbu ko bata do!
