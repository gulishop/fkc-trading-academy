# Day 12 — "1 se 10 tak Ginti: Likhein aur Bolen!"

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhein ge ke 1 se 10 tak ki ginti kaise likhein aur bolin. Yeh humari roz ki zindagi mein bhi kaam aati hai, jaise jab hum ginti kartein hain ya koi cheez count kartein hain. Yaad rakhna, tumhara jism tumhara hai, isliye agar koi tumhe "na" kehne ko bole, toh foran ammi/abbu ko batana.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Babli ne apne ghar mein 5 chhote chidiya dekhein. Usne unhein ginte hue kaha, "1, 2, 3, 4, 5!" Phir usne apne ammi ko bataya, "Ammi, maine 5 chidiya dekhein!" Ammi ne usko gale lagaya aur kaha, "Bara acha hai, Babli!"

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi tumhe koi cheez karne ko bole jo tumhe acha na lage, toh tum kya karogi?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhara jism tumhara hai, isliye "na" kehne ka pura haq hai!
