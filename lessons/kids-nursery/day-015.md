# Day 15 — **Apna naam aur ghar ka pata bolna seekhna**

**Concept:**
Beta, aaj hum seekhein ge ke apna naam aur ghar ka pata kaise bolna hai. Yeh bohat zaroori hai kyun ke agar tum kabhi akela ho, toh kisi ko bata sako ke tumhara naam kya hai aur tum kahan rehte ho. Is se tumhe help milegi aur tum safe raho ge.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Ek din, Riya apne school se wapas aate waqt raaste mein kho gayi. Usne ek police wale uncle ko dekha aur unse poochha, "Uncle, mera naam Riya hai aur main 123, Green Park, Delhi rehti hoon." Uncle ne uski help ki aur uske ghar tak pohancha diya. Isliye, hamesha apna naam aur pata yaad rakhna!

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
Beta, agar tum kabhi kisi unfamiliar place par ho aur tumhe apna pata yaad na aaye, toh kya karoge?

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Hamesha apna naam aur ghar ka pata yaad rakhna. Agar koi unfamiliar insaan tumse yeh pooche, toh zaroor batana!
