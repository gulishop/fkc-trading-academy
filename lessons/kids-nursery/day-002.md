# Day 2 — "1, 2, 3... Ginti ka khushiyon ka silsila!"

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke 1 se 10 tak ki ginti kaise likhte hain aur bolte hain. Yeh zaroori hai taake tum apne toys, fruits, ya khaana gina kar khush ho sakein!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rahul ne apne 5 teddy bears ko ek ke peeche ek rakha. Usne gina: "1, 2, 3, 4, 5!" Uske baap ne kaha, "Bohot achha! Ab tum in teddy bears ko apne dost ko de sakte ho."

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi tumhe kaha ke 'yeh ginti ka secret rakhna hai', toh tum kya karogi?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhari jism tumhara hai—koi bhi tumhe 'na' kehne ka haq nahi rakhta! Agar koi cheez ajeeb lage, toh turant ammi/abbu ko batana.
