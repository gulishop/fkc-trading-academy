# Day 29 — **Adab: "Please", "Thank You", "Sorry" kab bolein?**

**Concept:**
Bachon, adab se baat karna hamari pehchaan hai. Jab hum "please" kehte hain, toh hum doosre ki meherbani ki darkar dikhate hain. "Thank you" se shukar ada hota hai, aur "sorry" se hum apne galat kaam ki zimmadari lete hain. Yeh teen shabd hamare rishton ko aur bhi meheka dete hain!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rohan ne apne dost ki pencil maangi, "Please de dena," kehte hue. Dost ne pencil de di, toh Rohan ne "Thank you" kaha. Phir ek din, Rohan ki accidental pencil doosre ke paimaan se gir gayi. Usne "Sorry" kaha aur doosri pencil le aaya. Dono dost aur bhi khush ho gaye!

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar tum kisi se kuch maangte ho, toh kaise kehte ho?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Adab se bole gaye shabd dil ko bhi khush karte hain. Hamesha "please", "thank you", aur "sorry" kehte raho!
