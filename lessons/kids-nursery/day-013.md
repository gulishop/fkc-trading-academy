# Day 13 — Rangon ke Naam Angrezi Mein

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke rangon ke naam angrezi mein kya hote hain. Rang sabke pasand hote hain—jaise gulaabi, neela, ya hara. Har rang ka apna ek khas naam hota hai, aur hum usko angrezi mein bhi bol sakte hain.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Areeba ne apne doodh mein thoda sa lal rang ka syrup daal diya. Usne maan se poochha, "Maa, yeh doodh lal kyun ho gaya?" Maan ne muskurate hue kaha, "Yeh lal rang hai, English mein hum isko 'red' kehte hain. Aaj hum aur rangon ke naam bhi seekhenge!"

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, tumhein sabse zyada kaunsa rang pasand hai? Uske naam ko angrezi mein kya kehte hain?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Rangon ke naam yaad rakhne ke liye unko dekh kar practice karo—jaise sabz patta 'green', aasmaan 'blue'!
