# Day 17 — **Chhoti chhoti awazon se lafz banana**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke kaise chhoti awazoon se bade lafz banate hain! Jaise 'c-a-t' bolne se 'cat' ban jaata hai. Yeh fun hai aur humare dimaag ko strong banata hai. Yaad rakhna, tumhare jism ka hissa sirf tumhara hai—koi tumhe 'na' kehne ka haq nahi rakhta.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Billi Mili ne socha ke woh apne dost ko ek surprise dena chahegi. Usne 'm-e-o-w' bolke 'meow' kaha! Uske dost ne haath taleel diya. Tum bhi apne dost ko surprise de sakte ho—bas 'w-a-v-e' bolke 'wave' karo!

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi tumhe 'secret' rakhne ko kahe, toh kya tum mujhse poochoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Hamesha yaad rakhna—tumhare jism ka hissa sirf tumhara hai! Agar koi cheez ajeeb lage, toh foran ammi/abbu ko batana. 💙
