# Day 4 — "Gol, Chauraha, Tara: Shaklein aur unke naam!"

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke har cheez ki shakl hoti hai, jaise gol gend, chauraha traffic signal, ya tara jo aasmaan par chamakti hai. In shaklein ke naam seekhna aasan hai, lekin yaad rakhna zaroori hai ke tumhari shakl sirf tumhari hai—koi tumse poochhe bhi to tumhe "na" kehne ka poora haq hai.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Areeba ne apne school ke playground mein ek lal tara dekha. Usne socha, "Yeh toh aasmaan ke taare jaisa hai!" Usne apne dost se kaha, "Chalo, hum bhi ek tara banaein!" Lekin jab uske dost ne kaha, "Mera bana hua tara tera bana hua hai," toh Areeba ne "na" keh diya. Usne apne teacher ko bataya, aur sab ne uski madad ki.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi tumse pooche ke tumhara gola (ya chauraha/taara) le le, toh tum kya karogi?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhari shakl sirf tumhari hai—koi bhi tumse poochhe bhi toh tumhe "na" kehne ka poora haq hai! Agar koi cheez ajeeb lage, toh foran humein batana. 💖
