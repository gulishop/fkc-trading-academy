# Day 25 — "Apna naam aur ghar ka pata bolna seekhna"

**Concept:**
Bachay, aaj hum seekhein ge ke apna naam aur ghar ka pata kaise bolna hai. Yeh zaroori hai ke tum apna naam aur ghar ka pata achi tarah yaad rakho, taake agar tum kabhi akela ho, toh tum apne aap ko pehchana sako aur apne ghar tak pohanch sako.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Ek din, Ali park mein khel raha tha. Usne ek nayi ball dekhi aur uske peeche bhagna shuru kar diya. Akela reh gaya toh usko yaad aaya ke usko apna naam aur ghar ka pata yaad rakhna hai. Usne apne dost se poochha, aur woh usko ghar le gaya.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, tumhara naam kya hai? Aur tumhara ghar kahan hai?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhara jism sirf tumhara hai. Agar koi tumse aise pooche ke tum apna naam ya ghar ka pata na do, toh foran ammi/abbu ya kisi trusted bade insaan ko batana hai.
