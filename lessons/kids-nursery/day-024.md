# Day 24 — **Shaklein aur unke naam (Square, Circle, Star)**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke har cheez ki ek shakl hoti hai! Jaise aapka doodh ka glass, aapki roti, ya aapki pencil ka box—har cheez ek na ek shakl mein hoti hai. Yeh shaklein humein cheezon ko pehchane mein madad karti hain. Aur sabse mazedaar baat? Har shakl ka ek khas naam hota hai!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Aapke dost Ayaan ne ek sundar greeting card banaya. Usne ek *square* (chautha) mein ek *circle* (gol) aur *star* (sitara) banaye. Ayaan ne kaha, "Yeh star mummy ko pasand aayega!" Aap bhi apni favourite shaklein bana kar ek khubsurat drawing bana sakte hain!

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar tum school mein kisi cheez ki shakl pehchane mein mushkil ho, toh tum kya karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Har cheez ki shakl ka naam yaad rakhna—chahe woh *square*, *circle*, ya *star* ho. Lekin yaad rakhna, aapki jism ki shakl sirf aapki hai, aur koi bhi aapko 'na' kehne ka haq nahi rakhta! Agar koi ajeeb baat ho, toh turant humein batana. 💖
