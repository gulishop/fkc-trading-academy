# Day 27 — **Chhoti chhoti awazon se lafz banana**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke kaise chhoti awazoon se bade lafz banaye jaate hain! Jaise 'c-a-t' se 'cat' ban jaata hai. Yeh fun hai aur humare dimaag ko strong banata hai. Yaad rakhna, tumhara jism tumhara hai—kisi ko tumse yeh kehne ka haq nahi ke tum 'na' keh do.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Billi Rani ne apne bachay ko kaha, "Beta, 'm-e' bol." Bacha bola, "Me!" Billi Rani muskuraayi, "Shabash! Ab 'm-e-o-w' bol." Bacha bola, "Meow!" Uske baad sab ne kaha, "Wah! Tumne toh 'meow' bol liya!"

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi tumse 'secret' rakhne ko kahe, toh kya tum mujhse poochoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhara jism tumhara hai—kisi ko bhi tumse 'na' kehne ka haq nahi! Agar koi cheez ajeeb lage, toh foran batana.
