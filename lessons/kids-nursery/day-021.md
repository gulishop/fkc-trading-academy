# Day 21 — **A se Z tak alphabets pehchanna**

**Concept:**
Bachay, aaj hum seekhenge ke har cheez ka pehla letter kya hota hai. Jaise ‘A’ for Apple, ‘B’ for Ball — isse hum zindagi bhar har cheez ko pehchaneinge. Har cheez ka pehla letter yaad rakhna, humein zyada confident banata hai!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rohan ne apni maasi se ek chota sa alphabet book liya. Usne har page par ek cheez dekhi — ‘A’ par Apple, ‘B’ par Ball. Usne socha, “Agar main har cheez ka pehla letter yaad rakh loon, to main kisi bhi cheez ka naam bata sakta hoon!”

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
“Beta, tumhari favourite cheez ka pehla letter kya hai? Aur agar kisi cheez ka pehla letter yaad na ho, to tum kya karoge?”

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Har cheez ka pehla letter yaad rakhna, tumhein zyada smart banayega — jaise ek detective jo har clue ko samajh leta hai!
