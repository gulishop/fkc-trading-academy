# Day 26 — **Din aur Mausam ke Saath Pyar Se Milna**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke din kaun-koun se hain aur mausam kaise hota hai. Har din alag hota hai, aur har mausam alag rang layega. Yeh sab humare liye khushiyon ka hissa hai!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rohan ko subah uthte hi pata chalta hai ke aaj *Monday* hai. "Maa, Monday kya hota hai?" poochta hai. Maa muskurati hain, "Beta, Monday pehla din hai jisme hum school jaate hain aur naye dost milte hain. Aaj mausam bhi dhoop wala hai, toh tum apna cap pehen lena!"

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, tumhe sabse zyada kaunsa din pasand hai aur kyun?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Har mausam alag hai, par tumhara jism sirf tumhara hai. Agar koi cheez ajeeb lage, toh chup mat rehna—ammi-abbu ko zaroor batana!
