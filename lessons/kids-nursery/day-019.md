# Day 19 — "Adab: Please, Thank You, Sorry Kab Bolein?"

**Concept:**
Bachay, adab se baat karna hamari pehchan hai. Jab hum "please" kehte hain, toh hum dusron ko respect dete hain. "Thank you" se hum shukar ada karte hain. Aur "sorry" kehne se hum apni galti ka ehsaas karte hain. Yeh teen shabd hamari zindagi ko khushiyon bhara banate hain!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rohan ne apne dost se pencil maangi. Usne "Please, de dena" kaha. Dost ne pencil de di. Rohan ne "Thank you" kaha. Phir Rohan ne apne dost ki copy par rang laga diya. Usne "Sorry, maine galat kiya" kaha. Dono dost khush ho gaye!

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar tumhari koi cheez koi le le aur tum usko "please" kehte ho, toh kya lagta hai?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Adab se baat karna hamara imaan hai. "Please", "Thank you", aur "Sorry" kehna humesha yaad rakhna!
