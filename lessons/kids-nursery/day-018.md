# Day 18 — **Phal, Sabzi aur Janwaron ki Angrezi Mein Pehchan**

**Concept:**
Bachon, aaj hum seekhenge ke phal, sabzi aur janwaron ke naam angrezi mein kaise hote hain. Lekin saath hi ek important baat: koi bhi insaan aapko jabardasti chhua nahi kar sakta. Aapka shareer sirf aapka hai, aur agar koi aapko pareshan kare toh zaroor ammi/abbu ya kisi trusted bade ko batana hai—chahe woh koi bhi ho!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rohan ne park mein ek badi si teddy bear dekha. Usne socha, "Mummy, kya main usko pakad sakta hoon?" Par usne yaad kiya ke ammi ne kaha tha ke koi bhi janwar ya insaan jabardasti nahi chhua karna chahiye. Isliye Rohan ne teddy bear ko dekh kar muskura diya, par pakadna nahi.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi janwar ya insaan tumhe chhune ki koshish kare toh tum kya karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Apne shareer ka khayal rakhna sabse zaroori hai. Agar koi cheez ajeeb lage toh zaroor batana! 💖
