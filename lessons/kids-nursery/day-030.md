# Day 30 — **Pencil Pakadna aur Seedhi Lakeerein Banana**

**Concept:**
Beta, pencil pakadna aur ache se likhna seekhna bhi ek kala hai! Jab tum pencil thoda teek se pakadte ho, toh tumhari lakeerein bhi saaf aur seedhi hoti hain. Yeh sirf likhne mein hi madad nahi karta, balki tumhari writing bhi khoobsurat banati hai. Hamesha yaad rakhna ke tumhari pencil tumhare hath ka hissa hai – use kisi aur ko dena ya use kisi aur ke hath mein dene ki zaroorat nahi hai.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Aaj Riya ne apne papa se poochha, "Papa, maine ek sundar sundar drawing banaayi hai!" Papa ne muskurate hue kaha, "Beta, dekh kya tumne pencil theek se pakadi hai! Isliye tumhare rang bhi itne saaf hain. Maine bhi tumhe dekha tha ke tumne pencil ko hath ki ungliyon se pakda hai, jaise teacher ne bataya tha."

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
Beta, tumhari pencil ko pakadne ka jo tareeka tum apne aap seekh rahe ho, uske baare mein tumhe kaisa lagta hai? Kya tumhe lagta hai ke tumhari lakeerein aur bhi behtar ho rahi hain?

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Hamesha yaad rakhna ke tumhari pencil sirf tumhare hath ki cheez hai. Agar koi tumse pooche ke "Mujhe apni pencil do," toh tum seedha ammi-abbu ya kisi trusted bade ko batana.
