# Day 18 — **YouTube Automation Ka "Evergreen Content" Formula: Channel Ko Har Season Mein Grow Rakhein**

**Concept:**
Aaj seekhein ke kaise "Evergreen Content" banayein jo har season/month mein views deta rahe. Evergreen content wo hai jo kabhi outdated nahi hota (jaise "How to tie a tie" ya "Best budget laptops 2024"). AI tools (jaise Google Trends, AnswerThePublic) se topics dhundein jo long-term interest rakhein. Consistency ke saath in videos ko publish karne se channel hamesha grow karta rahega.

**Example:**
1. **Topic Research:** Google Trends par "AI tools for students" search karen. Agar yeh topic har month stable interest rakhta hai, to is par video banayein.
2. **Script:** "5 Best Free AI Tools For Students In 2024 (Step-by-Step Guide)" — yeh title har saal update hoga lekin core content same rahega.
3. **Upload:** Har 2 haftay baad ek naya evergreen video upload karen.

**Practice:**
Apne niche ke 3 evergreen topics dhundein (Google Trends ya AnswerThePublic se) aur unke titles likhein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek 1-minute script outline likhein ek evergreen topic par (jaise "How to start a YouTube channel in 2024").

**Answer Key:**
trends check karein, stable topics, long-term interest
