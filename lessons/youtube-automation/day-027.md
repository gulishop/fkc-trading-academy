# Day 27 — **YouTube Automation Mein "Evergreen Content" Kaise Banaye? (Jo Har Season Mein Views Dete Rahen)**

**Concept:**
Evergreen content wo videos hote hain jo har season mein search hote rehte hain—jaise "How to tie a tie" ya "Best budget laptops 2024". In videos par traffic kabhi kam nahi hota, jabki trending topics 1-2 mahine baad fade ho jate hain. Automation ke liye yeh goldmine hain kyunki inko baar-baar promote kar sakte hain bina naye videos banaye.

**Example:**
Ek channel jo "Home workout for beginners" par videos banata hai, usko har mahine 50K views milte hain kyunki yeh topic kabhi outdated nahi hota. Aise topics dhundne ke liye **Google Trends** ya **TubeBuddy** ka use karo aur "All time" filter lagao.

**Practice:**
Apne niche se 3 evergreen topics select karo jo:
1. Har saal search kiye jate hain
2. Competition kam hai (low competition keywords)
3. 5+ saal purane videos bhi views le rahe hain

**Mini Project:**
Ek spreadsheet banao jisme:
- Topic name
- Search volume (Ahrefs/Google Keyword Planner se)
- Competition score (TubeBuddy se)
- Pichle 3 saal ka average views (VidIQ se)
Top 3 topics ko highlight karo jo sabse zyada potential rakhte hain.

**Answer Key:**
trends, low-competition, high-retention, recurring-traffic
