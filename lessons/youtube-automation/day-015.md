# Day 15 — **YouTube Automation Ka Secret Weapon: "Hook Formula" Jo 3 Seconds Mein Viewer Ko Roke**

**Concept:**
Aaj seekhenge **"Hook Formula"** – wo 3-5 second ka opening jo viewer ko video pe rok deta hai. 80% viewers sirf isi par decide karte hain ke video continue karni hai ya nahi. AI tools se bhi acha hook banaya ja sakta hai. Formula 3 parts mein hota hai: **Problem + Curiosity + Promise**.

**Example:**
Ek "AI se paise kamane" video ka hook:
*"Tumne socha hai ke AI se paise kamana mumkin hai lekin har koi fail ho raha hai? Maine 1 mahine mein $10,000 kamaye hain – aur main tumhe wo 3 chhoti galtiyan batata hun jo sab karte hain…"* (Problem + Curiosity + Promise)

**Practice:**
Apne agle video ke liye 3 alag hooks likhein (har ek 30 words se kam). Use kar ke dekhein kon sa sabse zyada "retention" badhata hai (YouTube Studio > Analytics > Engagement).

**Mini Project:**
Ek 15-second ka "hook teaser" video banao (CapCut/Pictory se) aur usko Shorts par upload karo. Dekhein kitne viewers 5+ seconds tak roke.

**Answer Key:**
problem, curiosity, promise, retention
