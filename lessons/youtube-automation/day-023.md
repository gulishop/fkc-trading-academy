# Day 23 — **AI-Powered Video Editing: CapCut Se Pictory Tak (Best Tools 2024)**

**Concept:**
AI tools ne faceless video editing ko 10x fast aur pro level par le aaya hai. CapCut (free) se lekar Pictory, InVideo, aur Runway ML tak AI-powered features jaise auto-subtitles, voice cloning, aur background removal use karke videos 30 minutes mein edit ki ja sakti hain. Har tool ka apna strength hai—CapCut editing ke liye best hai, jabki Pictory long-form content (jaise documentaries) ke liye perfect hai.

**Example:**
1. **CapCut mein AI editing:**
   - Upload video clips → "Auto Captions" (AI subtitles) → "Text to Speech" (AI voiceover) → "Background Remover" (green screen effect).
   - Example: Ek travel channel ke liye "Top 5 Places in Pakistan" video mein AI subtitles aur background music add karke 15-minute video 5 minutes mein edit karo.

2. **Pictory se AI script-to-video:**
   - Apna script paste karo → AI "selects best visuals" (stock footage) → auto voiceover (110+ AI voices) → export MP4.
   - Example: "How to Start a YouTube Channel" topic par 3-minute video banao jo 100% AI-generated ho.

**Practice:**
Apna ek pehle se edit kiya hua video lein (chahe manual ho ya AI se). CapCut mein "Auto Captions" aur "Background Music" add karke dekhain. 10 minutes mein complete karna hai.

**Mini Project:**
Ek 2-minute AI-generated video banao:
- Topic: "AI Tools 2024: Best Free Tools for Beginners"
- Tools: Pictory (script → video) + CapCut (final touch)
- Deliverables: MP4 file + 3 hashtags (#AITools #YouTubeAutomation #FreeEditing)

**Answer Key:**
AI subtitles, auto-voiceover, stock footage, export MP4
