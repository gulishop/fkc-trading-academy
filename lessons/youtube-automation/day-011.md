# Day 11 — **YouTube Shorts Se Traffic Kaise Le Aayein (0 Se 10K Views Tak)**

**Concept:**
YouTube Shorts aajkal sabse tezi se grow karne ka zariya hai. Yeh 60 seconds ke vertical videos hain jo explore page par bhi aate hain. Agar aapke long-form videos pe traffic nahi aa raha, to Shorts se start karen. Isme algorithm fast feedback deta hai—agar video achha hai to 24-48 ghante mein hi views badh jate hain.

**Example:**
Ek student ne "AI se paise kamane ke tarike" par 10 Shorts banaye:
1. "AI se free mein logo kaise banayein?"
2. "AI se YouTube thumbnails kaise design karen?"
3. "AI se script kaise likhein?"
Har Short ne 5K+ views liye aur uske long-form video pe bhi traffic aaya.

**Practice:**
Aaj hi 3 Shorts banayein ek trending niche par (jaise "AI tools", "side hustles", "tech gadgets"). Har Short ka pehla 3 second zyada engaging ho (hook).

**Mini Project:**
Ek YouTube Shorts playlist banayein jismein aapke 5 best-performing Shorts hon. Us playlist ka title rakhein: "Top 5 [Niche] Shorts | Quick Tips".

**Answer Key:**
trending niche, 3-second hook, playlist, 24-48 ghante
