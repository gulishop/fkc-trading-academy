# Day 4 — YouTube Voiceover aur Background Music: Pro-Level Setup

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ki kaise ek engaging voiceover record karna hai (AI ya human) aur background music kaise choose karna hai jo video ko professional banaye. Voiceover ki quality aur music ka mood video ke message ke hisaab se hona chahiye. AI tools jaise ElevenLabs, Murf.ai ya natural human voice recording se aap clear aur engaging audio pa sakte hain.

**Example:**
1. **Voiceover:** Apne script ko ElevenLabs par upload karke "Neural" voice select karo. Pitch aur speed adjust karke natural lagne wala audio generate karo.
2. **Background Music:** YouTube Audio Library ya Epidemic Sound se royalty-free music download karo. Volume ko 10-15% par set karo taake voiceover clear rahe.

**Practice:**
Apne Day 2 ke script ka voiceover banane ke liye ElevenLabs ya Murf.ai ka free trial use karo. Audio export karke CapCut mein import karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek 1-minute ka voiceover record karo (AI ya apni awaz se), uske saath background music add karo, aur video mein sync karo.

**Answer Key:**
ElevenLabs, Murf.ai, YouTube Audio Library, royalty-free
