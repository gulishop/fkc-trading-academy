# Day 22 — **AI Se "Viral Hooks" Banane Ka Advanced Formula**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum AI se **viral hooks** banayenge jo pehle 5 seconds mein hi viewers ko "rukne" par majboor kar dein. Hooks hei woh cheez hai jo CTR badhaati hai, retention badhaati hai aur algorithm ko signal deti hai ke aapka video valuable hai. AI tools (jaise Jasper, Copy.ai, ya even YouTube ke apne AI suggestions) se hum **emotional triggers**, **curiosity gaps** aur **pain points** ko target karke hooks likhenge jo 10x zyada engaging hon.

**Example:**
Ek **AI-generated hook** for a "Make Money Online" video:
*"Agar main aapko bataun ke aaj hi 10,000 rupay kaise kamaye ja sakte hain… lekin yeh trick sirf 0.1% log jaante hain? 👀 Dekhiye video poori… warna aapka next month ka salary bhi khatam ho sakta hai!"*
- **Emotional trigger:** Fear of missing out (FOMO) + curiosity gap ("0.1% log")
- **Pain point:** Financial struggle (salary khatam hona)

**Practice:**
Apne channel ke **top 3 trending videos** ke transcripts ya descriptions ko copy karein. Unke hooks ko AI (jaise [Hypotenuse.ai](https://www.hypotenuse.ai/) ya [Jasper.ai](https://www.jasper.ai/)) se **rewrite karwayein** taake woh zyada engaging aur clickable bane. **Prompt example:**
*"Rewrite this YouTube hook to make it more viral: [paste original hook]. Use emotional triggers and curiosity gaps."*

**Mini Project:**
**1 viral hook ka A/B test banana:**
- Ek video ke do different hooks banaen (ek AI se, ek manual).
- Unhe **YouTube Studio** mein "A/B test" ke liye upload karein (same thumbnail, title, aur video content, bas first 5 seconds alag).
- **7 days** tak wait karein aur **CTR** compare karein. Jo zyada CTR de, usko permanent rakhein.

**Answer Key:**
AI-prompts, emotional-triggers, curiosity-gaps, A/B-test
