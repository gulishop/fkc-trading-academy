# Day 20 — **Team Banane Se Pehle: Apne Automation Channel Ka "System" Banana**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke team banane se pehle apne channel ka ek "system" kaise banaye. Automation channel ko scale karne ka pehla step hai: apne processes ko document karna, tools automate karna, aur team members ko train karne ke liye ek blueprint banana. Bina system ke team banana chaos hai—har cheez repeat karni paregi aur quality control mushkil ho jayegi.

**Example:**
1. **Process Document:** Har video ke liye ek checklist banayein (script, voiceover, editing, thumbnail, upload).
2. **Tools:** CapCut templates, AI voiceover scripts, aur Canva thumbnails ka template save karke rakhein.
3. **Training:** Ek video ya PDF banayein jisme team ko batayein ke har step kaise karna hai (e.g., "Voiceover ka pitch 0.8x se 1.2x ke beech hona chahiye").

**Practice:**
Apne channel ke liye ek **1-pager "Standard Operating Procedure" (SOP)** banayein. Ismein likhein:
- Har video ke liye kaun kaun se steps hain?
- Har step ka time limit kya hai?
- Quality check ke liye kya criteria hain?

**Mini Project:**
Ek **SOP template** banayein (Google Docs/Notion) jismein aapke channel ke sabhi processes likhein. Is template ko apne team (ya future team) ke saath share karne ke liye taiyar rakhein.

**Answer Key:**
SOP, checklist, time limits, quality criteria
