# Day 10 — YouTube Automation Ka Next Level: AI Tools Se 10x Faster Content Banana

**Concept:**
AI tools ab itne advanced ho gaye hain ke aap ek hi din mein 5-10 videos automate kar sakte hain—lekin sirf "automate" karne se kaam nahi chalta. Aaj seekhenge ke kaise AI se **script se thumbnail tak sab kuch** 10x faster generate karna hai, bina quality sacrifice kiye. Hum CapCut AI, Pictory, aur Runway ML jaise tools ka use karenge jo aapke existing content ko repurpose karke new videos banayenge.

**Example:**
1. **Script Generation:** Sudhar AI ya Jasper se 10 niche-specific scripts generate karwao (e.g., "AI se paise kaise kamaye").
2. **Video Creation:** Pictory ya CapCut AI se har script ka video banwao (stock footage + AI voiceover).
3. **Thumbnail & Title:** Canva ya MidJourney se 3-5 thumbnail options generate karo, phir sabse best CTR wala select karo.
4. **Upload & Schedule:** TubeBuddy ya VidIQ se SEO optimize karke, sab videos ko 1 week ke schedule mein upload karo.

**Practice:**
Aaj **ek existing video** ko lekar uska **script, video, aur thumbnail** AI tools se generate karo. (E.g., agar aapka "5 YouTube Growth Hacks" video hai, to usko repurpose karke "5 AI-Powered Growth Hacks" video banao.)

**Mini Project:**
**AI Content Repurposing Pipeline** banayein:
1. Ek YouTube video ya blog post select karo.
2. Uske topic se 3-5 new video ideas generate karo (AI tools se).
3. Har idea ka script, video, aur thumbnail AI se banayein.
4. Sabko ek Excel sheet mein track karo (title, script link, video link, upload date).

**Answer Key:**
AI script generator, Pictory/CapCut AI, Canva/MidJourney, TubeBuddy/VidIQ
