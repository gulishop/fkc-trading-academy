# Day 13 — **AI-Powered Video Editing: CapCut Aur Pictory Se Pro-Level Videos Banana**

**Concept:**
AI tools jaise CapCut aur Pictory se videos edit karna seekhiye—automated transitions, auto-subtitles, aur AI voiceovers ke saath. Ye tools beginner-friendly hain lekin advanced effects bhi offer karte hain, jaise background music sync, auto-captions, aur even AI-powered voice cloning.

**Example:**
1. **CapCut mein AI editing:** Apne footage ko upload karo → "Auto Captions" par click karo → AI subtitles generate honge. Phir "Auto Cut" feature use karke boring parts hata do.
2. **Pictory se AI video banane ka step:** Apne script ko paste karo → Pictory AI se relevant clips dhundega → Background music aur transitions automatically add kar dega.

**Practice:**
Apne phone ya laptop par CapCut install karo, koi 1-minute ka raw footage upload karo, aur "Auto Captions" + "Auto Cut" features try karo. 2-3 variations save karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek 1-minute ka AI-edited video banayein (topic: "Top 3 AI Tools For YouTube Automation")—CapCut/Pictory use karke, subtitles, background music, aur transitions add karke.

**Answer Key:**
auto captions, auto cut, background music sync, AI transitions
