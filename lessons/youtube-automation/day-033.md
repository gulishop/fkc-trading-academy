# Day 33 — **YouTube Automation Mein "Shorts Se Viral Growth" Kaise Le? (Algorithm Se Samjho, Views Badhane Ka Hack)**

**Concept:**
YouTube Shorts algorithm short-form content ko bohat priority deta hai. Agar aap isko samajh kar consistently Shorts banate hain, toh channel ko organic reach milti hai. Aaj seekhein ke Shorts ka algorithm kaise kaam karta hai, konse elements viral hote hain, aur kaise apne existing videos ko bhi Shorts mein convert karke extra views le sakte hain.

**Example:**
Ek gaming channel ne apne 10-minute video ke "Best 5 Moments" ko 30-second Shorts mein convert kiye. Pehle 10 videos ke 5K views the, lekin Shorts banane ke baad 20K+ views aane lage. Algorithm ne in Shorts ko "trending" section mein push kiya kyunke engagement rate high tha.

**Practice:**
Apne channel ke kisi bhi video se 30-60 second ka engaging clip nikalo (yaad rakhna: first 3 seconds mein hook ho). Us clip ko Shorts format mein edit karo (CapCut/Premiere Pro se). Title mein trending keyword add karo (e.g., "This 1 Trick Broke The Game!").

**Mini Project:**
1 existing video ko 3 Shorts mein convert karo (har Short ka alag angle ho). Har Short ko 3 different titles se upload karo aur check karo ke konsa best perform karta hai. 7 days ke analytics dekh kar conclusion nikalo.

**Answer Key:**
hook, trending keyword, first 3 seconds, high CTR
