# Day 14 — **Background Music Ka Perfect Choice: Emotion Se Views Badhane Ka Formula**

**Concept:**
Background music sirf "fill" nahi, balki viewers ki emotion control karne ka powerful tool hai. Wrong music se viewers bore ho sakte hain, lekin perfect track se video viral bhi ho sakti hai. Aaj seekhoge ki kaise music choose karna hai jo video ki mood ko match kare, copyright issues se bache, aur YouTube algorithm ko bhi impress kare.

**Example:**
Ek "AI vs Human" topic par video bana rahe ho. Agar serious research discuss kar rahe ho, to "epic cinematic" music use karo. Lekin agar funny comparison kar rahe ho, to "lighthearted electronic" ya "comedy background" track use karo. CapCut mein music library se "trending" category mein se select karo.

**Practice:**
Apne last video (ya kisi bhi video) ke liye 3 different background music options choose karo—ek serious, ek funny, ek neutral. Har ek ka 10-second preview sun kar decide karo ki kaun sa best fit hai.

**Mini Project:**
Ek 1-minute ka "AI Tools Review" video banao (ya existing video edit karo) aur 3 different background music tracks test karo. Check karo ki kaun sa track viewers ko zyada engage karta hai (agar possible ho to friends se feedback lo).

**Answer Key:**
emotional match, copyright-free, trending genre, algorithm-friendly
