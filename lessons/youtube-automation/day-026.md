# Day 26 — **YouTube Automation Mein "Shorts Se Traffic Kaise Le Jaen"? (Trending Shorts Ko Long-Form Video Mein Convert Karne Ka Hack)**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke trending YouTube Shorts ko kaise apne long-form videos mein convert kar ke channel par traffic le jaen. Shorts se views milte hain, lekin unhe long-form videos mein daal kar permanent subscribers aur watch time badhaen. AI tools (jaise CapCut, Pictory) aur editing tricks se yeh kaam automated bhi ho sakta hai.

**Example:**
1. Trending Shorts par jaen (jaise "AI se logo banane ka tarika").
2. Us Shorts ko download karen (4K Video Downloader se).
3. CapCut mein import karen, original video ko 2x speed se badhaen (agar Shorts 15s ka hai to 30s ka video ban jayega).
4. Uske niche "AI se logo banane ke 5 advanced tips" likh kar ek long-form video script add karen.
5. Background mein royalty-free music lagayen aur thumbnail mein "AI Logo Generator" likh kar clickable banayen.

**Practice:**
Apne channel par ek trending Shorts ko select karen aur usko 1-2 minute ke long-form video mein convert karen. CapCut ya Pictory ka istemal karen.

**Mini Project:**
Ek Shorts ko long-form video mein convert kar ke upload karen aur uske description mein "Full Guide: [Topic]" likh kar link add karen.

**Answer Key:**
Shorts download, CapCut edit, long-form video, description link
