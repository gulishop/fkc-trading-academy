# Day 3 — AI-Powered Video Editing: CapCut aur Pictory Se Professional Videos Banana

**Concept:**
AI tools jaise CapCut aur Pictory se faceless videos edit karna seekhna hai. In tools se auto-captioning, transitions, aur background music lagana asaan ho jata hai. CapCut mobile/PC par free hai, jabki Pictory cloud-based hai aur stock footage provide karta hai.

**Example:**
1. **CapCut mein video import karo** (mobile/PC par).
2. **AI se auto-captioning** (Text > Auto Captions) lagao.
3. **Transitions** (Effects > Basic) add karo (e.g., fade in/out).
4. **Background music** (Audio > AI Music) select karo.
5. **Export** (1080p, 30fps) karo.

**Practice:**
Apne phone/PC par CapCut install karo, koi 30-second ka clip import karo, aur uspar auto-captioning + ek transition add karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek 1-minute ka faceless video clip banayo jisme:
- 1 stock footage clip ho (Pexels/Pixabay se download karo).
- Auto-captioning + 2 transitions hon.
- Background music ho (CapCut ke AI music se).

**Answer Key:**
stock footage, auto captions, transitions, background music
