# Day 6 — **YouTube Thumbnails: Click-Through Rate (CTR) Badhane Ke 5 Pro Secrets**

**Concept:**
Thumbnail aur title mil kar 90% CTR decide karte hain. Aaj seekhoge ki kaise "click magnet" thumbnails banaye jo YouTube algorithm ko impress kare. AI tools (Canva, Photoshop, MidJourney) se professional designs banane ke tarike bhi cover kareinge.

**Example:**
1. **Contrast:** Peela background + Black bold text (e.g., "5 LAKH RUPEES KAMAAO!")
2. **Face/Emotion:** Close-up photo + surprised expression (AI se generate kar sakte hain)
3. **Minimal Text:** 3-5 words max, bold font (e.g., "AI SE PAISA")
4. **Border/Shadow:** White border + drop shadow for visibility
5. **Test:** 2-3 variants banakar YouTube Studio mein A/B test karein

**Practice:**
Apne channel ke 1 trending video ka thumbnail lein. Usmein **1 hi change** karein (e.g., text color ya background) aur upload karein. Next 24 hours mein CTR check karein.

**Mini Project:**
AI se 3 thumbnail variants generate karein (MidJourney prompt: *"YouTube thumbnail, minimal text, high contrast, trending topic, professional design, 16:9 aspect ratio"*). Sabse best 1 select karke upload karein.

**Answer Key:**
yellow+black, 3-5 words, A/B test, CTR 2-5%
