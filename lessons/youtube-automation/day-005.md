# Day 5 — YouTube SEO: Search Mein Top Par Aana (Basics Se Start)

**Concept:**
YouTube SEO ka matlab hai apne videos ko YouTube ke search aur suggested videos mein top par lana. Iske liye keywords, tags, description, aur engagement (CTR) ka istemal hota hai. Aaj hum YouTube search results mein rank karne ke liye **keywords research** aur **video metadata** (title, description, tags) ko optimize karna seekhenge.

**Example:**
1. **Keyword Research:** Google Keyword Planner ya TubeBuddy/VIDIQ se "how to make money online in Pakistan" search karo. Volume (searches/month) aur competition check karo.
2. **Title Optimization:** Agar keyword "make money online" hai, to title ho sakta hai: *"Make Money Online in Pakistan (2024) | Best 5 Ways | Urdu/Hindi"*
3. **Description:** Pehle 2-3 lines mein keyword repeat karo, phir video summary likho, aur last mein relevant links (social media, playlist) add karo.
4. **Tags:** Main keyword + related keywords (e.g., "online earning, freelancing, YouTube se paise kamana").

**Practice:**
Apne niche ka **1 main keyword** aur **2-3 related keywords** dhundho (Google Keyword Planner ya YouTube search bar ka istemal karo). Result ko not karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **YouTube video ka draft title aur description** likho jo SEO-friendly ho. Apne niche ka keyword zaroor include karo.

**Answer Key:**
keyword research, title optimization, description structure, tags usage
