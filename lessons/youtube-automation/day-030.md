# Day 30 — **YouTube Automation Ka Next Level: AI Tools Se Kaam Ko 10x Fast Karne Ka Hack**

**Concept:**
AI tools jaise **Jasper, Midjourney, Descript, aur Runway ML** aapki YouTube Automation ko 10x fast banate hain. Inse script likhne se lekar video edit karne tak kaam 50% kam time mein ho sakta hai. AI se **auto-captioning, auto-subtitles, aur even AI voiceovers** bhi generate ki ja sakti hain jo natural lagti hain. Bas in tools ko samajh kar istemal karna hai.

**Example:**
1. **AI Script Writer (Jasper.ai)** – Apne topic ka title daal kar 5-10 minute mein script generate karwa lo.
2. **AI Voiceover (ElevenLabs)** – Apne script ko AI se realistic voice mein convert kar lo (10+ languages support).
3. **AI Video Editor (Runway ML)** – Auto-cut, auto-subtitle, aur background music add karke video ready kar lo.

**Practice:**
Aaj hi **ElevenLabs** par ek free account bana kar apne script ka AI voiceover generate karo aur CapCut mein use karke video edit karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **AI-generated short video** (30-60 seconds) banao jo **trending topic** par ho. Use **YouTube Shorts** par upload karo aur engagement check karo.

**Answer Key:**
ElevenLabs, Jasper.ai, Runway ML, auto-subtitles
