# Day 31 — **YouTube Automation Mein "AI Se Competitor Research" Kaise Kare? (Niche Aur Content Ideas Chune Ka Smart Hack)**

**Concept:**
AI tools se competitors analyze karke unke best-performing videos, keywords, aur audience engagement patterns samajhna seekhein. Yeh strategy aapko unique content ideas dhundne aur YouTube algorithm ko samajhne mein madad karegi. Tools jaise TubeBuddy, VidIQ, aur AI-powered platforms (jaise ChatGPT + YouTube search) use karen.

**Example:**
1. **Competitor Video Analysis:** TubeBuddy ya VidIQ mein kisi top channel ka URL paste karen.
2. **Best Videos Filter:** "Top Videos" section mein jaakar highest views aur engagement wale videos dekhhein.
3. **Keyword Extraction:** Video titles aur descriptions se main keywords copy karen.
4. **AI Se Ideas Generate:** ChatGPT ko prompt dein: *"YouTube channel [niche] ke liye 5 unique video ideas suggest karo jo high engagement rakhti hain."*

**Practice:**
Apne niche ke 3 top competitors ke YouTube channels ko TubeBuddy/VidIQ se analyze karen aur unke top 3 videos ke titles aur keywords ka note banayein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek simple spreadsheet banayein jismein:
- Competitor ka channel name
- Top video ka title
- Main keyword
- Views aur engagement (likes/comments)
- Aapki us keyword par video banane ki planning

**Answer Key:**
competitor analysis, high-engagement keywords, unique content ideas, spreadsheet tracking
