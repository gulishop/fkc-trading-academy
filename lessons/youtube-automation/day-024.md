# Day 24 — **YouTube Automation Mein "Royalty-Free Music" Ka Sahi Istemal: Copyright Strike Se Bachne Ke 5 Golden Rules**

**Concept:**
YouTube par video upload karte waqt **background music** aur **sound effects** ka istemal zaroori hai, lekin **copyright claim** ya **strike** se bachna bhi utna hi important hai. **Royalty-Free music** ka matlab hai wo music jo copyright-free hai ya license ke saath aati hai. **YouTube ki Content ID system** automatically copyrighted music detect kar leti hai, isliye sahi source se music download karna zaroori hai.

**Example:**
1. **Wrong:** YouTube Audio Library se music download karke use karna (lekin yeh bhi copyrighted ho sakta hai agar license clear na ho).
2. **Right:**
   - **Epidemic Sound** ya **Artlist** jaise paid platforms se license wala music le sakte hain.
   - **YouTube Audio Library** se free music le sakte hain, lekin **license check** karna zaroori hai.
   - **Free Music Archive (FMA)** ya **Pixabay** se bhi free music use kar sakte hain.

**Practice:**
Aaj apne **pichle video** ke liye **3 royalty-free music options** search karo (ek free, ek paid, ek YouTube Audio Library se). Har option ka **license type** (e.g., Creative Commons, Commercial Use) note karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **1-minute ka test video** banayo jisme:
- Ek royalty-free background music use karo.
- Ek copyright-free sound effect (jaise "click" ya "whoosh") add karo.
- Video ko **unlisted** mode mein upload karo aur check karo ki **Content ID claim** aati hai ya nahi.

**Answer Key:**
Epidemic Sound, Artlist, YouTube Audio Library, Creative Commons, Content ID claim
