# Day 2 — Facebook Par Chalne Wala Content: Kya Post Karna Hai?

**Concept:**
Facebook par engagement aur reach badhane ke liye content strategy sabse zaroori hai. Basics mein aapko yeh samajhna hoga ke kaun sa content Facebook par viral hota hai—jaise emotional posts, trending topics, ya educational content. Har post ka purpose hona chahiye: ya toh audience ko entertain karna, ya inform karna, ya phir unse interact karna.

**Example:**
Ek fitness page ke liye "Morning Walk Benefits" naam ka post kar sakte hain. Ismein aap 3 bullet points likhein:
1. "Tension kam hota hai" (emotional hook)
2. "Heart health improve hoti hai" (educational)
3. "Free hai, kisi equipment ki zaroorat nahi" (practical benefit)
Iske saath ek motivational image ya short video clip bhi add kar sakte hain.

**Practice:**
Apne page ke niche (jaise cooking, tech, fitness) ka ek draft post likhein. Use 3-4 lines ka banao, aur ek emotional ya educational hook zaroor add karna. (Hint: "Aapko pata hai ke..." ya "Yeh galti sab karte hain...")

**Mini Project:**
Apne draft post ko 3-5 friends ko share karke unse feedback lo. Poochhein ke unko kya engaging laga aur kya improve kar sakte hain.

**Answer Key:**
emotional hook, educational value, practical benefit
