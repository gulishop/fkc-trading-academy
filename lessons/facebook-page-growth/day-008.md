# Day 8 — Facebook Page Se Affiliate Marketing Se Paise Kamana

**Concept:**
Affiliate marketing ka matlab hai kisi aur ki product ya service promote karke commission kamana. Facebook Page par affiliate links share karke aap passive income generate kar sakte hain. Aapko sirf trusted brands ke programs join karne hain aur unke products ko apne audience ke liye relevant content ke saath promote karna hai.

**Example:**
Maana aapki Facebook Page fitness se related hai. Aap "MyProtein" ya "Amazon Associates" jaise affiliate programs join kar sakte hain. Phir aap posts mein unke protein powder ya gym equipment ke links share kar sakte hain. Har sale par aapko 5-20% commission milega.

**Practice:**
1. Apne niche ke 3 trusted affiliate programs dhundhein (jaise Amazon Associates, ClickBank, ya Flipkart Affiliate).
2. Unke registration process ko complete karein aur affiliate links generate karein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek Facebook post create karein jisme aap ek affiliate product promote kar rahe hain. Post mein product ka naam, benefits, aur affiliate link share karein. Post ko apne page par publish karein aur engagement check karein.

**Answer Key:**
trusted programs, commission rates, relevant products, engagement check
