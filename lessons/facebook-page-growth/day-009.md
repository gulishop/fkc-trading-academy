# Day 9 — Facebook Page Ko Fake/Copyright Issues Se Bachane Ke Advanced Tips

**Concept:**
Facebook par fake accounts, copyright strikes, ya plagiarism se bachna zaroori hai. Aaj hum advanced strategies seekhenge jaise copyright-free content kaise dhundein, fake followers ka pata kaise lagayein, aur DMCA takraavat se kaise bachain. Bina license ke copyrighted music ya images use karne se page band hone ka risk hai.

**Example:**
Agar aap koi viral video banana chahte hain, to copyright-free music ke liye **Epidemic Sound** ya **YouTube Audio Library** use karen. Agar kisi ne aapka content chori karke use kiya hai, to **Facebook Copyright Center** par report karen.

**Practice:**
Apne page par upload kiye gaye kisi bhi post ya video mein copyrighted content (music/image) check karen. Agar hai to use remove karen ya replace karen.

**Mini Project:**
Ek copyright-free content plan banayein — 3 post ideas jo aapko copyright issues se bachayein. Har idea ke liye source bhi mention karen (jaise Pexels, Pixabay, ya Creative Commons).

**Answer Key:**
Pexels, Pixabay, Creative Commons, Epidemic Sound
