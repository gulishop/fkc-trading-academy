# Day 3 — Facebook Reels aur Video Content: Engagement Badhaane ka Powerful Weapon

**Concept:**
Facebook Reels aur video content aaj kal sabse engaging content formats hain. Short-form videos (15-60 seconds) zyada reach aur interaction dete hain kyun ke users ko quick entertainment ya value milti hai. Reels banane ka fayda yeh hai ke algorithm unhe organic reach mein boost deta hai, aur agar content engaging ho to page par traffic bhi badhta hai.

**Example:**
Ek fitness page ka example lete hain. Unhone ek 30-second ka Reel banaya:
1. 0:00-0:05: "Aaj main batata hoon 5-minute workout jo ghar par hi ho sakti hai!"
2. 0:05-0:20: Quick workout steps (fast forward ya split-screen mein)
3. 0:20-0:30: CTA: "Agar aapko yeh workout pasand aaye to like karein, comment mein apna feedback dijiye!"

**Practice:**
Apne phone se ek 15-30 second ka Reel shoot karein. Topic apne page ke niche se choose karein (e.g., tutorial, behind-the-scenes, customer testimonial). Editing ke liye CapCut ya InShot use karein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek "3-Day Reels Challenge" shuru karein:
- Day 1: Ek educational Reel (e.g., "5 tips for [apne niche ka topic]")
- Day 2: Ek trending audio par funny/relatable content
- Day 3: Customer ya follower ka testimonial share karein

**Answer Key:**
trending audio, quick tips, CTA, engagement boost
