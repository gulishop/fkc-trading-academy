# Day 4 — Facebook Groups Se Traffic Lekar Apne Page Ko Grow Karna

**Concept:**
Facebook Groups aise communities hain jahan log specific topics par discuss karte hain. Agar aap apne page ka link share karte hain, toh targeted traffic mil sakta hai. Lekin **spam** na karein—groups ke rules follow karna zaroori hai. Engagement aur value provide karne se hi traffic aata hai.

**Example:**
1. Apne niche se related 3-5 active groups dhundhein (search karke).
2. Group ke rules check karein—kya links share karne diye hain?
3. Agar allowed hai, toh **valuable content** share karein (e.g., "Maine yeh trick use ki aur 1000+ followers badhe—dekhein kaise!" + apne page ka link).
4. Agar links share karne par pabandi hai, toh **profile se link add karein** ya group ke admin se poochhein.

**Practice:**
1. Apne niche ke 1 Facebook Group mein jayein.
2. 5 minutes uske posts par padhein—dekhein log kya pooch rahe hain.
3. **Ek valuable comment/post** karein (e.g., "Maine yeh problem solve ki thi, yeh solution try karein!" + apne page ka link agar allowed ho).

**Mini Project:**
Ek **Facebook Group Engagement Plan** banaein:
- 3 groups select karein.
- Har group mein 1 post ya comment karein (1 week ke liye).
- Track karein ki kitne visitors aaye (Facebook Insights se).

**Answer Key:**
Group rules follow karein, valuable content, 3-5 groups, 1 week plan
