# Day 21 — **Mobile Charging Port Repair: Loose ya Broken Port ka Fix**

**Concept:**
Mobile charging port repair ek common aur zaruri skill hai jo har repair shop ke liye essential hai. Agar port loose ho ya broken ho to charging aur data transfer mein dikkat hoti hai. Isme port ko replace karna ya solder karna sikhaya jata hai.

**Example:**
1. Port loose ho to PCB se solder ko reflow karo ya port ko naya replace karo.
2. Broken port ko nikalne ke liye hot air gun se solder melt karo, phir naya port lagao.

**Practice:**
Apne old/broken mobile ka charging port check karo. Agar loose hai to soldering iron se reflow karo, warna port replace karne ka practice karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek broken charging port wale mobile ko repair karke charging aur data transfer test karo.

**Answer Key:**
port loose, solder reflow, replace, test charging
