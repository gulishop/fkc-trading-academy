# Day 6 — **BGA IC Reballing: Chip Repair Ka Advanced Step**

**Concept:**
BGA (Ball Grid Array) ICs mobile motherboard ke sensitive chips hote hain jo solder balls ke zariye chip aur board se connect hote hain. Reballing ka matlab hai chip ke neeche ke solder balls ko nikal kar naye solder balls lagana taaki chip dobara properly chipke. Agar chip loose ho ya short circuit ho, reballing se repair hoti hai.

**Example:**
1. Chip ko hot air gun se 150-200°C par heat karo taaki solder balls soft ho jayein.
2. Chip ko gently uthao aur board se clean karo.
3. New solder balls (0.4mm-0.6mm diameter) lagao aur chip ko dobara chipka do.
4. Hot air gun se 220-250°C par heat karo taaki solder balls melt ho kar chip aur board se chipk jayein.

**Practice:**
Apne pasand ke kisi broken BGA chip (jaise CPU ya PMIC) ko uthao aur board se clean karo. Ab uske neeche ke solder pads ko isopropyl alcohol se clean karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek old/damaged BGA chip (jaise RAM ya Flash IC) ko reball karke dobara chipkao. Use a test circuit ya phone mein check karo ki repair successful hai ya nahi.

**Answer Key:**
loose chip, solder balls remove, new balls apply, hot air gun reflow
