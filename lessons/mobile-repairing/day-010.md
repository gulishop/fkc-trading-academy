# Day 10 — **Motherboard Power Delivery Faults: Diagnosis aur Repair**

**Concept:**
Motherboard ki power delivery system (VRM circuits, capacitors, MOSFETs) mein faults hone se phone chalta hi nahi ya bar-bar restart hota hai. Isme voltage fluctuations, short circuits, ya damaged components ka pata lagana aur repair karna seekhna hai. VRM (Voltage Regulator Module) aur PMIC (Power Management IC) ko test karna bhi aata hona chahiye.

**Example:**
*Real case:* Samsung Galaxy S8 chalta nahi, charger lagane par bhi "0%" show hota hai. Multimeter se check karne par charger IC ke 5V output par voltage nahi aa raha. Investigation se pata chalta hai ki PMIC (S2MP15) ka VCC pin short hai. Hot air gun se PMIC ko remove karke PCB ko clean karne ke baad naya PMIC solder karne se phone theek ho gaya.

**Practice:**
Apne pas mein koi dead phone lein (ya kharab motherboard). Multimeter use karke:
1. Charger IC ke input/output pins par voltage check karen (charger lagane par).
2. Agar 0V hai to trace karen ki kahan short hai (use continuity mode).
3. Short ki wajah se damaged capacitors ya MOSFETs ko identify karen.

**Mini Project:**
Ek kharab phone ka motherboard lein jisme power issue ho. Steps follow karke:
- Charger IC ke pas ke capacitors check karen (bulging ya leaked).
- MOSFETs aur VRM circuits par voltage test karen.
- Agar short milta hai to component replace karen aur phone test karen.
- Result document karen (photos + notes).

**Answer Key:**
VRM short, PMIC damaged, capacitors bulging, MOSFET test, 5V output
