# Day 29 — **Mobile Motherboard IC Replacement: Dead Boot & Power IC Fix**

**Concept:**
Mobile motherboard par **Boot IC** (CPU ko start karne wali chip) aur **Power IC** (battery voltage ko regulate karne wali chip) ka kharab hona sabse common hardware faults hain. Agar phone chalta nahi, charging nahi hoti, ya dead rehta hai, toh yeh ICs hi sabse pehle check karni chahiye. Replacement ke liye **hot air gun**, **IC extractor**, **soldering iron**, aur **flux** ki zaroorat hoti hai.

**Example:**
1. **Symptoms:** Samsung Galaxy S7 chalta nahi, charging nahi hoti, screen black rehti hai.
2. **Diagnosis:** Multimeter se check karne par Power IC par voltage nahi aa rahi.
3. **Repair Steps:**
   - Back cover remove karke motherboard nikal lo.
   - Hot air gun se **Power IC** (PMIC) ko 300°C par heat karke remove karo.
   - Naye IC ko flux lagake soldering iron se carefully place karo.
   - Phone ko test karo—agar chalta hai toh repair successful.

**Practice:**
Apne ek kharab phone ka motherboard lekar **Power IC** ki location identify karo (Google par "motherboard diagram" search karke). IC ko remove karne ka practice karo (agar spare part available ho).

**Mini Project:**
Ek **dead iPhone 6/7** ka motherboard lekar **Power IC** replace karke phone ko chalao. Agar successful ho toh video record karke class group mein share karo.

**Answer Key:**
Power IC remove, flux apply, soldering, test
