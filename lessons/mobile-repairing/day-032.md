# Day 32 — **Mobile Speaker & Microphone Repair: Crackling, No Sound & Distortion Fixes**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum mobile ke speaker aur microphone repair karna seekhenge. Inke issues—jaise crackling sound, no audio, distorted voice ya mic not working—ko diagnose karne ke liye hardware aur software donon aspects check karne hote hain. Speaker ki wires, solder joints, aur ICs check karne ke saath-saath microphone ki sensitivity aur noise cancellation bhi test karni hoti hai.

**Example:**
Ek Samsung phone ka speaker crackling sun raha hai. Step-by-step:
1. Phone ko off karke back cover hatao.
2. Speaker ko disconnect karke directly test karo (agar sound aata hai to speaker kharab hai).
3. Speaker ki wires check karo—kya koi loose ya broken hai?
4. Agar wires theek hain to speaker replace karo ya soldering fix karo.

**Practice:**
Apne ek old phone ka speaker disconnect karke, usko ek aur device (jaise amplifier ya second phone) se test karo. Agar sound nahi aata to speaker replace karo ya wires repair karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek mobile ka speaker repair karo jisme "no sound" issue hai. Steps:
- Back cover hatao, speaker disconnect karo.
- Speaker ko test karo (agar kharab hai to new speaker lagao).
- Wires solder karo agar loose hain.
- Phone ko reassemble karke test karo.

**Answer Key:**
Speaker disconnect test, wires check, soldering fix, replacement
