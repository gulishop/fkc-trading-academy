# Day 15 — **Speaker & Earpiece Repair: Broken Audio ya Distorted Sound ka Fix**

**Concept:**
Mobile ke speaker ya earpiece mein distorted sound, no sound ya low volume hona common issue hai. Ye hardware (loose connection, damaged speaker) ya software (muted settings, driver issue) ki wajah se ho sakta hai. Aaj hum hardware-level troubleshooting aur repair par focus karenge.

**Example:**
1. **Diagnosis:** Phone ko speaker mode mein check karo (call ya music). Agar sound nahi aa raha ya distorted hai, to speaker ko inspect karo.
2. **Step-by-Step Fix:**
   - Back cover hatao aur speaker ke connections check karo (loose ya corroded wires?).
   - Speaker ko gently remove karo (agar possible ho) aur new speaker install karo.
   - Agar speaker damaged hai, to replace karo (spare parts ki list Day 8 mein di thi).

**Practice:**
Apne ek old phone ke speaker ko inspect karo—agar sound distorted hai ya nahi aa raha, to connections check karo aur agar possible ho to speaker ko remove karo (agar nahi aata to repair shop se help lo).

**Mini Project:**
Ek broken speaker wale phone ko repair karo (agar available ho) ya ek spare speaker install karo aur test karo ki sound clear aa raha hai ya nahi.

**Answer Key:**
loose connection, corroded wires, damaged speaker, speaker replacement
