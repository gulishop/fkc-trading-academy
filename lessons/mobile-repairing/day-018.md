# Day 18 — **Mobile Repair Shop ka Layout: Practical Space Planning**

**Concept:**
Ek mobile repair shop ka layout efficient hona chahiye taake tools, spare parts aur workflow smooth ho. Space ko zones mein divide karo: diagnosis area, repair station, charging/soldering spot, aur customer interaction space. Ventilation, lighting aur safety bhi zaroori hai—khaskar soldering aur battery repair ke liye.

**Example:**
Ek 10x12 feet ke shop ka layout:
1. **Front Counter (3x2 ft):** Customer se baat karne aur diagnosis karne ke liye.
2. **Repair Bench (4x2 ft):** Tools aur spare parts ke liye drawer ke saath.
3. **Soldering Station (2x1 ft):** Fireproof mat ke saath, extractor fan laga hua.
4. **Charging Area (2x1 ft):** Batteries charge karne ke liye safe shelf.
5. **Storage (3x2 ft):** Spare parts aur tools ke liye locked cabinet.

**Practice:**
Apne ghar ya garage mein ek chhota sa layout sketch karo—har zone ka size aur use mention karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **3x4 feet ka cardboard model** banana hai jismein apne shop ka 3D layout banaya jaaye. Har zone ke liye color-coded labels lagao (e.g., red = soldering, blue = diagnosis).

**Answer Key:**
diagnosis area, repair bench, soldering station, storage, ventilation
