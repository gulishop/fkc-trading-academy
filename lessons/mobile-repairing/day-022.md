# Day 22 — **Mobile Speaker & Microphone Repair: Sound Issues ka Fix**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum mobile ke speaker aur microphone ki common problems aur unke repair techniques seekhenge. Speaker mein sound nahi aana, mic ka kaam na karna, ya phir distorted audio jaise issues kaise diagnose aur fix karenge — yeh sab practical steps ke saath samjhaenge.

**Example:**
1. **Speaker ka sound nahi aana:**
   - Phone ko silent mode se normal mode par le aao.
   - Volume check karo (kya koi headphone lagay hue to nahi hai?).
   - Speaker grill ko clean karo (dust ya debris se block ho sakta hai).
   - Agar sound nahi aata, to speaker ko replace karo ya soldering se check karo.

2. **Microphone ka kaam na karna:**
   - Call karke test karo (agar caller ko aapki awaaz nahi sunai deti).
   - Mic grill ko clean karo (earpiece ke pas ka chota sa hole).
   - Software se check karo (settings > accessibility > hearing aids > test).

**Practice:**
Apne ek old phone ka speaker/mic test karo:
- Speaker: Koi audio file play karo aur check karo kya sound aa raha hai.
- Mic: Voice recorder open karo, "test" bol kar record karo, phir sun kar verify karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek broken speaker/mic wala phone lekar:
1. Speaker grill ko clean karo.
2. Agar sound nahi aata, to speaker ko remove karke naya install karo (agar available ho).
3. Mic test karo aur report likho ki problem solve hui ya nahi.

**Answer Key:**
speaker grill clean, volume check, speaker replace, mic grill clean
