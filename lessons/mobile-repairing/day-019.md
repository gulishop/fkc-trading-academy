# Day 19 — **Mobile Repair Shop ka Profit Margin: Kaise Set Kare aur Business Ko Grow Kare**

**Concept:**
Har repair shop ka profit margin depend karta hai: **cost of parts + labor + overhead expenses (rent, bills, etc.)** par. Beginner ko pehle **cost calculation** samajhna chahiye—parts ka rate, time lagana, aur market demand. Phir **pricing strategy** decide karni hai (fixed price vs. per-hour). Customer dealing mein **transparency** aur **trust** build karna zaroori hai—overcharging ya hidden charges se business kharab hoti hai.

**Example:**
Ek customer ka **screen replacement** ka case: Part cost = ₹2000, labor = 30 mins (₹150/hour rate), overhead = ₹50. Total cost = ₹2000 + ₹75 + ₹50 = **₹2125**. Agar market mein ₹2500 charge hota hai, toh **₹375 profit** per repair. Agar customer ko pata chal gaya toh next time wo doosre shop par chale jayega—isliye **fair pricing** zaroori hai.

**Practice:**
Apne local market mein **3-4 repair shops** ke screen replacement ke rates note karo. Apne cost calculation ke hisaab se **apna price range** decide karo (e.g., ₹2100–₹2300). Compare karo ki kya tumhara price **competitive** hai ya **premium** (better quality claim karke).

**Mini Project:**
**"Pricing Strategy Report"** banao:
1. 5 common repairs (screen, battery, charging port, speaker, fingerprint) ke **cost breakdown** likho.
2. Market rates ke comparison ke baad **apna suggested price list** banao.
3. Ek **customer-friendly pricing policy** likho (e.g., "No hidden charges, parts ki receipt zaroor milegi").

**Answer Key:**
cost breakdown, market comparison, fair pricing, customer trust
