# Day 27 — **Mobile Water Damage Repair: Step-by-Step Recovery Guide**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke mobile mein water damage hone par usay kaise repair karna hai. Sabse pehle phone ko off kar ke battery nikal dein, phir internal components ko dry karne ke liye silica gel ya rice ka use karen. Circuit board ko alcohol se clean karne ke baad proper drying ke baad test karen.

**Example:**
Ek Samsung Galaxy S20 mein water damage hua. Phone ko off kiya, battery nikali, aur 24 ghante silica gel box mein rakha. Phir circuit board ko isopropyl alcohol (90%+) se brush kiya, contacts ko dry kiya, aur phone ko 12 ghante aur dry kiya. Baad mein phone chala aur charging port bhi theek ho gaya.

**Practice:**
Apne phone ya kisi old phone ke circuit board ko isopropyl alcohol (90%+) se gently clean kar ke dry box mein 24 ghante rakhein. Phir phone ko test karen.

**Mini Project:**
Ek old phone ko water damage kar ke usay repair karne ka full process video banaen (agar possible ho to).

**Answer Key:**
off karo, battery nikaal, silica gel, alcohol clean
