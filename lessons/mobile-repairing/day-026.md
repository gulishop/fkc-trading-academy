# Day 26 — **Mobile Circuit Tracing: Fault Finding Without Schematic**

**Concept:**
Har repair ke liye circuit tracing zaroori hai—agar schematic na ho to bhi. Aaj seekhoge ki multimeter aur visual inspection se kaise traces karte hain, short circuits, open circuits aur faulty components identify karte hain. Isse repair time aur cost dono kam hote hain.

**Example:**
Agar charging na ho rahi ho, to:
1. Battery connector se charging IC tak trace karo (visual check: solder joints, burns).
2. Multimeter se continuity test karo (0 ohm = short, ∞ = open).
3. USB port se charging IC tak voltage check karo (5V expected).

**Practice:**
Apne ek kharab mobile ka charging circuit trace karo—battery connector se charging IC tak ke traces check karo aur koi short/open ya damaged component identify karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek kharab mobile ka charging circuit diagram manually sketch karo (sirf important points: battery connector, charging IC, USB port, resistors/capacitors).

**Answer Key:**
battery connector, charging IC, continuity test, 5V voltage
