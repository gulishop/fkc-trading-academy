# Day 20 — **Mobile Repair Shop ke Social Media Presence ka Setup**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke apne mobile repair shop ka social media (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp Business) kaise set kare taake customers attract hoin. Social media se aap apne repair services, spare parts, aur deals ko promote kar sakte hain. Regular posts, customer reviews, aur before/after photos se trust build hota hai.

**Example:**
1. **Profile Setup:** Facebook Page banayein, shop ka naam, address, contact number, aur services list karein.
2. **Content Strategy:** Har 2-3 din mein ek post karein—jaise "Screen Replacement ki video," "Battery health tips," ya "10% discount this week."
3. **Engagement:** Customers ke comments/queries ka reply dein, aur positive reviews share karein.

**Practice:**
Apne phone se apne shop ka ek Facebook Page ya Instagram Profile banayein aur uske liye 3 sample posts likhein (jaise repair ka video, spare parts ka photo, ya discount offer).

**Mini Project:**
Ek WhatsApp Business Account setup karein aur uske "Catalog" mein apne top 5 repair services aur spare parts ke photos upload karein.

**Answer Key:**
Facebook Page, Instagram Profile, 3 posts, WhatsApp Catalog
