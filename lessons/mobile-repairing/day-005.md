# Day 5 — **Motherboard Basics: Components aur Circuitry Samajhna**

**Concept:**
Motherboard mobile ka "brain" hoti hai jisme sabhi major components (CPU, RAM, charging circuit, etc.) connected hote hain. Is lesson mein hum motherboard ke key parts aur unke functions samjhenge taake future repairs (jaise short circuit, charging issues) ko better diagnose kar saken.

**Example:**
1. **CPU (Processor):** Mobile ki processing power ka source. Agar phone slow ho raha hai, CPU ko check karna hoga.
2. **Power IC:** Charging aur battery management handle karti hai. Agar charging port ka issue hai, Power IC ko test karna zaroori hai.
3. **Capacitors:** Small cylindrical components jo voltage fluctuations ko control karte hain. Agar phone randomly restart ho raha hai, capacitors check karen.

**Practice:**
Apne ek old PCB (printed circuit board) ya kisi disassembled phone ki motherboard ko closely observe karo aur neeche diye components ko identify karne ki koshish karo:
- CPU (usually largest chip with gold contacts)
- Power IC (near charging port)
- Capacitors (small cylinders with colored bands)

**Mini Project:**
Ek simple circuit diagram (ya mobile motherboard ka diagram) lein aur uske 5 major components ko label karke batayein ke unka function kya hai.

**Answer Key:**
CPU, Power IC, Capacitors, RAM, Charging circuit
