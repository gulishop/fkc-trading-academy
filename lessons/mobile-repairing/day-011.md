# Day 11 — **Mobile Charging Port Repair: Common Issues aur Step-by-Step Fix**

**Concept:**
Mobile charging port mein dust, lint, ya damaged pins ki wajah se charging issues hote hain. Kabhi-kabhi port ke pins bend ho jate hain ya solder joints weak ho jate hain. Aaj hum port ko clean karne, pins ko straight karne, aur soldering se repair karne ka tareeqa seekhenge.

**Example:**
Agar koi phone charging nahi ho raha lekin charger work kar raha hai, to sabse pehle port ko torch se check karen. Agar dust ya lint jam hai, to isi needle se clean karen. Agar pins bent hain, to tweezers se carefully straight karen. Agar solder joints weak hain, to soldering iron se rework karen.

**Practice:**
Apne ek old phone ka charging port khol kar usko torch se check karen. Agar dust ya lint hai to clean karen. Agar pins bent hain to unko straight karen.

**Mini Project:**
Ek damaged charging port ko repair kar ke us phone ko charge karne ka test karen.

**Answer Key:**
clean port, straight pins, solder joints, test charge
