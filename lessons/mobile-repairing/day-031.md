# Day 31 — **Mobile Charging Port Repair: Cleaning, Replacement & Troubleshooting**

**Concept:**
Charging port repair mobile repairing ka sabse common aur zaruri skill hai. 90% users charging issues report karte hain—loose connection, dust blockage, ya broken pins ki wajah se. Aaj hum port cleaning, soldering, aur replacement ke basics seekhenge.

**Example:**
1. **Cleaning:** Toothbrush + isopropyl alcohol se port ke pins saaf karo.
2. **Inspection:** Microscope ya magnifying glass se check karo ke pins bent na hon.
3. **Replacement:** Agar port damaged hai, to naya port solder karo (hot air gun se 300°C par).

**Practice:**
Apne ek old phone ka charging port kholo, brush aur alcohol se saaf karo, aur check karo ke connection sahi ho raha hai ya nahi.

**Mini Project:**
Ek dead charging port wale phone ko repair karo (ya kisi friend se lekar practice karo). Video banao ya notes likho ke kaise kaam kiya.

**Answer Key:**
port cleaning, pins check, soldering, hot air gun
