# Day 17 — **Mobile Motherboard Faults: Common IC Issues aur Repair Techniques**

**Concept:**
Mobile motherboard par IC (Integrated Circuit) ki kharabiya sabse challenging hoti hain. Common faults mein charging IC, power IC, audio IC ya baseband IC shamil hain. Inko diagnose karne ke liye multimeter, DC power supply, aur schematic diagrams ki zaroorat hoti hai. Repair mein hot air gun, flux, aur soldering iron ka istemal hota hai.

**Example:**
Agar phone charge nahi ho raha aur charging IC ka voltage check karne par 0V aata hai, to:
1. IC ka power pin identify karo (schematic se).
2. DC power supply se 3.7V apply karo aur check karo ki kya IC voltage pass kar rahi hai.
3. Agar nahi, to IC ko replace karo ya reballing karo.

**Practice:**
Apne pasandeeda 2-3 mobile motherboards lein aur unke charging IC ya power IC ko identify karen. Multimeter se voltage check karen aur notes likhein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek faulty charging IC wali motherboard lein. Uske power pins par voltage check karen, IC ko desolder karen, aur new IC solder karen. Repair ke baad phone ko test karen.

**Answer Key:**
voltage check, IC replace, soldering, test
