# Day 17 — **"TikTok Par ‘Engagement Kaise Track Karein’ — Analytics Se Growth Kaise Badhayein"**

**Concept:**
TikTok par engagement bas likes/comments se hi nahi, balki **analytics** se bhi hota hai. Aaj seekhein ke kaise TikTok ke **Pro Account** ke analytics section se apne content ka performance check karna hai, konse videos zyada engage ho rahe hain, aur audience ke behavior ko samajh kar future content plan karna hai.

**Example:**
1. Apne TikTok profile par jayein, teeno lines (☰) par click karen.
2. **"Creator Tools"** > **"Analytics"** select karen.
3. **"Content"** tab mein dekhein ke **views, likes, shares, watch time** ka breakdown hai.
4. **"Audience"** tab se pata lagayein ke followers ki age, gender, aur active hours kya hain.

**Practice:**
Abhi apne TikTok Pro Account ke analytics check karen aur **top 3 videos** ke engagement metrics note karen (views, likes, shares, average watch time).

**Mini Project:**
Ek **1-minute ka short video** banayein jisme aap apne **top 3 engagement metrics** (jaise "Mere videos mein sabse zyada ‘shares’ wale video ne 50K views liye") ko screen-record karke dikhayein + ek **1-line suggestion** bhi add karen (jaise "Meri audience 8PM par active hoti hai, isliye main is time par post karunga").

**Answer Key:**
views, likes, shares, watch time
