# Day 4 — **TikTok Par "Engagement Hacks" — Comments, Duets & Polls Se Views Badhaye**

**Concept:**
TikTok par engagement sirf likes se nahi, comments, duets aur polls se bhi badhta hai. Algorithm un videos ko promote karta hai jo zyada interaction generate karte hain. Comments ka response dena, duets karna aur polls lagana viewers ko "involved" rakhta hai, jisse video viral hone ke chances badh jaate hain.

**Example:**
1. **Comments ka jawab:** Agar koi comment "Kya yeh real hai?" likhe, to reply mein "Haan bilkul! 👀" ya "Nahi, yeh fake hai 😂" likh kar conversation shuru karo.
2. **Duet ka istemal:** Apne video par "Try this trick!" bol kar viewers ko duets karne ke liye challenge do. Example: "Agar tum bhi yeh trick try karo to duets karke show karo!"
3. **Polls:** Video description mein "Aapko kya pasand hai?" likh kar do options de do (e.g., "Option 1: Fast editing, Option 2: Slow motion").

**Practice:**
Apne last video par **1 comment ka jawab** de aur **1 duet request** accept karo. Agar possible ho, to **1 poll** bhi add karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **30-second ka "Engagement Challenge" video** banaen jisme:
- Ek question poocho (e.g., "Tumhara favorite TikTok trend kya hai?")
- Duet karne ka challenge do
- Poll ke liye 2 options suggest karo

**Answer Key:**
comment response, duet interaction, poll engagement, conversation starter
