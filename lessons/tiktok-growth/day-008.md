# Day 8 — **TikTok Par "Monetization Ke Alag Raste" — Creator Fund Ke Baad Kya?**

**Concept:**
Creator Fund ke baad bhi TikTok par paise kamane ke aur bhi tareeke hain! Affiliate marketing, sponsorships, digital products, aur even freelancing bhi TikTok se jod sakte hain. Har method ke apne rules hain—jaise affiliate links ke liye disclosures zaruri hain, aur sponsorships ke liye FTC guidelines follow karni hain.

**Example:**
*Step-by-step affiliate marketing:*
1. Amazon Associates ya Daraz Affiliate join karo.
2. TikTok video mein product recommend karo (e.g., "Mera favorite phone charger—link in bio!").
3. Video description mein affiliate link add karo.
4. Agar koi us link se khareedta hai, to tumhe commission milegi.

**Practice:**
Apne niche ke 1 trending product ya service ko choose karo aur uske affiliate link ke saath ek 15-20 second ka video banao (hook: "Yeh product mere life ko badal diya—dekho kaise!").

**Mini Project:**
Ek "Monetization Plan" banao—3 tareeke likho jinse tum apne niche mein paise bana sakte ho (e.g., sponsorships, digital guides, merch). Har tareeke ke liye 1-2 line ka explanation bhi do.

**Answer Key:**
affiliate links, disclosures, FTC guidelines, commission
