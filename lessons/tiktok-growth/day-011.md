# Day 11 — **TikTok Par "Shadowban" Se Bachne Ke 5 Asaan Tarike**

**Concept:**
TikTok ka algorithm aapke content ko "shadowban" kar sakta hai agar aapke videos par spam, copyrighted content, ya community guidelines ke khilaaf cheezain hain. Shadowban ka matlab hai ke aapke videos ko kam reach milti hai, lekin aapko pata nahi chalta. Is se bachne ke liye content quality, hashtags, aur engagement par dhyaan dena zaroori hai.

**Example:**
Agar aap bar-bar same sound ya hashtag use kar rahe hain jo TikTok ne flag kar rakha hai, to aapka video shadowban ho sakta hai. Iske bajaye trending aur unique sounds ka istemal karein.

**Practice:**
Apne last 5 videos ke hashtags check karein. Kya koi repeat ya banned hashtag use hua hai? Agar haan, to unhe remove karke naye trending hashtags add karein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek naya video upload karein aur usme sirf 3-5 relevant hashtags use karein (banned ya spammy hashtags se bachein).

**Answer Key:**
unique hashtags, trending sounds, content quality, community guidelines
