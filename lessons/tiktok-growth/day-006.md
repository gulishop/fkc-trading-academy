# Day 6 — **TikTok Par "Collaborations" — Dusre Creators Se Kaise Grow Karein**

**Concept:**
Collaborations se TikTok par reach badhti hai kyunki algorithm do accounts ki audience ko milata hai. Agar aap kisi trending creator ya niche ke influential person ke sath video banate hain, toh aapki video unke followers ko bhi dikhayi deti hai. Isse engagement bhi badhta hai aur followers bhi increase hote hain.

**Example:**
1. **Duet/Stitch Collaboration:** Kisi popular creator ke video par duet karke unse direct interaction karein. Jaise agar koi dance trend chal raha hai, uske video par apna version add karke tag karein.
2. **Split-Screen Video:** Dono creators ek saath screen share karke content banayein. Jaise "POV" style ya "Before vs After" videos.
3. **Challenge Collaboration:** Kisi trending challenge ko lekar ek saath video banayein aur doosre creator ko mention karein.

**Practice:**
Apne niche ke 3 creators ko shortlist karein jinke followers aapke jaisay hi hain ya unse zyada hain. Unke videos par comments karke ya duet karke unse interact karein. Agar unse video banane ka mauka mile toh poochhein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **split-screen collaboration video** banayein aur doosre creator ko tag karein. Video ko niche ke relevant hashtags ke saath post karein.

**Answer Key:**
duet, stitch, split-screen, challenge
