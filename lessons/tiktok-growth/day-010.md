# Day 10 — **TikTok Par "Cross-Promotion" — Apne Content Ko Doosre Platforms Par Faila Kar Grow Karein**

**Concept:**
TikTok ke alawa bhi apne content ko Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook aur Twitter par share karke followers badha sakte hain. Har platform ka algorithm alag hota hai, isliye har jagah apne content ko adapt karna zaroori hai. Cross-promotion se aapka reach badhta hai aur ek platform par fail hone par bhi doosre par traffic milta hai.

**Example:**
Ek creator ne TikTok par fitness tips ke videos banaye. Usne:
1. TikTok video ko Instagram Reels par bhi upload kiya (caption change ki: "Full guide in comments!").
2. YouTube Shorts par bhi same video daala lekin thoda modify kiya (extra text overlay add kiya).
3. Twitter par video ka teaser clip share kiya ("Full video on TikTok!").
Nateeja: TikTok followers ke alawa Instagram par 5K+ aur YouTube par 2K+ subscribers mile.

**Practice:**
Aaj ke 3 videos ko doosre 3 platforms par bhi upload karo. Har jagah:
- Caption/description alag rakhna (platform ke hisab se).
- Hashtags adjust karna (TikTok ke trending hashtags Instagram ke liye zyada relevant nahi hote).
- Thumbnail ya cover photo alag chune (har platform ka style alag hota hai).

**Mini Project:**
Ek "Cross-Promotion Tracker" spreadsheet banao jisme:
- Har video ka link (TikTok, Reels, Shorts).
- Har platform par kitne views/likes/comments aaye.
- Kya modification ki thi (caption, hashtags, etc.).
Is se aapko pata chalega ki kaun sa platform aapke liye sabse behtar hai.

**Answer Key:**
TikTok, Reels, Shorts, traffic, followers
