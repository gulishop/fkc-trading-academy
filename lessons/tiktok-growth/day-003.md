# Day 3 — **TikTok Par "Hook" Kaise Banayein (Jo 3 Seconds Mein Hi Viewer Ko Roke)**

**Concept:**
TikTok ka algorithm sirf un videos ko promote karta hai jo pehle 3 seconds mein hi viewer ko "hook" kar le. Agar aapka intro boring hai, to user scroll kar jayega—chahe video kitni bhi achi ho. Ek strong hook banane ke liye **emotion (shock, curiosity, FOMO), problem/solution ya unexpected twist** use karo. Example: "Maine 10,000 rupees ka loss isliye kiya kyun..." ya "Yeh 1 cheez aapke TikTok growth ko 10x kar degi!"

**Example:**
Ek fitness creator ka hook: *"Agar aap yeh 1 exercise roz karoge, to 1 mahine mein 5 kg fat burn ho jayega—lekin yeh video last 30 seconds tak dekhna!"* (Phir video mein exercise batata hai). Hook ne curiosity pakad li.

**Practice:**
Apne phone se **3 different hooks** likho (emotion, problem ya twist based). Har hook ko **3-5 words** mein summarize karo. Ab apne niche se kisi 1 topic par **10-second ka draft video** banaye aur sirf hook par focus karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **fake "breaking news" style hook** banaye aur uske liye ek 15-second ka script likhe (e.g., *"Breaking: TikTok ne apna algorithm change kar diya—ab yeh 3 cheezen karne se aapka account ban jayega BAN!"*). Video record karne ki zaroorat nahi, bas script taiyar karo.

**Answer Key:**
emotion + curiosity + FOMO
