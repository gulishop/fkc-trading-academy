# Day 16 — **"TikTok Par 'AI Tools' Se Content Creation Ko 10X Fast Banayein"**

**Concept:**
AI tools ab TikTok creators ke liye game-changer ban gaye hain. Caption likhne se lekar video editing tak, AI har step par help kar sakti hai. Aaj hum seekhenge ki kaise AI se apna content creation process fast, easy aur professional banaya ja sakta hai—bina coding ya advanced skills ke.

**Example:**
1. **Caption Generation:** AI tools jaise *Copy.ai* ya *Jasper* ko use karke apne video ke topic par 5-10 engaging captions generate karo.
2. **Video Editing:** *CapCut* ya *Runway ML* mein AI-powered "auto-captioning" feature se subtitles add karo—bas 2 clicks mein.
3. **Trend Prediction:** *TikTok Creative Center* ki AI insights se samjho ki next week kaun sa trend viral ho sakta hai.

**Practice:**
Apne agle video ke liye 3 AI tools (caption, editing, trend research) ka istemal karke ek draft taiyar karo. Har step ke screenshots save karlo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **AI-generated TikTok script** banayein—jismein:
- AI se 3 caption options
- AI editing se 10-second clip ka auto-subtitle
- AI trend prediction se 1 trending sound ka suggestion
Sabko ek PDF ya Canva poster mein arrange karo.

**Answer Key:**
AI captions, auto-subtitles, trend prediction, CapCut/Runway ML
