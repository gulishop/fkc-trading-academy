# Day 12 — **TikTok Par "Evergreen Content" Banane Ke 3 Golden Rules**

**Concept:**
Evergreen content wo videos hain jo har samay relevant rehte hain—chahe season ho ya trend badal jaye. Isse aapka account long-term grow karta hai kyunki yeh videos bar-bar views aur engagement laate rehte hain. Key hai: timeless topics, universal appeal, aur high-quality production.

**Example:**
Ek fitness creator ne "5 Minute Morning Stretch" series banayi—har roz 100K+ views milte hain chahe summer ho ya winter. Ismein na toh trend change hota hai, na hi audience shift hoti hai.

**Practice:**
Apne niche mein 3 evergreen topics identify karo jo aapke audience ko har samay chahiye honge (e.g., "TikTok growth tips for beginners").

**Mini Project:**
Ek 15-second evergreen video shoot karo—topic: "3 Apps Jo Har Student Ko Pata Hona Chahiye" (agar aapka niche education hai).

**Answer Key:**
timeless topics, universal appeal, high-quality
