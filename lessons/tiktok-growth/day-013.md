# Day 13 — **"TikTok Par 'Duet & Stitch' Se Engagement 10X Kaise Badhaye"**

**Concept:**
Duet aur Stitch TikTok ke powerful features hain jo aapke content ko doosre creators ke saath jod kar viral banane mein madad karte hain. Duet se aap kisi ke video par apna reaction ya response add kar sakte hain, jabki Stitch se aap us video ka hissa lekar apna context add kar sakte hain. Yeh engagement badhane ke saath-saath aapki reach bhi increase karte hain.

**Example:**
1. **Duet ka istemal:** Koi viral dance video dekhein, uspar apna reaction record karein, aur niche "Duet this" ka option use karein.
2. **Stitch ka istemal:** Kisi tutorial video ka important hissa lekar uske baare mein apna opinion ya additional tip add karein.

**Practice:**
Apne niche se related ek viral video ko Stitch karein aur uspar apna unique perspective add karein. Video upload karne se pehle yeh zaroor check karein ki original creator ne Stitch/Duet allow kiya hai.

**Mini Project:**
Ek "Duet Challenge" create karein jismein aap 5-10 creators ke videos par Duet banayein aur har Duet ke caption mein relevant hashtag (#DuetChallenge) add karein. Har Duet ke baad creator ko tag karein.

**Answer Key:**
original creator ko tag karna, relevant hashtag, unique perspective, engagement increase
