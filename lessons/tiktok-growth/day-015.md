# Day 15 — **"TikTok Par ‘For You Page (FYP) Ko Hack Karne’ Ke 3 Advanced Tricks"**

**Concept:**
FYP algorithm ko samajhna aur usko apne benefit ke liye use karna TikTok growth ka ultimate hack hai. Aaj hum 3 advanced tricks seekhenge jo aapki videos ko algorithm ki nazar mein ‘high-value’ banayenge: **1) Watch Time Optimization** (video ke pehle 3 seconds ka use), **2) Keyword Placement** (caption aur hashtags mein smart positioning), aur **3) ‘Sticky Ending’** (video ke last 5 seconds mein engagement badhane ka tareeka).

**Example:**
Ek 15-second video ke liye:
1. **Pehlay 3 seconds:** "Aaj main tumhe yeh *secret* bataunga jo 10x views la sakta hai…" (curiosity hook).
2. **Caption:** "🔥 Real estate ka yeh *nuskha* 2024 mein sabse zyada kamaya 💰 #RealEstateTips #SideHustle" (keywords: "nuskha", "2024", "SideHustle").
3. **Last 5 seconds:** "Agar yeh video pasand aaye to *like* karke comment mein ‘GOLD’ likh dena…" (CTA + engagement trigger).

**Practice:**
Apne next video ke liye:
- Pehle 3 seconds mein ek *question* ya *bold statement* use karo.
- Caption mein 1-2 *high-search* keywords add karo (e.g., "2024", "free", "trend").
- Video ke last 5 seconds mein ek *simple CTA* do (e.g., "Dobara dekhna mat bhulna!").

**Mini Project:**
Ek 20-second video banayein jisme:
1. Pehle 3 seconds: "Yeh *1 trick* tumhari life badal sakta hai…" (hook).
2. Caption: "🚀 #LifeHack #2024 ka yeh *must-try* tip 👇" (hashtags + keyword).
3. Last 5 seconds: "Agar pasand aaye to *share* zarour karna!" (CTA).

**Answer Key:**
watch_time_hook, keyword_placement, sticky_ending
