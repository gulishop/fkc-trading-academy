# Day 14 — **TikTok Par "Green Screen" Effect Se Content Ko Kaise Professional Banayein**

**Concept:**
Green Screen effect se aap apne background ko badal sakte hain ya kisi bhi image/video ko apne video ka hissa bana sakte hain. Yeh TikTok par content ko unique aur engaging banane ka powerful tool hai. Isse aap apni creativity badha sakte hain—jaise virtual backgrounds, tutorials, ya funny mashups.

**Example:**
1. **App se kaise karein:**
   - TikTok app khol kar "+" (create) par click karein.
   - Recording mode select karein (15s ya 60s).
   - "Effects" (star icon) par tap karein > "Green Screen" category select karein.
   - Background image/video upload karein ya TikTok ke existing options se choose karein.
   - Record karte waqt apne aap ko green screen ke peeche rakhein (ya hata dein agar already hua hai).

2. **Editing ke baad:**
   - Video trim karein, text add karein, ya trending sound lagayein.

**Practice:**
Apne phone se ek 15-second ka video record karein jisme aap Green Screen effect use karke apne background ko kisi trending meme ya product image se replace karein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek "Before & After" video banayein—pehle normal video, phir Green Screen effect lagakar same content ko professional look den.

**Answer Key:**
Green Screen, background replace, effects, creative content
