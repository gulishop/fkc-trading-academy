# Day 11 — **Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) Mein Trading Kaise Shuru Hota Hai?**

**Concept:**
PSX (Pakistan Stock Exchange) ek regulated marketplace hai jahan investors shares khareedte aur bechte hain. Yeh ek auction system par chalta hai jahan buyers aur sellers ke beech orders match hote hain. Har company ke shares ki price demand aur supply par depend karti hai. PSX mein trading ke liye aapko ek brokerage account ki zaroorat hoti hai jo aapki behalf par orders place karta hai.

**Example:**
Imagine aap XYZ Company ke 100 shares khareedna chahte hain. Aapka broker aapki behalf par PSX par order place karega. Agar koi aur investor 100 shares bech raha hai usi price par, to aapka order fulfill ho jayega. Agar nahi, to aapko higher price par bechne wale ya lower price par khareedne wale ka wait karna hoga.

**Practice:**
Apne mobile ya laptop par PSX ki official website ya kisi brokerage app (jaise JS Bank, UBL Funds, ya AKD) ko open karein. Dekhein ki "Buy" aur "Sell" buttons kaise dikhte hain aur ek dummy order place karne ki koshish karein (real transaction na karein).

**Mini Project:**
Ek simple Excel sheet banayein jismein aap 5 random PSX companies (jaise OGDC, MCB, PTCL, etc.) ke last 7 days ke closing prices note karein. Har din ke price mein kya change aaya? Kya aapko koi pattern nazar aata hai?

**Answer Key:**
Brokerage account, Auction system, Demand-supply, Order fulfillment
