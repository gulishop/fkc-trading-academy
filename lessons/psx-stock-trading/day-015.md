# Day 15 — **Candlestick Patterns: Reading Price Movements Ka Tajurba**

**Concept:**
Candlestick patterns PSX par trading karne ke liye sabse zyada use hone wale tools hain. Har "candle" 4 cheezon ko show karti hai: Open, High, Low, aur Close price. In patterns se market ki mood (bullish/bearish) aur future price movement ka pata chalta hai. Jaise "Hammer" ya "Doji" patterns se reversal ka signal milta hai.

**Example:**
PSX par KSE-100 index ke chart par dekhein:
1. Agar ek candle ka "lower shadow" (wala part) uski body se 2x zyada lamba hai aur close price open se upar hai → **Hammer pattern** (bullish reversal ka signal).
2. Agar do candles hain: pehli lambi green candle, doosri chhoti red candle jo pehli ke "body" ke andar close ho → **Bearish Harami** (downtrend ka signal).

**Practice:**
Apne PSX trading app (jaise JS Bank, Arif Habib ya AKD) par kisi stock (e.g., ENGRO, LUCK) ka 1-day timeframe chart kholiye. 5 candlestick patterns identify kijiye (kuch common: Hammer, Shooting Star, Doji, Bullish Engulfing, Bearish Engulfing).
