# Day 14 — **Market Psychology: Fear, Greed, aur Trading Decisions**

**Concept:**
Market psychology samajhna trading ka hidden power hai. Fear aur greed investors ke decisions ko kaise distort karte hain—jaise FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out) ya panic selling. Fear jab market girta hai to investors jaldi se shares bech dete hain, jabke greed jab market upar chalta hai to log zyada risk lete hain. In emotions ko recognize karke aap better trading decisions le sakte hain.

**Example:**
Imagine PSX mein 2023 ke election results ke baad market ne 10% gain kiya. Aapke friend ne kaha: *"Yeh toh sirf shuru hai, ab toh market 50% upar jayega!"* Yeh greed ka example hai—log unrealistic expectations rakhte hain. Vise versa, jab 2022 mein global recession ka fear tha, logon ne shares bech diye jabke companies strong the. Yeh fear-driven decision tha.

**Practice:**
Aaj ke PSX market ko check karein (yaad rakhein, PSX ka closing time 3:30 PM hai). Kya aapko koi news ya event nazar aaya jo market ko move kar raha hai? Note karein ki kya yeh fear, greed, ya rational decision se driven lag raha hai.
