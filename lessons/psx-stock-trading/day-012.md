# Day 12 — **Margin Trading: Leverage Se Zyada Profit (Aur Risk) Kaise Manage Karen**

**Concept:**
Margin trading mein aap broker se paise uthate hain (leverage) aur uske zariye zyada shares khareed sakte hain. Isse profit toh badhta hai, lekin loss bhi badh jata hai—agar market aapke khilaaf chalta hai. Isliye risk management zaroori hai. PSX mein margin trading ke liye aapka account "Margin Account" hona chahiye aur broker ki margin policy pata honi chahiye.

**Example:**
Maana aapka account balance **₨100,000** hai aur broker **2:1 leverage** offer karta hai. Aap **₨50,000** ke shares khareedte hain, toh broker **₨50,000** aur dega. Agar share **10% up** jata hai, aapka profit **₨10,000** hoga (₨50,000 ka 10%), lekin agar **10% down** jata hai, aapka loss bhi **₨10,000** hoga—aur aapka original **₨100,000** bhi **₨90,000** reh jayega.

**Practice:**
Apne broker ki website ya app par jaakar dekhein:
1. Kya wo margin trading allow karta hai?
2. Uske margin rates kya hain (e.g., 2:1, 3:1)?
3. Margin call ka kya matlab hai aur kaise avoid kar sakte hain?

**Mini Project:**
Ek hypothetical scenario banao:
- Aapka account balance: **₨200,000**
- Broker ka leverage: **3:1**
- Aap **₨150,000** ke shares khareedte hain.
- Share **5% up** jata hai: Aapka profit kitna hoga?
- Share **5% down** jata hai: Aapka loss kitna hoga aur aapka remaining balance?

**Answer Key:**
leverage ratio check karo, margin call avoid karo, hypothetical profit/loss calculate karo
