# Day 10 — **Portfolio Performance Metrics: Apne Investments Ki Health Kaise Check Karein?**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke portfolio ki performance ko kaise measure karna hai. Yeh metrics batayein gi ke aapka investment plan kitna successful hai — jaise ROI (Return on Investment), CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate), aur Sharpe Ratio. Har metric ka matlab samajh kar, aap apne decisions ko better optimize kar sakte hain.

**Example:**
Maana aapne 2020 mein 100,000 rupees invest kiye the, aur ab 2024 mein yeh 150,000 ho gaye hain. To:
- **ROI** = (150,000 - 100,000) / 100,000 × 100 = **50%**
- **CAGR** = (150,000/100,000)^(1/4) - 1 ≈ **10.67% per year**
Agar aapka portfolio 10% CAGR de raha hai, lekin market average 12% hai, to aapko apne strategy par again review karna chahiye.

**Practice:**
Apne PSX portfolio (ya demo account) se kisi ek stock ya mutual fund ka 1-year, 3-year, aur 5-year ka ROI aur CAGR calculate karein. Agar data available na ho, to PSX ki official website ya broker app se historical prices nikalein.
