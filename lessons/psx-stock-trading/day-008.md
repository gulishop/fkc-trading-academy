# Day 8 — **Swing Trading: Middle Ground Between Long-Term & Day Trading**

**Concept:**
Swing trading intermediate-term trades hain jo kuch din se lekar kuch hafton tak rakhe jaate hain. Isme price movements ke "swings" (up-down patterns) capture kiye jaate hain. Long-term investing se zyada active hai, lekin day trading se kam stress aur time-consuming hai. Technical analysis (charts, indicators) aur thoda fundamental bhi use hota hai.

**Example:**
Imagine PSX mein **Engro Corporation** ke shares ka chart dekhein. Agar 50-day moving average neeche se 200-day moving average ko cross kar raha hai (Golden Cross), aur RSI 30 se neeche hai (oversold), toh yeh ek potential buy signal ho sakta hai. Agar price 10% increase hota hai, toh profit book kar sakte hain.

**Practice:**
1. PSX ka koi 1-2 companies select karein jinke charts mein clear "swing" patterns dikh rahe hain.
2. 1-hour ya 4-hour timeframe par support/resistance levels mark karein.
3. Agar price support level se bounce karta hai, toh buy ka signal ho sakta hai.

**Mini Project:**
Apne selected 2 stocks ke charts par:
- 50-day aur 200-day moving averages draw karein.
- RSI aur MACD indicators add karein.
- Ek potential entry/exit point identify karein (agar price kisi level se bounce ya break hota hai).

**Answer Key:**
Moving averages cross, RSI oversold, entry point support level, exit point resistance level
