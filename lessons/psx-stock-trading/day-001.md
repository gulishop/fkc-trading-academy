# Day 1 — PSX Ki Pehchaan: Pakistan Stock Exchange Kya Hai?

**Concept:**
PSX (Pakistan Stock Exchange) ek aisa market hai jahan companies apne shares bechti hain aur investors un shares ko khareed kar company ke hissaidar ban sakte hain. Yahan par shares ki khareedi-bikri hoti hai, jise trading kehte hain. PSX ek regulated platform hai jahan har din lakhon log trading karte hain aur company ke performance ke hisab se shares ke prices badalte rehte hain.

**Example:**
Imagine a company "XYZ Ltd" jo apne business ko grow karne ke liye investors se paise lena chahti hai. Wo PSX par apne 1 million shares 100 rupees ke price par offer karti hai. Agar aap 100 shares khareedte hain, to aap XYZ Ltd ke 0.01% ke malik ban jate hain. Agar company aage badti hai, to share ka price 150 rupees ho sakta hai, aur aapko profit hoga.

**Practice:**
Apne mobile ya computer par "PSX" search karein aur PSX ki official website ya app open karein. Dekhein ki "Market Summary" ya "Indices" section mein PSX-100, KSE-30 jaise terms hain. Inhe samajhein ki yeh kya represent karte hain.
