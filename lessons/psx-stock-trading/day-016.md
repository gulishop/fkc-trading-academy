# Day 16 — **Market Orders vs Limit Orders: Apne Trade Ko Kab Aur Kaise Lagana Hai?**

**Concept:**
Market order turant execute hota hai lekin price fluctuate hone par thoda zyada cost aa sakta hai. Limit order aapko ek specific price set karne deta hai—agar market uss price tak nahi aata to order execute nahi hota. Risk management ke liye yeh zaroori hai ki aap samajhein ke kab konsa order use karna hai.

**Example:**
Maana aap XYZ company ka 100 share khareedna chahte hain jo ab ₹50 par hai. Agar aap **Market Order** lagate hain, to ₹50 ke aas-paas hi share mil jayega lekin price badhne par ₹51-52 bhi ho sakta hai. Agar aap **Limit Order** ₹49.50 par lagate hain, to sirf tab hi share milega jab market ₹49.50 tak gir jaye.

**Practice:**
Apne brokerage app mein ja kar ek **Limit Order** aur ek **Market Order** lagane ka try karein (real trade na karein, sirf practice mode mein). Dono ke price difference ko note karein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek hypothetical scenario banaein: Agar aap ₹1000 ke budget se shares khareedna chahte hain aur stock ab ₹200 par hai, to:
1. Kitne shares khareed sakte hain (Market Order se)?
2. Agar aap Limit Order ₹195 par lagate hain, to kitne shares milenge agar market uss price tak gir jaye?

**Answer Key:**
Market Order: 5 shares, ₹1005, price fluctuate, Limit Order: 5 shares, ₹975, price fixed
