# Day 3 — **Shares, Stocks aur Dividends: Apna Investment Kaise Badhaye?**

**Concept:**
Shares (ya stocks) kisi company ka hissa hote hain – jab tum ek share khareedte ho, toh tum us company ke malik ban jaate ho. Dividends company ke profit ka woh hissa hota hai jo shareholders ko share ki quantity ke hisab se diya jata hai. Yeh regular income ka zariya bhi ho sakta hai aur long-term wealth banane ka.

**Example:**
Imagine XYZ Ltd. ke 100 shares hain aur tum 10 shares khareedte ho. Agar company ko profit hota hai aur har share par Rs. 5 dividend declare karta hai, toh tumhe Rs. 50 milenge (10 shares × Rs. 5). Agar company badhti hai, toh share price bhi badhega – tumhara investment badhega.

**Practice:**
Apne broker ke app ya website par jao aur kisi 1-2 companies ke **dividend history** check karo (e.g., PSO, NBP, or ENGRO). Note karo ki:
- Last 2-3 years mein kitna dividend mila?
- Share price mein kitna change aaya?

**Mini Project:**
Ek **fictional portfolio** banaye:
1. 3 companies select karo (e.g., Habib Bank, Lucky Cement, Fauji Fertilizer).
2. Har company ke 10 shares khareedne ka "khayal" karo (real mein nahi).
3. 1 month ke dividend aur price change ka estimate lagao.
