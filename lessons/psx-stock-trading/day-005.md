# Day 5 — **Trading Psychology: Apne Emotions Par Qabooz Kaise Rakhein?**

**Concept:**
Trading mein emotions (dar, greed, excitement) sabse bade dushman hote hain. Jab bhi market volatile ho ya loss ho, investors panic karke galat decisions lete hain. Is lesson mein hum seekhenge ki kaise apne emotions ko control karke disciplined trading kiya ja sakta hai.

**Example:**
Imagine aap ne ek stock ₹100 par khareeda. Price 10% gir gaya (₹90). Agar aap panic karke beche, to loss lock ho jayega. Lekin agar aapne stop-loss ₹85 set kiya hota, to sirf 5% loss hota. Discipline se rule follow karne se emotions ka asar kam ho jata hai.

**Practice:**
Aaj se hi apne har trade ke liye **pre-defined entry, exit, aur stop-loss** set karna shuru karein. Kisi bhi trade ko emotion se na dekhain—bas apna plan follow karain.

**Mini Project:**
Ek hypothetical trading scenario banao:
- Stock: XYZ Ltd.
- Entry Price: ₹200
- Stop-Loss: ₹190
- Target: ₹220
Ab 3 din tak market observe karo. Agar price stop-loss ya target tak nahi pahuncha, to kya action lena hai? Likh kar rakho.

**Answer Key:** stop-loss, target, discipline, plan
