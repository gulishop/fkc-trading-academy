# Day 4 — **Risk Management: Apne Investments Ko Kaise Bachaen?**

**Concept:**
Risk management trading ka sabse zaroori hissa hai. Yeh samajhna hai ke market unpredictable hai—chahe kitna bhi strong analysis kar lo, loss hone ka chance hamesha rehta hai. Risk management ka matlab hai apne capital ko protect karna, over-trading se bachna, aur har trade ke liye clear rules set karna. Jaise ke "1-2% se zyada capital ek trade mein mat lagana" ya "stop-loss zarur lagana".

**Example:**
Maana aapne 10,000 ka capital hai. Rule yeh hai ke ek trade mein max 2% (200 rupees) ka risk lena hai. Agar aap 100 rupees ka share khareed rahe hain, toh aapka stop-loss 80 rupees par hoga (100-80=20, 20/100=20% loss, lekin capital ka sirf 2% hi lag raha hai).

**Practice:**
Apne trading account ka capital note karein. Ab ek simple rule set karein: "Ek trade mein capital ka 1.5% se zyada risk nahi lena". Ab koi 3-4 stocks ko shortlist karein aur har ek ke liye possible stop-loss calculate karein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek "Risk Management Plan" banayein:
1. Total capital = ______
2. Max risk per trade (e.g., 1.5%) = ______
3. 3 stocks select karein aur har ek ke liye stop-loss price likhein.
4. Agar aapka 50,000 ka capital hai, toh har trade ka max loss = 750 rupees hoga.

**Answer Key:**
capital note karein, 1.5% calculate karein, stop-loss set karein, plan document karein
