# Day 7 — **Portfolio Track Karna: Apne Investments Ko Monitor Karein**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke kaise apne PSX portfolio ko effectively track karna hai taake har investment ka performance pata chal sake. Portfolio tracking se aapko pata chalta hai ke konsa stock gain kar raha hai, konsa loss mein hai, aur kab re-balance karna hai. Iske liye aap mobile apps (jaise PSX app, IBBL, JS Bank), Excel, ya online tools (TradingView, Yahoo Finance) use kar sakte hain.

**Example:**
1. **App se track karein:** PSX app mein apna portfolio add karein, har stock ka current price, % change, aur total value check karein.
2. **Excel sheet banaein:** Har stock ka purchase price, quantity, aur current market price enter karein, phir formula `=(Current Price - Purchase Price) * Quantity` se profit/loss calculate karein.
3. **Alerts set karein:** TradingView par price alerts set karein taake jab koi stock aapke target price par reach kare to notification mile.

**Practice:**
Apne trading account ya Excel mein ek simple portfolio tracker banaein. 3 stocks select karein jo aapke portfolio mein hain (ya hypothetical), aur unka current market price note karein. Har stock ka % gain/loss calculate karein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **weekly portfolio review** ka routine banaein. Har shanivaar ko apne portfolio ka performance check karein, aur 1 paragraph mein likhein ke aapko kya adjustments karne hain (jaise koi stock bechna hai ya aur invest karna hai).

**Answer Key:**
Current prices, % change, total P&L, adjustments
