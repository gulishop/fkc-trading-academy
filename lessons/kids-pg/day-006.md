# Day 6 — "Chhote-Bade, Upar-Neeche: Apne Aap Ko Pehchano!"

**Concept:**
Bachon, aaj hum seekhenge ke chhote-bade aur upar-neeche kaisay concepts hamari madad karte hain. Jaise, agar tum ek chhoti si ball utha rahe ho toh usko 'chhota' kehte hain, lekin agar ek badi football utha rahe ho toh usko 'bada' kehte hain. Isi tarah, agar tum apne haath utha rahe ho toh woh 'upar' hai, aur agar haath niche kar rahe ho toh woh 'neeche' hai.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Tumhari dost Ayesha ne ek chhoti si teddy bear pakdi thi aur usko 'chhota' kaha. Phir usne ek badi si football pakdi aur usko 'bada' kaha. Ayesha ne apne haath utha kar 'upar' kaha, phir haath niche kar ke 'neeche' kaha. Aise hi concepts hamari zindagi mein bhi kaam aate hain!

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, tumne kal kya kya 'chhota' aur 'bada' dekha?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Chhote-bade aur upar-neeche jaise concepts hamari madad karte hain apne aap ko aur cheezon ko pehchane mein.
