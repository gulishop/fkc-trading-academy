# Day 19 — **Roz ki Cheezein: Apne Hisse hain!**

**Concept:**
Bachon, aaj hum seekhenge ke roz ki cheezein jaise cup, ball, kitab aur khilona sabke apne hisse hain. Koi cheez tumhari nahi hai jab tak tumhe maaluum na ho ya tum use use karne ko kaha jaaye. Agar koi cheez tumhe pasand nahi ho, toh "na" kehne ka tumhara poora haq hai!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Ayaan ne school se ek naya football laaya. Usne sabko kaha ke "Yeh mera hai!" Lekin jab uske dost ne poocha, "Kya main bhi khel sakta hoon?" toh Ayaan ne muskurate hue kaha, "Haan, chalo mil kar khelte hain!" Sabne mil kar khushii se khela.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi cheez tumhe pasand nahi ho, toh kya tum "na" keh sakte ho?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Roz ki cheezein sabke hisse hain. Koi cheez tumhari nahi hai jab tak tumhe maaluum na ho!
