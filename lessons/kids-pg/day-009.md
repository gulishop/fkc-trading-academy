# Day 9 — Roz ki cheezein

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhein ge ke roz ki cheezein kya hain aur inhe kaise pehchanein. Har cheez ka apna naam aur istemal hota hai. In cheezon ko pehchanna seekhna aapki zindagi ko aur bhi asaan bana dega!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Babli ne apne ghar mein ek lal cup dekha. Usne kaha, "Ammi, yeh cup hai!" Ammi ne muskurate hue kaha, "Haan beta, yeh cup hai jismein hum pani piate hain." Babli ne cup ko uthaya aur apne hath mein pakda. Usne socha, "Kitna sundar hai yeh cup!"

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, tumhari pasandida cheez kya hai? Kya woh cheez tumhe pasand hai kyun?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Roz ki cheezein ko pehchanna seekhein aur unhe apne hath mein pakadne ka maza lo!
