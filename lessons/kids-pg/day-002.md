# Day 2 — "Gol, Chauraha, Teeno Kon"

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke har cheez ka apna ek khas shape hota hai! Gol (circle), chauraha (square), aur teeno kon (triangle) jaise shapes ko pehchaan karna seekhna bahut mazedar hai. In shapes ko pehchaan karke tum apne aas-paas ki cheezon ko bhi classify kar sakte ho.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rani ne apne school ke bag mein ek gol pen, ek chauraha wala notebook, aur ek teeno kon wala traffic sign dekha. Usne socha, "Yeh sab shapes kitne alag hain!" Usne apni teacher se poocha, aur teacher ne usko aur bhi shapes bataye. Rani ko bahut maza aaya!

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar tum kisi bhi shape ko dekh kar uska naam bata sakte ho, toh mujhe batao, tumne aaj kya kya shapes dekhe hain?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Har cheez ka apna ek shape hota hai, aur yeh seekhna bahut hi aasaan hai!
