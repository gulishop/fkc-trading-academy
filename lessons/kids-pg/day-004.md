# Day 4 — "Apne Hathon, Paon, Aankhon aur Naak ko Pehchano!"

**Concept:**
Bachon, aaj hum apne jism ke khubsurat hisson ke baare mein seekhenge! Haath, pair, aankh aur naak sabhi alag-alag kaam karte hain. Inhe pehchanna seekhna zaroori hai taake hum inhe achhe se istemaal kar saken aur apne aap ki dekhbhaal bhi kar saken.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rani ne apne haath se doodh ka glass uthaaya, pair se race lagaayi, aankhon se chand dekha aur naak se phool ki khushboo li. Har hissa apna alag kaam kar raha tha, lekin sab mil kar Rani ki madad karte the!

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar tumhe koi hissa dard kar raha ho ya ajeeb lag raha ho, toh tum kya karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhara jism tumhara hai—kisi ko bhi tumse yeh kehne ka haq nahi ke tum apne haath, pair ya kisi aur hisse ko na chhuno! Agar koi tumhe daraaye ya koi cheez ajeeb lage, toh turant Ammi Abbu ko batana.
