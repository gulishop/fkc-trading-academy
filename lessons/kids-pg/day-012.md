# Day 12 — **Gol, Chaur, Teeno: Aaj Seekhein Shapes!**

**Concept:**
Bachon, aaj hum seekhenge ke kaun si cheez gol hai, kaun si chaur hai, aur kaun si teeno wali hai! Har shape ka apna naam hai, aur har shape ki apni khasiyat hoti hai. Jaise gol shape goli hoti hai, chaur shape tahta hai, aur teeno shape pahaad jaisi hoti hai.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Ameer ko park mein khelte waqt ek gol ball mila. Usne dekha ke uski dadaji ne ek chaur plate rakhi hai. Phir usne dekha ke uski behen ne ek teeno wali chapati banayi hai. Ameer ne sab shapes ko pehchaan liya aur muskuraya!

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, tumhari favourite shape kaun si hai? Gol, chaur, ya teeno?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Har shape ka apna naam aur apni khasiyat hoti hai. Agar koi cheez ajeeb lage, toh foran ammi/abbu ko batana mat bhulna!
