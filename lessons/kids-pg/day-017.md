# Day 17 — **Phalon aur Sabziyon ki Pehchan**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhein ge ke phalon aur sabziyon ko kaise pehchaanain. Har cheez ka apna alag rang, shape aur swad hota hai. Agar kisi cheez ka swad ya smell ajeeb lage, to use mat khayein aur kisi bade ko batayein.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Areeba ne ek lal seb dekha. Usne socha, "Yeh toh mera favourite hai!" Lekin jab usne kha, toh uska swad meetha nahi tha. Usne ammi se kaha, "Ammi, yeh seb kaise hai?" Ammi ne kaha, "Beta, kabhi kabhi phalon ka swad alag hota hai. Agar aisa ho, to use mat khayein."

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar tumhe koi phal ya sabzi ka swad ajeeb lage, to tum kya karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Har cheez ka apna swad hota hai. Agar ajeeb lage, to use mat khayein!
