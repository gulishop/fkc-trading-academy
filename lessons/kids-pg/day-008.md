# Day 8 — "Ammi, Abbu, aur ghar walon ke naam"

**Concept:**
Bachay, aaj hum seekhein ge ke apne ghar walon ke naam kaise yaad rakhein. Jaisa ke hum apne doston ke naam yaad rakhte hain, waise hi apne parivar ke logon ke naam bhi yaad rakhna zaroori hai. Is se humein pata chalta hai ke hum kaun hain aur humein kaun dekhbhaal raha hai.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rahul ne apne naye school mein ek dost banaya. Uske dost ne poocha, "Tumhara naam kya hai?" Rahul ne socha, "Mera naam toh main jaanta hoon, par mera ghar wala kaun hai?" Usne apne ammi, abbu, dada-dadi aur behen ke naam yaad kare. Ab jab koi poochega, "Tumhara ghar kaun hai?" toh woh confidently jawab de sakta hai.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, tumhare ghar mein sabse pehle tumhara naam kaun lete hain?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Apne ghar walon ke naam yaad rakhna, taake jab bhi zaroorat ho, tum unhein bulaa sako.
