# Day 20 — "Haan, Na, Please, Shukriya: Apne Isharon aur Alfaz"

**Concept:**
Bachon, aaj hum seekhenge ke kaise chhote se isharon aur alfaz se hum apne dil ki baat kehte hain. Agar koi cheez aapko pasand nahi, toh "na" kehna bilkul theek hai. Agar koi aapko help kar raha hai, toh "please" kehna ya "shukriya" kehna achha lagta hai. Yeh sabhi alfat humein zyada achhe se samjhne mein madad karte hain.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rohan ne apne dost ko kaha, "Mujhe yeh khel pasand nahi." Uske dost ne samjha aur khel badal diya. Fir Rohan ne kaha, "Shukriya, yeh khel mujhe pasand hai!" Uske dost ko bhi khushi hui. Aise hi, agar koi cheez aapko pasand nahi, toh "na" kehna bilkul theek hai.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
Beta, agar tumhe koi cheez pasand nahi, toh tum kya kehoge? "Na" ya "please"?

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhare jism par sirf tumhara haq hai. Agar koi cheez ajeeb lage, toh foran ammi/abbu ko batana.
