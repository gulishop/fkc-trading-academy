# Day 5 — "Ginti 1 se 5 tak: Apne Hathon se Seekhein!"

**Concept:**
Bachon, aaj hum seekhenge ke apne hathon se ginti karna kitna asaan hai! Har hath mein 5 ungaliyan hoti hain, aur har ungali ek number ko dikhati hai. Yeh humein yeh yaad rakhne mein madad karta hai ke har cheez ka ek number hota hai.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rani ne apne hathon se ginti karni shuru ki. Usne ek ungali uthai aur kaha, "Ek!" Phir do ungaliyan, "Do!" Aise hi usne 5 tak ginti kar li. Uske hathon ne usko yaad dila diya ke har cheez ka ek number hota hai.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, tum apne hathon se ginti karke dekho, kitni ungaliyan hain?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Hathon se ginti karna asaan hai, lekin yaad rakhna ke tumhara jism sirf tumhara hai, aur tumhe koi bhi cheez ajeeb lage to foran ammi/abbu ko batana hai!
