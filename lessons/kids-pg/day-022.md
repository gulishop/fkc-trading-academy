# Day 22 — "Gol, Chauraha, Teen Kona: Apne Aas-Paas ki Shaklein!"

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhein ge ke kaise hum apne aas-paas ki cheezon mein gol, chauraha, aur teen kona shapes pehchaan sakte hain. Har shape ka apna ek khas naam aur zariya hai—jaise gol gol hai, chauraha chaar side wala, aur teen kona teen side wala!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rida ne park mein ek gol fountain dekha. Usne kaha, "Ammi, yeh toh gol hai!" Phir usne ek traffic sign board dekha—"Yehi chauraha hai!" Aur ek ice-cream cone—"Aur yeh teen kona!" Rida ne sab ko pehchaan liya aur muskuraayi.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar tum kisi cheez ki shape pehchaan lo, toh tum usko zyada achhe se samajh sakte ho. Kal tum kya kya shapes dekhte ho?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Har shape ka apna ek naam hai—gol, chauraha, teen kona! Inhe pehchaan karna seekh lo, aur har cheez ko achhe se dekhna mat bhulo. 💖
