# Day 16 — "Chhote-Bade, Upar-Neeche: Apne Aas-Paas ki Cheezon ko Pehchaanain!"

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhein ge ke chhote-bade aur upar-neeche kaisay concepts hain jo humari roozana zindagi mein kaam aate hain. Yeh seekhna zaroori hai taake hum apne aas-paas ki cheezon ko behtar samajhein aur apni hifazat bhi kar saken.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Bachay ne dekha ke uska dost ka ghara chhota hai, lekin uske school ka ghara bade hai. Usne socha ke "chhota" aur "bada" ka matlab kya hota hai? Uske teacher ne bataya ke har cheez ka size alag hota hai, aur humein yeh pehchaanna hai ke kon si cheez chhoti hai aur kon si badi. Isi tarah, jab hum stairs chadte hain, toh hum upar jaate hain, aur jab neeche utarte hain, toh neeche aate hain.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, tumne aaj kya cheez dekhi jo chhoti thi aur kya cheez dekhi jo badi thi?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Chhote-bade aur upar-neeche jaise concepts seekh kar hum apni duniya ko behtar samajh sakte hain.
