# Day 21 — **Rangon ki Pehchan: Laal, Neela, Peela, Hara**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhein ge ke rangon ki pehchan kaise karni hai! Har rang ka apna ek khas matlab hota hai. Jaise laal rang 'ruk jao' ya 'khatar' ka signal ho sakta hai. Isliye rangon ko pehchanna seekhna bahut zaroori hai.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rani ne apne ghar ke bagh mein ek laal phool dekha. Usne socha, "Laal rang ka matlab hai 'ruk jao' ya 'khatar'." Isliye usne apne haath ko wahan se hata liya. Phir usne neela aasmaan dekha aur socha, "Neela rang shaan ka hai!" Usne hara patta chuna aur kaha, "Hara rang zindagi ka hai!" Aur peela rang uske liye khushi ka signal tha.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar tumhe koi rang ajeeb lage ya lagta ho ke woh tumhe darwa raha hai, toh tum kya karogi?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Rangon ko pehchanna seekhna zaroori hai, lekin agar koi rang tumhe ajeeb lage toh foran ammi/abbu ko batana mat bhulna!
