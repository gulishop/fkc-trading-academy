# Day 10 — "Haan, Na, Please, Shukriya: Apne Dil ki Aawaaz Sunain!"

**Concept:**
Bachay, aaj hum seekhein ge ke kaise apne dil ki aawaaz sunni hai. Agar koi cheez aapko achchi nahi lagti, toh "na" keh sakte hain. "Please" aur "shukriya" bolna bhi seekhein ge taake sab log aapko pasand karein.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rohan ko school jaana bilkul pasand nahi tha. Uske teacher ne usko ek chhota sa gift diya. Rohan ne "na" kaha aur apne ma ke hath pakad liye. Ma ne usko samjhaya ke agar koi cheez aapko achchi nahi lagti, toh "na" keh sakte hain. Rohan ne apne teacher ko "shukriya" kaha aur ma ke saath ghar chala gaya.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi cheez tumhe pasand nahi aati, toh tum kya kehoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhara jism tumhara hai. "Na" kehne ka pura haq hai. Agar koi cheez ajeeb lage, toh foran ammi/abbu ko batana.
