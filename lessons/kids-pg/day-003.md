# Day 3 — "Janwaron ki Aawazein aur Naam"

**Concept:**
Bachon, aaj hum seekhenge ke har janwar ki alag awaaz hoti hai. Jaise kutta "bhoo bhoo" karta hai, billi "meow" karti hai. Lekin yaad rakhna, insaan ki awaaz sabse alag hai—hum apni awaaz se hi baat karte hain. Agar koi janwar ya insaan tumhe takleef de raha ho, toh tumhe "na" kehne ka poora haq hai!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rohan ne park mein ek kutta dekha jo "bhoo bhoo" kar raha tha. Rohan ne usko hath lagaya, lekin kutta dar gaya aur hath se kaat liya. Rohan ne roti hui maan ko bataya. Maan ne kutta ko dawa di aur Rohan ko samjhaya ke janwaron ko bhi apna haq hai—agar woh takleef de rahe hain, toh "na" kehna zaroori hai.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar tumhe koi janwar ya insaan takleef de raha ho, toh tum kya karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhara jism tumhara hai—agar koi tumhe chhoo raha ho toh "na" kehne mein sharam nahin!
