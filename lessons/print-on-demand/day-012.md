# Day 12 — **Print on Demand Ke Liye Best Vendors: Printful vs. Local vs. Alternatives**

**Concept:**
Print on Demand (POD) mein vendor choose karna ek foundation hai—jahan se quality, pricing, aur delivery depend karti hai. **Printful** global leader hai (high quality, reliable shipping), **local vendors** (India/Pakistan) mein cost-effective options mil sakte hain, lekin shipping time zyada ho sakta hai. **Alternatives** (Printify, Gelato, Redbubble) bhi options hain, har ek ke pros-cons hain.

**Example:**
1. **Printful** (Global): Agar aap US/EU mein sell kar rahe hain, toh shipping fast hoga (3-5 days), lekin cost thoda zyada.
2. **Local Vendor** (India/Pakistan): Agar aap local audience ko target kar rahe hain, toh cost kam ho sakti hai, lekin shipping time 7-15 days ho sakta hai.
3. **Printify** (Alternative): Multiple print providers se quotes compare kar sakte hain, lekin quality vary kar sakti hai.

**Practice:**
Apne niche ke hisab se **3 vendors** (Printful, 1 local, 1 alternative) ko shortlist karo aur unki **pricing, shipping time, aur quality** compare karo. Ek Excel sheet banao aur har ek ke pros-cons likho.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **mock order** place karo (agar possible ho) ya phir vendor ki website se **sample product** order karo taake quality check kar sako. (Agar budget na ho, toh online reviews aur YouTube unboxing videos dekh kar decision lo.)

**Answer Key:**
Printful: high quality, global shipping, premium pricing; Local: low cost, slow shipping; Printify: flexible, variable quality
