# Day 22 — **Print on Demand Ke Liye Best Platforms: Local vs. International Vendors Ka Comparison**

**Concept:**
Print on Demand (POD) mein platform choose karna ek crucial decision hai. Local vendors (jaise India-based) low shipping cost aur faster delivery offer karte hain, lekin limited product range aur higher cost per unit ho sakti hai. International vendors (jaise Printful, Printify) global reach, wide product catalog, aur automated fulfillment provide karte hain, lekin shipping time zyada ho sakta hai. Har ek ke apne pros-cons hain—apne business goals ke hisab se decide karna zaroori hai.

**Example:**
Agar aap India mein customers ko target kar rahe hain, to **local vendor** (jaise **Printrove** ya **PrintXpand**) use karna better hai—kyunki shipping 3-5 days mein ho jata hai aur cost bhi kam rehta hai. Lekin agar aap global audience ko serve karna chahte hain, to **Printful** ya **Printify** better options hain, kyunki unke worldwide fulfillment centers hain aur product range bhi zyada hai.

**Practice:**
Apne business model ke hisab se **3 platforms** (2 international + 1 local) shortlist karo aur unke:
- Shipping time (local vs. global)
- Product cost per unit
- Return policy
- Integration options (Shopify, Etsy, etc.)
Compare karo. Notion ya Excel mein ek simple table banao.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **hypothetical POD store** ke liye **2 product ideas** select karo (ek local vendor se, ek international se). Unke:
- Final cost (product + shipping)
- Expected delivery time
- Profit margin
Calculate karo aur decide karo ki kaunsa zyada suitable hai.

**Answer Key:**
Local: fast shipping, low cost per unit, limited products; International: global reach, automated fulfillment, higher cost per unit
