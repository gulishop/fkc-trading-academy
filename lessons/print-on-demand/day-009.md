# Day 9 — **Orders Handle Karna: Customer Experience Se Repeat Sales Tak**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum orders process karne se lekar customer experience improve karne tak sab kuch cover karenge. Yeh step sirf fulfillment nahi—yeh hai aapki brand ki reputation aur repeat customers banane ka raaz. Quick response, professional packaging, aur smooth delivery se customers aapko yaad rakhte hain aur dobara khareedte hain.

**Example:**
Ek customer ne aapka T-shirt order kiya. Aapko yeh karna hai:
1. Order confirm karo (email/Shopify par notification).
2. Printful se order place karo (agar wo vendor hai) ya local printer ko details provide karo.
3. Shipping confirm karne ke baad customer ko tracking number share karo.
4. Delivery ke baad polite follow-up email bhej kar feedback maango.

**Practice:**
Apne ek existing order (ya ek test order) ka complete process likho:
- Order receive hone se lekar delivery confirm hone tak ke steps.
- Har step par aapko kya action lena hai?

**Mini Project:**
Ek "Order Confirmation Email" template banao jo:
- Order details (product, size, color)
- Shipping time estimate
- Tracking link
- Feedback request
Include kare. Canva ya plain text mein bana sakte ho.

**Answer Key:**
order confirmation, tracking, feedback request, delivery follow-up
