# Day 13 — **Trending Designs Kaise Dhundhein? (2024 Ke Top 5 Trends + Free Sources)**

**Concept:**
Print on Demand mein successful designs trending topics par based hote hain. Aaj hum 2024 ke top 5 trending design categories (minimalist quotes, retro gaming, pet lovers, fitness motivation, eco-friendly) explore karenge aur free sources (Pinterest, Etsy, Redbubble, Google Trends) se kaise trends track karein seekhenge.

**Example:**
1. **Minimalist Quotes:** "Be Kind" ya "Good Vibes Only" jaise simple text designs.
2. **Retro Gaming:** 8-bit pixel art ya "Pac-Man" inspired designs.
3. **Pet Lovers:** "Dog Mom" ya "Cat Dad" wale designs.
4. **Fitness Motivation:** "Sweat Now, Shine Later" jaise quotes.
5. **Eco-Friendly:** "Save the Planet" ya "Reuse, Reduce, Recycle" slogans.

**Practice:**
Apne niche ke hisab se **1 trending design category** choose karein (e.g., fitness). **Pinterest ya Etsy** par search karein aur **top 3 designs** ke keywords (tags) note karein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **trending design idea** ke liye **3 different color schemes** banayein (e.g., neon, pastel, dark mode). Canva ya AI tool (DALL·E) use karke rough drafts banaein.

**Answer Key:**
minimalist quotes, retro gaming, pet lovers, fitness motivation, eco-friendly
