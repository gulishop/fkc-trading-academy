# Day 6 — **Store Ko Optimize Karna: Conversion Rate Badhane Ke 5 Must-Have Steps**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke kaise apne store ko aise optimize karein jo visitors ko customers mein badal de. Basics setup ke baad abhi focus hota hai: product pages, SEO, trust badhana, aur checkout process ko smooth banana. Yeh hee woh cheez hai jo 80% beginners chhod dete hain aur isse hi unke sales ka 50% loss hota hai.

**Example:**
Ek student ne Etsy par "Cat Lover Hoodie" upload kiya. Usne product title mein sirf "Cat Lover Hoodie" likha, description mein 2 lines likhi, aur 1 hi photo use ki. Result: 100 views par 0 sales. Phir usne title ko "Soft Unisex Cat Lover Hoodie - Funny Cat Design - Gift for Cat Owners" kar diya, description mein 5 bullet points add kiye, aur 3 high-quality mockups upload kiye. Result: 100 views par 8 sales.

**Practice:**
Apne ek existing product page ko lein aur usko optimize karein:
1. Title ko SEO-friendly banayein (keywords include karein)
2. Description mein 5-7 bullet points add karein (features + benefits)
3. 3 alag-alag mockups upload karein (front, back, worn)
4. Price ko $24.99 se $29.99 karke test karein (agar possible ho)

**Mini Project:**
Ek "Before vs After" comparison post taiyar karein:
- Ek screenshot lijiye apne product page ka (current state)
- Uske neeche ek screenshot lijiye optimized version ka
- 3 bullet points likhein ke kya improvements kiye gaye hain
- Is post ko apne Instagram/Facebook par share karein (community se feedback lo)

**Answer Key:**
SEO keywords, bullet points, high-quality mockups, price testing
