# Day 5 — **Store Setup Kaise Karein? (Shopify vs Etsy vs Amazon Merch)**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum apna print-on-demand store physically setup karenge. Sabse pehle platform choose karna hai (Shopify, Etsy, Amazon Merch), phir store ka naam, branding, aur product listings add karni hain. Har platform ke rules alag hote hain, isliye step-by-step guide follow karna zaroori hai.

**Example:**
1. **Shopify par store banana:**
   - Shopify.com par signup karein (14-day free trial).
   - "Online Store" > "Themes" se ek free theme select karein.
   - "Products" > "Add product" par click karein, apne POD design upload karein, aur description likhein.
   - "Payments" mein Stripe ya PayPal add karein.

2. **Etsy par store banana:**
   - Etsy.com par jayein, "Sell on Etsy" par click karein.
   - Store ka naam, billing details, aur shop policies (return, shipping) set karein.
   - "Listings" mein apne product ka photo, title, aur tags add karein (SEO ke liye zaroori hai).

**Practice:**
Apne chune huye platform par ek dummy product add karein (design upload karne ki zaroorat nahi, bas basic details fill karein).

**Mini Project:**
Apne store ka naam, logo (Canva se bana sakte hain), aur 1 product listing complete karein. Screenshot ya link apne notes mein save karein.

**Answer Key:**
Store name, product title, theme selection, payment setup
