# Day 7 — **Facebook Ads Se First Sale Kaise Lein? (Beginner-Friendly Guide)**

**Concept:**
Facebook Ads POD business ke liye sabse powerful marketing tool hai. Isse targeted audience tak pahuncha ja sakta hai jo actual buyers bante hain. Budget control karna, right audience choose karna aur creatives (ads) ko optimize karna seekhna zaroori hai.

**Example:**
1. **Audience Targeting:** "Men's T-Shirts" niche mein "Fitness Enthusiasts" (30-45 age, interested in gym, weight loss) ko target karo.
2. **Ad Creative:** Canva se banaye gaye design ka mockup + "Get Your Gym Motivation Tee" caption ke saath carousel ad.
3. **Budget:** $5/day test karo, agar CTR > 2% ho to scale up karo.

**Practice:**
Apne POD store ka Facebook Business Manager setup karo. Ek basic ad create karo (target audience: 25-35 age, interested in "T-Shirts", "Streetwear"). Ad mein apne best-selling design ka mockup use karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek 3-day Facebook Ads campaign run karo (budget: $15 total). Har din ad performance check karo aur 3rd day ke baad yeh decide karo:
- Agar CTR > 1.5% ho → Scale up karo
- Agar CTR < 1% ho → Ad creative ya audience change karo

**Answer Key:**
Audience testing, CTR >1.5%, mockup quality, budget scaling
