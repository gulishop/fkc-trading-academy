# Day 10 — **Store Ko Automate Karna: Time Save Karein, Profit Badhaein**

**Concept:**
Store scale karne ka matlab hai manual kaam kam karna aur systems ko automatic banana. Automation se orders handle karne, emails bhejne, aur even product research tak ka time bachta hai. Isse aap apne business ko bade level par le ja sakte hain bina extra stress ke.

**Example:**
Imagine aapko har order ke baad customer ko email bhejna hai. Aise mein **Zapier** ya **Shopify Flow** use karke yeh kaam automatic kar sakte hain. Bas ek trigger set karo (jaise "order placed") aur action (jaise "email send")—ab aapka kaam khatam!

**Practice:**
Apne store ke kisi ek manual process ko identify karo (jaise order confirmations, inventory updates, ya social media posts scheduling) aur uske liye automation tool dhundho (Zapier, Make.com, ya store ke built-in tools).

**Mini Project:**
Apne store ke liye **3 automation rules** banayein:
1. New order aane par customer ko thank you email bhejna.
2. Low stock alert aapke email par bhejna.
3. Har naye product ke launch par Instagram par post schedule karna.

**Answer Key:**
Zapier, Shopify Flow, triggers, actions
