# Day 18 — **Profit Margin Ko Optimize Karne Ke 5 Advanced Tips**

**Concept:**
Profit margin ka matlab hai aapki har sale ke baad kitna profit bachta hai. Print on demand mein yeh depend karta hai: product cost, shipping, platform fees, marketing cost, aur aapki pricing strategy. Agar margin kam hai to scale karna mushkil ho jata hai. Is lesson mein advanced tactics seekhein jo aapki profit ko 20-50% tak badha sakte hain.

**Example:**
Maana aap ek T-shirt $25 mein bech rahe hain. Cost breakup:
- Printful cost: $12
- Shipping: $5
- Etsy fees: $3.50
- Marketing: $2
Total cost: $22.50 → Profit: $2.50 (10% margin).
**Solution:** Agar aap same T-shirt ko apne website par $29 mein bechen (aur shipping free karen), to:
- Printful cost: $12
- Shipping: $0 (free shipping threshold set karke)
- Website fees: $1 (Shopify basic)
- Marketing: $2
Total cost: $15 → Profit: $14 (48% margin).

**Practice:**
Apne ek existing product ka cost breakup nikalein (Excel ya Google Sheets mein). Har cost ko alag-alag likhein aur total profit calculate karein. Ab sochein ke kaunse cost ko reduce kiya ja sakta hai (e.g., bulk orders ke discounts, free shipping threshold, ya marketing cost optimization).
