# Day 17 — **Email Marketing Se Customers Ko Repeat Karein**

**Concept:**
Email marketing ek powerful tool hai jo customers ko repeat orders lane mein madad karta hai. Isse hum unhe naye products, discounts, aur updates ke zariye engage rakhte hain. Best part: yeh free ya sasta hai aur high ROI deta hai.

**Example:**
Ek T-shirt store ka example lein. Pehle customer ko welcome email bhejen (e.g., "Thanks for your first order! Here's 10% off next purchase"). Phir weekly/monthly updates bhejen (e.g., "New designs launched!" ya "Flash sale this weekend!").

**Practice:**
Apne store ke liest mein ek "Welcome Email" draft karein. Subject line, greeting, aur 1-2 lines ka offer shamil karein (e.g., "Get 15% off your next order!").

**Mini Project:**
Ek 3-email sequence banayein:
1. Welcome email (order ke baad)
2. "Your order is on the way!" (shipping update)
3. "Rate your purchase" (feedback request)

**Answer Key:**
welcome email, 10-15% discount, shipping update, feedback request
