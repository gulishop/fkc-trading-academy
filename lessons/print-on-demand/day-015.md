# Day 15 — **Store Ko Brand Karna: Logo, Banner & Visual Identity Banaein**

**Concept:**
Ek POD store ki pehchan uska **branding** hai. Isse logo, banner, product mockups, aur consistent color scheme se define karte hain. Strong branding se customers yaad rehte hain aur trust banta hai. Aaj seekhein ke kaise ek professional-looking store setup karein jo alag nazar aaye.

**Example:**
1. **Logo:** Canva par ek simple text-based logo banain (e.g., "Artify" + ek brush stroke icon).
2. **Banner:** Store ke niche ke hisab se ek 1200x300px banner banain (e.g., "Custom Art for Gamers" + trending colors).
3. **Color Scheme:** 2-3 main colors choose karein (e.g., dark blue + neon green) aur sab designs mein use karein.

**Practice:**
Apne store ke niche ke hisab se **ek logo aur banner** banain (Canva ya Looka use karein). Color scheme bhi decide kar lein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **social media post** design karein jo aapke store ke branding ko reflect kare (e.g., Instagram story mein "New Drop Alert!" + logo + trending design ka mockup).

**Answer Key:**
Logo: Text+icon, Banner: 1200x300px, Colors: 2-3, Post: Mockup+CTA
