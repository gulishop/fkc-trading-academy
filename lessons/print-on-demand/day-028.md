# Day 28 — **Profit Margin Ko Maximize Karne Ke 5 Advanced Hacks**

**Concept:**
Profit margin ko badhana sirf price badhane ka matlab nahi—costs control karne, wastage kam karne, aur customer value badhane ka combination hai. Har product ki actual cost (vendor + shipping + platform fees), hidden expenses (mockup tools, ads, refunds), aur perceived value (design quality, niche demand) ko analyze karna zaroori hai.

**Example:**
Ek T-shirt ka cost breakdown:
- Vendor price: $8
- Shipping: $3
- Platform fee (Etsy): $0.20 + 6.5% = ~$0.80
- Payment processing: ~$0.50
- **Total cost: $12.50**
- Selling price: $25 → **Profit: $12.50 (50% margin)**
*Lekin agar tum 10% discount offer karte ho, to profit $10.25 ho jata hai. Isliye hidden costs ko pehle se calculate karna zaroori hai.*

**Practice:**
Apne ek best-selling product ka cost breakdown nikalo (vendor website se rates check karke). Excel/Google Sheets mein ek simple table banao:
`Product | Vendor Cost | Shipping | Platform Fees | Other Costs | Total Cost | Selling Price | Profit Margin (%)`

**Mini Project:**
Ek "Profit Booster" plan banao:
1. Apne top 3 products ke margins check karo.
2. Har product ke liye 1-2 cost-cutting idea socho (e.g., bulk shipping discounts, free mockup tools use karna).
3. Ek "premium pricing" strategy likho—kaise apne product ko higher perceived value de sakte ho (e.g., limited edition, bundled offers).

**Answer Key:**
cost breakdown table, 50% margin, discount impact, bulk shipping, premium perceived value
