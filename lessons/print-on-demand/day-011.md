# Day 11 — **Niche Research: Apna Target Audience Kaise Dhundhein?**

**Concept:**
Print on demand mein success ka pehla raaz hai ek *specific niche* choose karna. Niche research ka matlab hai apne ideal customer ko samajhna, unke interests, problems, aur trends ko analyze karna. Ek achha niche wo hai jo: (1) Passion/interest-based ho, (2) Competition kam ho, aur (3) Profit margin achha de. Bina niche research ke, aapka design generic ho jayega aur marketing mushkil ho jayegi.

**Example:**
Ek student ne "Pets" niche choose kiya, lekin usne further research kiya to pata chala ki "Golden Retriever lovers" ka sub-niche aur bhi profitable hai. Usne Google Trends par "Golden Retriever gifts" search kiya aur dekha ki demand barh rahi hai. Phir Etsy par search kiya to "Golden Retriever mugs" ka competition kam tha lekin orders zyada the.

**Practice:**
Apne interest ke 3 niches list karein (e.g., fitness, gaming, parenting). Phir har niche ke liye:
1. Google Trends par search karein (last 5 years ka data dekhein).
2. Etsy/Amazon par search karein (kitne sellers hain? kitne orders hain?).
3. Reddit/Facebook groups mein poochhein (log kya pooch rahe hain?).

**Mini Project:**
Apne top 3 niches mein se *ek niche* choose karein aur uske liye:
- 3 trending keywords list karein (e.g., "Gym Motivation Quotes", "Cute Cat Lover Gifts").
- 1 design idea draft karein (sketch ya Canva mein).
- 1 social media post idea likhein (e.g., "Which one is your favorite? Vote below!").

**Answer Key:**
Google Trends data, Etsy competition analysis, trending keywords, design draft
