# Day 2 — Print on Demand Ke Liye Best Platform Kaise Chunein?

**Concept:**
Print on Demand (POD) mein platform choose karna ek critical decision hai jo aapke business ki cost, quality, aur scalability par asar dalta hai. Aapko in factors par dhyaan dena hoga: shipping time, product quality, pricing, aur integration options (jaise Shopify, Etsy, Amazon). Local vendors vs international POD platforms (jaise Printful, Printify, Redbubble) mein se kya choose karna hai, yeh decide karna hai.

**Example:**
Agar aap India se hain aur local customers ko target kar rahe hain, toh **Printrove** (Indian POD service) better ho sakta hai kyunki shipping fast hoga aur cost bhi kam. Lekin agar global audience hai, toh **Printful** ya **Printify** (jo multiple warehouses ke saath work karte hain) better options hain.

**Practice:**
Apne business model ke hisaab se 2 POD platforms ko shortlist karein aur unke:
1. Shipping time aur cost
2. Product range
3. Integration options
Compare karein. (Hint: Printful aur Printify ki websites par jaake check karein.)

**Mini Project:**
Ek simple comparison chart banaein (Excel ya Canva mein) jismein aapne shortlisted 2 platforms ke upar 3-4 key factors compare karein.

**Answer Key:**
Printful: global warehouses, high quality, Shopify integration; Printify: cheaper, more product options, flexible
