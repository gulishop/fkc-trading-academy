# Day 27 — **Email Marketing Se Repeat Customers Banayein: DRS (Drip, Retarget, Sell) Strategy**

**Concept:**
Email marketing POD ka hidden gem hai—cheap, scalable, aur repeat buyers banata hai. Aaj seekhoge **DRS Strategy**: **Drip** (automated welcome series), **Retarget** (abandoned cart emails), aur **Sell** (upsell/cross-sell). Har email ka goal hona chahiye: trust build karna, urgency create karna, ya direct sale.

**Example:**
1. **Drip (Welcome Series):**
   - Day 1: "Thank you for subscribing! Here’s 10% off your first order." (Discount code attach karen)
   - Day 3: "Your favorite design is back in stock!" (Social proof + FOMO)
   - Day 7: "How’s your order? Leave a review for 5% off next purchase."

2. **Retarget (Abandoned Cart):**
   - Subject: "🛒 Your [Product Name] is waiting!"
   - Body: "You left something behind… Complete your order now and get free shipping!"

3. **Sell (Upsell):**
   - Subject: "Complete your look with matching accessories!"
   - Body: "Love your hoodie? Pair it with our best-selling socks—just $9!"

**Practice:**
Apne Shopify/Etsy store ke liye **Klaviyo** ya **Mailchimp** par ek **welcome email series** set up karo. 3 emails likho: welcome discount, product highlight, aur review request.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **abandoned cart email** draft karo (subject line + 1st paragraph). Use this template:
> Subject: "🚨 Your [Product] is almost gone!"
> Body: "Hi [Name], your [Product Name] is waiting in cart. Only 2 left in stock! Complete checkout now before it’s gone."

**Answer Key:**
welcome discount, product highlight, review request
