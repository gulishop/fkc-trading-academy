# Day 14 — **AI Tools Se Designs Ko Professional Banana (Midjourney, DALL·E, Canva Magic)**

**Concept:**
AI tools se designs ko fast, unique aur professional level par le ja sakte hain. Midjourney/DALL·E se concept art generate karke Canva mein refine karo. AI se "prompt engineering" seekhna zaroori hai—clear instructions se hi behtareen results milte hain.

**Example:**
1. **Prompt:** *"A minimalist cat wearing a graduation cap, vector art, white background, 4K, trending on Pinterest"*
2. **Midjourney/DALL·E** se 4-6 variants generate karo.
3. **Canva** mein AI-generated image ko upload karke:
   - Background remove karo (Canva AI se hi).
   - Text add karo ("Class of 2024").
   - Mockup par lagao (T-shirt/phone case).

**Practice:**
Apne niche ke hisab se **3 AI prompts** likho aur Midjourney/DALL·E se generate karo. Best variant ko Canva mein edit karke ek simple mockup banao.

**Mini Project:**
**"AI + Canva Se Ek Product Mockup Banana"**
- Ek trending niche (e.g., "Gamer Humor" ya "Pet Lovers") choose karo.
- AI se 1 design generate karo.
- Canva mein:
  - Text add karo (urdu/hindi).
  - Mockup (T-shirt/phone case) par lagao.
  - Export karo (PNG, 300 DPI).

**Answer Key:**
AI prompt, Canva edits, mockup variant, niche relevance
