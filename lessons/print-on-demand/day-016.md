# Day 16 — **Store Ko Optimize Karna: Traffic Badhaein & Conversions Badhaein**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum store ko is tarah se optimize karenge ke visitors zyada aayein aur jo aaye wo khareed lein. SEO, user experience (UX), aur conversion rate optimization (CRO) ke basics cover karenge. Yeh isliye zaroori hai ke store professional dikhe, fast load ho, aur customers ko purchase karne mein koi problem na aaye.

**Example:**
Ek store par 1000 visitors aate hain lekin sirf 10 log khareedte hain. Agar hum:
1. **SEO:** Product titles aur descriptions mein keywords add karen (e.g., "Custom Cat Lover T-Shirt for Women – Funny Cat Design").
2. **UX:** Website speed check karen (Google PageSpeed Insights) aur mobile-friendly banayein.
3. **CRO:** "Add to Cart" button ka color change karen (red se green) — isse 20% zyada conversions ho sakte hain.

**Practice:**
Apne store ke homepage ya kisi product page ka URL copy karen aur:
1. Google PageSpeed Insights par check karen (score 70+ hona chahiye).
2. Ek product title aur description likhein jismein 2-3 relevant keywords hon (e.g., "Vintage Travel Poster – Minimalist Wall Art for Home Decor").

**Mini Project:**
Ek **A/B test** setup karen:
- Apne store par do alag-alag "Add to Cart" button colors try karen (e.g., blue vs. orange).
- 1 week tak track karen ke konse color se zyada conversions aaye. (Tools: Google Optimize ya Facebook Ads Manager ka built-in A/B test feature.)

**Answer Key:**
PageSpeed score, keywords, mobile-friendly, A/B test
