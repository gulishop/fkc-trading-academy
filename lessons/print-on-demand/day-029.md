# Day 29 — **Customer Service: Aapki Store Ki Reputation Ka Foundation**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke customer service Print on Demand business mein kyun zaroori hai. Yeh sirf complaints solve karne ka nahi, balki customers ko loyal banane aur positive reviews lene ka bhi zariya hai. Ek achha customer service system badhiya user experience provide karta hai, jo repeat sales aur word-of-mouth marketing ke liye zaroori hai.

**Example:**
Ek customer ko order delivery mein delay hota hai. Aap usko proactive message bhejte hain: *"Assalamu Alaikum! Apka order #12345 slightly delayed ho raha hai due to high demand. Hum 2-3 extra days ka time le rahe hain. Aapko koi inconvenience to nahi ho raha?"* Isse customer ko feel hota hai ke aap unki parwah karte hain, aur wo negative review dene se pehle hi satisfied ho jata hai.

**Practice:**
Apne ek existing order ka order ID note karen. Ab us order ke customer ko ek friendly follow-up message bhejiye (email ya store ke chat system ke zariye). Message mein order status update karen aur unse feedback maangen.

**Mini Project:**
Ek simple **FAQ page** banayein jo aapki store ke common questions ke answers de. Ismein shipping times, return policy, aur customization options shamil karen. Canva ya Google Docs ka istemal kar sakte hain.

**Answer Key:**
proactive communication, customer satisfaction, repeat purchases, positive reviews
