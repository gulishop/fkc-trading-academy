# Day 1 — "Adaab ki Jadoo: Please, Thank You, Sorry"

**Concept:**
Bachon, humein rozana bohat se logon se milte hain. Unse baat karte waqt 'Please', 'Thank You', aur 'Sorry' kehtay hain. Yehi hamari 'Adaab ki Jadoo' hain. Agar hum yeh shabd achi tarah istemal karen, to sab humse khush rehte hain aur hum bhi khush rehte hain.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rahim ne apne dost Ali ko apna pencil doya. Ali ne kaha, "Thank You, Rahim bhai!" Rahim ko bhi bahut khushi hui. Ek din Ali ne apne cycle se Rahim ko lift di. Rahim ne kaha, "Please, Ali bhai, cycle par bitha do." Ali ne muskurate hue kaha, "Bilkul!" Is tarah, dono dost khush rehte hain.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, kal tumne kisi ko 'Thank You' kaha tha? Usne kaise jawab diya?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Hamesha 'Please', 'Thank You', aur 'Sorry' kehtay raho. Isse sab tumse khush rehte hain!
