# Day 7 — "Dost ke sath pyaar se rehna aur mil kar khelna"

**Concept:**
Dost banana bahut achha hai, lekin yaad rakhna zaroori hai ke har dost ke sath pyaar aur respect se baat karni chahiye. Agar koi dost tumhe maarte hai, dhakka deta hai, ya tumhe "na" kehne par majboor karta hai, toh woh dost nahi hai. Hamesha yaad rakhna ke tumhara jism tumhara hai, aur tumhe uske saath khelne ya baat karne ka poora haq hai.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rohan aur Ayaan bade dost the. Ek din, Ayaan ne Rohan se kaha, "Mera naya football lekar aaya hoon, chalo park mein khele?" Rohan ne muskurate hue kaha, "Bilkul! Lekin pehle mujhe pata hai ke tumhe football pasand hai, isliye main bhi apna ball le aaya hoon." Ayaan ne haan mein sir hila diya. Woh dono park mein mil kar khushii se khele. Ayaan ne kabhi bhi Rohan ko maara nahi, dhakka nahi diya, aur na hi usse "na" kehne par majboor kiya. Isliye, woh dono ki dosti aur bhi mazboot ho gayi.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi dost tumhe kisi cheez ke liye majboor kare ya tumhe "na" kehne par ruka de, toh tum kya karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Dost ke sath pyaar se rehna, lekin kabhi bhi apne aap ko majboor na samajhna. Agar koi dost tumhe uncomfortable feel karwaye, toh turant ammi abbu ko batana.
