# Day 28 — **Janwaron aur paudon se pyaar aur nazuk sulook**

**Concept:**
Janwaron aur paudon se pyaar karna seekhna bhi zaroori hai, kyunke woh bhi hamare jaise zindagi jeena chahte hain. Unko pareshan mat karo, unki dekhbhaal karo, aur unse dosti karo. Paudon ko pani dena, janwaron ko chhoona ya unke saath softly baat karna, sab seekhna hai.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rohan ne ek chhota sa kutha apne ghar laaya. Usne usko doodh pilaya, uske saath khela, aur usko bhookh lagne par khana diya. Kuthe ne bhi Rohan ke paimaan se uska haath chhua, jaise kehte hain, "Tum mere dost ho!" Un dono ne ek doosre se pyaar karna seekha.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar tum kisi janwar ya paudhe ko pareshan karte ho, toh kya lagta hai usko?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Janwaron aur paudon se pyaar karna hamari zindagi ko bhi meetha banata hai.
