# Day 27 — **Dosti ka meetha rishta: Pyaar aur mil kar khelna**

**Concept:**
Dost banana aur unke sath mil kar khelna bahut hi khushiyon bhara hota hai! Lekin yaad rakhna, har dost ki baat sunna aur uske dil ki baat samajhna bhi zaroori hai. Jab hum mil kar khelte hain, toh hum ek doosre ki madad karte hain aur sab sath mil kar khush rehte hain.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rohan aur Aisha ek hi class mein padhte the. Ek din, Aisha ne kaha, "Chalo, hum ek saath football khelte hain!" Rohan ne muskurate hue kaha, "Bilkul! Lekin pehle tum decide karo ki team kaun karega." Aisha ne sabko ek-ek kar ke team banayi aur sabne mil kar khela. Sab khush the kyunki sabne ek doosre ki baat mani aur mil kar khela.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar tumhara koi dost tumse koi aisa kaam karne ko bole jo tumhe achha na lage, toh tum kya karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Dosti ka matlab hai ek doosre ki baat sunna aur uske dil ki baat samajhna. Kabhi kisi dost ko chhota ya beizzat na samjho—sabke saath pyaar se rehna seekho!
