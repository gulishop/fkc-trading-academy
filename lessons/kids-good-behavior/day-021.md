# Day 21 — "Pyaar bhare shabd: 'Please', 'Thank You' aur 'Sorry'"

**Concept:**
Bachay, jab hum kisi se kuch maangte hain to "Please" kehna seekhna chahiye. Jab koi humein kuch de to "Thank You" kehna chahiye. Aur agar hum se koi galti ho jaye to "Sorry" kehna chahiye. Yeh shabd humein achhe dost banate hain aur sab log humse pyaar karte hain.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Aman ne apne dost ki pencil tod di. Usne kaha, "Sorry bhai, maine galat kiya." Dost ne muskurate hue kaha, "Koi baat nahi, chal abhi naya le le." Phir Aman ne kaha, "Thank You bhai!" Uske baad dono ne mil kar khela.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
Aaj tumne kisi ko "Please", "Thank You" ya "Sorry" kaha hai?

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Pyaar bhare shabd dil se nikalte hain, sirf munh se nahi.
