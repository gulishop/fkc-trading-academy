# Day 26 — Apna saman khud sametna aur safai rakhna

**Concept:**
Beta, jab tum apne kapde, khilone ya kitaabain khud samete aur apne kamre ko saaf rakhte ho, toh tum ek responsible bachay ban jaate ho! Is se tumhara bhi dil khush rehta hai, aur ammi-abbu ko bhi madad milti hai. Har cheez ko uski jagah par rakhna seekhna bhi ek accha habit hai.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rohan ne apne kamre mein khilone bikhaye the. Jab usne khud unhein saaf kiya, toh usko pata chala ke sab kuch alag-alag jagah par rakhne se usko sab cheezein dhundne mein bhi aasani hoti hai. Usne socha, "Agar main roz thoda saametun, toh mera kamra hamesha saaf rahega!"

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
Beta, tum apne kamre mein sabse pehle kya cheez sametoge? Kya tum sochte ho ke isse tumhara kamra kitna saaf ho jayega?

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Hamesha apni cheezein uski jagah par rakhna, taake tumhe unhein dhundne mein mushkil na ho!
