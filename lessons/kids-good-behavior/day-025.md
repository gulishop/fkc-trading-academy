# Day 25 — **Sabar rakhna aur baari ka intezar karna**

**Concept:**
Bachon, kabhi-kabhi humein cheezon ka intezar karna hota hai. Jaise jab aap ice cream khana chahte hain, lekin abbu ne kaha ke "Thoda intezar karo, abhi nahi." Ya jab aap khel rahe hain, aur doosra bachcha apni baari ka intezar kar raha hai. Is waqt sabar rakhna seekhna bahut zaroori hai. Sabar rakhne se hum sabko respect karte hain aur sabar karne wale ko Allah bhi pasand karte hain.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Ameer ek chhota bachcha hai jo har roz school se aate hi apne dost ke sath football khelta tha. Ek din uske dost ne kaha, "Aaj main pehle khelunga, tum baad mein." Ameer ko gussa aaya, lekin usne sabar kiya. Usne football khela, aur doosre din usne apne dost ko pehle khelne diya. Uske dost ne uski sabar ki wajah se usko aur zyada pasand kar liya.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, tumhein kabhi koi cheez ka intezar karna hota hai? Us waqt tum kya sochte ho?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Jab bhi intezar karna ho, deep breath lo aur socho ke "Allah sabar walon ko pasand karte hain."
