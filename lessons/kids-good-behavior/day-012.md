# Day 12 — "Bado ki izzat karna aur unki baat sunna"

**Concept:**
Bado ki izzat karna aur unki baat sunna bahut zaroori hai, kyunke woh tumse zyada jaante hain aur tumhe seekhate hain. Unki baat dhyaan se sunne se tumhe bhi achha lagega aur woh tumse aur zyada pyaar karenge.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rohan ke dadaji ne usko ek khubsurat kahani sunayi. Rohan ne poori dhyaan se suni aur phir apne dadaji ko gale laga liya. Dadaji ne usko kaha, "Tumne meri baat sunne ka time diya, isliye tumhe yeh kahani sunane ka mauqa mila."

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar tumhari koi baat koi bade insaan ne mana kar di ho, to tum kya karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Bado ki baat sunna aur unki izzat karna tumhe bhi achha insaan banayega.
