# Day 14 — **Apni cheezein doosron ke sath share karna**

**Concept:**
Bachon, yaad rakhna ke har cheez humari apni nahi hoti. Jo cheezein humare paas hain, unhe doosron ke sath share karna bhi seekhna zaroori hai. Jaise agar tumhare paas ek naya pencil hai, toh usko doosre bachay ko bhi dikhakar use karne do. Isse doosron ko bhi khushi milti hai aur tumhare dil mein bhi khushiyan aati hain.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rohan ke paas ek naya football tha. Usne usko apne dost Ali ko bhi dikhaya. Ali ne bhi us football se khela aur dono ne milkar khub maza liya. Rohan ko samajh aaya ke share karne se doosron ko bhi khushi milti hai aur uska dil bhi khush ho gaya.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
Tumhare paas kon si cheez hai jo tum doosron ke sath share karte ho?

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Share karna seekhna ek achha akhlaq hai. Isse doosron ke dil bhi khush hote hain aur tumhara dil bhi.
