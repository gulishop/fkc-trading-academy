# Day 15 — **Sabar rakhna aur baari ka intezar karna**

**Concept:**
Bachon, kabhi-kabhi humein cheezon ka intezar karna hota hai, jaise traffic light ka green hone ka, ya kisi dost ka game khatam karne ka. Is waqt sabar rakhna seekhna bahut zaroori hai. Jab hum sabar rakhte hain, toh humare dil ko bhi chain milta hai, aur doosron ko bhi achha lagta hai.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rahul ne apne dost ke saath board game khelna tha. Lekin uska dost pehle hi game khatam kar raha tha. Rahul ko gussa aaya, lekin usne apne aap ko samjha liya. Usne socha, "Agar main ab gussa karunga, toh game ka maza hi kharab ho jayega." Thoda intezar karne ke baad, uska dost game khatam karke uske paas aaya, aur dono ne mil kar game khela. Kya yeh sabar ka result accha nahi raha?

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
Beta, tumhein kabhi koi cheez ka intezar karna pade toh tum kya karoge?

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Sabar rakhna ek shaan hai! Agar tum sabar karoge, toh doosre bhi tumse khush honge.
