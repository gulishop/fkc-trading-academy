# Day 13 — **Sach bolna kyun zaroori hai?**

**Concept:**
Bachay, sach bolna ek bohat hi achha aur zaroori aadab hai. Jab tum sach bolte ho, toh log tum par bharosa karte hain aur tumhari baat sunte hain. Sach bolne se tumhara dil bhi saaf rehta hai, aur kisi ko bhi duniya mein chupane ki zaroorat nahi hoti.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rohan ne apne dost ke sath football khela. Usne galati se ball ko zyada zor se maar diya, jisse uske dost ka glass tut gaya. Rohan ko darr lag raha tha ke uske dost ke abbu usko pyaar se nahi lenge. Lekin uske dost ne kaha, "Rohan, tum sach bol do, main tumhe maaf kar dunga." Rohan ne sach bola, aur dono ne mil kar glass theek karwaya. Ab dono ki dosti aur mazboot ho gayi!

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
Agar tumhari koi galti ho jaye, toh tum kya karoge? Sach bologe ya chup raho ge?

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Hamesha sach bolna chahiye, chahe kuch bhi ho. Sach bolne se tumhara dil hamesha saaf rehta hai!
