# Day 2 — "Badey ki izzat, baat ka samman"

**Concept:**
Badey log—jaise ammi, abbu, dadi, nana—humari dekhbhaal karte hain. Unki baat sunna, unse izzat se pesh aana, aur unki madad karna hamari zimmedari hai. Agar woh koi baat poochhein, toh humara farz hai ke hum dhyaan se sunain.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rohan ke abbu ne poocha, "Beta, tumhara homework ho gaya?" Rohan ne haath utha kar kaha, "Ji abbu, maine sab kar liya!" Abbu ne muskurate hue kaha, "Bahut achha! Ab toh tum badey ho gaye ho." Rohan ko apne abbu ki izzat karke bahut khushi hui.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar tumhari koi baat main sun rahi hoon, toh tum kya karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Badey ki izzat karna, unki baat sunna, aur unki madad karna hamari shaan hai!
