# Day 19 — **Ghussa aane par khud ko kaise sambhalna hai?**

**Concept:**
Bachay, ghussa aana normal hai—har koi kabhi-kabhi gusse mein ho jata hai. Lekin jab gussa zyada ho jaye, toh hum apne aap ko thoda thahra ke dekh sakte hain. Aap apni saans ko gintein, ek glass pani piyein, ya apne dil ki baat kisi trusted bade ko batayein. Yeh sab aapki madad karenge ke aap khud par control rakhein.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rohan ko school mein ek bachay ne uski pencil tod di. Rohan ka gussa phat gaya! Usne apni saans ko 5 tak gina, aur phir apne teacher ko bataya. Teacher ne usko samjhaya, aur Rohan ko bhi acha laga ke usne apne gusse ko theek tareeke se sambhal liya.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar tumhe koi cheez pareshan kar rahi ho, toh tum kya karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Ghussa aana normal hai, lekin usko theek tareeke se nikalna zyada zaroori hai!
