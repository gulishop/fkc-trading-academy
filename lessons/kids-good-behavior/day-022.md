# Day 22 — **Bado ki izzat karna aur unki baat sunna**

**Concept:**
Bado ki izzat karna hamari culture ka bhi ek khubsurat hissa hai. Unki baat sunna, unki madad karna, aur unse pyaar se pesh aana seekhna chahiye. Bado ka experience zyada hota hai, isliye unki baat sunne se hum bhi bohat kuch seekh sakte hain. Unse pyaar aur respect ka matlab yeh nahi ke agar koi cheez aapko pasand na aaye toh use karna hi pade—agar aapko koi cheez theek na lage, toh bhi aap unse respect se baat kar sakte hain.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Aunty ne Ayaan ke ghar aakar kaha, "Beta, tumhara school ka homework poora ho gaya?" Ayaan ne kaha, "Nahi aunty, main abhi kar raha tha." Aunty ne muskurate hue kaha, "Thoda jaldi kar lena, kal subah school ka time ho jayega." Ayaan ne smile karke kaha, "Ji aunty, main try karunga." Uske baad Ayaan ne jaldi se apna homework poora kar liya. Aunty ko dekh kar usko yaad aaya ke bado ki baat sunna bhi seekhna chahiye.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar tumhari koi teacher ya dada-dadi koi advice dein, toh tum kya karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Bado ki izzat karna aur unki baat sunna hamari zindagi ko aur bhi khushiyon se bhar deta hai.
