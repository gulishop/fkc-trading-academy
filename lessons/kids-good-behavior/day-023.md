# Day 23 — **Sach bolna kyun zaroori hai?**

**Concept:**
Bachay, sach bolna ek bohat hi achha aur imaan wala kaam hai. Jab tum sach bolte ho, toh log tum par bharosa karte hain aur tumhara dil bhi saaf rehta hai. Jhoot bolne se sirf pareshaniyan hi aati hain, isliye hamesha sach hi bolna chahiye.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rohan ne ek din apne dost ki cycle chura li. Usne apne abbu se jhoot bola ke cycle uski nahi hai. Lekin jab cycle ka malik usko dhundta hua aaya, toh Rohan ko bohat sharm aayi. Usne sach bol diya, aur cycle wapas kar di. Uske abbu ne usko maaf kar diya aur Rohan ne promise kiya ke aage se hamesha sach hi bolega.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar tum kisi galti ke baad sach bologe, toh kya tumhe lagta hai ke hum tumse zyada pyaar karenge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Hamesha sach bolna chahiye, chahe kuch bhi ho. Sach bolna tumhara imaan hai!
