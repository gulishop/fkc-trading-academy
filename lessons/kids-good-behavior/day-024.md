# Day 24 — **Apni cheezein doosron ke sath share karna**

**Concept:**
Bachon, yaad hai jab tum apni favorite toy ya pencil le kar school jate ho? Agar tum usko apne dost ke sath share karoge, toh woh bhi khush hoga aur tumhe bhi khushi milegi! Share karna ek aisa khubsurti kaam hai jo sabko pasand hai. Isse doosron ke dil bhi khush hote hain aur tumhara bhi dil khush rehta hai.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rohan ko ek naya coloring book mila tha. Usne poora din usko dekh kar khush raha. Agle din jab uska dost Ali ne usko poochha, "Rohan, kya main bhi isko thoda use kar sakta hoon?" Rohan ne socha, "Agar main share karunga, toh Ali bhi khush ho jayega." Usne apna coloring book Ali ko diya. Ali ne bhi usko bohat pasand kiya aur dono ne mil kar usko rang diya. Dono ko bohat maza aaya!

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, tumhare pas koi aisa cheez hai jo tum apne dost ke sath share karna pasand karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Share karna ek khubsurti kaam hai, lekin yaad rakhna ki tumhare jism ka hissa sirf tumhara hai. Agar koi tumse share karne ke liye zyada zor de, toh foran ammi/abbu ko batana.
