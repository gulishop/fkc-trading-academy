# Day 16 — **Apna saman khud sametna aur safai rakhna**

**Concept:**
Beta, tumhari cheezein tumhari zindagi ko aasan banati hain. Apna saman khud sametna aur room/safai rakhna sirf "gandi cheez" nahi, balki tumhari aadat bhi hai jo tumhe responsible banati hai. Jab tum apna saman rakhte ho, to tumhe pata hota hai ke tumhara sab kuch apne paas hai, aur tumhe koi cheez khojne ki tension nahi rehti.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Arham ne school se aate hi bag khol kar sab cheezein khidki par rakh di. Uske abbu ne poocha, "Beta, tumhara bag itna khali kyun hai?" Arham ne hansa kar kaha, "Abbu, maine sab cheezein apne jagah rakh di hain!" Uske abbu ne usko gale lagaya aur kaha, "Wah! Tum to ek responsible bachay ho."

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
Beta, agar tum apna saman khud sametoge to tumhe kya maza aayega?

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhara room tumhara "safe space" hai—usko saaf aur saaf rakhna tumhara kaam hai!
