# Day 6 — "Apna saman khud sametna aur safai rakhna"

**Concept:**
Bachon, jaise hum apne kapde, khilone aur kitaaben saaf rakhte hain, waise hi humein apna poora saman bhi thik se rakhna hai. Isse na sirf chhota sa time bachta hai, balki humein bhi pata rehta hai ke kya kahan hai. Safai rakhna bhi ek acha aadaab hai, jisse hum sabko khush rakhte hain.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Aman ke room mein har cheez bikharri thi. Ek din uske abbu ne kaha, "Aman, aaj tum apne khilone khud samet lo." Aman ne samjha ke yeh koi game hai. Usne sab khilone ikattha kiye, aur dekha ke room bhi saaf lag raha hai. Ab usko khushii hui ke usne apna kaam khud kiya!

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, tum apne khilone ya kapde khud sametne mein maza aata hai ya nahi?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Har cheez ko uski jagah par rakhna, taake sabko asaani ho aur tum bhi khush raho!
