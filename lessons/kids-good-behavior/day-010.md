# Day 10 — **Jo mila us par khush rehna**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke shukar guzar hona kya hai. Shukar guzar hona matlab hai jo cheez humein milti hai, usse khush rehna. Agar tumhein koi naya khel, kapda, ya khana pasand hai, toh uske liye Allah ka shukar ada karna. Zindagi mein har cheez ka apna maza hai—chhoti cheezein bhi!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Ameer ke paas ek naya cycle tha. Usne usko dekh kar khush hona chahiye, lekin usko ek aur cycle aur achhi dikh rahi thi. Usne apne abbu se kaha, "Mera cycle itna acha nahi hai." Abbu ne muskurate hue kaha, "Ameer, tumhare cycle se tum rasta talash sikh rahe ho, isliye yeh cycle tumhare liye sabse achhi hai. Shukar guzar ho."

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
Tumhein kya cheez sabse zyada pasand hai jo tumhare paas hai?

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Jo tumhare paas hai, uske liye shukar guzar rehna. Zindagi ko khushiyon se bhara hona seekhna.
