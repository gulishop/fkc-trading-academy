# Day 20 — **Shukar guzar hona — jo mila us par khush rehna**

**Concept:**
Bachon, yaad rakhna ke zindagi mein har cheez humari pasand se nahi hoti. Kabhi kabhi humein jo milta hai, usse khush rehna seekhna chahiye. Jaise agar tumhein koi naya khelna ya khilona mila, to usse poora maza lo. Agar koi cheez tumhein pasand nahi, to usko bura mat samajhna — sabki apni pasand hoti hai!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rohan ko school se wapas aate hi pata chala ke uska dost ne usko ek naya football gift kiya hai. Rohan ko football pasand nahi tha, usko cricket zyada acha lagta hai. Usne pehle toh thoda udaas hua, lekin phir usne socha, "Chalo, is football se khel kar dekhte hain!" Usne apne dost ke sath football khela aur usko bhi maza aaya. Pata chala ke har cheez ka apna maza hota hai!

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar tumhein koi cheez pasand nahi aati, to tum kya karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Jo cheez tumhein milti hai, usse poora maza lo — chahe woh koi bhi cheez ho!
