# Day 3 — **Alibaba Par Trusted Suppliers Kaise Dhunde (Step-by-Step Guide)**

**Concept:**
Alibaba Amazon FBA ka backbone hai—yahan se hi aapka product source hota hai. Lekin **scam suppliers** se bachna zaroori hai. Aaj hum **verified suppliers** dhundne ke 3 asaan steps seekhenge: **Gold Supplier** filter, **Trade Assurance** check, aur **Factory Audit** kaise kare.

**Example:**
1. Alibaba.com par jaye → Search bar mein product likh (e.g., "Wireless Charger").
2. **Filters** lagaye: "Gold Supplier" (verified business), "Trade Assurance" (payment protection).
3. Supplier ki profile check kare: **Years in business**, **Response time**, aur **Product certifications** (e.g., CE, FCC).

**Practice:**
Apne **Day 2 ke product** (e.g., "Portable Bluetooth Speaker") ke liye Alibaba par **3 suppliers** dhundo. Har ek ke baare mein **Gold Supplier status** aur **Trade Assurance** confirm karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **Excel sheet** banaye jismein 3 suppliers ke naam, Gold Supplier status, aur Trade Assurance availability likhna hai.

**Answer Key:**
Gold Supplier, Trade Assurance, Response time, Factory audit
