# Day 10 — **Amazon FBA Mein Profit & Loss Track Karne Ka Complete Guide**

**Concept:**
Amazon FBA mein profit track karna zaroori hai taake pata chale ke business profitable hai ya nahi. Har sale ka cost (product cost, Amazon fees, PPC, shipping, etc.) nikal kar net profit calculate karna hai. Isse future decisions (pricing, scaling, product selection) mein help milti hai.

**Example:**
Maana aapne ek product ₹500 mein khareeda, Amazon fees ₹150 hai, PPC par ₹50 kharch hue, to total cost ₹700 hua. Agar product ₹1200 mein becha, to profit = 1200 - 700 = ₹500. Agar margin kam hai, to pricing ya supplier negotiation par sochna hoga.

**Practice:**
Apne ek product ka complete cost break-down nikalo (product cost + Amazon fees + PPC + shipping + any other expenses). Excel ya Google Sheets mein ek simple table banake calculate karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek simple **Profit Margin Calculator** spreadsheet banayein jisme:
- Product cost
- Amazon fees (referral + FBA fees)
- PPC cost
- Shipping cost
- Net profit (sale price - total cost)
Input karke automatically margin % nikalne ka formula add karen.
