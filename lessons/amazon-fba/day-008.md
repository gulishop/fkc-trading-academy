# Day 8 — **Amazon FBA Mein PPC Ads Ko Optimize Karna (Beginners Ke Liye Advanced Guide)**

**Concept:**
PPC ads ko chalane ke baad sabse zaroori kaam hai unko *optimize* karna. Isse matlab hai ke jo ads chal rahi hain unki performance check karke unmein improvements karna taake cost kam ho aur sales badhe. Iske liye keywords, bids, and targeting ko adjust karna hota hai.

**Example:**
Maana aapne ek product "Wireless Charger" ke liye PPC chalaya hai. Aapne *auto targeting* use kiya tha. Ab aapko *search term report* check karna hai. Wahan se pata chalta hai ke "fast wireless charger" aur "qi wireless charger" sabse zyada conversions de rahe hain. In keywords ko *manual targeting* mein add karke bid increase karo. Baki low-performing keywords ko pause kar do.

**Practice:**
1. Apne Amazon Seller Central mein jao.
2. *Campaign Manager* mein apne PPC campaign ko select karo.
3. *Search Term Report* download karo (last 30 days ka).
4. Top 3 keywords jo sabse zyada conversions de rahe hain, unko *manual targeting* campaign mein add karo.
5. Baki sab keywords ko pause kar do.

**Mini Project:**
Apne ek PPC campaign ke *search term report* ko analyze karke top 5 best-performing keywords ko identify karo. Unke liye bids ko 20% increase karo aur baki sabko pause kar do. 7 din baad check karo ke kya CTR ya conversions mein improvement aaya.

**Answer Key:**
search term report, manual targeting, pause low-performing, increase bids
