**Day 2 — Product Research: Demand, Competition aur Margin Kaise Check Karein**

**Concept:**
Product research Amazon FBA ka sabse important step hai. Ismein hum 3 cheezon par focus karte hain:
1. **Demand:** Kya log yeh product khareed rahe hain? (Search volume, BSR, trends)
2. **Competition:** Kitne sellers hain? Kitne reviews hain? (Low-competition niches dhundhein)
3. **Margin:** Profit kitna bachega? (Cost vs. Selling Price vs. Amazon fees)

**Example:**
Maana aap "Wireless Charging Pad" bechna chahte hain. Aapko pata karna hai:
- **Demand:** Amazon par "Wireless Charging Pad" search karne par 50,000+ monthly searches hain (Helium 10 ya Jungle Scout se check karein).
- **Competition:** Top 10 listings mein 1,000+ reviews hain → High competition. Lekin niche product "Wireless Charging Pad for Car" mein 500+ searches aur 200+ reviews hain → Better opportunity.
- **Margin:** Agar supplier se Rs. 1,000 mein milta hai, aur aap Rs. 2,500 mein bechte hain, toh Amazon fees (15%) + shipping = Rs. 2,000 cost → Rs. 500 profit per unit.

**Practice:**
Apne laptop ya phone se **Helium 10** (free trial) ya **Jungle Scout** (Chrome extension) install karein. Koi ek product category chunein (e.g., kitchen accessories) aur 3-5 products ka **demand, competition, aur estimated margin** check karein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **Google Sheet** banayein jismein aap 5 products ke liye:
- Product Name
- Monthly Searches (Demand)
- Top 3 Sellers’ Reviews (Competition)
- Estimated Profit (Margin)
- Final Verdict (Buy/Don’t Buy)
Fill karein.

**Answer Key:** demand, competition, margin, niche
