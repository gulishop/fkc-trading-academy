# Day 16 — **Amazon FBA Mein "Amazon SEO" Ka Complete Guide (Search Rank Badhane Ke 5 Powerful Tips)**

**Concept:**
Amazon SEO ka matlab hai apne product listing ko optimize karna taake Amazon ke search algorithm mein aapka product top par aaye. Iske liye keywords, title, bullet points, aur backend keywords ka sahi istemal zaroori hai. Bina SEO ke aapka product chhupa reh jata hai, chahe woh kitna bhi accha ho.

**Example:**
Ek seller "Wireless Charger" bechta hai. Usne title mein sirf "Wireless Charger" likha, lekin jab usne "Fast Wireless Charger 10W Qi Certified for iPhone Samsung" likha, toh uska ranking 10 se 3 tak badh gaya.

**Practice:**
Apne product ke lien se "Title" aur "Bullet Points" ko edit karke 3-5 relevant keywords add karein. (Jaise "Wireless Charger" ki jagah "Fast Wireless Charger 10W Qi Certified for iPhone Samsung" likhein).

**Mini Project:**
Ek Excel sheet banaen jismein 10 relevant keywords likhein jo aapke product ke liye best hon. Har keyword ke peeche search volume (Amazon Keyword Tool se) bhi likhein.

**Answer Key:**
Fast Wireless Charger, 10W Qi Certified, iPhone Samsung Compatible, High-Speed Charging, Universal Wireless Charger
