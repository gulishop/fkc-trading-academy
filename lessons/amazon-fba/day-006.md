# Day 6 — **Amazon FBA Mein Shipment Bhejna: Step-by-Step Guide (FBA vs FBM, Packaging, Labels, Shipping Plan)**

**Concept:**
Amazon FBA shipment bhejna ek critical step hai jo supplier se product receive karne se leke Amazon warehouse tak pohanchane tak ka process cover karta hai. FBA (Fulfillment by Amazon) mein hum apne product Amazon ke warehouse mein bhejte hain jo baad mein customer orders fulfill karte hain. FBM (Fulfillment by Merchant) mein hum khud shipping handle karte hain. Aaj hum FBA shipment ka complete process seekhenge: packaging, labels, shipping plan, aur Amazon ke warehouse tak pohanchane ka tarika.

**Example:**
1. **Packaging:** Apne product ko Amazon ke guidelines ke hisab se pack karein (e.g., 6-sided box, 2-inch space on all sides, no hanging polybags).
2. **Labels:** Har box aur pallet par Amazon FBA shipment labels lagayein (FNSKU barcode, shipment ID).
3. **Shipping Plan:** Amazon Seller Central mein shipment create karein, warehouse address select karein, aur carrier (UPS, FedEx) ya Amazon Partnered Carrier choose karein.

**Practice:**
Apne supplier se poochhein ke woh Amazon FBA ke hisab se packaging kar sakte hain ya nahi. Agar nahi, toh aap khud packaging ka arrangement karein aur Amazon ke guidelines follow karein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek dummy shipment plan banayein Amazon Seller Central mein (agar account hai toh real, warna hypothetical). Shipment ID, warehouse address, carrier, aur product details fill karein.

**Answer Key:**
FNSKU labels, 6-sided box, Amazon Partnered Carrier, Seller Central shipment plan
