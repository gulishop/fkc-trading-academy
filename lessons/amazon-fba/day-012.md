# Day 12 — **Amazon FBA Mein "Branding" Ka Complete Guide (Logo, Packaging, Brand Story)**

**Concept:**
Amazon FBA mein "branding" sirf ek logo ya naam nahi hai—yeh ek customer ko loyal banane ka process hai. Strong branding se aapki product ki pehchaan banti hai, trust banta hai, aur competition se alag nazar aate hain. Isme logo design, packaging, brand story, aur customer experience shamil hain.

**Example:**
Ek chhota sa "organic herbal tea" ka brand lo. Aapka logo green aur earthy colors mein hoga (nature ka feel), packaging biodegradable material ki hogi, aur product box par ek chhoti si "story" likhi hogi: *"Har cup se aap nature ko samajh sakte hain."* Isse customer ko emotional connection milti hai.

**Practice:**
Apne product ya niche ke liye ek simple logo design karne ki koshish karen. Canva ya Looka jaise free tools use karen. Logo ko aapki product ki personality reflect karna chahiye (e.g., premium, eco-friendly, tech-savvy).

**Mini Project:**
Ek "brand style guide" taiyaar karen:
1. Logo (color scheme + font)
2. Packaging design ka rough sketch
3. 3-line brand story
4. Product ke saath aane wale ek chhote note ka draft

**Answer Key:**
logo, packaging, brand story, customer trust
