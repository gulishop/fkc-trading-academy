# Day 14 — **Amazon FBA Mein "Launch Strategy" Ka Complete Guide (First 7 Days Ka Plan)**

**Concept:**
Launch strategy Amazon FBA mein product ki initial sales aur ranking boost karne ka blueprint hai. Pehle 7 dinon mein aapko PPC ads, promotions, aur customer engagement ka balance banana hai. Agar aapka product listing strong hai lekin sales nahi aa rahi, to launch strategy hi usko "visible" banati hai. Ismein timing, budget allocation, aur customer response ka analysis shamil hai.

**Example:**
Maana aapka product "Wireless Charging Pad" hai. Launch ke pehle din:
1. **PPC Ads:** $10/day budget rakhein, "Sponsored Products" ads ko "Auto" se "Manual" par switch karein (keywords: "wireless charger", "fast charging pad").
2. **Promotions:** Amazon Coupons ya Lightning Deals ke zariye 10% discount offer karein (limited time).
3. **Social Proof:** Friends/family se honest reviews likhwaein (Amazon ke TOS follow karke).
4. **Monitor:** Daily PPC performance check karein aur CTR/ACoS adjust karein.

**Practice:**
Apne product ke liye ek **7-day launch plan** banayein (timing, budget, aur actions ke saath). Example:
- Day 1-2: PPC ads launch + 10% discount coupon.
- Day 3-4: Social media par "unboxing" video share karein (agar possible ho).
- Day 5-7: Reviews increase karne ke liye follow-up emails bhejen (Amazon se "Request a Review" button use karein).

**Mini Project:**
Apne product ke listing ko **Amazon’s "Early Reviewer Program"** (agar available ho) ya **"Vine Program"** ke liye apply karein. Agar nahi ho, to apne network se 5-10 logon se honest reviews likhwaein (Amazon ke guidelines follow karke).

**Answer Key:**
PPC setup, discount coupon, social media promotion, reviews follow-up
