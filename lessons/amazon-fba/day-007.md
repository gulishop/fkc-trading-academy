# Day 7 — **Amazon FBA Mein PPC Ads Kaise Shuru Karen (Beginner-Friendly Guide)**

**Concept:**
Amazon PPC (Pay-Per-Click) ads aapke product ko search results mein upar dikhane ka sabse asaan tareeka hai. Jab koi customer aapke product se related keyword search karega, toh aapka ad show hoga aur aapko sirf tab pay karna hoga jab koi us par click karega. Yeh beginner se intermediate level par ads chalane ka tareeka hai, jisse aapka product rank improve ho aur sales badhein.

**Example:**
Maana aapne ek "Wireless Bluetooth Headphones" ka product launch kiya hai. Aap Amazon PPC mein ek campaign banayenge jisme aap "bluetooth headphones" ya "wireless earbuds" jaise keywords ko target karenge. Agar koi customer in keywords par search karega, toh aapka ad show hoga. Agar wo ad par click karega, toh aapko us click ka kuch amount pay karna hoga.

**Practice:**
1. Apne Amazon Seller Central mein jaayein.
2. "Advertising" tab par click karein aur "Create Campaign" select karein.
3. Campaign type "Sponsored Products" choose karein.
4. Apne product ko select karein aur daily budget set karein (shuru mein $5-$10 daily budget rakh sakte hain).
5. Relevant keywords add karein (jaise "bluetooth headphones", "wireless earbuds").
6. Bid amount set karein (shuru mein $0.50-$1.00 per click).

**Mini Project:**
Ek PPC campaign setup karein aur uske performance ko 7 din tak monitor karein. Har din ke clicks, impressions, aur spend ko note karein. Iske baad, jo keywords se best performance mil rahi hai, un par bid badhaayein aur jo keywords se koi click nahi aaya, unko hataayein ya bid kam karein.

**Answer Key:**
Campaign setup, keywords selection, bid management, performance tracking
