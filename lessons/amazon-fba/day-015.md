# Day 15 — **Amazon FBA Mein "Amazon Brand Registry" Ka Complete Guide (Trademark Se Brand Protect Karein)**

**Concept:**
Amazon Brand Registry aapki brand ko protect karta hai counterfeit products, hijacked listings, aur unauthorized sellers se. Iske liye aapke paas registered trademark hona zaroori hai (USPTO, EUIPO, ya kisi recognized country ka trademark). Yeh aapko extra tools deta hai—jaise A+ Content, Brand Analytics, aur Brand Dashboard—jisse aapki brand credibility aur sales badhti hai.

**Example:**
1. **Trademark Registration:** Pehle USPTO (USA) ya kisi relevant country mein trademark apply karo (₹50,000–₹1,50,000 tak cost hai).
2. **Amazon Brand Registry Apply:** [brandregistry.amazon.com](https://brandregistry.amazon.com) par jake trademark number aur brand details submit karo.
3. **Verification:** Amazon team aapki brand details verify karega (24–48 hours lag sakte hain).

**Practice:**
Apne brand name ka trademark check karo (USPTO ki website par) aur agar available ho to apply karne ka process start karo.

**Mini Project:**
Ek simple trademark application form bharne ka draft taiyar karo (brand name, logo, product category likh kar).

**Answer Key:**
Trademark, USPTO, Brand Registry, A+ Content
