# Day 5 — **Amazon Seller Account Setup: Step-by-Step Registration & Verification**

**Concept:**
Amazon seller account banana FBA business ka legal foundation hai. Yeh account create karne ke liye aapko personal ya business details (ID proof, bank account, tax info) ready rakhni hoti hain. Agar aap individual seller hain to "Individual" account choose karen, warna "Professional" (₹2,999/month) jo bulk listings aur ads ke liye zaroori hai.

**Example:**
1. [Amazon Seller Central](https://sellercentral.amazon.in/) par jayein → "Start Selling" par click karen.
2. Apna email ID aur password set karen → "Your information" mein business type (Individual/Professional) select karen.
3. Verification ke liye Aadhaar/PAN card upload karen → bank account details add karen (IFSC code, account number).
4. Tax details (GSTIN agar applicable ho) enter karen → "Submit" par click karen.

**Practice:**
Amazon Seller Central par jayein aur "Register Now" par click karke apna account create karen (agar pehle se nahi hai). Agar professional account chahiye to ₹2,999/month plan select karen.

**Mini Project:**
Ek Google Sheet banayein jismein aap apne seller account ke liye required documents ki list banayein (e.g., PAN card, bank statement, GSTIN). Har document ki expiry date bhi note karen.

**Answer Key:**
PAN card, bank details, tax info, verification email
