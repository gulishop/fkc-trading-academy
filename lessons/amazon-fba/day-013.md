# Day 13 — **Amazon FBA Mein "Supplier Negotiation" Ka Complete Guide (Alibaba Se Best Deal Kaise Karein)**

**Concept:**
Supplier negotiation Amazon FBA ka ek crucial step hai jismein aap apne product ki cost, quality aur delivery terms par best deal secure karte hain. Alibaba par suppliers ke saath negotiation ka art hai—pata karna ki kaun sa supplier flexible hai, minimum order quantity (MOQ) kam karna, aur unit price reduce karna. Yeh skill aapki profit margins badha sakti hai.

**Example:**
Maana aap ek wireless charger ke liye supplier dhund rahe hain. Alibaba par ek supplier ka price $5 hai with MOQ 500 units. Aap unse baat karte hain:
1. **MOQ Reduction:** "Agar main 300 units ka order dunga to price kya hoga?"
2. **Unit Price Negotiation:** "Agar main 1000 units ka order dunga to $4.5 per unit kaise milega?"
3. **Payment Terms:** "50% advance aur 50% delivery par kaise hoga?"

**Practice:**
Apne kisi existing ya future product ke liye Alibaba par 3 suppliers ko message karein aur:
- MOQ reduce karne ke liye poochhein.
- Unit price discount ke liye negotiate karein.
- Payment terms (T/T, LC, etc.) discuss karein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **Supplier Negotiation Script** taiyaar karein jismein aap:
1. Apne product ka clear description likhein.
2. 3 negotiation points (MOQ, price, payment) mention karein.
3. Supplier ko professional aur friendly message likhein (template Alibaba par available hai).

**Answer Key:**
MOQ reduction, unit price discount, payment terms, advance payment
