# Day 9 — **Amazon FBA Mein "Off-Page SEO" Se Sales Aur Ranking Kaise Badhayein (Backlinks, Social Media, Email Marketing)**

**Concept:**
Amazon ke algorithm mein "off-page SEO" ka bhi role hai—yani aapke product ke *baahar* se hone wali activities se ranking aur sales par asar. Ismein shamil hain: backlinks (product page par pointing links), social media promotion, aur email marketing. Ye sabhi cheezain Amazon ke bahar hoti hain lekin aapke product ki credibility aur reach badhati hain, jisse ranking improve hoti hai.

**Example:**
Ek seller ne "Wireless Charging Pad" becha. Usne Facebook Groups mein 100+ posts share ki, 30+ relevant blogs se backlinks liye (e.g., "tech gadgets ke liye best charging pads"), aur 500+ customers ko email bheje "Thank You" ke saath ek discount code. Result: Product ka ranking 15 se 3rd position par aaya aur sales 40% badhi.

**Practice:**
Aaj hi ek relevant Facebook Group ya Reddit community dhundein (e.g., "Amazon Sellers" ya "Tech Deals"). Apne product ki category ya niche se related post likhein—*directly* promote na karein, balki value add karein (e.g., "Mera experience: Wireless chargers ka best option kaun sa hai?").

**Mini Project:**
Ek simple **email campaign** design karein:
1. 3 email templates likhein (Welcome, Follow-up, Discount Offer).
2. 10 fake customer emails (Excel mein) par yeh templates send karein (use karein [Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com/) ya [Canva Email](https://www.canva.com/emails/)).
3. Track karein kaun sa email sabse zyada open hua.

**Answer Key:**
Facebook Groups mein engagement, 3 email templates, 10 test emails
