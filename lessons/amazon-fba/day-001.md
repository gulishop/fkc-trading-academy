# Day 1 — Amazon FBA Kya Hai? Basics Se Shuru

**Concept:**
Amazon FBA (Fulfillment by Amazon) ek business model hai jahan aap apna product Amazon ke warehouses mein store karte hain, aur Amazon usko pack, ship, aur customer service bhi handle karta hai. Aapko sirf product research, sourcing, aur marketing par focus karna hota hai. Iske liye aapko Amazon ko fees dete hain, lekin aapko global reach, trusted brand, aur Prime customers milte hain.

**Example:**
Maana aap "Wireless Charging Pad" bechna chahte hain. Aap isko Alibaba se khareedte hain, Amazon ke warehouse mein bhejte hain, aur listing banate hain. Jab koi customer order karta hai, Amazon usko pack karke deliver karta hai, aur aapko payment Amazon se milta hai (minus fees).

**Practice:**
Apne mobile ya laptop se Amazon App ya Website par jayein, aur "Amazon FBA" search karen. Dekhein ki FBA sellers ke products ka kya structure hota hai (title, images, price, etc.).

**Mini Project:**
Apne mind mein ek product choose karen jo aap FBA par bech sakte hain. Uske liye 3 reasons likhein kyun yeh product Amazon par bechne layak hai (demand, competition, ya margin ke hisaab se).

**Answer Key:**
product research, warehouse, Prime, fees
