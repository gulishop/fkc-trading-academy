# Day 1 — Mera jism sirf mera hai

**Concept:**
Bachay, tumhara jism tumhara hai—sirf tumhara! Koi bhi insaan tumse poochhe bina tumhare sharir ko chho sakta hai, tumhe gale laga sakta hai, ya tumhe kiss kar sakta hai. Agar tumhe koi cheez achchi nahi lagti, toh tumhe "na" kehne ka poora haq hai. Koi bhi tumhe "secret" rakhne ke liye majboor nahi kar sakta.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Aman ko school se aate waqt ek aadmi ne kaha, "Beta, mere gale lag jao!" Aman ne socha, "Mujhe yeh pasand nahi," aur usne "na" keh diya. Us aadmi ne kaha, "Arre, yeh toh chhota sa baap hai!" Aman ne apne teacher ko bataya, aur teacher ne us aadmi ko samjhaya.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi tumse koi cheez pooche bina tumhare sharir ko chho le, toh tum kya karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhara jism tumhara hai—kisi ko bhi tumse poochhe bina chho na! Agar koi cheez ajeeb lage, toh foran ammi-abbu ko batana.
