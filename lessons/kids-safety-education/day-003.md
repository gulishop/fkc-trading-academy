# Day 3 — Private parts private kyun hote hain?

**Concept:**
Tumhare private parts (jaise kapde ke neeche wale hisse) sirf tumhare hain, isliye woh private hote hain. Jaise tumhare toothbrush ya towel sirf tumhare hote hain, waise hi tumhare private parts bhi sirf tumhare hote hain. Kisi ko bhi unhe dekhne, chhoone ya unke baare mein baat karne ka haq nahi hai.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rahul ne apne school ke ek dost ko kaha ke "Mera private part itna lamba hai!" Uske dost ne usko chheda aur sabko bataya. Rahul ko bura laga aur usne apne teacher ko bataya. Teacher ne kaha, "Private parts private hote hain, unke baare mein kisi ko batana nahi chahiye."

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi tumhe koi ajeeb baat ya chhedkhani kare, toh tum kya karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Private parts private hote hain, unhe kisi ko bhi dekhne, chhoone ya unke baare mein baat karne ka haq nahi hai. Agar koi ajeeb baat kare, toh foran ammi abbu ko batana.
