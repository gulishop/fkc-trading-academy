# Day 7 — "Jaan-pehchaan wale se bhi khabardar rehna"

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke agar koi aisa insaan jo tumhe pehchaanata hai, jaise chacha, maamu, teacher ya koi dost ka bhai, aur woh tumse aise kaam karwana chahta hai jo tumhe ajeeb lage ya ghalat lage, toh tumhe "NA" kehna hai. Koi bhi insaan tumhare jism par haath nahin daal sakta, chahe woh tumhare liye kitna hi pyara kyon na ho!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Areeba ko school se aate waqt chacha ne kaha, "Areeba, mere ghar aa kar mujhe ek secret batana hai." Areeba ne socha, "Chacha toh mera pyara hai, lekin mummy ne kaha hai ke koi secret bhi ajeeb ho toh foran batana." Isliye Areeba ne chacha se kaha, "Nahi chacha, main abhi ghar ja rahi hoon." Chacha ne bhi usko chhod diya. Areeba ne yeh sab mummy ko bataya aur mummy ne usko gale laga kar kaha, "Tumne bahut achha kiya!"

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi tumse koi aise kaam karwana chahta hai jo tumhe ajeeb lage, toh tum kya karogi?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhara jism sirf tumhara hai. Koi bhi insaan tumse aise kaam nahin karwa sakta, chahe woh tumhare liye kitna hi pyara kyon na ho!
