# Day 2 — Achi choo aur buri choo mein farak

**Concept:**
Choo karna har insaan ki zindagi ka hissa hai, lekin har choo achi nahi hoti. Achi choo wo hai jo hamare jism ki saaf-safai ya dekhbhaal ke liye hoti hai, jaise haath dhona ya kapde badalna. Buri choo wo hai jo hamare jism ko nuqsan pohocha sakti hai ya humein pareshan kar sakti hai.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Bilal ne apne dost se kaha, "Mera abbu mujhe roz saaf kapde pehnate hain." Uske dost ne muskura kar kaha, "Mera bhi! Lekin ek din ek admi ne mujhe gandagi mein choo kar diya. Main ne usko roka aur kaha, 'Aap yeh kya kar rahe hain?' Usne maafi maangi." Bilal ne kaha, "Achha! Main bhi aise choo karne walon se door rehta hoon."

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi tumhein choo karne ki koshish kare, to tum kya karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Aapka jism sirf aapka hai. Koi bhi aapko choo karne ka haq nahi rakhta. Agar aisa hota hai, to turant ammi/abbu ko batana hai!
