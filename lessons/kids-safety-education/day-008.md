# Day 8 — "Video-call par ajnabi se bach kar!"

**Concept:**
Bachon, jab tum video-call par kisi se baat karte ho, toh yaad rakhna ke tumhari screen par jo bhi aata hai, woh sabhi achhe nahi hote. Koi bhi ajeeb insaan tumse baat kar raha ho, tumhari photo ya video maang raha ho, ya tumhe koi gift dene ka vaada kar raha ho—yeh sab cheezein "online safety" ke liye theek nahi hain. Tumhe kabhi bhi apne jism ya family ke baare mein aise logon ko batana nahi chahiye.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rohan ne video-call par ek "uncle" se baat ki. Uncle ne poocha, "Tumhara school kaisa hai?" Rohan ne jawab diya. Phir uncle ne kaha, "Mera beta tumhare school mein hi padhta hai, uska naam bhi Rohan hai! Kya tum usko photo bhej sakte ho?" Rohan ko yeh ajeeb laga. Usne apne abbu ko bataya. Abbu ne kaha, "Tumne bilkul theek kiya, Rohan. Kisi bhi ajnabi ko photo bhejni chahiye nahi."

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar tum video-call par kisi se baat karte ho aur woh tumse koi ajeeb cheez pooche, toh tum kya karogi?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
"Meri screen par jo hai, woh sab achhe nahi hote—agar koi ajeeb lage, toh foran ammi-abbu ko batana!"
