# Day 9 — "Dil ki baat: Apne jazbaat ko pehchanna"

**Concept:**
Bachay, aaj hum seekhenge ke apne jazbaat (feelings) ko pehchanna kitna zaroori hai! Kabhi dar lagta hai, kabhi sharm aati hai, ya phir samajh na aaye toh yeh sab normal feelings hain. Inhe pehchanna seekhna zindagi ka ek important hissa hai.

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Areeba ne ek din school mein ek ajeeb adaa dekhi. Uske dil mein dar bhi laga, sharm bhi aayi, lekin usne apne dil ki baat suni aur apni teacher ko bataya. Teacher ne usko samjhaya ke yeh sab feelings normal hain, aur uske liye aage se aur behtar tareeqe seekhaye.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar tumhe kabhi koi feeling ajeeb lage ya dar lage toh mujhe zaroor batana, kya?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhare jazbaat tumhare dil ki awaaz hain. Inhe pehchanna seekhna tumhare liye bohat zaroori hai!
