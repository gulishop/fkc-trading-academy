# Day 6 — "Trusted adult kaun hota hai aur unhe foran batana"

**Concept:**
Bachon, aaj hum seekhenge ke trusted adult kaun hote hain. Yeh woh bade log hote hain jin par tumhe poori trust hai, jaise ammi, abbu, teacher, ya dadi-dada. Agar tumhe kabhi koi cheez ajeeb lage, ya koi aapko "secret rakhne" ke liye khe, toh foran in trusted adults ko batana hai. Yeh tumhari hifazat ke liye zaroori hai!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rohan ko school ke baahar ek uncle ne chocolate di aur kaha, "Yeh hamare beech ka secret hai, kisi ko mat batana." Rohan ko yeh ajeeb laga, kyunki ammi ne kaha hai ke secrets sirf achhe hote hain. Usne foran apni teacher ko bataya. Teacher ne usko samjhaya ke trusted adults hi hote hain jinhe sab bataya ja sakta hai.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi tumhe secret rakhne ke liye khe, toh tum kya karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
Tumhara jism tumhara hai, aur tumhara dil bhi! Agar koi cheez ajeeb lage, toh foran trusted adult ko batana hai.
