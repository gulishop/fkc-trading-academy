# Day 5 — "Chhupa ke rakhne wale secrets aur asli secrets mein farak"

**Concept:**
Bachon, pata hai, kuch secrets hote hain jo hum apne dil mein rakhte hain, jaise koi surprise birthday gift ya koi naya toy. Par kuch secrets aise hote hain jo hum chhupa ke rakhte hain kyun ke woh humein darrate hain ya uncomfortable feel karate hain. Aise secrets humein kisi ko bhi batana chahiye, kyun ke humara jism sirf humara hai aur humein uski hifazat karni hai!

**Chhoti Kahani:**
Rohan ko uske chacha ne kaha, "Yeh chocolate kisi ko mat batana, secret hai!" Rohan ne socha, "Mummy ko pata chal jayega toh woh gusse ho jayengi." Lekin jab Rohan ne chocolate khate waqt apne pet mein dard mehsoos kiya, toh usko yaad aaya ke "Ajeeb choo ya dard hone par humein foran batana chahiye!" Usne apni ammi ko sab kuch bata diya, aur ammi ne uski dekhbhaal ki.

**Ammi Abbu Ke Sath Baat Karein:**
"Beta, agar koi tumse kaha ke 'Yeh secret rakhna hai,' toh tum kya karoge?"

**Yaad Rakhein:**
"Har waqt yaad rakhna: Mera jism mera hai, mujhe 'NA' kehne ka haq hai, aur koi bhi ajeeb secret ho toh foran batana hai!"
