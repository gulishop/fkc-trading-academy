# Day 18 — **Affiliate Marketing Ke Paise Kaise Track Karein (Analytics Tools & Reports)**

**Concept:**
Affiliate marketing mein paise track karna zaroori hai taake pata chale ke konsa link, content, ya traffic source sabse zyada sales la raha hai. Iske liye **analytics tools** (Google Analytics, affiliate dashboard) aur **custom reports** banane hote hain. Bina tracking ke, aapko pata nahi chale ga ke kya kaam kar raha hai aur kya nahi.

**Example:**
1. **Amazon Associates Dashboard** mein ja kar "Reports" section check karein. Yahan "Earnings" aur "Clicks" ka breakdown mile ga.
2. **Google Analytics 4 (GA4)** mein "Acquisition" > "Traffic Acquisition" par click karein. Yahan dekh sakte hain ke konsa social media platform (TikTok/Instagram) se zyada traffic aa raha hai aur uske baad kitni sales hui.

**Practice:**
Apne affiliate program (Amazon/Daraz) ke dashboard se **pichle 7 din ke clicks aur earnings ka screenshot lein**. Agar GA4 use kar rahe hain to "Traffic Sources" report ka screenshot bhi lein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **simple Excel/Google Sheets** file banayein jismein aap har din ke clicks, impressions, aur earnings note karein. Isse aapko trends samajhne mein aasani hogi.

**Answer Key:**
Clicks, Earnings, Traffic Sources, Conversion Rate
