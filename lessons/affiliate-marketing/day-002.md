**Day 2 — Affiliate Links Placement: Kahan aur Kaise Lagayein?**

**Concept:**
Affiliate links ko sahi jagah par place karna zaroori hai—nahi toh traffic bhi hai aur clicks bhi nahi. Links ko natural tarike se content mein integrate karna chahiye, taake readers ko lagay na ki tum unko "bech rahe ho". Sabse effective places hain: product reviews, comparison posts, "best of" lists, aur "how-to" guides. Har platform (blog, YouTube, social media) ke liye placement strategy alag hoti hai.

**Example:**
1. **Blog/Article:** Ek "Top 5 Budget Smartphones 2024" post mein har product ke naam ke neeche affiliate link lagao, lekin "Check Price" ya "Buy Now" button ke saath.
2. **YouTube:** Video description mein sabse pehle affiliate links dalo (Amazon Associates ke liye), phir aur details. Video ke do minute ke baad bhi link mention kar sakte ho.
3. **Instagram/Facebook:** Caption mein "Link in bio" use karo, ya phir reel ke description mein link add karo (agar allowed ho).

**Practice:**
Apne ek existing blog post ya social media post ko edit karo aur **2-3 natural affiliate links** add karo. Links ko aise place karo jaise tum kisi dost ko recommend kar rahe ho—na ki "hard sell" kar rahe ho.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **100-150 words ka micro-blog post** likho jisme ek product recommend karna hai. Post mein:
- Product ka naam aur ek short review.
- "Check Price" button (affiliate link).
- Disclosure line add karo (e.g., "Yeh post mein affiliate links hain").

**Answer Key:**
product review, natural placement, disclosure, CTA
