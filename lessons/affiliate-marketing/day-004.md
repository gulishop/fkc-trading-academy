# Day 4 — **High-Converting Affiliate Content Kaise Banayein**

**Concept:**
Affiliate content wo hai jo sirf information nahi deta, balki readers ko action lene par majboor kar deta hai. Aise content mein trust, value, aur urgency hona chahiye. Har paragraph ek step ho—problem solve karne se lekar product recommend karne tak. SEO ke saath-saath "human psychology" (jaise scarcity, social proof) bhi use karna hai.

**Example:**
Ek "Best Laptop Under 50,000" article likh rahe hain. Pehle 3 paragraphs mein:
1. Problem: "Slow performance se pareshan hain? Budget mein best laptop dhund rahe hain?"
2. Solution: "Yeh 5 laptops 2024 mein top rated hain—har ek ka pros/cons."
3. Recommendation: "Meri #1 choice hai [Product X] kyun? (Link + discount code)."
Har paragraph ka end "click here" ya "jaise hi" se ho.

**Practice:**
Apne niche ka ek 150-200 words ka draft likhein jisme ek product recommend kar rahe hain. Pehle 2 lines mein problem batayein, agle 2 mein solution, last line mein CTA (jaise "Yeh product try karne ke liye [link] par click karein").

**Mini Project:**
Ek "5 Best [Niche Product] 2024" article ka outline banayein (headings + 1-2 lines ka description har section ka). Example:
- "Budget-friendly options" (3 products)
- "Premium picks" (2 products)
- "Final verdict: Kaunsa choose karein?"

**Answer Key:**
problem, solution, urgency, CTA
