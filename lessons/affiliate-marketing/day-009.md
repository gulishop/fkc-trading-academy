# Day 9 — **Affiliate Marketing ke 3+ Income Sources Kaise Banayein (Beyond Single Program)**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke affiliate marketing mein apne income sources ko diversify kaise karein. Sirf ek program (jaise Amazon Associates) par depend rehne se risk hai. Iske bajaye 2-3 different programs, niches, aur traffic sources ka combination banayein. Har source ka apna commission structure, audience type, aur conversion rate hota hai – isliye multiple sources se aapka overall earning potential badhta hai.

**Example:**
1. **Niche Diversification:** Agar aap fitness products promote karte hain, toh sirf protein powder ke bajaye supplements, gym equipment, aur fitness courses bhi add karein.
2. **Program Diversification:** Ek hi niche mein Amazon Associates ke saath saath ClickBank, ShareASale, ya local brands ke affiliate programs join karein.
3. **Traffic Source Diversification:** Agar aapka traffic TikTok se hai, toh YouTube shorts, Pinterest, aur email list bhi use karein.

**Practice:**
Apne current affiliate links ko check karein aur 1-2 naye programs ya niches dhundein jo aapke content se match karte hain. Unke commission rates, cookie duration, aur approval process ko compare karein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek simple spreadsheet banayein jismein aapke har affiliate program ke naam, commission rate, cookie duration, aur aapki monthly earnings ka breakdown ho. Isse aapko pata chalega ke konsa program sabse zyada profitable hai.

**Answer Key:**
niche diversification, program switching, traffic sources, spreadsheet tracking
