# Day 19 — **Affiliate Programs Ke "Tiered Commission" System Ko Samajhein (Zyada Paise Kaise Kamayein)**

**Concept:**
Tiered commission system ek affiliate program ka woh model hai jahan aapki commission rate badhti hai jab aap zyada sales karte hain. Pehle tier mein 5% commission hai, lekin jab aap 100 sales kar lete hain, toh 10% milne lagti hai. Isse aapki income exponentially badhti hai. Yeh system mostly high-ticket affiliate programs (jaise hosting, SaaS, ya premium courses) mein milta hai.

**Example:**
Imagine aap "Bluehost" affiliate hain. Unka tiered commission yeh hai:
- 1-10 sales/month: 65$/sale
- 11-50 sales/month: 80$/sale
- 51+ sales/month: 100$/sale
Agar aap 50 sales kar lete hain, toh aapki commission 80$ se badh kar 100$ ho jati hai har sale par. Isse aapki monthly income 4000$ se 5000$ ho sakti hai!

**Practice:**
Apne current affiliate program ki commission structure check karo (Google search: "[Program Name] + affiliate commission"). Agar tiered system hai, toh apne dashboard mein "Performance" ya "Commission" section mein yeh details dhundho.

**Mini Project:**
Ek spreadsheet banayein jismein aap 3-4 affiliate programs ke tiered commission rates compare karo. Columns hon: Program Name, Tier 1 Commission, Tier 2 Commission, Tier 3 Commission, aur aapka target (e.g., 50 sales/month).

**Answer Key:**
tiered commission, sales volume, exponential growth, performance tiers
