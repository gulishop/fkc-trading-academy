# Day 7 — **Email Marketing Se Affiliate Sales Kaise Badhayein (Step-by-Step Guide)**

**Concept:**
Email marketing affiliate sales ka sabse powerful source hai kyun ke subscribers already trust karte hain. Yahan pehli baat hai **value-first approach** — sirf affiliate links bhejne se kaam nahi chale ga. Aapko subscribers ko educate karna hai, unki problems solve karni hain, aur phir naturally recommend karna hai. Key points: **Segmentation** (right audience ko right offer), **Engagement** (regular interaction), aur **Conversion** (clear CTA).

**Example:**
1. **Lead Magnet:** Free "Top 5 Smartphone Under 50,000" PDF bana kar offer karo.
2. **Email Sequence:**
   - **Email 1 (Welcome):** "Mera naam [X] hai, main [niche] ka expert hoon..."
   - **Email 2 (Problem Solve):** "Aaj kal ke smartphones mein yeh 3 features sabse zyada important hain..."
   - **Email 3 (Soft Pitch):** "Mera favorite under 50K phone hai [Product], kyun ke yeh [feature] offer karta hai..."
   - **Email 4 (Hard Pitch):** "Agar aap yeh phone khareedna chahte hain, toh yahan se best deal mil rahi hai [Affiliate Link]."

**Practice:**
Apne email list ke **top 5 subscribers** ko ek **personalized email** bhejiye jo unki kisi specific problem ko solve kare (e.g., "Mujhe lagta hai aapko [product type] ki zaroorat hai...").

**Mini Project:**
Ek **3-email sequence** likhiye jo ek affiliate product promote kare (subject lines + email body draft). Example:
- Subject: "Yeh 1 Feature Sab Smartphones Mein Missing Hai!"
- Body: Problem → Solution → Soft Pitch → CTA.

**Answer Key:**
educate, segment, CTA, trust
