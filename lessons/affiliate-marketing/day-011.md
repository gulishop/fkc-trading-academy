# Day 11 — **Affiliate Marketing Funnel: Kaise Apne Audience Ko Buyer Mein Badlo**

**Concept:**
Affiliate marketing ka asli secret yeh hai ke aapko sirf traffic lane se kaam nahi hai—us traffic ko *buyers* mein convert karna hai. Ek **funnel** (nali) banayein jo audience ko awareness se lekar purchase tak laye. Pehle awareness (content), phir interest (reviews), phir decision (comparisons), aur last mein action (CTA). Har stage par alag strategy hoti hai.

**Example:**
Ek fitness blogger hai jo protein powder promote kar raha hai. Pehle stage (awareness) mein wo "Protein Powder Kya Hai?" article likhta hai. Doosre stage (interest) mein "Top 5 Protein Powders 2024" review post karta hai. Teesre stage (decision) mein "Whey vs Plant Protein: Konsa Better Hai?" comparison post deta hai. Last stage (action) mein wo har post mein "Ab Khareedo" button lagata hai.

**Practice:**
Apne niche ka ek **funnel diagram** banayein (3-4 stages). Har stage ke liye ek content idea sochain jo us stage ko cover kare. (Example: "Mera niche 'home bakery' hai" → Stage 1: "Baking Tips for Beginners", Stage 2: "Best Baking Tools Review", Stage 3: "Cake vs Cupcake: Which Sells More?").

**Mini Project:**
Ek **simple funnel** banayein apne existing content par:
1. Ek awareness post (blog/video) likhein jo audience ko problem samjhaye.
2. Uske saath ek "solution" post link karein (review/comparison).
3. Dono posts mein ek clear CTA add karein (e.g., "Agar aap yeh chahte hain, toh yahan click karein").

**Answer Key:**
awareness, interest, decision, action
