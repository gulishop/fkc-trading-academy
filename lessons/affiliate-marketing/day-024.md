# Day 24 — **Affiliate Marketing Ke "Evergreen Content" Ko Rank Karne Ke Advanced SEO Tips**

**Concept:**
Evergreen content rank karne ke liye SEO ke advanced techniques use karna zaroori hai. Keywords research, on-page SEO, technical SEO, aur backlink strategy ko combine karke long-term traffic gain kiya ja sakta hai. Google ke algorithm updates (jaise Core Updates) ko samajh kar content ko optimize karna chahiye.

**Example:**
Ek "best budget laptops under 50,000" article ko rank karne ke liye:
1. **Keyword Clustering:** "budget laptop," "affordable laptop," "best laptop under 50k" ko ek cluster mein include karo.
2. **On-Page SEO:** Title mein primary keyword, meta description mein benefits mention karo, aur internal linking se related articles se connect karo.
3. **Technical SEO:** Page speed (Core Web Vitals) improve karo, mobile-friendly design use karo, aur schema markup add karo (jaise "Product" schema).
4. **Backlinks:** Tech blogs ya YouTube reviews se do-follow backlinks gain karo.

**Practice:**
Apne affiliate content ke liye 1-2 long-tail keywords identify karo aur unhe on-page SEO ke liye optimize karo (title, meta, H2/H3 headings, internal links).

**Mini Project:**
Ek existing evergreen article ko lekar usse update karo:
- 2-3 new sections add karo (jaise "Buying Guide" ya "FAQs").
- 1-2 high-authority sites se backlinks gain karne ke liye outreach email draft karo.

**Answer Key:**
keywords research, on-page optimization, backlinks, technical SEO
