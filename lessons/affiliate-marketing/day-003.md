# Day 3 — Amazon Associates vs Daraz Affiliate: Kaunsa Choose Karein?

**Concept:**
Amazon Associates aur Daraz Affiliate dono hi popular affiliate programs hain, lekin inke beech key differences hain. Amazon Associates global hai aur zyada products offer karta hai, jabki Daraz local hai aur Pakistan/SAARC regions par focus karta hai. Commission rates, payment methods aur approval criteria bhi alag hain.

**Example:**
1. **Amazon Associates:** Agar aap electronics par content bana rahe hain, toh Amazon par har product ka unique affiliate link milta hai. Commission 1-10% tak ho sakti hai.
2. **Daraz Affiliate:** Agar aap local fashion ya groceries par content bana rahe hain, toh Daraz par higher commission rates (10-20%) mil sakti hain, lekin approval process strict hai.

**Practice:**
Apne niche ke hisab se decide karein ke aapko Amazon Associates ya Daraz Affiliate program join karna chahiye. 2 reasons likhein kyun aapka choice sahi hai.

**Mini Project:**
Ek simple comparison table banaein (2 columns: Amazon Associates vs Daraz Affiliate) aur 3 key points (commission, approval process, best niche) fill karein.

**Answer Key:**
Amazon: global reach, low approval, 1-10%, Daraz: local focus, high commission, strict approval
