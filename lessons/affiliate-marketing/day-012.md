# Day 12 — **Affiliate Marketing Ke Liye "Evergreen Niches" Kaise Dhundein (High Demand + Low Competition)**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke kaise "evergreen niches" (long-term profitable topics) pehle hi identify karein jo affiliate marketing mein consistently sales generate karte hain. Evergreen niches wo hote hain jinke audience hamesha maujud rehti hai (jaise health, finance, beauty) lekin competition bhi zyada hoti hai. Isliye hum "sub-niches" (specific topics) dhundenge jo demand mein hain lekin itne competitive nahi.

**Example:**
Ek broad niche hai "fitness" — iske under sub-niches hain:
- "Home workout for beginners" (low competition)
- "Yoga for weight loss after 40" (specific audience)
- "Best resistance bands under $20" (product-focused)
In sub-niches mein affiliate links (Amazon fitness products, workout programs) lagane se zyada conversion milegi.

**Practice:**
Apne 3 favorite niches (broad) likhein, phir har ek ke 5 sub-niches dhundein. Google Trends ya Amazon Best Sellers se check karein ke konsa sub-niche demand mein hai.

**Mini Project:**
Ek simple Google Sheet banayein jisme:
- Column 1: Broad Niche
- Column 2: Sub-Niche
- Column 3: Demand Score (1-5, Google Trends se)
- Column 4: Competition Score (1-5, manual check)
