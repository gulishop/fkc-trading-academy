# Day 22 — **Affiliate Marketing Ke "Evergreen vs Trending Niches" Ka Faisla: Long-Term vs Quick Wins**

**Concept:**
Evergreen niches (jaise health, finance, parenting) long-term traffic deti hain, jabki trending niches (jaise AI tools, viral products) quick sales le aati hain. Sahi niche choose karne ke liye inke pros/cons samajhein, aur apne skills/interest ke hisaab se balance banayein.

**Example:**
Ek student fitness niche choose karna chahta hai. Uske liye:
- Evergreen: "Home Workouts for Beginners" (hamesha demand rahegi)
- Trending: "AI-Powered Fitness Apps" (abhi viral hai, lekin 1-2 saal baad demand kam ho sakti hai)

**Practice:**
Apne 3 interest areas list karein, phir har ek ke liye:
1. Evergreen content ka potential (Google Trends se check karo)
2. Trending topics (Twitter/X, Reddit, ya Amazon Best Sellers se)
Har niche ke liye 1-2 keywords banao jo aap use kar sakte ho.

**Mini Project:**
"Niche Comparison Spreadsheet" banayein:
- Column 1: Niche name
- Column 2: Evergreen score (1-10)
- Column 3: Trending score (1-10)
- Column 4: Apna interest level (1-10)
Top 3 niches select karein jo in sab criteria par best ho.

fitness,home workouts,AI fitness apps,spreadsheet
