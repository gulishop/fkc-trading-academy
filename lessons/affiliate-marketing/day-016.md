# Day 16 — **Social Media Se Affiliate Traffic: TikTok & Instagram Reels Ka Istemal**

**Concept:**
Social media par affiliate links share karne ka sabse engaging tareeka hai **short-form video content** (TikTok/Reels). Yahan aapko apne niche ke products ko **entertaining ya educational** tareeke se present karna hai taake audience trust banaye aur click karne par majboor ho. Key point: **Direct selling nahi, value provide karo** (e.g., "Top 3 budget smartphones under 20k" + affiliate links).

**Example:**
Ek **TikTok/Reels** banao jisme aap **3 budget smartphones** compare kar rahe hain. Har product ke liye:
1. 3-5 second ka clip show karo (e.g., camera quality, battery life).
2. Caption mein likho: *"Best under 20k? 👇 Link in bio"* (bio mein affiliate link rakho).
3. **Disclosure** add karo: *"#Ad #Affiliate"* (FTC/Facebook guidelines follow karo).

**Practice:**
Aaj hi apne phone se **1 TikTok/Reel** banayein:
- Niche: Apna affiliate program (e.g., Amazon, Daraz).
- Topic: "Top 3 [product category] under [price]" (e.g., "Top 3 wireless earbuds under 5k").
- Hashtags: #AffiliateMarketing #[Niche] (e.g., #TechGadgets).

**Mini Project:**
**"30-Second Product Demo Reel"** banayein:
- 10 sec: Product show karo.
- 10 sec: Benefits batayein.
- 10 sec: CTA: "Link in bio" + disclosure.
Post karein aur **24 ghante mein 5+ clicks** track karein.

**Answer Key:**
Product demo, CTA, disclosure, niche-specific, 5+ clicks
