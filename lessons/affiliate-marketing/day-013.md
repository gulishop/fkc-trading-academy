# Day 13 — **Affiliate Marketing Ke Liye "High-Ticket" Products Kaise Dhundein (Zyada Commission, Zyada Profit)**

**Concept:**
High-ticket affiliate products wo hain jinke bechne par commission zyada milti hai (₹5000+ ya USD $50+). Inka fayda yeh hai ke ek sale se hi zyada commission milti hai, lekin inke liye audience ka trust aur credibility zaroori hoti hai. Aise products un niches mein milte hain jahan customers expensive decisions lete hain (e.g., gadgets, courses, insurance, real estate tools).

**Example:**
1. **Amazon Associates:** "Amazon Luxury Beauty" category ke products (e.g., ₹8000 ka perfume) — 10% commission.
2. **Flipkart Affiliate:** "Smartphones" category — ₹1000-₹3000 commission per sale.
3. **Niche-Specific Programs:** "SEMrush" (₹5000+ commission per sale) ya "Bluehost" hosting (₹5000+ per signup).

**Practice:**
Apne niche ke 3 high-ticket products dhundein jo aap promote kar sakte hain. Unki commission rate, cookie duration, aur customer reviews check karen.

**Mini Project:**
Ek simple comparison post ya video banayein jisme 2-3 high-ticket products ke features, prices, aur aapki affiliate links share karen. (e.g., "2024 Ke 3 Best DSLR Cameras Under ₹80,000")

**Answer Key:**
Amazon beauty, Flipkart smartphones, SEMrush
