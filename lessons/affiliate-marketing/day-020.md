# Day 20 — **Affiliate Marketing Ko "Evergreen" Content Se Long-Term Traffic Kaise Banayein**

**Concept:**
Affiliate marketing mein long-term success ke liye "evergreen content" (aise content jo time ke saath outdated na ho) banana zaroori hai. Isse aapka traffic aur sales months/years tak chalta rehta hai. Evergreen topics (jaise "best budget laptops 2024" ya "how to start a blog") par focus karo jo hamesha search kiye jaate hain.

**Example:**
Ek blog post likho: *"Top 5 Laptops Under 50,000 Rupees in Pakistan (2024)"*. Isme latest models, specs, aur affiliate links add karo. Update karne ke liye sirf price/specs badlo—URL same rehne de.

**Practice:**
Apne niche ka 1 evergreen topic choose karo aur uske liye 300-500 words ka outline banao (headings + subheadings). Example: "Best Wireless Earbuds Under 10,000 in India (2024)".

**Mini Project:**
Ek 1000-word evergreen blog post draft karo (Google Docs mein). Include:
1. Introduction (problem + solution)
2. Top 3-5 products (with specs, pros/cons)
3. Comparison table
4. Call-to-action (affiliate links + discount codes)

**Answer Key:**
evergreen, traffic, update, affiliate links
