# Day 5 — Affiliate Links Ko Sahi Jagah Par Place Karne Ka Secret

**Concept:**
Affiliate links ko kahin bhi bina strategy ke place karne se spam ya trust loss ho sakti hai. Sahi placement se CTR (Click-Through Rate) badhta hai aur conversions increase hote hain. Link placement ka matlab hai apne content mein links ko aise arrange karna jo natural lage aur user ko action lene par majboor kar de.

**Example:**
Ek product review article mein:
1. **Introduction** mein sirf 1 link (product ka naam) — "Maine yeh XYZ vacuum cleaner liya hai..."
2. **Features section** mein har feature ke saath relevant link — "Iske powerful suction ke liye [yeh link] check karein"
3. **Conclusion** mein "Buy Now" ya "Deal Check Karein" ka prominent button/link

**Practice:**
Apne last article (Day 4 ka) mein 3 affiliate links ko aise place karein:
- 1st link: Product name ke saath (intro mein)
- 2nd link: Kisi ek key feature ke saath
- 3rd link: Strong CTA (Call-to-Action) ke saath conclusion mein

**Mini Project:**
Ek 300-400 words ka "Top 3 Budget Smartphones 2024" article likhein aur usmein:
- Har smartphone ka naam (1st link)
- Battery life feature ke saath link
- "Sabse sasta option yahan check karein" (3rd link)

**Answer Key:**
product name link, feature link, CTA link
