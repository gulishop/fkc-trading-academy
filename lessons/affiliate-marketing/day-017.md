# Day 17 — **Email Marketing Se Affiliate Sales: Apne Subscribers Ko Buyers Mein Badlo**

**Concept:**
Email marketing affiliate sales ka sabse powerful source hai kyunke subscribers already trust karte hain. Yahan pehle value provide karo, phir affiliate offers share karo. Important: Har email mein 1 hi CTA ho (clear aur specific).

**Example:**
1. **Welcome Email Series** (3-5 emails):
   - Email 1: "Mera 3-step formula for [niche problem]"
   - Email 2: "Log is solution ke liye [affiliate product] use karte hain"
   - Email 3: Case study + discount link
2. **Promotional Email**:
   - Subject: "Aaj hi le lo 20% discount!"
   - Body: "Mere favorite [product] ka yeh deal 24 hours tak hai"

**Practice:**
Apne email list ke 10 subscribers ko ek promotional email bhejiye. Subject line test karo (A/B testing ke liye do subject lines likhein).

**Mini Project:**
Ek 3-email welcome series draft karo (niche: fitness/tech/finance mein se koi ek). Har email ka purpose, subject line, aur 2-3 main points likhein.

**Answer Key:**
value-first, single-CTA, urgency, personalization
