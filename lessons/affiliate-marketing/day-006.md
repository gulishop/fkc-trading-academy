# Day 6 — **Social Media Se Affiliate Traffic Kaise Badhayein (TikTok & Instagram Reels Strategy)**

**Concept:**
Social media par affiliate links share karne ka sabse effective tareeka hai **short-form video content** (TikTok/Reels) jo naturally product ko promote kare. Basics: 1) **Problem-Solution** format (e.g., "Mera phone kharab ho gaya, yeh product ne bachaya!"), 2) **Demo-style** videos (e.g., "Yeh cream ne mere 10 din mein daag halka kar diye"), 3) **Trend-jacking** (trending sounds/hashtags use karke). Link placement: **Bio link** ya **Swipe-up** (10K+ followers ke liye) ya **Comment section** mein "Link in bio" mention karna.

**Example:**
Ek **TikTok Reel** banao jisme:
1. 3 second mein problem batayein ("Mera laptop slow ho gaya hai")
2. 5 second mein solution dikhayein ("Yeh SSD ne mere laptop ki speed double kar di!")
3. Last 2 second mein CTA: "Link in bio se le lo @50% discount!"

**Practice:**
Apne phone ya laptop ke **kisi 1 product** ke liye 15-second ka script likhein jo **problem-solution** format mein ho. Trending sound use karne ke liye **TikTok/Instagram Reels** explore page se 1 sound copy kar lein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **TikTok/Reels** video shoot karke upload karein (draft mein bhi chalega). Caption mein **affiliate link** ke liye "Link in bio" ya "Swipe up" mention karein. Hashtags: #AffiliateMarketing #ProductReview #[Niche].

**Answer Key:**
problem-solution, trend-sound, link-in-bio, CTA
