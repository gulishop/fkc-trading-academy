# Day 5 — **Automated Email Sequences: Drip Campaigns Jo Convert Karein**

**Concept:**
Drip campaigns automated email sequences hain jo specific triggers (signup, purchase, inactivity) par users ko targeted messages bhejte hain. Inka maqsad engagement badhana, trust build karna aur conversions increase karna hai. Har email ka apna purpose hota hai—welcome, nurture, offer ya abandoned cart recovery.

**Example:**
Ek SaaS company ka drip campaign:
1. **Day 1 (Welcome):** "Aapka 7-day free trial start ho gaya hai!" (Hook + benefit)
2. **Day 3 (Nurture):** "3 ways our tool saves you 10+ hours/week" (Social proof + value)
3. **Day 5 (Offer):** "Upgrade now & get 20% off—limited time!" (CTA + urgency)

**Practice:**
Apne ya kisi imaginary business ke liye ek 3-email drip sequence likhein jo:
- **Trigger:** User ne free trial sign up kiya ho
- **Goal:** Paid subscription convert karna ho

**Mini Project:**
Ek "Abandoned Cart Recovery" drip sequence design karein (3 emails) jo online store ke liye ho. Har email ka subject line, main body (2-3 lines) aur CTA specify karein.

**Answer Key:**
welcome email, nurture content, offer hook, urgency
