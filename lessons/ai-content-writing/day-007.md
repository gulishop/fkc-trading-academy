# Day 7 — **AI-Assisted Editing: Apni Writing Ko Pro-Level Par Le Jaana**

**Concept:**
AI tools (Grammarly, Hemingway, ProWritingAid) se apni writing ko edit karna seekhiye. AI sirf grammar fix nahi karta—yeh clarity, tone, aur engagement bhi improve karta hai. Lekin AI ko boss na banaen; use apne writing partner ki tarah treat karein.

**Example:**
Ek draft liyein:
*"Maine kal subah coffee pi aur office jaana tha lekin traffic itna zyada tha ki main late ho gaya."*
AI edits:
1. Grammar: "pi" → "pii"
2. Clarity: "aur" → "lekin" (better flow)
3. Tone: "itna zyada" → "bahut zyada" (more natural)
Final: *"Maine kal subah coffee pii aur office jaana tha, lekin traffic bahut zyada tha isliye main late ho gaya."*

**Practice:**
Apne 100-word kisi bhi topic par likhe hue text ko Hemingway Editor ya Grammarly par paste karein. Top 3 improvements note karein jo AI suggest karta hai.

**Mini Project:**
Ek 200-word blog paragraph likhein (kisi bhi topic par), phir:
1. AI se edit karain (Grammarly + ProWritingAid).
2. Manual edits bhi add karein (apne hisab se).
3. Compare karein ki kaun si edits better hai—AI ki ya aapki.

**Answer Key:**
grammar, clarity, flow, tone
