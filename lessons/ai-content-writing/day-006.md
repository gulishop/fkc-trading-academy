# Day 6 — **Voice Search Optimization: Bolo, Samjho, Rank Karo**

**Concept:**
Aaj hum seekhenge ke voice search ke liye content kaise optimize karein. Log ab "Hey Google" aur "Alexa" se pooch rahe hain, isliye apne content ko conversational, question-based aur short answer format mein likhna zaroori hai. Long-tail keywords aur natural language ka use karna hai.

**Example:**
Ek user pooche: *"Best budget smartphone under 20,000 in Pakistan 2024"*
Apna content is tarah likhein:
- **Introduction:** "2024 mein budget smartphones ka market bohot competitive hai..."
- **Answer:** "Under 20,000 mein aaj ke top 3 smartphones hain: [Model 1], [Model 2], [Model 3]."
- **FAQ Section:** "Kya in phones ki battery life achi hai?" (Voice search ke liye extra questions add karein)

**Practice:**
Apne laptop ya phone par voice search karke 3 questions poochen jo aapke niche likhe content se related hon. Phir un questions ke answers ko apne content mein add karein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek 200-300 words ka blog post likhein jismein aap ek common problem solve karein (e.g., "How to remove blackheads at home"). Post ke end mein ek FAQ section add karein jismein 5-7 voice search-style questions hon.

**Answer Key:**
voice search, conversational, long-tail keywords, FAQ, natural language
