# Day 10 — **AI-Powered Personalization: Har Customer Ko Unique Feel Karwayein**

**Concept:**
AI se apni copy ko personalize karna seekhiye taake har customer ko lagay ke yeh sirf uske liye likha gaya hai. AI tools (jaise Hyper-Personalization engines ya dynamic content generators) ki madad se aap apni writing ko audience ke interests, behavior aur past interactions ke hisaab se customize kar sakte hain. Isse engagement badhta hai aur conversions bhi.

**Example:**
Ek e-commerce brand ke liye product description likhte waqt, AI tool customer ke browsing history ya purchase pattern ke hisaab se dynamic content generate karega. Jaise:
*"Aapne last month mein running shoes dekhi hain—yeh new arrival shoes aapke style ke perfect match hain!"*

**Practice:**
Apne ya kisi doosre brand ke liye ek 3-line AI-generated copy likhein jo ek specific audience segment (jaise "new moms" ya "tech enthusiasts") ko target kare. AI tool (jaise Copy.ai ya Jasper) ka use karke dekhein.

**Mini Project:**
Ek **AI-powered email sequence** design karen jo customer ke past purchases ya website activity ke hisaab se personalize ho. 3 emails ka outline banaen (subject lines + first line of each email).

**Answer Key:**
personalized hooks, dynamic segments, higher engagement, conversion boost
