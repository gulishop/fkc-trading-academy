# Day 1 — Copywriting Basics: Hook, Benefit & CTA

**Concept:**
Copywriting ka matlab hai words ko istemal karke readers ko action lene par majboor karna. Aaj hum 3 core elements seekhenge:
1. **Hook** – Pehla sentence jo padhne wale ko roke rakhe.
2. **Benefit** – Reader ko yeh batao ke tum usko kya dene jaa rahe ho.
3. **CTA (Call-to-Action)** – Reader ko kya karna hai, clear instruction do.

**Example:**
Imagine you’re selling a *"10-Minute Morning Routine"* ebook.
❌ **Bad Hook:** "This book is about morning routines."
✅ **Good Hook:** "Wake up tired? This 10-minute routine will give you energy for the whole day—without coffee!"
**Benefit:** "Learn 5 science-backed habits to boost focus and lose weight effortlessly."
**CTA:** "Download now and start tomorrow!"

**Practice:**
Apne favorite product/service ka ad copy likho. Pehle 1 sentence ka **hook**, phir 1 line ka **benefit**, aur last mein **CTA** likhna hai.

**Mini Project:**
Ek WhatsApp status ya Instagram caption likho jo:
- Hook: "Tired of wasting hours on social media?"
- Benefit: "This free tool blocks distractions and saves 2 hours daily."
- CTA: "DM me ‘FOCUS’ to get it now!"

**Answer Key:**
hook, benefit, cta
