# Day 11 — **Brand Voice & Tone: Apne Brand Ki Personality Banana**

**Concept:**
Har brand ki ek unique "voice" hoti hai—jaise friendly, professional, humorous, ya inspirational. Tone is voice ka real-time expression hai (e.g., same brand "professional" tone se "urgent" ho sakta hai). Aaj seekhein ke apne brand ki personality define karna kyun zaroori hai, aur kaise consistent rahe bina monotonous lage.

**Example:**
Ek fitness brand:
- **Voice:** Motivational, encouraging
- **Tone variations:**
  - Blog post: *"Har step aapko stronger banata hai!"*
  - Urgent ad: *"Aaj hi shuru karo—kal zyada mushkil ho sakti hai!"*
  - Customer complaint response: *"Hum samajhte hain aapka frustration—chalo milkar hal nikalte hain."*

**Practice:**
Apne brand (ya imaginary brand) ke liye 3 sentences likhein—har sentence same "voice" mein lekin alag "tone" mein. Example:
1. Product launch: *"Yeh naya gadget aapki life badal dega!"* (excited)
2. Refund policy: *"Hum aapki satisfaction ko priority dete hain."* (professional)
3. Social media reply: *"Bhai, yeh toh mazaak hai—chalo discuss karte hain!"* (casual)

**Mini Project:**
Ek "Brand Voice Guide" banao (1 paragraph):
- Brand name: [Apna naam]
- Voice: [2 adjectives, e.g., "friendly, quirky"]
- 3 tone examples (different scenarios ke liye)
- 3 words jinse avoid karna hai (e.g., "boring," "too salesy")

motivational, consistent, adaptable, personality
