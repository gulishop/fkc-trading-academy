# Day 9 — **Portfolio Se Khud Ko Market Karo: Trust Badhao, Gig Jeeto**

**Concept:**
Aaj seekhoge ke apna content writing portfolio kaise banaya jaye jo clients ko attract kare. Portfolio sirf apni writing samples nahi, balki ek "trust builder" hai. Ismein aapko apne best work ko showcase karna hai, niche specify karna hai ke aap kya kar sakte hain, aur potential clients ko yakeen dilana hai ke aap unke project ko handle kar sakte hain.

**Example:**
1. **Niche Choose Karo:** Agar aap health, tech, ya finance mein specialize karte hain, toh portfolio bhi usi area ka ho.
2. **Platform Chuno:** Behance, Contently, Journo Portfolio, ya apni personal website (WordPress/Wix) par host karein.
3. **Samples Add Karo:** 3-5 best articles/copy jo aapne likhe hain (chahen toh AI se edit karke bhi). Har sample ke saath ek short description likhein ke ismein aapne kya skills use ki hain (e.g., "SEO-optimized blog post with keyword density 2%").
4. **Testimonials:** Agar koi client ya friend ne aapki writing ki tareef ki ho, toh usko add karein.

**Practice:**
Apne portfolio ke liye 1-2 writing samples select karein (agar nahi hain toh pehle se likhe gaye kisi bhi article ko improve karein). Har sample ke saath 2-3 lines ka description likhein ke aapne ismein kya techniques use ki hain.

**Mini Project:**
Ek simple portfolio page banayein (Google Docs ya Notion par bhi chalega) jismein:
- Aapka introduction (1 paragraph)
- 2-3 writing samples (title + short description)
- Contact info (email/phone)

**Answer Key:**
writing samples, niche, testimonials, contact details
