# Day 4 — **Landing Page Ka Magic: Trust Badhao, Conversions Badhao**

**Concept:**
Landing page copywriting ek product/service ko bechne ka art hai—aisa page jo visitor ko sirf 5-10 seconds mein action lene par majboor kar de. Ismein **headline**, **benefit-driven bullet points**, **social proof**, aur **urgent CTA** ka combination hota hai. AI se yeh kaam fast ho sakta hai, lekin *human touch* (emotion + clarity) zaroori hai.

**Example:**
Ek fitness app ka landing page:
- **Headline:** "30 Days Mein 10 KG Kam Karne Ka Secret"
- **Bullet Points:** "AI-powered diet planner + live coach + progress tracker"
- **Social Proof:** "10,000+ users ne 5-star reviews diye"
- **CTA:** "Free Trial Shuru Karein — 7 Din Ke Liye"

**Practice:**
Apne pasandida product/service ka landing page draft likhein. Headline + 3 bullet points + 1 CTA likhein (AI se help lena allowed hai).

**Mini Project:**
Ek **fake landing page** banayein (Canva/WordPress par) ek imaginary SaaS product ke liye (e.g., "AI Resume Builder"). Include karein:
1. Hero section (headline + subheadline)
2. 3 key benefits
3. Testimonial (fake bhi chalega)
4. CTA button ("Start Free Now")

**Answer Key:**
headline, benefits, social proof, urgent CTA
