Day 2 — TikTok Par Trending Sounds Kaise Dhunde (Aur Unka Sahi Istemal Karein) Concept. TikTok ka algorithm trending sounds par bhatakta hai—yeh videos ko zyada reach dete hain. Har sound ki apni "trend cycle" hoti hai: kuch 1-2 din mein viral hote hain, aur kuch hafte tak chalte hain. Aapko Discover page, TikTok’s Creative Center, aur competitor analysis se sounds dhundne hain jo aapke niche ke hisab se relevant ho. Example. 1. Discover Page. TikTok app kholo → "Discover" tab par jao → "Trending" section check karo. 2. Creative Center. https://www.tiktok.com/business/en/creativecenter → "Trending Sounds" filter lagao. 3. Competitor Check. Kisi top creator ke video par click karo → "Use this sound" par tap karo → dekh lo ki kitne creators isko use kar rahe hain. Practice. Aaj ke 10 minutes mein: - Apne niche ke 3 trending sounds note karo (Creative Center se). - Unmein se 1 sound select karo jo aapke content style se match kare. - Us sound ka audio waveform screenshot karo (future reference ke liye). Sound Trend Tracker. Ek Google Sheet/Notion page banayein jismein aap: - Sound name | Trend duration | Use cases | Competitor count (kitne creators use kar rahe hain) - Har hafte 2-3 naye sounds add karo aur purane ko update karo.
