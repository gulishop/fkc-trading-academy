Day 3 — Trending Designs Kaise Dhundein? (2024 Ke Liye) Concept. Print on Demand (POD) mein successful designs trending topics, audience interests, aur seasonal demand par depend karte hain. Aaj hum seekhenge ke kaise free aur paid tools se latest design trends dhundein, aur apne niche ke hisab se relevant ideas kaise select karen. Trending designs ka pata lagana ek ongoing process hai – har mahine naye trends aate hain. Example. 1. Pinterest Trends. Pinterest pe search karen "2024 t-shirt trends" ya "trending quotes for [niche]". Top pins ke designs dekhein aur unke color schemes, fonts, aur themes note karen. 2. Etsy/Redbubble. Best-selling products ke designs check karen. "Trending" ya "Top Seller" filters use karen aur dekhein ke kaun se designs repeat ho rahe hain. 3. Google Trends. "Print on demand trends 2024" search karen aur related queries dekhein. Agar "AI art for t-shirts" trending hai, toh is par focus kar sakte hain. Practice. Apne niche (e.g., motivational, pet lovers, gaming) ke liye 3 trending design ideas dhundein. Har idea ke saath ek short reason bhi likhein ke yeh trend kyun relevant hai. Mini Project. Ek Pinterest board banayein aur usme 10 trending design ideas save karen. Har design ke niche ek line likhein ke yeh trend kyun popular ho raha hai (e.g., "Minimalist cat quotes – 2024 mein pet niche mein demand hai").
